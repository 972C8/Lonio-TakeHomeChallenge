every stakeholder. This view answers, for each piece: what, when we ask, when
it is required, what it depends on, and who needs it.

## Derived results (never asked)

- **Insurance start date** – The pension fund insurance start date is derived from the latest first working day: if the first working day is the 1st of a month, insurance starts that day; otherwise on the 1st of the following month (e.g. 15.03 -> 01.04). It must always be the 1st of a month and follows every change of the first working day. ([First working day and insurance start date](feature.md#SEC-02-start-date-insurance))
- **Civil status for the pension fund** – The civil status is entered at the fund from its list (Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed). Provisionally, Registered partnership and Separated are entered as Married. ([Civil status and spouse for the pension fund](feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse))
- **Civil status for source tax** – For source tax, married and registered partnership count as married; single, divorced, separated and widowed count as single, as in the calculator. ([Civil status and spouse for the pension fund](feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse))
- **Spouse exemption from source tax** – An employee who is married or in a registered partnership with a spouse/partner who lives in Switzerland and is Swiss or holds a C permit is not subject to source tax. Separated employees count as single and get no exemption. ([Source tax liability check](feature.md#SEC-04-source-tax-liability))
- **Subject to source tax** – An employee living abroad is always subject to source tax, whatever the nationality. An employee living in Switzerland is subject only if not Swiss (Swiss citizenship counts even with another nationality), without a C permit, and not exempted by a Swiss or C-permit spouse/partner living in Switzerland. Only employees subject to source tax get the source tax questions. ([Source tax liability check](feature.md#SEC-04-source-tax-liability))
- **Residence for the source tax tariff** – The calculator's 'main residence in Switzerland' and 'state of residence' answers are derived from the home address country: Switzerland, Germany, Liechtenstein, or other country. Switzerland and other countries leave the base tariff unchanged; Germany and Liechtenstein open the cross-border questions. ([Employees living abroad (Germany, Liechtenstein)](feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad))
- **Church-tax suffix** – The tariff code ends with Y for Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed, and N for none or other. ([Source tax tariff basics and computed result](feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics))
- **Married tariff letter** – For employees counting as married for source tax: C if the spouse/partner works or receives replacement income, otherwise B. ([Source tax tariff basics and computed result](feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics))
- **Child result (per child)** – For each child, the answers decide whether the child counts for the tariff digit and, for employees counting as single, whether it counts as an H child (single-parent tariff). Rows marked inferred were not observed in the calculator and must be verified before go-live (D-009). ([Children for the source tax tariff](feature.md#SEC-06-children))
- **Child digit of the tariff code** – The digit of the tariff code is the number of children whose child result is 'counts' or 'counts as H child' (0 if no children). Evaluated per child and summed over all children. ([Children for the source tax tariff](feature.md#SEC-06-children))
- **Single-parent H tariff** – For an employee counting as single, if any child counts as an H child, the tariff letter is H (e.g. A1 + H1 -> H2N). True if this holds for at least one child. ([Children for the source tax tariff](feature.md#SEC-06-children))
- **Cross-border (Grenzgänger) tariff applies** – For employees living in Germany the cross-border tariff applies if they return daily and have provided a certificate of residence, or if they do not return daily, a daily return is reasonable and they do not expect more than 60 work-related non-return days. Every other case gets the normal tariff. 'Not yet' for the certificate counts as 'no'. ([Employees living abroad (Germany, Liechtenstein)](feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad))
- **Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability** – For employees living in Liechtenstein who return daily, or do not expect more than 45 work-related non-return days, the result is 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code; it is flagged for HR. With more than 45 non-return days the normal tariff code applies. ([Employees living abroad (Germany, Liechtenstein)](feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad))
- **Tariff letter** – Base letter: married -> C (spouse works) or B (spouse does not); single -> H if any child counts as H child, otherwise A. If the cross-border tariff applies, the letter is mapped A->L, B->M, C->N, H->P. ([Source tax tariff basics and computed result](feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics))
- **Source tax result** – Lonio computes the source tax result for every employee subject to source tax and records it for HR: the full tariff code = tariff letter + child digit + church-tax suffix (e.g. C1Y, L0Y). Liechtenstein residents with daily return or at most 45 non-return days get 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' flagged for HR; with more than 45 days the tariff code subject to the double taxation agreement. Employees not subject to source tax get no result. ([Source tax tariff basics and computed result](feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics))
- **Permit reminder** – At the end of the questionnaire, every non-Swiss employee and every employee living abroad is reminded to bring their permit on the first working day. HR words the reminder; it collects no data. ([Completing and submitting the questionnaire](feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission))
- **Discard answers that no longer apply** – When the employee changes an earlier answer so that later questions no longer apply, their answers may be kept while the employee goes back and forth, but are discarded on submission. Only answers to questions that apply are recorded. ([Completing and submitting the questionnaire](feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission))
