# Information catalogue

Every piece of information the process needs, **asked once** and reused by
every stakeholder. This view answers, for each piece: what, when we ask, when
it is required, what it depends on, and who needs it.

## Derived results (never asked)

- **Insurance start date** – The pension fund insurance start date is derived from the latest first working day: if it is the 1st of a month, insurance starts that day; otherwise on the 1st of the following month (e.g. 15.03. → 01.04.). It is always derived again when the first working day changes. ([First working day and insurance start date](feature.md#SEC-02-start-date-insurance))
- **Civil status for the pension fund** – The civil status entered at the pension fund: Single, Married, Divorced and Widowed are taken as answered; registered partnership and separated are entered as Married (provisional). ([Civil status and spouse for the pension fund](feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse))
- **Civil status for source tax** – For source tax, Married and Registered partnership count as 'Married or registered partnership'; Single, Divorced, Widowed and Separated count as 'Not married'. ([Civil status and spouse for the pension fund](feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse))
- **Spouse exempts from source tax** – An employee who is married or in a registered partnership is exempt from source tax if the spouse/partner lives in Switzerland and is Swiss or holds a C permit. ([Source tax liability check](feature.md#SEC-04-source-tax-liability))
- **Subject to source tax** – An employee living abroad is subject to source tax whatever their nationality. An employee living in Switzerland is not subject if Swiss (including dual citizens) or holding a C permit, or if exempted by a Swiss or C-permit spouse/partner living in Switzerland; otherwise subject. Only subject employees get the source tax questions. ([Source tax liability check](feature.md#SEC-04-source-tax-liability))
- **State of residence for source tax** – The calculator's residence answers are derived from the home address country: Switzerland, Germany, Liechtenstein, or other country. Switzerland and other countries leave the base tariff unchanged. ([Employees living abroad (Germany, Liechtenstein)](feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad))
- **Church-tax suffix** – The tariff code ends in Y for Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed, and N for none or other. ([Source tax tariff basics and computed result](feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics))
- **Base letter for married employees** – For employees married or in a registered partnership: C if the spouse/partner is employed or receives replacement income, otherwise B. ([Source tax tariff basics and computed result](feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics))
- **Child result (per child)** – For each child the answers decide whether the child is not counted, counted, or (for non-married employees) counted with H, following the calculator's child logic. Rows marked inferred were not observed in the calculator and must be verified (OQ-calculator-coverage). ([Children for the source tax tariff](feature.md#SEC-06-children))
- **Child digit of the tariff code** – The digit of the tariff code is the number of children whose result is 'Counted' or 'Counted with H'; 0 without children. (The formal expression covers one child block; the digit is the sum over all children.) ([Children for the source tax tariff](feature.md#SEC-06-children))
- **H tariff for non-married employees** – A non-married employee with at least one child counted with H gets an H tariff (e.g. A1 + H1 → H2N). ([Children for the source tax tariff](feature.md#SEC-06-children))
- **Cross-border tariff applies** – For employees living in Germany the cross-border tariff applies if they return daily and have provided a certificate of residence, or if they do not return daily, a daily return is reasonable and they do not expect more than 60 non-return days. 'Not yet' for the certificate counts as no. In all other cases the normal tariff applies. ([Employees living abroad (Germany, Liechtenstein)](feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad))
- **Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability** – For employees living in Liechtenstein who return daily or expect at most 45 non-return days, the result is 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR. With more than 45 non-return days the normal tariff code applies. ([Employees living abroad (Germany, Liechtenstein)](feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad))
- **Tariff letter** – Base letter: C or B for married employees/registered partners (spouse employed or not); H for non-married employees with an H child; otherwise A. If the cross-border tariff applies, the letter is mapped A→L, B→M, C→N, H→P. ([Source tax tariff basics and computed result](feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics))
- **Source tax result** – Lonio computes the full Zurich tariff code for every employee subject to source tax: tariff letter, child digit and church-tax suffix (e.g. C1Y, L0Y, H2N). For Liechtenstein residents with daily return or at most 45 non-return days the result is 'In principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement', flagged for HR. Employees not subject get no result. The result is always derived again from the latest answers. ([Source tax tariff basics and computed result](feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics))
- **Permit reminder** – At the end of the questionnaire, every non-Swiss employee and every employee living abroad (whatever their nationality) is reminded to bring their permit on the first working day. HR words the reminder, so that a Swiss citizen living abroad can see it does not apply to them. It collects no data. ([Completing and submitting the questionnaire](feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission))
- **Discard answers that no longer apply** – If the employee changes an earlier answer so that later questions no longer apply, those answers may be kept while the employee goes back and forth, but are discarded on submission. After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee; later changes are made by HR. ([Completing and submitting the questionnaire](feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission))
