# Information catalogue

Every piece of information the process needs, **asked once** and reused by
every stakeholder. This view answers, for each piece: what, when we ask, when
it is required, what it depends on, and who needs it.

## Derived results (never asked)

- **Questionnaire flow** – The questionnaire has exactly eight steps in this order (assumption D-047). Each question appears only when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person. Answers are saved as the employee goes; the employee can resume until submitting; going back and changing an earlier answer keeps the other answers until submission; submitting needs one confirmation on a summary page (D-050). For Liechtenstein residents the cross-border questions come before the source tax details, which are skipped when there is in principle no source tax liability (D-040). ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
- **Insurance start date** – The pension fund insurance start date is derived from the first working day (the contractual start date, assumption D-039), never asked: if the first working day is the 1st of a month, insurance starts that day; otherwise on the 1st of the following month (e.g. 15.03.2026 gives 01.04.2026). It is always derived again from the latest first working day. ([First working day and insurance start date](feature.md#SEC-03-employment-start))
- **Civil status for the pension fund** – The fund only accepts Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed. Registered partnership and Separated are entered as Married (with the partner's/spouse's date of birth); the others unchanged (assumption D-003). ([Civil status and spouse data](feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse))
- **Canton for the pension fund** – Employees living in Switzerland: the canton they chose. Employees living abroad: the canton of the place of work, Zurich for Muster AG, not asked (assumption D-002). ([Personal details and home address](feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address))
- **Subject to source tax** – An employee living abroad is subject to source tax whatever their nationality. An employee living in Switzerland is subject only if not Swiss (Swiss citizenship, also as dual nationality, counts as Swiss), without a C permit, and not married or in a registered partnership with a Swiss national or C-permit holder who lives in Switzerland. Separated counts as single, so no spouse exemption. ('Here' in HR's wording means Switzerland; assumption D-043.) Always derived again from the latest answers. ([Who is subject to source tax](feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability))
- **Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability** – Liechtenstein residents are asked the source tax questions (HR's rule). With daily return, or without daily return and at most 45 non-return days, the result is 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR, and the remaining tariff questions are skipped. With more than 45 non-return days the normal tariff code applies with the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement', flagged for HR (assumption D-040). ([Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence))
- **Civil status for source tax** – Married and Registered partnership count as 'married or registered partnership'; Single, Divorced, Widowed and Separated count as 'single, divorced, separated or widowed', as in the calculator. ([Civil status and spouse data](feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse))
- **Residence for source tax** – The country of residence is grouped as in the calculator: Switzerland, Germany, Liechtenstein or other country. Switzerland and other country lead to the base tariff without further residence questions. ([Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence))
- **Church-tax suffix** – Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed gives suffix Y; none or other gives N. ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Per-child source tax result** – For each child, the answers give one of: counted, counted with H (single parents), or not counted - following the calculator. Observed calculator rows win over the derived logic (assumption D-046); rows marked inferred are not observed and are checked before go-live (D-009). ([Source tax tariff: children](feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children))
- **Child digit of the tariff code** – The child digit equals the number of children whose per-child result is 'counted' or 'counted with H' (e.g. three counted children give C3Y). Without children the digit is 0. (The expression gives, per child, whether that child is counted; the digit is the number of children for which it is true.) ([Source tax tariff: children](feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children))
- **Base tariff letter** – Married/registered partners: C if the spouse is gainfully employed, otherwise B. Single, divorced, separated or widowed: H if at least one child is counted with H, otherwise A. ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Cross-border tariff applies (Germany)** – For Germany residents the cross-border tariff applies with daily return and a certificate of residence ('not yet' counts as no), or without daily return when daily return is reasonable and the employee does not expect more than 60 non-return days. Otherwise the normal tariff applies. Other countries never get the cross-border tariff. ([Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence))
- **Tariff letter** – Without the cross-border tariff the base letter applies. With it: A becomes L, B becomes M, C becomes N, H becomes P (and G would become Q, as the calculator shows; G does not occur because replacement income is out of scope, D-006). ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Source tax tariff code** – Lonio computes the full Zurich tariff code for every employee subject to source tax, replacing HR's manual calculator step: one text made of tariff letter, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. 'C1Y', 'L0Y', 'N1Y'. Employees not subject to source tax get 'no tariff code'. Liechtenstein residents with in principle no liability get 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement', flagged for HR; with more than 45 non-return days the code carries the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement', flagged for HR. If HR later changes an answer so that unanswered questions apply, the result is 'incomplete - HR must complete' until HR enters them (assumption D-048). Always derived again from the latest answers. Computed codes are not legally binding, like the calculator's. ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Permit reminder** – At the end of the questionnaire, every non-Swiss employee living in Switzerland and every employee living abroad (whatever their nationality, which is not asked of them) is reminded to bring their permit on the first working day. HR provides the wording before go-live, worded so that a Swiss citizen living abroad sees it does not apply to them. It collects no data. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
- **Discard answers that no longer apply** – Answers to questions that no longer apply after an earlier answer changes are kept while the employee goes back and forth, and discarded on submission. The same applies when HR changes an answer after submission (D-048). ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
- **Read-only after submission** – After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Later changes (a move, marriage, new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality, a moved first working day) are made by HR (specified later). All derived results are always derived again from the latest answers. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
