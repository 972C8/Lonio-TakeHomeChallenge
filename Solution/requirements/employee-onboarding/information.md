# Information catalogue

Every piece of information the process needs, **asked once** and reused by
every stakeholder. This view answers, for each piece: what, when we ask, when
it is required, what it depends on, and who needs it.

## Derived results (never asked)

- **Questionnaire flow overview** – The questionnaire is in English and asks each piece of information once. Steps in order: (1) Personal details - first name, last name, date of birth. (2) Home address - street, house number, postcode, town, country; canton only if living in Switzerland. (3) Start - first working day. (4) Civil status - civil status; spouse's/partner's date of birth if married, in a registered partnership or separated. (5) Source tax status - only for employees living in Switzerland: nationality; C permit if not Swiss; for non-Swiss without C permit who are married or in a registered partnership: spouse's nationality, spouse's C permit if not Swiss, spouse's country of residence if Swiss or C permit. (6) Source tax details - only if subject to source tax: confession; spouse works (married/registered partnership); children and the per-child questions (repeated per child); then cross-border questions for residents of Germany or Liechtenstein. (7) End - permit reminder for non-Swiss employees and everyone living abroad. On submission answers that no longer apply are discarded and the questionnaire becomes read-only for the employee; derived results are computed from the latest answers. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
- **Insurance start date** – The pension fund insurance start date is derived from the first working day: if the first working day is the 1st of a month, insurance starts that day; otherwise on the 1st of the following month (e.g. 15.03 gives 01.04). It is always derived again from the latest first working day, so a moved start date updates it. ([First working day and insurance start date](feature.md#SEC-03-employment-start))
- **Civil status entered at the pension fund** – The fund only offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed. Provisionally, Registered partnership and Separated are entered as Married (with the partner's/spouse's date of birth); the other values are entered as given. ([Civil status and spouse data](feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse))
- **Subject to source tax** – An employee living abroad is subject to source tax regardless of nationality. An employee living in Switzerland is subject only if not a Swiss citizen (anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss), without a C permit, and not married to / in a registered partnership with a Swiss citizen or C-permit holder who lives in Switzerland. Only employees subject to source tax are asked the source tax questions. Liechtenstein residents count as subject (they are asked the questions); their result may still be 'in principle no source tax liability'. ([Who is subject to source tax](feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability))
- **Civil status group for source tax** – For source tax, Married and Registered partnership count as 'married or registered partnership'; Single, Divorced, Widowed and Separated count as 'single, divorced, separated or widowed'. ([Civil status and spouse data](feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse))
- **Residence for source tax** – The calculator's residence answer is derived from the country of the home address: Switzerland, Germany, Liechtenstein, or any other country. Switzerland and other countries lead straight to the base tariff; Germany and Liechtenstein have their own follow-up questions. ([Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence))
- **Church-tax suffix** – The tariff code ends in Y for Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed, and N for none or other. ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Per-child result** – Each child is evaluated on its own: it counts, counts with H (single group only), or does not count. Rows marked as inferred were not observed in the calculator and must be checked before go-live (OQ-calculator-coverage). ([Source tax tariff: children](feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children))
- **Child digit of the tariff code** – The digit of the tariff code is the number of children whose per-child result is 'counts' or 'counts with H'; 0 if the employee has no children. (The expression shows the contribution of one child; for several children the contributions are added up.) ([Source tax tariff: children](feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children))
- **Base tariff letter** – Married or registered partnership: C if the spouse/partner works or receives replacement income, otherwise B. Single, divorced, separated or widowed: H if any child counts with H, otherwise A. Tariffs G and Q (replacement income) and special tariffs such as D do not occur in onboarding. ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Cross-border (Grenzgänger) tariff applies** – For employees living in Germany the cross-border tariff applies if they return daily and have handed in a certificate of residence ('not yet' counts as no), or if they do not return daily, a daily return would be reasonable, and they do not expect more than 60 non-return days. In all other cases (and for all other countries) the normal tariff applies. ([Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence))
- **Final tariff letter** – If the cross-border tariff applies, the base letter is replaced: A by L, B by M, C by N, H by P. Otherwise the base letter is used. ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability** – An employee living in Liechtenstein who returns daily, or does not return daily but expects at most 45 work-related non-return days, gets 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR. With more than 45 non-return days the normal tariff code applies. ([Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence))
- **Source tax tariff code** – For every employee subject to source tax Lonio computes the Zurich tariff code from the answers: final tariff letter + child digit + church-tax suffix (e.g. A0N, C1Y, L0Y, N1Y). For Liechtenstein residents with daily return or at most 45 non-return days the result is 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead, flagged for HR. Employees not subject to source tax get no tariff code. The result is always derived again from the latest answers and is recorded for HR. ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Permit reminder** – At the end of the questionnaire every non-Swiss employee and every employee living abroad (whatever their nationality, as it is not asked of them) is reminded to bring their permit on the first working day. HR words the reminder so that a Swiss citizen living abroad can see it does not apply to them. It collects no data. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
- **Discard answers that no longer apply** – If the employee changes an earlier answer so that a later question no longer applies, its answer may be kept while the employee goes back and forth, but is discarded on submission. No personal data that no party needs is kept. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
- **Read-only after submission** – After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Later changes (move, marriage, new child, certificate of residence handed in later, corrected nationality, moved first working day) are made by HR (later HR-side spec). Every derived result - insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code - is always derived again from the latest answers; for the first working day only the latest date counts and no history is kept. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
