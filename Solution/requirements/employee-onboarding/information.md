# Information catalogue

Every piece of information the process needs, **asked once** and reused by
every stakeholder. This view answers, for each piece: what, when we ask, when
it is required, what it depends on, and who needs it.

## Derived results (never asked)

- **Questionnaire flow overview** – The questionnaire has exactly eight steps in this order. Each question is asked once and its answer is reused for every receiving party; a question is only asked when at least one party needs the answer for this person. Answers are saved as the employee goes and they can resume until they submit; submitting needs one confirmation on a summary page. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
- **Insurance start date** – The pension fund insurance start date is derived from the first working day only: if the first working day is the 1st of a month, insurance starts that day; otherwise on the 1st of the following month. It is always derived again from the latest first working day. ([First working day and insurance start date](feature.md#SEC-03-employment-start))
- **Civil status for the pension fund** – The fund only accepts Single, Married, Divorced and Widowed. Registered partnership and Separated are entered as Married (with the spouse's/partner's date of birth); the other values are entered as they are (assumption D-003). ([Civil status and spouse data](feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse))
- **Canton for the pension fund** – Employees living in Switzerland: the canton they chose. Employees living abroad: the canton of the place of work, Zurich for Muster AG, not asked (assumption D-002). ([Personal details and home address](feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address))
- **Subject to source tax** – An employee living abroad is subject to source tax whatever their nationality. An employee living in Switzerland is subject only if not Swiss, without a C permit, and not married to (or in a registered partnership with) a Swiss national or C-permit holder who lives in Switzerland. Separated counts as single. 'Here' in HR's wording means Switzerland (assumption D-043). ([Who is subject to source tax](feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability))
- **Liechtenstein resident in principle not liable** – An employee living in Liechtenstein who returns daily, or who does not return daily but expects at most 45 non-return days, is in principle not liable to source tax, subject to the double taxation agreement. Lonio shows this instead of a tariff code, flags it for HR and skips the remaining tariff questions. ([Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence))
- **Double taxation agreement note (Liechtenstein)** – For an employee living in Liechtenstein without daily return and with more than 45 non-return days, the plain tariff code is recorded and the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement' is recorded separately next to it and flagged for HR; the note is not part of the code. ([Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence))
- **Civil status group for source tax** – For source tax, Married and Registered partnership count as 'married or registered partnership'; Single, Divorced, Widowed and Separated count as 'single, divorced, separated or widowed', as in the calculator. ([Civil status and spouse data](feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse))
- **Residence for the tariff calculation** – The calculator's residence answers are derived from the home address country instead of being asked again: Switzerland, Germany, Liechtenstein or another country. Switzerland and other countries give the base tariff without further residence questions. ([Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence))
- **Church-tax suffix** – Roman Catholic, Christ Catholic or Evangelical Reformed gives the suffix Y; none or other gives N. ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Per-child result** – Each child block yields one result: counted, counted with H (single, divorced, separated or widowed employees only) or not counted. Observed calculator results win over the general rule (D-046); rows marked inferred are checked against the calculator before go-live (D-009). ([Source tax tariff: children](feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children))
- **Number of counted children (child digit)** – The child digit of the tariff code is the number of children whose result is 'counted' or 'counted with H', summed over all child blocks. Without children it is 0. (The internal expression evaluates one child block; it is summed over all blocks.) ([Source tax tariff: children](feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children))
- **Base tariff letter** – Married or registered partnership: C if the spouse/partner is gainfully employed (or receives replacement income), otherwise B. Single, divorced, separated or widowed: H if any child is counted with H, otherwise A. Earned income is always assumed, so G does not occur (D-006). ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Cross-border (Grenzgänger) tariff applies** – For an employee living in Germany the cross-border tariff applies if they return daily and have a certificate of residence ('not yet' counts as no), or if they do not return daily, daily return would be reasonable and they do not expect more than 60 non-return days. Otherwise the normal tariff applies. ([Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence))
- **Tariff letter** – When the cross-border tariff applies, the base letter is replaced: A by L, B by M, C by N, H by P (and G by Q, which cannot occur for onboarding). Otherwise the base letter is used. ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Full source tax tariff code** – Lonio computes the full Zurich tariff code as one text: tariff letter, number of counted children and church-tax suffix (e.g. 'C1Y', 'L0Y'). Not subject to source tax: 'no tariff code'. Liechtenstein resident in principle not liable: 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement', flagged for HR. When answers needed for the code are missing (e.g. after an HR change made new questions apply): 'incomplete - HR must complete'. Always derived again from the latest answers. ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Permit reminder** – At the end of the questionnaire, every non-Swiss employee living in Switzerland and every employee living abroad (whatever their nationality, which is not asked) is reminded to bring their permit on the first working day. HR provides the wording before go-live, so that a Swiss citizen living abroad can see it does not apply to them. It collects no data. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
- **Discard answers that no longer apply** – Answers to questions that no longer apply after an earlier answer changes are kept while the employee goes back and forth, and discarded on submission. The same applies after an HR change. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
- **Read-only after submission** – After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee and never reopened. Later changes (move, marriage, new child, late certificate of residence, corrected nationality, first working day) are made by HR; every derived result is derived again from the latest answers, and results depending on questions never asked show 'incomplete - HR must complete' until HR enters the answers. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
