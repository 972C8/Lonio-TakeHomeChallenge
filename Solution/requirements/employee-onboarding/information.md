every stakeholder. This view answers, for each piece: what, when we ask, when

## Derived results (never asked)

- **Subject to source tax** – An employee whose home address is outside Switzerland is subject to source tax, whatever their nationality. An employee living in Switzerland who is Swiss or holds a C permit is not subject. An employee living in Switzerland who is not Swiss and has no C permit is not subject if married to a spouse who is Swiss or holds a C permit and lives in Switzerland. Every other employee is subject. Only employees subject to source tax are asked the source tax questions. ([Decide whether the employee is subject to source tax](feature.md#SEC-04-source-tax-liability))
- **Pension insurance start date** – The insurance start date is derived from the latest confirmed first working day and is not asked. If the first working day is the 1st of a month, insurance starts that day; otherwise it starts on the 1st of the following month (e.g. first working day 15.03.2026 gives 01.04.2026). The result is always the 1st of a month, as the fund requires, in the format DD.MM.YYYY. ([Confirmed first working day and derived pension insurance start date](feature.md#SEC-01-first-working-day-insurance-start))
