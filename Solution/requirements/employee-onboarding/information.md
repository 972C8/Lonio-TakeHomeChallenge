# Information catalogue

Every piece of information the process needs, **asked once** and reused by
every stakeholder. This view answers, for each piece: what, when we ask, when
it is required, what it depends on, and who needs it.

## Derived results (never asked)

- **Questionnaire flow overview** – The employee answers the questionnaire in English in these steps and this order. Each piece of information is asked once and reused for every receiving party; a question appears only when at least one party needs it for this person. Swiss residents with Swiss citizenship or a C permit never see source tax questions. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
- **Insurance start date** – The pension fund insurance start date is derived from the first working day (the contractual start date): if it is the 1st of a month, insurance starts that day; otherwise on the 1st of the following month. It is always derived again from the latest first working day. Assumption on the contractual start date to be verified by a domain expert. ([First working day and insurance start date](feature.md#SEC-03-employment-start))
- **Civil status for the pension fund** – The civil status entered at the pension fund is the employee's civil status, except that registered partnership and separated are entered as Married (with the spouse's/partner's date of birth). Assumption to be verified by a domain expert. ([Civil status and spouse data](feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse))
- **Canton for the pension fund** – The pension fund 'Canton' is the canton of residence for employees living in Switzerland; for employees living abroad it is the canton of the place of work, Zurich. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert. ([Personal details and home address](feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address))
- **Subject to source tax** – An employee living abroad is subject to source tax whatever their nationality. An employee living in Switzerland is subject only if not Swiss (anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss), without a C permit, and not married or in a registered partnership with a Swiss national or C permit holder who lives in Switzerland. 'Here' in HR's wording means Switzerland. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert. ([Who is subject to source tax](feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability))
- **Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability** – For an employee living in Liechtenstein who returns daily, or who does not return daily and expects at most 45 non-return days, Lonio records 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR, and skips the tariff questions. With more than 45 non-return days the normal tariff code is computed with the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement', flagged for HR. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert. ([Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence))
- **Civil status for source tax** – For source tax, married and registered partnership count as 'married or registered partnership'; single, divorced, separated and widowed count as 'single'. ([Civil status and spouse data](feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse))
- **Residence for source tax** – The calculator's residence answer is derived from the country of residence: Switzerland, Germany, Liechtenstein, or other country. It is not asked separately. ([Who is subject to source tax](feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability))
- **Church-tax suffix** – The tariff code ends in Y for Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed, and N for none or other. ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Per-child result** – Each child gets a result: 'counts', 'counts with H' (single persons only; makes the tariff H) or 'does not count'. Observed calculator rows win over the derived pattern; rows marked inferred are subject to the go-live check against the calculator. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert. ([Source tax tariff: children](feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children))
- **Child digit** – The child digit of the tariff code is the number of children whose result is 'counts' or 'counts with H'; 0 if the employee has no children. The expression gives each child's contribution (1 or 0), summed over all children. There is no maximum number of children. ([Source tax tariff: children](feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children))
- **Base tariff letter** – Married employees and registered partners get C if the spouse/partner works or receives replacement income, otherwise B. Single, divorced, separated and widowed employees get H if at least one child counts with H, otherwise A. Tariffs G/Q (replacement income) and special tariffs do not occur. The expression is per child; H applies if any child gives H. ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Cross-border tariff applies** – For Germany residents the cross-border tariff applies with daily return and a certificate of residence, or without daily return when daily return is reasonable and no more than 60 non-return days are expected. A certificate answered 'not yet' counts as 'no'. In all other cases the normal tariff applies. ([Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence))
- **Tariff letter** – With the cross-border tariff the base letter is replaced: A by L, B by M, C by N, H by P. Otherwise the base letter is used. ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Source tax tariff code** – For every employee subject to source tax (and not in principle exempt as a Liechtenstein resident), Lonio computes the full Zurich tariff code: tariff letter, child digit and church-tax suffix written together (e.g. C1Y, L0Y). HR no longer uses the calculator. For Liechtenstein residents with more than 45 non-return days the code carries the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement' and is flagged for HR. It is always derived again from the latest answers. Before go-live a domain expert compares it with the calculator for unexplored paths. ([Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Permit reminder** – At the end of the questionnaire, every non-Swiss employee and every employee living abroad (whatever their nationality, as it is not asked) is reminded to bring their permit on the first working day. HR words the reminder so that a Swiss citizen living abroad can see it does not apply to them. It collects no data. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
- **Discard answers that no longer apply** – Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
- **Read-only after submission** – After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Later changes (move, marriage, new child, certificate of residence handed in later, corrected nationality, changed first working day) are made by HR (later HR-side spec). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
