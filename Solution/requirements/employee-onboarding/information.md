every stakeholder. This view answers, for each piece: what, when we ask, when
it is required, what it depends on, and who needs it.

## Derived results (never asked)

- **Employee is subject to source tax** – An employee who lives outside Switzerland is subject to source tax regardless of nationality. An employee who lives in Switzerland is subject only if they are not Swiss, do not hold a C permit, and are not married to a Swiss national or C-permit holder who lives in Switzerland. Only employees who are subject answer the source tax questions. ([Who is subject to source tax](feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability))
- **Pension insurance start date** – The insurance start date is derived from the first working day, not asked. If the first working day is the 1st of a month, insurance starts that day. Otherwise it starts on the 1st of the following month. If the first working day changes, the insurance start date must be derived again and the pension fund's date corrected. The result is always the 1st of a month, as the pension fund requires. ([First working day and insurance start date](feature.md#SEC-03-employment-start))
- **Civil status answer for the source tax calculator** – The source tax calculator's civil status question is answered from the employee's civil status, not asked again: Married maps to 'married or registered partnership' (Verheiratet oder eingetragene Partnerschaft). Single, Divorced and Widowed map to 'single, divorced, separated, widowed' (Ledig, Geschieden, Getrennt, Verwitwet). ([Civil status and spouse data for the pension fund](feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse))
- **Residence answers for the source tax calculator** – The calculator's 'main residence in Switzerland?' and 'country of residence' questions are answered from the country of the home address, not asked again: Switzerland gives main residence in Switzerland. Germany and Liechtenstein give that country. Any other country gives 'other country' (Anderes Land). Switzerland and other countries give the base tariff without more residence questions. ([Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence))
- **Church-tax suffix of the tariff** – For earned income, the employee's denomination sets the church-tax suffix of the source tax tariff: Y for Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed, N for none or other (e.g. A0Y vs A0N). Replacement income tariffs (G/Q) have no suffix. ([Source tax tariff: income, denomination and spouse employment](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Base tariff letter (source tax)** – The calculator's base tariff letter: replacement income gives G. Married with a working spouse gives C, married with a non-working spouse gives B. Not married gives A, or H if at least one child is counted with H (child in household, and sole custody or the employee is the main contributor). For employees in Germany the cross-border tariff replaces A→L, B→M, C→N, H→P, G→Q when it applies. The tariff digit is the number of counted children. HR keeps the calculator as the authority for the final tariff. ([Source tax tariff: income, denomination and spouse employment](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics))
- **Questionnaire flow overview** – The questionnaire runs in this order, each step only with the questions that apply: 1 Personal details (first name, last name, date of birth). 2 Home address (street, house number, postcode, town, country, and the canton if the employee lives in Switzerland). 3 Employment start (confirm the first working day, from which the insurance start date is derived). 4 Nationality and permit (only if the employee lives in Switzerland: nationality, and the permit if not Swiss). 5 Family (civil status. Spouse's date of birth if married. Spouse's nationality, permit and residence only if married and otherwise subject to source tax). 6 Source tax (only if subject to source tax: type of income, denomination, spouse works, children and one block per child, then the Germany or Liechtenstein cross-border questions). Address comes before nationality because employees living abroad need no nationality question. Civil status comes before the spouse questions. ([Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow))
