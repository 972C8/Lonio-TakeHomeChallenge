# US-02.3 – Use the home address country as the country of residence

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the country of the employee's home address to be used as their country of residence, without asking for it a second time, **so that** Lonio can tell whether the employee lives abroad (and is therefore subject to source tax whatever their nationality) and which follow-up questions apply, while the employee states the country only once

Priority **P1** – Country of residence decides who is subject to source tax and which further questions are asked. HR today does not know up front who has to answer the source tax questions (F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-ab3cba, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, G-01, G-10, D-002, D-038, D-041, D-043, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Country (of residence)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.3.1 Country is given once and named in English**
  - **Given** a new employee enters their home address
  - **When** they give the country
  - **Then** they choose the country by its English name (e.g. Switzerland, Germany, Liechtenstein). This one answer counts as their country of residence on the first working day. The employee is not asked about their country of residence anywhere else in the questionnaire. The answer is recorded for HR (postal address), for source tax (residence) and for the pension fund (to decide the canton). The residence-on-the-first-working-day reading is an assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-041).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, F-hr_transcript-ab3cba, G-01, G-10, D-041_
- **AC-02.3.2 Living abroad means subject to source tax and no nationality question**
  - **Given** a new employee whose home address is outside Switzerland
  - **When** they continue the questionnaire
  - **Then** Lonio treats them as subject to source tax whatever their nationality, as in HR's rule where 'here' means Switzerland. They are not asked for their nationality or for a canton. An employee whose home address is in Switzerland is asked for their nationality and canton, and their source tax liability depends on those further answers. Applying HR's rule as stated is an assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-043).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, D-038, D-043, D-002_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | → | Subject to source tax | Nationality | Canton of residence | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Stefan, Swiss citizen living abroad (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Germany | → | yes | not asked | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | yes | not asked | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in another country (illustrative) | France | → | yes | not asked | – | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | – | asked | asked | ✅ |
