# US-02.3 – Use the home address country as the country of residence

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the country of the employee's home address on the first working day to count as their country of residence, without asking for it a second time, **so that** Lonio can decide whether the employee lives in Switzerland or abroad - which decides source tax liability, the cross-border questions and the canton for the pension fund - from one answer

Priority **P1** – Every employee living abroad is subject to source tax; without the country of residence neither source tax liability nor the pension fund canton can be decided, which blocks both registrations. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-ab3cba, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, G-08, G-01, D-002, D-041, D-049, D-050

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Country of residence (home address country)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.3.1 Country chosen from a fixed country list**
  - **Given** the employee enters the country of their home address in step 2 'Home address'
  - **When** they choose the country
  - **Then** they choose it from the ISO 3166 country list with English country names (for example Switzerland, Germany, Liechtenstein); free text is not accepted and the country is required. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-050, D-049).
  - _Sources: G-10, D-049, D-050_
- **AC-02.3.2 Residence in Switzerland is not asked again**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the country of their home address
  - **When** Lonio needs to know whether the main residence is in Switzerland and, if not, the country of residence (as the Zurich source tax calculator asks)
  - **Then** both are taken from the home address country and are not asked again; the country that counts is the one of the address on the first working day (assumption to be verified by a domain expert, D-041).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, G-08, D-041_
- **AC-02.3.3 The country of residence decides what is asked about the canton**
  - **Given** the employee has chosen the country of their home address
  - **When** the rest of step 2 'Home address' is shown
  - **Then** an employee living in Switzerland is asked for the canton of residence; an employee living in any other country is not asked for a canton.
  - _Sources: D-002, D-047_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | → | Country of residence (home address country) | Canton of residence | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Lives in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | asked | ✅ |
  | Lives in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | required | not asked | ✅ |
  | Lives in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | – | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-02.3.4 Who receives the country of residence**
  - **Given** the employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** the answers are recorded
  - **Then** the country is recorded for HR as part of the postal address, is used for the source tax decision (anyone living outside Switzerland is subject to source tax, whatever their nationality) and decides which canton is entered for the pension fund.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-ab3cba, D-002, D-034_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | → | Subject to source tax | Canton for the pension fund | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Living abroad is enough for source tax (illustrative) | Germany | → | yes | Zurich | ✅ |

- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. - **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
- **G-10** (product owner guidance): Business wording for values (used in allowed values and examples): countries by English name ("Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein"), permits as "B permit", "C permit", civil status exactly as the pension fund's list shows it ("Single", "Married", ...), dates as DD.MM.YYYY, yes/no as "yes"/"no".
