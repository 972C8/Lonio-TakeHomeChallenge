# US-06.1 – Ask the type of income only of employees subject to source tax

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Source tax tariff: income, denomination and spouse employment](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees who are subject to source tax (rule subject_to_source_tax) to state whether the tariff is determined for earned income or replacement income, **so that** the Zurich tariff calculator's first question is answered and the right tariff path (earned income or replacement income) is followed, while employees not subject to source tax are not asked

Priority **P2** – Needed to start the calculator path, but for a new hire the answer is presumably always earned income and may be fixed by a product decision (see OQ-income-type); the source tax pain points are mainly about who answers and the spouse follow-ups. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e06ffd, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-feb9df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d5a6fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-351289, F-hr_transcript-34494f, F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.1.1 Income type is asked and required only when the employee is subject to source tax**
  - **Given** the employee's answers on residence, nationality, permit, civil status and spouse decide whether they are subject to source tax (rule subject_to_source_tax)
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax step
  - **Then** the type of income question is asked and must be answered if the employee is subject to source tax; it is not asked at all if the employee is not subject. Until the open question OQ-income-type is decided, the question is asked rather than fixed to earned income.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e06ffd, G-01_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | Permit type | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | Spouse's country of residence | → | Employee is subject to source tax | Type of income (source tax) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9): married to a Swiss wife living in Switzerland, not subject | Switzerland | Brazil | B permit | Married | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | no | not asked | ✅ |
  | Maria (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204): Portuguese with a C permit, not subject | Switzerland | Portugal | C permit | – | – | – | → | no | not asked | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss single employee with a B permit living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Single | – | – | → | yes | required | ✅ |
  | Swiss employee living abroad (like Stefan, hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | – | – | Single | – | – | → | yes | required | ✅ |
  | Swiss employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | Single | – | – | → | no | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.1.2 Allowed answers for the type of income**
  - **Given** the type of income question is asked
  - **When** the employee answers it
  - **Then** the employee chooses exactly one of "Earned income" or "Replacement income" (the calculator's two options), and the answer is used for the source tax tariff determination.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-feb9df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e06ffd, G-09_
- **AC-06.1.3 Replacement income skips the personal and child tariff questions**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax answers "Replacement income"
  - **When** the questionnaire continues
  - **Then** the denomination, spouse employment and children questions are not asked; the employee continues with the residence-related source tax questions, as the calculator does.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-351289_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | Permit type | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | Spouse's permit type | Type of income (source tax) | → | Religious denomination (church tax) | Spouse works or receives replacement income | Has biological or adopted children | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married employee with replacement income (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Married | Germany | B permit | Replacement income | → | not asked | not asked | not asked | ✅ |

_Trace check: partial – The priority reason's claim that the answer is 'presumably always earned income' for a new hire is not in the cited facts, and AC-06.1.3 equates the calculator's 'personal/child questions' with the specific denomination, spouse employment and children items without a fact saying so._
