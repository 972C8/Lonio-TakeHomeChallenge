# US-08.4 – Employee can get an emailed copy of their submitted answers

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Completing and submitting the questionnaire](../feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission).

**As** new employee, **I want** to choose, when I submit the questionnaire, to receive an email with a copy of my submitted answers at an email address I type in, **so that** I can look up what I entered myself instead of calling HR to ask

Priority **P2** – HR reports that new employees keep calling to ask what they entered - a recurring burden for HR - but the copy is optional and blocks no registration. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-hr_email_copy-37cede, F-hr_email_copy-f75a41, F-hr_email_copy-6d2b5a, F-hr_email_copy-029105, F-hr_email_copy-b67d2f, F-hr_email_copy-9e81ef, F-hr_email_copy-daa6b1, F-hr_email_copy-607694, D-036

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission) and [information.md](../information.md)): Send me a copy of my answers, Email address for the copy, Content of the employee's copy

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.4.1 The copy is offered at submission and is optional**
  - **Given** an employee has reached the end of the questionnaire
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether they want an email with a copy of their submitted answers (yes or no); every employee is asked, and the question may be left empty, in which case no copy is sent
  - _Sources: F-hr_email_copy-37cede, F-hr_email_copy-f75a41, F-hr_email_copy-6d2b5a_

  | Example | → | Send me a copy of my answers | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Any employee at submission (illustrative) | → | optional | ✅ |

- **AC-08.4.2 Email address asked only when a copy is wanted**
  - **Given** an employee is submitting the questionnaire
  - **When** the employee answers whether they want a copy
  - **Then** only if they answer yes are they asked for the email address the copy should go to, and then it must be given; if they answer no or leave the question empty, no email address is asked and no copy is sent
  - _Sources: F-hr_email_copy-029105, F-hr_email_copy-b67d2f, F-hr_email_copy-f75a41_

  | Example | Send me a copy of my answers | → | Email address for the copy | Content of the employee's copy | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee wants a copy (illustrative) | yes | → | required | yes | ✅ |
  | Employee does not want a copy (illustrative) | no | → | not asked | no | ✅ |
  | Employee leaves the copy question empty (illustrative) | – | → | not asked | no | ✅ |

- **AC-08.4.3 The copy shows the submitted answers exactly, nothing more**
  - **Given** an employee has asked for a copy and typed in an email address
  - **When** the questionnaire is submitted
  - **Then** a copy is sent to the email address the employee typed in; it shows the answers exactly as they were submitted and nothing more - no derived values such as the insurance start date, the civil status for the fund or for source tax, whether the employee is subject to source tax or the tariff code; answers discarded on submission because their questions no longer apply are not part of the submitted answers and so do not appear
  - _Sources: F-hr_email_copy-37cede, F-hr_email_copy-9e81ef, F-hr_email_copy-daa6b1, F-hr_email_copy-029105, D-036_
_Trace check: partial – AC-08.4.3 names specific excluded derived values (insurance start date, civil status for fund/source tax, source-tax subject, tariff code) that the cited facts never mention, since they only say "nothing more"._
