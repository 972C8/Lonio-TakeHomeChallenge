# US-07.1 – Derive the calculator's residence answers from the home address

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Employees living abroad (Germany, Liechtenstein)](../feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the calculator's 'main residence in Switzerland?' and 'state of residence' answers to be taken from the country of the employee's home address instead of being asked again, **so that** the tariff code is based on where the employee really lives, the employee answers their country of residence only once, and only employees living in Germany or Liechtenstein get the extra cross-border questions

Priority **P1** – Lonio now computes the tariff code itself (D-001); the residence decides which cross-border questions apply, and HR finds the abroad cases the hardest part of the calculator (F-hr_transcript-ab8886). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f29009, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-80bf97, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-52b67a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, F-hr_transcript-e505f8, F-hr_transcript-ab8886, G-01, G-08, D-001

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad) and [information.md](../information.md)): Residence for the source tax tariff

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.1.1 Residence for source tax follows the home address country**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the country of their home address
  - **When** Lonio determines the employee's residence for the source tax tariff
  - **Then** the residence is Switzerland if the home address is in Switzerland, Germany if it is in Germany, Liechtenstein if it is in Liechtenstein, and 'other country' for any other country; the employee is not asked about their main residence or state of residence again
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f29009, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-80bf97, F-hr_transcript-e505f8, G-08, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | → | Residence for the source tax tariff | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Lives in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | Switzerland | ✅ |
  | Lives in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | Germany | ✅ |
  | Lives in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | Liechtenstein | ✅ |
  | Lives in France (illustrative) | France | → | Other country | ✅ |

- **AC-07.1.2 Switzerland and other countries leave the base tariff unchanged**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax lives in Switzerland or in a country other than Germany or Liechtenstein
  - **When** the questionnaire reaches the source tax questions
  - **Then** no cross-border questions are asked and the base tariff code applies unchanged (e.g. A0N for a single employee without children living in another country)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-52b67a, G-01, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | → | Residence for the source tax tariff | Daily return to home in Germany | Daily return to home in Liechtenstein | Cross-border (Grenzgänger) tariff applies | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator run 'Anderes Land' -> A0N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-52b67a) | France | – | – | – | → | Other country | not asked | not asked | no | – | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss employee without C permit living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | no | Single | → | – | not asked | not asked | no | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-07.1.3 Germany and Liechtenstein open their own cross-border questions**
  - **Given** an employee lives in Germany or in Liechtenstein
  - **When** the questionnaire reaches the source tax questions
  - **Then** employees living in Germany get only the Germany cross-border questions (see US-07.2) and employees living in Liechtenstein get only the Liechtenstein questions (see US-07.3)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, G-01, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | → | Daily return to home in Germany | Daily return to home in Liechtenstein | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Lives in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | asked | not asked | ✅ |
  | Lives in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | asked | ✅ |

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
