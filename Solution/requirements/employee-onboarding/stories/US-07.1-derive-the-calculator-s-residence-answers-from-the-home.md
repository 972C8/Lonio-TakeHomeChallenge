# US-07.1 – Derive the calculator's residence answers from the home address

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Employees living abroad (Germany, Liechtenstein)](../feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad).

**As** new employee, **I want** Lonio to work out where I live for source tax from the home address I already gave, instead of asking me again whether my main residence is in Switzerland and in which state I live, **so that** I answer each question only once, and Lonio can still work out the source tax tariff code the way the Zurich calculator does

Priority **P1** – Lonio computes the tariff code itself (D-001). The state of residence decides whether the Germany or Liechtenstein questions apply and whether the tariff can change, so without it no tariff code can be computed for an employee living abroad. · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f29009, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-52b67a, F-hr_transcript-e505f8, G-01, D-001

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad) and [information.md](../information.md)): State of residence for source tax

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.1.1 State of residence follows from the home address country**
  - **Given** an employee has entered the country of their home address in the 'Home address' step
  - **When** Lonio works out the state of residence for source tax
  - **Then** the state of residence is Switzerland when the country is Switzerland, Germany when it is Germany, Liechtenstein when it is Liechtenstein, and 'other country' for any other country. The employee is not asked separately whether their main residence is in Switzerland or which state they live in.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f29009, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-hr_transcript-e505f8, G-01_

  | Example | Country (home address) | → | State of residence for source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Konstanz (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-1a20bb) | Germany | → | Germany | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | Liechtenstein | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | Switzerland | ✅ |
  | Employee living in France (illustrative) | France | → | Other country | ✅ |

- **AC-07.1.2 Switzerland and other countries leave the base tariff unchanged and get no cross-border questions**
  - **Given** an employee lives in Switzerland or in a country other than Germany or Liechtenstein
  - **When** the employee goes through the questionnaire
  - **Then** no cross-border commuting questions (daily return, certificate of residence, non-return days) are asked, and the state of residence does not change the base tariff letter
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-52b67a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, G-01_

  | Example | Country (home address) | → | Daily return to the home in Germany | Daily return to the home in Liechtenstein | Cross-border tariff applies | Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in France (illustrative) | France | → | not asked | not asked | no | no | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | not asked | not asked | – | – | ✅ |

- **AC-07.1.3 Residence is derived again from the latest address**
  - **Given** the home address country changes, either because the employee goes back and changes it before submitting or because HR records a move after submission
  - **When** Lonio works out the state of residence again
  - **Then** the state of residence and everything that depends on it (which cross-border questions apply, the tariff code) follow the latest country. Cross-border answers that no longer apply are discarded on submission. HR's change after submission belongs to the later HR-side spec.
  - _Sources: D-036, D-037, D-034_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **F-hr_transcript-e505f8** (hr_transcript): Whether the employee lives abroad can be derived from the country of the home address, which is relevant for source tax liability.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
