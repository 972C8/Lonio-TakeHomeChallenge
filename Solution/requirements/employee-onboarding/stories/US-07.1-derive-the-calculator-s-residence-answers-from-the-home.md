# US-07.1 – Derive the calculator's residence answers from the home address

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Employees living abroad (Germany, Liechtenstein)](../feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the employee's state of residence for the Zurich tariff (Switzerland, Germany, Liechtenstein or another country) to be taken from the country of the home address, without asking the calculator's residence questions again, **so that** each fact is asked once, and the right cross-border questions and base tariff follow from where the employee actually lives

Priority **P1** – Lonio computes the tariff code itself (D-001). Without the state of residence, no tariff code can be computed for employees living abroad, and today HR has to work out the extra cross-border questions by hand (F-hr_transcript-ab8886). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f29009, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-52b67a, F-hr_transcript-e505f8, F-hr_transcript-ab8886, G-08, D-001, D-036

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad) and [information.md](../information.md)): Residence for source tax

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.1.1 State of residence follows the home address country**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the country of their home address
  - **When** Lonio works out the source tax state of residence
  - **Then** Switzerland gives 'main residence in Switzerland'. Germany gives Germany, Liechtenstein gives Liechtenstein, and any other country gives 'other country'. The employee is not asked the calculator's 'Main residence in Switzerland?' or 'State of residence' questions.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f29009, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-hr_transcript-e505f8, G-08, D-001_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | → | Residence for source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás, living in Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | → | Switzerland | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | Germany | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | Liechtenstein | ✅ |
  | Employee living in another country (illustrative) | France | → | Other country | ✅ |

- **AC-07.1.2 Switzerland and other countries get no cross-border questions**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland or in a country other than Germany or Liechtenstein
  - **When** the employee goes through the questionnaire
  - **Then** no cross-border questions for Germany or Liechtenstein are asked, and the base tariff is used unchanged (for example A0N for a single person without children living in another country)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-52b67a, D-001_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | Holds a C permit | Civil status | → | Daily return to the home in Germany | Daily return to the home in Liechtenstein | Cross-border tariff applies | Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in another country (illustrative) | France | – | – | – | → | not asked | not asked | no | no | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Single | → | not asked | not asked | no | – | ✅ |

- **AC-07.1.3 Cross-border answers that no longer apply are discarded**
  - **Given** the employee answered cross-border questions for Germany or Liechtenstein and then changes the home address country
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** the cross-border answers for the country they no longer live in are discarded. Until submission they are kept, so going back and forth loses no input.
  - _Sources: D-036, G-01_

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
