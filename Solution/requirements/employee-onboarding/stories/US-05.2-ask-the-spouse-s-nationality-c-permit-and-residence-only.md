# US-05.2 – Ask the spouse's nationality, C permit and residence only when they can exempt the employee

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Who is subject to source tax](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to ask about the spouse or registered partner (nationality, C permit, whether they live in Switzerland) only of a non-Swiss employee living in Switzerland without a C permit who is married or in a registered partnership, and only as far as the answers can still exempt the employee, **so that** the spouse exemption from source tax can be decided, as for Tomás, without collecting spouse data that no party needs

Priority **P1** – The spouse exemption changes who has to do source tax at all; without it a married employee such as Tomás would wrongly be treated as subject (F-hr_transcript-b90bd9). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-f3ce84, F-hr_transcript-76eacc, F-hr_transcript-e5337d, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-54d011, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01, D-003, D-005, D-008, D-036, D-041, D-043, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability) and [information.md](../information.md)): Spouse's nationality, Spouse holds a C permit, Spouse's country of residence

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.2.1 Spouse's nationality is asked only when the exemption could matter**
  - **Given** an employee lives in Switzerland, is not Swiss, has answered no to holding a C permit and has given their civil status
  - **When** the civil status is Married or Registered partnership
  - **Then** Lonio asks for the spouse's or registered partner's nationality and an answer is required; a spouse with Swiss citizenship counts as Switzerland. It is not asked for Single, Divorced, Widowed or Separated (separated counts as single for source tax, decision D-003, an assumption to be verified by a domain expert), and not asked of Swiss employees, C permit holders or employees living abroad. That the spouse questions are needed only when the employee would otherwise be subject is an inference from HR's rule.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-f3ce84, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-54d011, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, D-003, D-008, D-043, G-01_

- **AC-05.2.2 Spouse's C permit is asked only for a non-Swiss spouse**
  - **Given** the spouse's nationality has been asked
  - **When** the spouse's nationality is not Switzerland
  - **Then** Lonio asks whether the spouse or registered partner holds a C permit, with the answers yes or no, and an answer is required. No other permit type is asked. When the spouse is Swiss, the question is not asked.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-76eacc, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-54d011, D-005, D-003_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | → | Spouse holds a C permit | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss spouse (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Italy | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-05.2.3 Spouse's residence is asked only when the spouse is Swiss or holds a C permit**
  - **Given** the spouse questions are asked
  - **When** the spouse is Swiss or holds a C permit
  - **Then** Lonio asks for the country where the spouse or registered partner lives on the employee's first working day (decision D-041, an assumption to be verified by a domain expert) and an answer is required; only whether it is Switzerland matters. When the spouse is neither Swiss nor a C permit holder, the question is not asked, because the exemption cannot apply.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-e5337d, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, D-041, D-043, G-01_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | Spouse holds a C permit | → | Spouse's country of residence | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | – | → | required | ✅ |
  | Spouse with C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Italy | yes | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Spouse neither Swiss nor C permit holder (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Italy | no | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-05.2.4 Who receives the spouse answers**
  - **Given** the spouse questions of this story were asked
  - **When** the questionnaire is submitted
  - **Then** The answers are recorded for the source tax side only, to decide the spouse exemption. They are not sent to the pension fund. If an earlier answer changes so that a spouse question no longer applies, its answer is discarded on submission (decision D-036).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-f3ce84, F-hr_transcript-76eacc, F-hr_transcript-e5337d, D-036, G-01_
