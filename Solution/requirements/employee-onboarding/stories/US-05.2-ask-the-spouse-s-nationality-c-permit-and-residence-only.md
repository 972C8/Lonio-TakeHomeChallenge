# US-05.2 – Ask the spouse's nationality, C permit and residence only when they can exempt the employee

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Who is subject to source tax](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability).

**As** new employee, **I want** to be asked about my spouse's or registered partner's nationality, C permit and residence only when the answer can change whether I am subject to source tax, **so that** the spouse exemption can be checked without collecting spouse data that no party needs (data minimisation)

Priority **P1** – HR states the spouse exemption changes who has to pay source tax at all (F-hr_transcript-b90bd9); without these answers liability is wrong for cases like Tomás. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-f3ce84, F-hr_transcript-76eacc, F-hr_transcript-e5337d, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-54d011, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, G-01, D-003, D-005, D-008, D-036, D-043, D-047, D-050

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability) and [information.md](../information.md)): Spouse's nationality, Spouse holds a C permit, Spouse lives in Switzerland

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.2.1 Spouse's nationality is asked only when the spouse exemption could apply**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland, is not Swiss and has answered 'no' to holding a C permit
  - **When** the employee gives the civil status Married or Registered partnership in step 4 'Civil status and spouse'
  - **Then** the spouse's or registered partner's nationality is asked and required (country list by English name, or 'stateless'; a spouse with Swiss citizenship answers Switzerland). It is not asked for any other civil status, nor of employees who live abroad, are Swiss or hold a C permit, because the answer cannot change their source tax liability. Assumptions - to be verified by a domain expert: registered partnership counts as married and separated as single for source tax (D-003); asking only when the employee would otherwise be subject is inferred (F-hr_transcript-54d011).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-f3ce84, F-hr_transcript-54d011, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003, D-008, D-050_

- **AC-05.2.2 Spouse's C permit is asked only for a non-Swiss spouse**
  - **Given** the spouse's nationality has been asked
  - **When** the spouse's nationality is not Switzerland
  - **Then** the question whether the spouse or registered partner holds a C permit is asked and required, with the answers 'yes' or 'no'; for a Swiss spouse it is not asked. No other permit type of the spouse is asked (assumption D-005, to be verified by a domain expert).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-76eacc, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, D-005_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | → | Spouse holds a C permit | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás, Swiss wife (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Spouse from Italy (illustrative) | Switzerland | France | no | Married | Italy | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-05.2.3 Spouse's residence is asked only for a Swiss or C-permit spouse**
  - **Given** the spouse's nationality has been asked
  - **When** the spouse is Swiss or holds a C permit
  - **Then** the question 'Does your spouse live in Switzerland?' is asked and required, with the answers 'yes' or 'no'; it is not asked when the spouse is neither Swiss nor a C-permit holder, because the exemption cannot apply. 'Here' in HR's wording is read as Switzerland. Assumptions - to be verified by a domain expert: D-043 and the yes/no form of the question (D-050).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-e5337d, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, D-043, D-050_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | Spouse holds a C permit | → | Spouse lives in Switzerland | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | – | → | required | ✅ |
  | Spouse with C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | France | no | Registered partnership | Spain | yes | → | required | ✅ |
  | Spouse without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | France | no | Married | Italy | no | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-05.2.4 Spouse answers that no longer apply are not kept**
  - **Given** the employee has answered spouse questions and then changes an earlier answer (for example civil status to Single, or C permit to 'yes') so that they no longer apply
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** the spouse answers that no longer apply are discarded and not recorded; before submission they are kept, so going back and forth loses no input.
  - _Sources: D-036, D-050, G-01_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-005** (product decision (Product owner)): Do not ask for the permit type. Ask non-Swiss employees only whether they hold a C permit (permanent residence), yes or no, and the same for a non-Swiss spouse. No party needs any other permit type. This closes OQ-permit-list. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-008** (product decision (Product owner)): Anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss for source tax, even with another nationality. This closes OQ-dual-nationality. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **D-043** (product decision (Product owner)): Apply HR's consolidated source tax rule as stated; 'here' in HR's wording means Switzerland. This closes OQ-source-tax-rule-confirmation. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-050** (product decision (Product owner)): Build-time values. HR provides the permit reminder wording before go-live. Country of residence and nationality are chosen from the ISO 3166 country list; nationality also offers 'stateless'. The date of birth must make the employee at least 14 and at most 100 years old on the first working day; the first working day must lie within one year after the questionnaire is sent. Answers are saved as the employee goes and they can resume until they submit; submitting needs one confirmation on a summary page. Answers are kept when the employee goes back and changes an earlier answer (until submission), and this is testable. The spouse's residence is asked as 'Does your spouse live in Switzerland? yes/no', not as a country. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
