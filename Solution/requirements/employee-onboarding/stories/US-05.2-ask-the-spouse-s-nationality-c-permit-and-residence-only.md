# US-05.2 – Ask the spouse's nationality, C permit and residence only when they can exempt the employee

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Who is subject to source tax](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to ask the spouse's or registered partner's nationality, C permit and country of residence only when the answers can exempt the employee from source tax, **so that** I no longer check the spouse exemption by hand and chase spouse details by email, and no spouse data is collected when it changes nothing

Priority **P1** – HR checks the spouse exemption by hand and asks by email, which 'takes forever' (F-hr_transcript-a45422); the exemption decides who answers the tariff questions. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-f3ce84, F-hr_transcript-76eacc, F-hr_transcript-e5337d, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-54d011, F-hr_transcript-a45422, G-01, G-10, D-003, D-005, D-008

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.2.1 Spouse's nationality is asked only of employees who would otherwise be subject**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland, is not a Swiss citizen and has answered 'no' to holding a C permit
  - **When** their civil status is Married or Registered partnership
  - **Then** they are asked their spouse's or registered partner's nationality (country named in English; 'Switzerland' for anyone holding Swiss citizenship) and must answer it. It is not asked of employees living abroad, Swiss citizens, C-permit holders, or employees who are Single, Divorced, Widowed or Separated (separated counts as single for source tax). The answer is recorded for source tax.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-f3ce84, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-54d011, G-01, D-003, D-008_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | → | Spouse's / registered partner's nationality | Checked |

  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | → | required | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss employee in a registered partnership (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | no | Registered partnership | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Married C-permit holder (illustrative) | Switzerland | Portugal | yes | Married | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Married Swiss citizen (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | Married | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Separated non-Swiss employee without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Separated | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Single non-Swiss employee without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Single | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Married employee living abroad (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Married | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-05.2.2 Spouse's C permit is asked only for a non-Swiss spouse**
  - **Given** the employee was asked their spouse's or registered partner's nationality
  - **When** the spouse's nationality is not Switzerland
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether the spouse holds a C permit (permanent residence), answered 'yes' or 'no', and must answer it; the spouse's permit type is never asked. For a Swiss spouse this question is not asked. The answer is recorded for source tax.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-76eacc, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-10, D-005_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | Spouse's / registered partner's nationality | → | Spouse / registered partner holds a C permit | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás, Swiss wife (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Married employee with an Italian spouse (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Italy | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-05.2.3 Spouse's country of residence is asked only for a Swiss or C-permit spouse**
  - **Given** the employee was asked about their spouse or registered partner
  - **When** the spouse is Swiss or holds a C permit
  - **Then** the employee is asked the spouse's country of residence (country named in English) and must answer it, because the exemption only applies if that spouse lives in Switzerland ('lives here' is read as Switzerland, see OQ-source-tax-rule-confirmation). For a non-Swiss spouse without a C permit it is not asked. The answer is recorded for source tax.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-e5337d, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-54d011, G-01, G-10_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | Spouse's / registered partner's nationality | Spouse / registered partner holds a C permit | → | Spouse's / registered partner's country of residence | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás, Swiss wife (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | – | → | required | ✅ |
  | Spouse is Italian with a C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Italy | yes | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Spouse is Italian without a C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Italy | no | → | not asked | ✅ |
