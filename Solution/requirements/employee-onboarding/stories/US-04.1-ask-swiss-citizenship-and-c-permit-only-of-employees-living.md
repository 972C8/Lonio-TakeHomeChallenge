# US-04.1 – Ask Swiss citizenship and C permit only of employees living in Switzerland

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Source tax liability check](../feature.md#SEC-04-source-tax-liability).

**As** new employee, **I want** to be asked whether I am a Swiss citizen and, if I am not, whether I hold a C permit, but only when I live in Switzerland and never for my full nationality or any other permit type, **so that** Lonio learns exactly what decides my source tax liability without collecting personal data that no receiving party needs

Priority **P1** – Swiss citizenship and C permit decide source tax liability. Today HR works this out by hand and does not know in advance who has to answer the source tax questions (F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-hr_transcript-b84498). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-35fae3, F-hr_transcript-4ad146, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, G-01, D-005, D-008, D-010, D-038

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-source-tax-liability) and [information.md](../information.md)): Swiss citizenship, Holds a C permit

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.1.1 Swiss citizenship is asked only of employees living in Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the country of their home address
  - **When** the country is Switzerland
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether they hold Swiss citizenship, with the answers "Swiss" or "Not Swiss", and must answer. Employees whose home address is in another country are not asked, because they are subject to source tax whatever their nationality. The answer is recorded for source tax, and for HR to decide whether the permit reminder is shown.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-35fae3, F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, D-008, D-010, D-038, G-01_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | → | Swiss citizenship | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Konstanz, Germany (F-hr_transcript-1a20bb) | Germany | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (F-hr_transcript-1a20bb) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-04.1.2 Dual citizens answer as Swiss citizens**
  - **Given** an employee living in Switzerland holds Swiss citizenship and also another nationality
  - **When** they answer the Swiss citizenship question
  - **Then** they answer "Swiss". Anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss for source tax. Their other nationality is not asked.
  - _Sources: D-008, G-01_
- **AC-04.1.3 C permit is asked only of non-Swiss employees living in Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland
  - **When** the employee has answered that they are "Not Swiss"
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether they hold a C permit (permanent residence), with the answers "yes" or "no", and must answer. Swiss citizens and employees living abroad are not asked. No other permit type is asked. The answer is recorded for source tax.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-4ad146, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, D-005, G-01_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | → | Holds a C permit | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Non-Swiss employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | → | required | ✅ |
  | Swiss employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Swiss | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Konstanz, Germany (F-hr_transcript-1a20bb) | Germany | – | → | not asked | ✅ |

## Evidence
- **D-005** (product decision (Product owner)): Do not ask for the permit type. Ask non-Swiss employees only whether they hold a C permit (permanent residence), yes or no, and the same for a non-Swiss spouse. No party needs any other permit type. This closes OQ-permit-list. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-008** (product decision (Product owner)): Anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss for source tax, even with another nationality. This closes OQ-dual-nationality. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-010** (product decision (Product owner)): At the end of the questionnaire, remind every non-Swiss employee and every employee living abroad to bring their permit on the first working day. HR words the reminder; the spec states only its purpose. This closes OQ-permit-reminder.
- **D-038** (product decision (Product owner)): Employees living abroad get the permit reminder whatever their nationality, because nationality is not asked of them (data minimisation, G-01). HR words the reminder so that a Swiss citizen living abroad, such as Stefan, can see that it does not apply to them. Asking everyone 'Swiss citizen yes/no' only for the reminder is not worth the extra question.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
