# US-06.5 – Ask household and maintenance for a child of age in first education

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees with a child of age in first education to state whether they live with the other parent and whether they cover more than 50% of the child's maintenance, **so that** such a child counts for the tariff only when the calculator would count it

Priority **P1** – Wrong child counting gives a wrong tariff code (D-001). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b008fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cb54d9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cd1015, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-50f74a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-562299, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, D-001, D-009

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child: common household with the other parent, Child: employee covers more than 50% of maintenance, Child result (per child)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.5.1 Child in the household: common household with the other parent**
  - **Given** a child of age in first education lives in the employee's household
  - **When** the employee describes that child
  - **Then** the employee is asked, and must answer yes or no, whether they live in a common household with the other parent; custody is not asked. With a common household the child counts for a married employee; for a single employee the larger contributor decides (the employee - H child, the other parent - does not count; both inferred, see OQ-calculator-coverage).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-50f74a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, D-001, D-009_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or of age and in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: common household with the other parent | Child: who makes the larger financial contribution | → | Child: common household with the other parent | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | Child: employee covers more than 50% of maintenance | Child result (per child) | Checked |

  | Married, child in first education, common household -> B1N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-50f74a) | Germany | Married | no | yes | Of age and in first education | yes | yes | – | → | asked | not asked | not asked | counts | ✅ |

- **AC-06.5.2 Child in the household without common household: more than 50% maintenance**
  - **Given** a child of age in first education lives in the employee's household and the employee does not live with the other parent
  - **When** the employee is asked, and must answer yes or no, whether they mainly (more than 50%) provide the child's maintenance
  - **Then** yes - the child counts (married, inferred) or counts as an H child (single); no - the child does not count (inferred).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b008fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cb54d9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cd1015, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-562299, D-001, D-009_

  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

  | Married, in household, no common household, >50% maintenance (inferred) (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Of age and in first education | yes | no | yes | → | – | counts | ✅ |
  | In household, no common household, not mainly maintained (inferred) (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Of age and in first education | yes | no | no | → | – | does not count | ✅ |

- **AC-06.5.3 Child outside the household: more than 50% maintenance**
  - **Given** a child of age in first education does not live in the employee's household
  - **When** the employee is asked, and must answer yes or no, whether they mainly (more than 50%) provide the child's maintenance
  - **Then** the common-household question is not asked; yes - the child counts (not as an H child; observed for single, inferred for married); no - the child does not count.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cd1015, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-562299, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-50f74a, D-001, D-009_
