# US-06.5 – Ask household and maintenance for a child of age in first education

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** for each child of age in first education, to know whether the employee lives with the other parent and, where needed, whether the employee pays more than 50% of the child's maintenance, **so that** Lonio decides, as the calculator does, whether an adult child in first education counts for the tariff code and whether it counts with H for a single employee

Priority **P1** – Without these answers Lonio cannot compute the tariff code for employees with children in first education; Lonio owns the tariff result instead of HR (D-001). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b008fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cb54d9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cd1015, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-50f74a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-562299, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-38dd4a, D-001, D-003, D-009

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Common household with the other parent, Employee pays more than 50% of the child's maintenance, Larger financial contributor to the child's maintenance, Child result (per child)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.5.1 Questions for a child of age in first education**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with a child of age and in first education
  - **When** the employee has answered the household question
  - **Then** if the child lives in the household, the employee is asked whether they live in a common household with the child's other parent (yes or no, required); the employee is asked whether they pay mainly (more than 50%) for the child's maintenance (yes or no, required) when the child lives outside the household, or in the household without a common household with the other parent

- **AC-06.5.3 Result for a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee**
  - **Given** a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee subject to source tax with a child of age in first education
  - **When** the employee has answered the child questions
  - **Then** a child in the household counts with H if the employee pays more than 50% of its maintenance (no common household with the other parent) or makes the larger financial contribution (common household); a child outside the household that the employee mainly maintains counts without H; in all other cases the child does not count. ## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are out of scope for onboarding. Keep OQ-calculator-coverage open as a non-blocking go-live condition.
