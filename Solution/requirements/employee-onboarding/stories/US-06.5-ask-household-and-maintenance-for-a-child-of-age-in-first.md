# US-06.5 – Ask household and maintenance for a child of age in first education

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees subject to source tax with a child of age in first education to say whether they live with the child's other parent and whether they mainly (more than 50%) pay for the child's maintenance, **so that** Lonio decides, as the calculator does, whether such a child counts and whether it counts with H

Priority **P1** – This decides the child digit and the H tariff in the tariff code that Lonio now computes instead of HR (D-001). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b008fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cb54d9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cd1015, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-50f74a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-562299, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-001, D-003, D-009

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Common household with the other parent, Child mainly maintained by the employee (more than 50%), Child result (per child)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.5.1 Common household and main maintenance questions**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has a child of age in first education
  - **When** the employee answers that child's block
  - **Then** if the child lives in the employee's household, the employee is asked whether they live in a common household with the child's other parent ("yes"/"no", required). If the child does not live in the household, or lives in it without a common household with the other parent, the employee is asked whether they mainly (more than 50%) pay for the child's maintenance ("yes"/"no", required). Answers are recorded for source tax.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b008fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cb54d9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cd1015, D-001_

- **AC-06.5.2 Result for married employees and registered partners**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax who is married or in a registered partnership has a child of age in first education
  - **When** Lonio derives the child's result
  - **Then** the child counts if it lives in the household with a common household with the other parent, or if the employee mainly pays for its maintenance. Otherwise it does not count. Rows not observed in the calculator are verified before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-50f74a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-562299, D-003, D-009_

- **AC-06.5.3 Result for non-married employees**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax who is single, divorced, separated or widowed has a child of age in first education and was asked about main maintenance
  - **When** Lonio derives the child's result
  - **Then** a mainly maintained child living in the household (without a common household with the other parent) counts with H. A mainly maintained child outside the household counts, but not with H. A child who is not mainly maintained does not count. (A child living with the employee and the other parent depends on who contributes more; see US-06.4.) Rows not observed in the calculator are verified before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-562299, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-50f74a, D-003, D-009_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N).
