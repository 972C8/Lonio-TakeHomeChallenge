# US-06.5 – Ask household and maintenance for a child of age in first education

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** for each child of age in first education, to know whether the employee lives with the other parent and, where needed, whether the employee pays more than 50% of the child's maintenance, **so that** Lonio decides, as the calculator does, whether an adult child in first education counts for the tariff code and whether it counts as an H child for a single employee

Priority **P1** – Without these answers Lonio cannot compute the tariff code for employees with children in first education; Lonio owns the tariff result instead of HR (D-001). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b008fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cb54d9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cd1015, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-50f74a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-562299, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-332e2a, D-001, D-003, D-009

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child: common household with the other parent, Child: more than 50% of maintenance provided, Child: larger financial contributor, How a child counts for the tariff

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.5.1 Questions for a child of age in first education**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with a child of age and in first education
  - **When** the employee has answered the household question
  - **Then** if the child lives in the household, the employee is asked whether they live in a common household with the child's other parent (yes or no, required); the employee is asked whether they pay mainly (more than 50%) for the child's maintenance (yes or no, required) when the child lives outside the household, or in the household without a common household with the other parent

- **AC-06.5.2 Result for a married employee or registered partner**
  - **Given** a married employee or registered partner subject to source tax with a child of age in first education
  - **When** the employee has answered the child questions
  - **Then** the child counts if it lives in the household with a common household with the other parent; it does not count if it lives outside the household and the employee does not pay more than 50% of its maintenance. In the other cases (outside the household and mainly maintained, or in the household without a common household with the other parent) the result is not determined: the calculator exploration did not show them, so Lonio cannot compute the tariff code for this employee until they are verified (see OQ-child-paths-undetermined).

- **AC-06.5.3 Result for a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee**
  - **Given** a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee subject to source tax with a child of age in first education
  - **When** the employee has answered the child questions
  - **Then** a child in the household counts as an H child if the employee pays more than 50% of its maintenance (no common household with the other parent) or makes the larger financial contribution (common household); a child outside the household that the employee mainly maintains counts without H. In the other cases (common household with the other parent as larger contributor, or a child not mainly maintained by the employee) the result is not determined: the calculator exploration did not show them, so Lonio cannot compute the tariff code for this employee until they are verified (see OQ-child-paths-undetermined).

- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated.
