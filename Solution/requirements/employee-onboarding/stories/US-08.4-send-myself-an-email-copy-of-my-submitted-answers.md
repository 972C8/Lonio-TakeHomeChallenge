# US-08.4 – Send myself an email copy of my submitted answers

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Completing and submitting the questionnaire](../feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission).

**As** new employee, **I want** to choose, when I submit the questionnaire, to receive an email with a copy of my answers at an address I type in, **so that** I can look up what I entered myself instead of calling HR, who today get repeated calls from new employees asking what they entered

Priority **P2** – HR reports a recurring nuisance ('New employees keep calling us to ask what they entered'), but the copy is optional and blocks no registration with any receiving party. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-hr_email_copy-37cede, F-hr_email_copy-f75a41, F-hr_email_copy-6d2b5a, F-hr_email_copy-029105, F-hr_email_copy-b67d2f, F-hr_email_copy-ab2538, F-hr_email_copy-daa6b1, F-hr_email_copy-fd28be, G-01, D-036

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-copy-email-retention](../open-questions.md#oq-copy-email-retention)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission) and [information.md](../information.md)): Email copy of the answers wanted, Email address for the copy, Email copy of the submitted answers

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.4.1 Every employee is offered an optional copy at submission**
  - **Given** an employee has reached the end of the questionnaire
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether they want an email with a copy of their answers, answered with "yes" or "no"; the question may be left unanswered, and no answer means no copy is sent
  - _Sources: F-hr_email_copy-37cede, F-hr_email_copy-f75a41, G-10_

  | Example | → | Email copy of the answers wanted | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Employee is offered the copy at submission (illustrative) | → | optional | ✅ |

- **AC-08.4.2 Email address asked only when a copy is wanted**
  - **Given** an employee is submitting the questionnaire
  - **When** the employee answers whether they want a copy
  - **Then** if the answer is "yes", the employee must type in the email address the copy is sent to; if the answer is "no" or the question is left unanswered, no email address is asked
  - _Sources: F-hr_email_copy-029105, F-hr_email_copy-b67d2f, F-hr_email_copy-f75a41_

  | Example | Email copy of the answers wanted | → | Email address for the copy | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee wants a copy (illustrative) | yes | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee does not want a copy (illustrative) | no | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-08.4.3 The copy shows the submitted answers exactly, nothing more**
  - **Given** an employee has asked for a copy and typed in an email address
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** an email with a copy of the employee's answers is sent to that address; it shows the answers exactly as submitted (only the answers to questions that still apply, see the discarding of answers that no longer apply) and nothing more - no derived results such as the insurance start date, the civil status for the pension fund, the source tax result or the tariff code, and no other information; if the employee did not ask for a copy, no email is sent
  - _Sources: F-hr_email_copy-37cede, F-hr_email_copy-029105, F-hr_email_copy-ab2538, F-hr_email_copy-daa6b1, F-hr_email_copy-f75a41, D-036_

  | Example | Email copy of the answers wanted | Email address for the copy | → | Email copy of the submitted answers | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee asks for a copy and gives an address (illustrative) | yes | anna.muster@example.com | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Employee declines the copy (illustrative) | no | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Employee leaves the copy question unanswered (illustrative) | – | – | → | no | ✅ |

- **AC-08.4.4 The copy address is used only to send the employee's own copy**
  - **Given** an employee has typed in an email address for the copy
  - **When** the questionnaire is submitted and the copy is sent
  - **Then** the address serves only to send the employee their own copy; it is not passed to the pension fund or to source tax, as no receiving party needs it. Whether the address is kept after sending or recorded for HR is open (see OQ-copy-email-retention)
  - _Sources: F-hr_email_copy-fd28be, G-01_

## Evidence
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **F-hr_email_copy-029105** (hr_email_copy): If the employee wants a copy, they type in the email address the copy should be sent to (asked at the end of the questionnaire, at submission).
  - "They just type in the email address the copy should go to." — hr_email_copy/T-001, Anna
- **F-hr_email_copy-37cede** (hr_email_copy): At the end of the questionnaire, on submission, the employee should be able to send themselves an email with a copy of their form data.
  - "at the end of the questionnaire, when they submit, the employee should be able to send themselves an email with a copy of their form data" — hr_email_copy/T-001, Anna
- **F-hr_email_copy-6d2b5a** (hr_email_copy): New employees keep calling HR (Muster AG) to ask what they entered in the onboarding questionnaire.
  - "New employees keep calling us to ask what they entered" — hr_email_copy/T-001, Anna
- **F-hr_email_copy-ab2538** (hr_email_copy): The emailed copy should show the answers exactly as they were submitted, nothing more.
  - "The copy should show the answers exactly as they were submitted, nothing more." — hr_email_copy/T-001, Anna
- **F-hr_email_copy-b67d2f** (hr_email_copy, inferred): The copy email address is only needed when the employee chooses to receive a copy of their form data.
  - "It is optional, not everyone wants it." — hr_email_copy/T-001, Anna
  - "They just type in the email address the copy should go to." — hr_email_copy/T-001, Anna
- **F-hr_email_copy-daa6b1** (hr_email_copy, inferred): The emailed copy must not contain anything beyond the submitted answers (e.g. no derived values or additional information).
  - "nothing more" — hr_email_copy/T-001, Anna
- **F-hr_email_copy-f75a41** (hr_email_copy): Requesting the email copy of the form data is optional for the employee; not everyone wants it.
  - "It is optional, not everyone wants it." — hr_email_copy/T-001, Anna
- **F-hr_email_copy-fd28be** (hr_email_copy, inferred): The copy email address serves only to send the employee their own copy; it is requested by HR for the employee's benefit, not as data a receiving party (pension fund, source tax) needs.
  - "the employee should be able to send themselves an email with a copy of their form data" — hr_email_copy/T-001, Anna
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-10** (product owner guidance): Business wording for values (used in allowed values and examples): countries by English name ("Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein"), permits as "B permit", "C permit", civil status exactly as the pension fund's list shows it ("Single", "Married", ...), dates as DD.MM.YYYY, yes/no as "yes"/"no".
