# US-05.3 – State the denomination for the church-tax part of the tariff

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Source tax tariff basics and computed result](../feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** every employee who is subject to source tax to state whether they belong to the Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed church, or to none or another denomination, **so that** the church-tax suffix (Y or N) at the end of the Zurich tariff code is correct, and employees who are not subject to source tax are not asked about their religion

Priority **P1** – Without the denomination the tariff code cannot be completed, so HR would have to fall back to the manual calculator step that Lonio replaces (F-hr_transcript-563a9f, D-001). Asking it only of subject employees is data minimisation (G-01, F-hr_transcript-b84498). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b05b44, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c78e0c, F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-563a9f, G-01, D-001, D-006

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.3.1 Denomination is asked only of employees subject to source tax**
  - **Given** an employee whose earlier answers (home address country, nationality, C permit, civil status and spouse details) decide whether they are subject to source tax
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax questions
  - **Then** employees subject to source tax are asked their denomination and must answer it. Employees who are not subject to source tax are not asked. The question does not depend on the type of income, because a new employee always has earned income (D-006).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b05b44, G-01, D-001, D-006_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | Spouse's / partner's country of residence | Spouse's / partner's nationality | → | Subject to source tax | Denomination | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) - Brazilian, B permit, Swiss wife living in Switzerland, not subject | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | no | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany - subject whatever the nationality (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Single | – | – | → | yes | required | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss resident of Switzerland without C permit, single - subject (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Single | – | – | → | yes | required | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen living in Switzerland - not subject (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | Single | – | – | → | no | not asked | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss resident of Switzerland with C permit - not subject (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | yes | Single | – | – | → | no | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.2 Denomination offers exactly the calculator's two choices**
  - **Given** an employee who is asked their denomination
  - **When** the employee answers
  - **Then** they choose exactly one of "Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed" or "None or other". The answer cannot be left empty. It is recorded for the source tax tariff code only.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, D-001_
- **AC-05.3.3 Denomination sets the church-tax suffix of the tariff code**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax who has answered the denomination question
  - **When** Lonio derives the church-tax suffix of the tariff code
  - **Then** the suffix is Y for "Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed" and N for "None or other".
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c78e0c, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | Denomination | Has children | → | Church-tax suffix | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator run A0Y - single, no children, church member (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c78e0c) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Single | Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed | no | → | Y | ✅ |
  | Calculator run A0N - single, no children, none or other (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Single | None or other | no | → | N | ✅ |
