# US-05.3 – State the denomination for the church-tax part of the tariff

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Source tax tariff basics and computed result](../feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** every employee who is subject to source tax to state whether they belong to the Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed church, or to none or another denomination, **so that** the church-tax suffix (Y or N) of the Zurich tariff code can be set, and employees who are not subject to source tax are not asked about their religion

Priority **P1** – The church-tax suffix is part of every tariff code Lonio computes (D-001); without it no tariff code can be produced, and asking non-subject employees would collect sensitive data no party needs (G-01). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, G-09, D-001

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.3.1 Denomination is asked only from employees subject to source tax**
  - **Given** the employee has answered the residence and residence-status questions, and Lonio has determined whether the employee is subject to source tax (rule 'subject to source tax': everyone living abroad; in Switzerland only non-Swiss employees without a C permit who are not exempted by a Swiss or C-permit spouse living in Switzerland)
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax details
  - **Then** the denomination question is asked, and must be answered, only if the employee is subject to source tax; employees not subject to source tax are never asked it
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, G-01, D-001_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's Swiss citizenship | Spouse lives in Switzerland | → | Denomination | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás - Brazilian, B permit, Swiss wife, living in Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Married | Swiss | yes | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Single | – | – | → | required | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Swiss | – | Single | – | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss with C permit living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | yes | Single | – | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss without C permit, single, living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Single | – | – | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.2 Allowed answers for the denomination**
  - **Given** the employee is asked the denomination question
  - **When** the employee answers it
  - **Then** exactly two answers are possible: 'Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed' or 'None or other'; the question cannot be left empty; no more detailed denomination is collected, and the answer is recorded for HR as an input to the source tax tariff code
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, G-01, G-09, D-001, D-034_
- **AC-05.3.3 Denomination sets the church-tax suffix**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has answered the denomination question
  - **When** Lonio computes the tariff code
  - **Then** the church-tax suffix is 'Y' for 'Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed' and 'N' for 'None or other', as in the Zurich calculator
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, D-001_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Civil status | Denomination | → | Church-tax suffix | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Recognised church member living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | Single | Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed | → | Y | ✅ |
  | No or other denomination, living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | Single | None or other | → | N | ✅ |
