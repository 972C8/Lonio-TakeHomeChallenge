# US-05.3 – State the denomination for the church-tax part of the tariff

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Source tax tariff basics and computed result](../feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees subject to source tax to state their denomination as one of the calculator's two groups, **so that** the tariff code ends with the correct church-tax suffix (Y or N)

Priority **P1** – Without the denomination no complete tariff code can be computed, and Lonio replaces HR's manual calculator step (D-001, F-hr_transcript-563a9f). · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, D-001

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-calculator-coverage](../open-questions.md#oq-calculator-coverage)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics) and [information.md](../information.md)): Denomination, Church-tax suffix

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.3.1 Denomination is asked only from employees subject to source tax**
  - **Given** the employee's answers so far show whether they are subject to source tax
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax step
  - **Then** the denomination is asked, and must be answered, only if the employee is subject to source tax; employees not subject to source tax are never asked. The answer is recorded for source tax (church-tax suffix).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, G-01, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Nationality | Holds a C permit | → | Denomination | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Lives abroad (other country) - subject (illustrative) | France | Single | – | – | → | required | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss without C permit living in Switzerland - subject (illustrative) | Switzerland | Single | Germany | no | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen living in Switzerland - not subject (illustrative) | Switzerland | Single | Switzerland | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | C permit holder living in Switzerland - not subject (illustrative) | Switzerland | Married | Italy | yes | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.2 Denomination offers the calculator's two groups**
  - **Given** the employee is asked the denomination
  - **When** they answer
  - **Then** they choose exactly one of 'Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed' or 'None or other'; no specific religion beyond these groups is collected.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, G-01_
- **AC-05.3.3 Denomination sets the church-tax suffix**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has stated their denomination
  - **When** Lonio computes the tariff code
  - **Then** the code ends with Y for 'Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed' and with N for 'None or other'.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Denomination | → | Church-tax suffix | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | A0Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a) | Switzerland | Germany | no | Single | Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed | → | Y | ✅ |
  | A0N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a) | Switzerland | Germany | no | Single | None or other | → | N | ✅ |
