# US-08.4 – Liechtenstein: daily return and more than 45 non-return days

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence).

**As** HR, **I want** employees living in Liechtenstein to be asked whether they return daily to their main residence in Liechtenstein and, if not, whether they expect more than 45 work-related non-return days in the calendar year, **so that** Lonio records, as the calculator would, either 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' (flagged for me) or the normal tariff code

Priority **P1** – HR names Liechtenstein residents among the employees living abroad (F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a). Without these answers there is no source tax result for them (D-001, D-007). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-981b6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a2041b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e96210, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0ff846, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f4ece9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cc19c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, D-001, D-007, D-034, D-035

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence) and [information.md](../information.md)): Returns daily to the home in Liechtenstein, More than 45 work-related non-return days expected (Liechtenstein), Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.4.1 Daily return to Liechtenstein is asked only of employees living in Liechtenstein**
  - **Given** an employee lives in Liechtenstein and therefore counts as subject to source tax, so they are asked the source tax questions
  - **When** they reach the residence questions for source tax
  - **Then** They are asked whether they return daily to their main residence in Liechtenstein, answered 'yes' or 'no'. The answer is required. Employees living in any other country are not asked. The answer is recorded for source tax.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-981b6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a2041b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, D-007_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Returns daily to the home in Liechtenstein | Returns daily to the home in Germany | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Liechtenstein | → | required | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Konstanz (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Germany | → | not asked | – | ✅ |

- **AC-08.4.2 More than 45 non-return days is asked only when the employee does not return daily**
  - **Given** an employee living in Liechtenstein is asked the source tax questions
  - **When** they answer that they do not return daily to their main residence in Liechtenstein
  - **Then** They are asked whether they expect more than 45 work-related non-return days in the calendar year, answered 'yes' or 'no'. The answer is required. The employee states what they expect for the year; no proof is collected in Lonio. Employees who return daily are not asked.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e96210, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0ff846, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f4ece9, D-035_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Returns daily to the home in Liechtenstein | → | More than 45 work-related non-return days expected (Liechtenstein) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Liechtenstein resident not returning daily (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | no | → | required | ✅ |
  | Liechtenstein resident returning daily (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | yes | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-08.4.3 'In principle no source tax liability' or the normal tariff code for Liechtenstein residents**
  - **Given** an employee living in Liechtenstein has answered the Liechtenstein questions
  - **When** Lonio determines the source tax result
  - **Then** If the employee returns daily, or does not return daily and expects at most 45 non-return days, the result is 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, and it is recorded for HR as a flag. If they expect more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code applies. For employees living outside Liechtenstein the rule does not apply and the tariff code is computed as usual. How HR sees and handles the flag is part of the later HR-side spec.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cc19c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, D-007, D-034_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Returns daily to the home in Liechtenstein | More than 45 work-related non-return days expected (Liechtenstein) | → | Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator run: daily return to Liechtenstein (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1) | Liechtenstein | yes | – | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Calculator run: no daily return, not more than 45 days (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1) | Liechtenstein | no | no | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Calculator run: more than 45 days, result C1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cc19c7) | Liechtenstein | no | yes | → | no | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Konstanz (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Germany | – | – | → | no | ✅ |
