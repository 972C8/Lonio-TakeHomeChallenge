# US-08.4 – Liechtenstein: daily return and more than 45 non-return days

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence).

**As** source tax office, **I want** an employee living in Liechtenstein to be asked, before denomination, spouse and children, whether they expect to return daily to their main residence in Liechtenstein and, if not, whether they expect more than 45 work-related non-return days in the calendar year, **so that** Lonio can record, as the calculator does, either 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' or the normal tariff code subject to the double taxation agreement, flagged for HR, without asking tariff questions that are not needed

Priority **P1** – HR's rule makes every Liechtenstein resident subject to source tax while the calculator says in principle not; this conflict must be handled for each Liechtenstein resident (D-007) and the result decides whether a tariff code exists at all. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-981b6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a2041b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e96210, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0ff846, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f4ece9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cc19c7, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, D-007, D-034, D-035, D-040, D-047

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence) and [information.md](../information.md)): Daily return to Liechtenstein, More than 45 work-related non-return days (Liechtenstein), Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.4.1 Liechtenstein residents are asked the daily return question first**
  - **Given** the employee's home address is in Liechtenstein (so HR's rule makes them subject to source tax and they get the source tax questions, D-007)
  - **When** the employee reaches step 5 'Cross-border commuting'
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether they expect to return daily to their main residence in Liechtenstein, answering 'yes' or 'no'; the answer is required and recorded for source tax; this comes before the questions on denomination, spouse gainful employment and children (assumption D-040, to be verified by a domain expert); employees living elsewhere are not asked
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-981b6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a2041b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, D-007, D-040, D-047_

  | Living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-08.4.2 More than 45 non-return days is asked only without daily return**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Liechtenstein
  - **When** the employee answers that they do not return daily
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether they expect more than 45 work-related non-return days in the calendar year, answering 'yes' or 'no'; the answer is a statement of what the employee expects and no document is asked (D-035); it is required and recorded for source tax; with daily return it is not asked
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e96210, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0ff846, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f4ece9, D-035_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Daily return to Liechtenstein | → | More than 45 work-related non-return days (Liechtenstein) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|

  | Daily return - not asked (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | yes | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-08.4.3 In principle no source tax liability: tariff questions are skipped**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Liechtenstein
  - **When** the employee returns daily, or does not return daily and does not expect more than 45 non-return days
  - **Then** instead of a tariff code Lonio records 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement', flagged for HR; the source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, children) are not asked (assumption D-040, to be verified by a domain expert); how HR handles the flag belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1, D-007, D-040, D-034_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Daily return to Liechtenstein | More than 45 work-related non-return days (Liechtenstein) | → | Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability | Denomination | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator: daily return 'Ja' (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-fb87f1) | Liechtenstein | yes | – | → | yes | not asked | ✅ |
  | Calculator: not more than 45 days (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-fb87f1) | Liechtenstein | no | no | → | yes | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-08.4.4 More than 45 non-return days: normal tariff subject to the double taxation agreement**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Liechtenstein and does not return daily
  - **When** the employee expects more than 45 non-return days
  - **Then** the source tax details are asked as for any employee subject to source tax and the normal tariff code is recorded with the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement', flagged for HR (assumption D-040, to be verified by a domain expert)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cc19c7, D-007, D-040, D-034_

## Evidence
- **D-007** (product decision (Product owner)): Liechtenstein residents are asked the source tax questions (HR's rule decides who is asked). The result follows the calculator - with daily return or at most 45 non-return days, Lonio shows 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR; with more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code. This closes OQ-liechtenstein-conflict. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-035** (product decision (Product owner)): For the cross-border questions the employee states what they expect for the year (non-return days). For the certificate of residence 'not yet' is a valid answer and counts as 'no' for the tariff code. Documents go to HR outside Lonio; no upload.
- **D-040** (product decision (Product owner)): For employees living in Liechtenstein, the questions on daily return and on more than 45 days abroad come before denomination, spouse and children. When the answers mean 'in principle no source tax liability', the tariff questions are skipped. When the employee stays abroad more than 45 days, the normal tariff is recorded with the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement' and flagged for HR. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
