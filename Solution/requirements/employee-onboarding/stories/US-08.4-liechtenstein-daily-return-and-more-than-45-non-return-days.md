# US-08.4 – Liechtenstein: daily return and more than 45 non-return days

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees who live in Liechtenstein to say whether they return daily to their home there and, if not, whether they have more than 45 work-related non-return days in the calendar year, **so that** the calculator can decide whether the normal tariff applies or whether there is in principle no source tax liability (subject to the double taxation agreement)

Priority **P1** – HR names Liechtenstein residents as a real case of employees living abroad (F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a); without these answers the calculator gives no result for them. · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-981b6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cf291a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-634043, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a2041b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-589042, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e96210, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b207ef, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f4ece9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a24a52, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0ff846, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-dd0a17, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cc19c7, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.4.1 Daily return to Liechtenstein is asked only of Liechtenstein residents**
  - **Given** the employee is subject to source tax (anyone living abroad is) and has answered the type of income
  - **When** the country of the home address is Liechtenstein
  - **Then** the employee must answer 'yes' or 'no' to whether they return daily to their main residence in Liechtenstein (for replacement income: during or before receiving it); employees living in any other country are not asked. With daily return the calculator gives 'in principle no source tax liability', subject to the double taxation agreement. Whether HR then still treats the employee as subject is open (see OQ-liechtenstein); the questions are asked either way. The answer is needed by source tax only
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-981b6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cf291a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-634043, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a2041b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Type of income (source tax) | → | Employee is subject to source tax | Returns daily to the home in Liechtenstein | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Liechtenstein | Earned income | → | yes | required | ✅ |
  | Liechtenstein resident with replacement income (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Replacement income | → | – | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Konstanz (F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Germany | Earned income | → | – | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-08.4.2 More than 45 non-return days is asked only without daily return**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Liechtenstein and is subject to source tax
  - **When** the employee answers that they do not return daily to their home in Liechtenstein
  - **Then** the employee must answer 'yes' or 'no' to whether they have more than 45 work-related non-return days in the calendar year (for replacement income: during or before receiving it). 'yes' leads to the normal tariff, subject to the double taxation agreement; 'no' leads to in principle no source tax liability (see OQ-liechtenstein). Employees who return daily are not asked. The answer is needed by source tax only
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e96210, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b207ef, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f4ece9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a24a52, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0ff846, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cc19c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Type of income (source tax) | Returns daily to the home in Liechtenstein | → | More than 45 work-related non-return days | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Liechtenstein resident not returning daily (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Earned income | no | → | required | ✅ |
  | Liechtenstein resident returning daily (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Earned income | yes | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Liechtenstein resident with replacement income not returning daily (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Replacement income | no | → | required | ✅ |
