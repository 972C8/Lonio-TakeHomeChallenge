# US-07.1 – Ask about children only of employees who need a source tax tariff

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** Lonio to ask whether the employee has biological or adopted children, but only when the employee is subject to source tax, **so that** the child digit of the tariff code can be worked out, and no data about children is collected from employees who do not need a tariff

Priority **P1** – Without this answer Lonio cannot compute the tariff code (D-001). Today HR has to click through the calculator by hand. Asking everyone would collect personal data that no party needs (G-01). · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a33b65, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-75cd87, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, D-001, D-003

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.1.1 The children question is asked only of employees subject to source tax**
  - **Given** The employee has answered the questions that decide whether they are subject to source tax (rule subject_to_source_tax: country of residence and, for residents of Switzerland, Swiss citizenship, C permit, civil status and spouse details)
  - **When** the questionnaire reaches the source tax questions
  - **Then** Employees subject to source tax are asked whether they have children and must answer it. This includes everyone living abroad, Liechtenstein residents among them. Employees who are not subject to source tax are not asked.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, G-01, D-001, D-007_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | Spouse's / registered partner's nationality | Spouse's / registered partner's country of residence | → | Subject to source tax | Has children | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Maria (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | Portugal | yes | Single | – | – | → | no | not asked | ✅ |
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | no | not asked | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | Married | – | – | → | no | not asked | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss without C permit living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Single | – | – | → | yes | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Divorced | – | – | → | yes | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | – | – | Married | – | – | → | yes | required | ✅ |

- **AC-07.1.2 The question covers the spouse's or partner's children for married employees and registered partners**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax is asked whether they have children
  - **When** the employee's civil status is Married or Registered partnership
  - **Then** The question asks about biological or adopted children of the employee or of the spouse / registered partner. For Single, Divorced, Widowed and Separated employees it asks only about the employee's own biological or adopted children. The answer is "yes" or "no".
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003_
- **AC-07.1.3 No children: no child questions and child digit 0**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax
  - **When** they answer "no" to having children
  - **Then** No per-child questions are asked and the child digit of the tariff code is 0. When they answer "yes", the questions for the first child start.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, D-001_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Civil status | Has children | → | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child digit of the tariff code | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | No children (illustrative) | Germany | Single | no | → | not asked | 0 | ✅ |
  | Has children (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | → | required | – | ✅ |
