# US-07.1 – Ask about children only of employees who need a source tax tariff

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** new employee, **I want** to be asked whether I have children only when Lonio needs a source tax tariff code for me, **so that** I do not give family details that no receiving party needs, while the source tax side still gets the child part of my tariff code

Priority **P1** – The child digit is part of every tariff code Lonio computes (D-001); without it no tariff can be set. Asking employees who are not subject to source tax would break data minimisation (G-01, F-hr_transcript-b84498). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1, G-01, D-001, D-003, D-007, D-040, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Has children

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.1.1 Asked when a tariff code is needed**
  - **Given** the employee is subject to source tax and a tariff code is needed for them (everyone subject to source tax, except Liechtenstein residents who return daily or expect at most 45 non-return days)
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax questions
  - **Then** Lonio asks whether the employee has biological or adopted children, answered yes or no; an answer is required. The answer is recorded for HR and used for the source tax tariff code.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, D-001, D-007, D-040_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Civil status | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Daily return to Liechtenstein | More than 45 non-return days (Liechtenstein) | → | Subject to source tax | Has children | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Lives in Germany, single (illustrative) | Germany | Single | – | – | – | – | → | yes | required | ✅ |
  | Lives in Switzerland, not Swiss, no C permit, single (illustrative) | Switzerland | Single | Portugal | no | – | – | → | yes | asked | ✅ |
  | Lives in Liechtenstein, more than 45 non-return days (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Married | – | – | no | yes | → | – | asked | ✅ |

- **AC-07.1.2 Not asked when the employee is not subject to source tax**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland and is not subject to source tax (Swiss, holds a C permit, or married to / in a registered partnership with a Swiss national or C permit holder living in Switzerland)
  - **When** the employee completes the questionnaire
  - **Then** Lonio does not ask about children and none of the child questions appear.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | Spouse's country of residence | → | Subject to source tax | Has children | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | no | not asked | ✅ |
  | Maria (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | Portugal | yes | Single | – | – | → | no | not asked | ✅ |
  | Swiss employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | Married | – | – | → | no | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-07.1.3 Not asked of Liechtenstein residents in principle without source tax liability**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Liechtenstein and expects to return there daily, or does not return daily but expects at most 45 non-return days in the year
  - **When** the employee completes the questionnaire
  - **Then** Lonio does not ask about children, because no tariff code is computed; the result is 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement'. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert: the Liechtenstein questions come before the children questions and skip them (D-040).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1, D-007, D-040_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Civil status | Daily return to Liechtenstein | More than 45 non-return days (Liechtenstein) | → | Has children | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Liechtenstein, daily return (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Single | yes | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Liechtenstein, no daily return, at most 45 non-return days (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Single | no | no | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-07.1.4 Which children are meant depends on civil status**
  - **Given** the question about children is asked
  - **When** the employee is married or in a registered partnership
  - **Then** the question covers biological or adopted children of the employee or of their spouse or registered partner; for single, divorced, separated or widowed employees it covers the employee's own biological or adopted children. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert: registered partnership counts as married and separated as single, as in the calculator (D-003).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, D-003_
- **AC-07.1.5 No children means no child questions and child digit 0**
  - **Given** the employee is asked about children
  - **When** the employee answers no
  - **Then** no per-child questions are asked and the child digit of the tariff code is 0.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c53e3_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Civil status | Has children | → | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child digit | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Germany, single, no children (illustrative) | Germany | Single | no | → | not asked | 0 | ✅ |

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-007** (product decision (Product owner)): Liechtenstein residents are asked the source tax questions (HR's rule decides who is asked). The result follows the calculator - with daily return or at most 45 non-return days, Lonio shows 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR; with more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code. This closes OQ-liechtenstein-conflict. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-040** (product decision (Product owner)): For employees living in Liechtenstein, the questions on daily return and on more than 45 days abroad come before denomination, spouse and children. When the answers mean 'in principle no source tax liability', the tariff questions are skipped. When the employee stays abroad more than 45 days, the normal tariff is recorded with the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement' and flagged for HR. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
