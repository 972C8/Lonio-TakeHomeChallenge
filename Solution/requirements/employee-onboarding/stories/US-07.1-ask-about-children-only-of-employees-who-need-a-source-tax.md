# US-07.1 – Ask about children only of employees who need a source tax tariff

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the employee to say whether they (or, if married, they or their spouse) have biological or adopted children, asked only when the employee is subject to source tax and has earned income, **so that** the Zurich tariff calculator's children questions can be answered without follow-up emails, while employees who are not subject to source tax are not asked about their family (data minimisation)

Priority **P1** – Without this answer the source tax tariff cannot be determined; HR today cannot tell up front who must answer source tax questions, and data minimisation forbids asking everyone (G-01). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a33b65, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-75cd87, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-351289, F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-calculator-coverage](../open-questions.md#oq-calculator-coverage)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Has biological or adopted children

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.1.1 Asked only of employees subject to source tax**
  - **Given** the employee's answers on residence, nationality, permit and spouse decide whether they are subject to source tax (rule subject_to_source_tax, owned by another section) and they have earned income
  - **When** the questionnaire reaches the source tax questions
  - **Then** the children question is asked, and must be answered, only if the employee is subject to source tax; employees who are not subject never see it
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-845204, G-01_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Civil status | Type of income (source tax) | Religious denomination (church tax) | Nationality | Permit type | Spouse's nationality | Spouse's permit type | Spouse works or receives replacement income | Spouse's country of residence | → | Has biological or adopted children | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single employee living abroad (illustrative) | Germany | Single | Earned income | None or other | – | – | – | – | – | – | → | required | ✅ |
  | Married B-permit holder with a non-Swiss spouse (illustrative) | Switzerland | Married | Earned income | None or other | Italy | B permit | Italy | B permit | no | – | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Married | – | – | Brazil | B permit | Switzerland | – | – | Switzerland | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Maria (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | – | – | – | Portugal | C permit | – | – | – | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Swiss national living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Married | – | – | Switzerland | – | – | – | – | – | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-07.1.2 Not asked for replacement income**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax whose income type is replacement income
  - **When** the source tax questions are asked
  - **Then** the children question is not asked, because the calculator skips all personal and child questions for replacement income (see OQ-income-type on whether a new employee can have replacement income at all)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-351289_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Civil status | Type of income (source tax) | → | Has biological or adopted children | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Replacement income (illustrative) | Germany | Single | Replacement income | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-07.1.3 Position and scope of the question depend on civil status**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with earned income
  - **When** the children question is asked
  - **Then** for a married employee it comes after the question whether the spouse works and covers biological or adopted children of the employee or the spouse; for an employee who is not married it comes after the denomination question and covers the employee's own biological or adopted children
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a33b65, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-75cd87_
- **AC-07.1.4 Answer yes or no; yes opens the per-child questions**
  - **Given** the children question is asked
  - **When** the employee answers
  - **Then** the answer is "yes" or "no" and cannot be left empty; only "yes" leads to the per-child questions, "no" asks nothing about children; the answer is needed by the source tax office (Zurich calculator)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Civil status | Type of income (source tax) | Religious denomination (church tax) | Has biological or adopted children | → | Child: minor or adult in first education | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | No children (illustrative) | Germany | Single | Earned income | None or other | no | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Has children (illustrative) | Germany | Single | Earned income | None or other | yes | → | asked | ✅ |
