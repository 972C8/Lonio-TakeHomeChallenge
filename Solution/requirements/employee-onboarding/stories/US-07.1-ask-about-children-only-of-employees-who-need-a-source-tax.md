# US-07.1 – Ask about children only of employees who need a source tax tariff

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** new employee, **I want** to be asked about my children only when a source tax tariff code has to be worked out for me, **so that** I give no personal data about my children that no party needs, while Lonio still has what it needs to compute my tariff code

Priority **P1** – Data minimisation is a core goal (G-01), and HR said source tax questions should only go to people who are subject to source tax (F-hr_transcript-b84498). · Status: **decision** · Sources: G-01, F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, D-001, D-007, D-040, D-047

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child: minor or adult in first education

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.1.1 The children step is shown only after a 'yes' to having children**
  - **Given** the employee is subject to source tax and source tax details are asked (step 6 Source tax details), and the employee answered whether they (or, if married or in a registered partnership, either partner) have biological or adopted children
  - **When** the employee continues to step 7 Children
  - **Then** the child questions are asked only if the answer was 'yes'. With 'no', no child question is asked and the child digit of the tariff code is 0.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, D-047_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Civil status | Has children | Nationality | Holds a C permit | → | Child: minor or adult in first education | Number of counted children (child digit) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married cross-border commuter from Germany with children (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | – | – | → | required | – | ✅ |
  | Single resident of Germany without children (illustrative) | Germany | Single | no | – | – | → | not asked | 0 | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss without C permit living in Switzerland, with children (illustrative) | Switzerland | Single | yes | Portugal | no | → | required | – | ✅ |

- **AC-07.1.2 No child questions for employees not subject to source tax**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland and is Swiss, or is non-Swiss with a C permit, or is otherwise not subject to source tax
  - **When** the employee goes through the questionnaire
  - **Then** neither the question about having children nor any child question is asked.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Nationality | Civil status | Holds a C permit | → | Subject to source tax | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Swiss citizen living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | Married | – | → | no | not asked | not asked | ✅ |
  | Maria: Portuguese with a C permit (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | Portugal | Single | yes | → | no | – | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-07.1.3 Liechtenstein residents: child questions only with more than 45 non-return days**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Liechtenstein
  - **When** the employee answers the cross-border questions (step 5), which come before denomination, spouse and children
  - **Then** the child questions are asked only if the employee does not return daily and expects more than 45 work-related non-return days in the year (and has children). With daily return or at most 45 non-return days, the result is 'in principle no source tax liability' and no child question is asked.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cc19c7, D-007, D-040_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Civil status | Daily return to Liechtenstein | More than 45 non-return days (Liechtenstein) | Has children | → | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Liechtenstein, daily return (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1) | Liechtenstein | Married | yes | – | – | → | not asked | not asked | ✅ |
  | Liechtenstein, no daily return, at most 45 days (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1) | Liechtenstein | Single | no | no | – | → | – | not asked | ✅ |
  | Liechtenstein, more than 45 non-return days, with children (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Married | no | yes | yes | → | – | required | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-007** (product decision (Product owner)): Liechtenstein residents are asked the source tax questions (HR's rule decides who is asked). The result follows the calculator - with daily return or at most 45 non-return days, Lonio shows 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR; with more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code. This closes OQ-liechtenstein-conflict. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-040** (product decision (Product owner)): For employees living in Liechtenstein, the questions on daily return and on more than 45 days abroad come before denomination, spouse and children. When the answers mean 'in principle no source tax liability', the tariff questions are skipped. When the employee stays abroad more than 45 days, the normal tariff is recorded with the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement' and flagged for HR. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
