# US-01.5 – Discard answers that no longer apply on submission

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Questionnaire flow overview](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow).

**As** new employee, **I want** answers I gave to questions that no longer apply after I change an earlier answer to be kept while I move back and forth, but discarded when I submit, **so that** I lose no input while correcting myself, and no personal data that no party needs is kept about me

Priority **P2** – Required by data minimisation (G-01); the keep-while-editing part is a convenience. · Status: **decision** · Sources: G-01, D-034, D-036, D-048, D-050

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow) and [information.md](../information.md)): Discard answers that no longer apply

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.5.1 Answers are kept while going back and forth**
  - **Given** an employee has answered questions that depend on an earlier answer (for example the spouse's date of birth after answering Married)
  - **When** before submitting they change the earlier answer so that those questions no longer apply, and later change it back
  - **Then** the earlier answers to those questions reappear unchanged; this is testable before submission. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert.
  - _Sources: D-036, D-050_
- **AC-01.5.2 Answers that no longer apply are discarded on submission**
  - **Given** an employee has answered questions that, after a later change of an earlier answer, no longer apply to them
  - **When** they submit the questionnaire
  - **Then** those answers are discarded and are not recorded for any receiving party; only answers to questions that apply are kept.
  - _Sources: D-036, G-01_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Nationality | Civil status | → | Spouse's or registered partner's date of birth | Daily return to Germany | Denomination | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee first answered Married, then changed to Single (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | Single | → | not asked | – | – | ✅ |
  | Employee first entered Germany, then changed the home address to Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | → | – | not asked | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-01.5.3 The same applies after an HR change**
  - **Given** a submitted questionnaire
  - **When** HR changes an answer so that some recorded answers no longer apply
  - **Then** those answers are discarded, as on the employee's submission. How HR makes the change belongs to the later HR-side spec. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert.
  - _Sources: D-048, D-034, D-036_

## Evidence
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **D-048** (product decision (Product owner)): When HR changes an answer after submission so that questions apply that were never asked, the derived results that depend on them (for example the tariff code) show 'incomplete - HR must complete' instead of a value, until HR enters the missing answers. Answers that no longer apply after an HR change are discarded, as on the employee's submission. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-050** (product decision (Product owner)): Build-time values. HR provides the permit reminder wording before go-live. Country of residence and nationality are chosen from the ISO 3166 country list; nationality also offers 'stateless'. The date of birth must make the employee at least 14 and at most 100 years old on the first working day; the first working day must lie within one year after the questionnaire is sent. Answers are saved as the employee goes and they can resume until they submit; submitting needs one confirmation on a summary page. Answers are kept when the employee goes back and changes an earlier answer (until submission), and this is testable. The spouse's residence is asked as 'Does your spouse live in Switzerland? yes/no', not as a country. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
