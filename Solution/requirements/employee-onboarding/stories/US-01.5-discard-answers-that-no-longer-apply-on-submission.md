# US-01.5 – Discard answers that no longer apply on submission

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Questionnaire flow overview](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow).

**As** new employee, **I want** answers to questions that no longer apply after I change an earlier answer to be discarded when I submit, while being kept until then, **so that** I can go back and forth without losing input, and no personal data is kept that no party needs

Priority **P2** – Required by data minimisation (G-01) but it affects only employees who change earlier answers; it blocks no registration. · Status: **decision** · Sources: D-036, G-01, D-045

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-hr-side-follow-ups](../open-questions.md#oq-hr-side-follow-ups)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow) and [information.md](../information.md)): Discard answers that no longer apply

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.5.1 Answers kept while editing**
  - **Given** the employee has answered a question and then changes an earlier answer so that the question no longer applies
  - **When** they change the earlier answer back before submitting
  - **Then** their previous answer to the question is still there and they do not have to enter it again
  - _Sources: D-036_
- **AC-01.5.2 Answers that no longer apply are discarded on submission**
  - **Given** the employee's final answers mean that a question they answered earlier no longer applies
  - **When** they submit the questionnaire
  - **Then** the answer to that question is discarded and not recorded for any party; only answers to questions that apply to the final answers are recorded
  - _Sources: D-036, G-01_

## Evidence
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **D-045** (product decision (Product owner)): Business text (stories, acceptance criteria, notices) uses no internal snake_case ids and does not speak of 'expressions'. Any behaviour that has no cited fact or decision is stated in the story as an assumption. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
