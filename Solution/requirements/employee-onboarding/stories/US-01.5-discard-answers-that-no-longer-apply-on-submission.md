# US-01.5 – Discard answers that no longer apply on submission

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Questionnaire flow overview](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow).

**As** new employee, **I want** to go back and change an earlier answer without losing my other answers, while answers to questions that no longer apply are not kept once I submit, **so that** I can correct myself freely, and Lonio keeps no personal data about me that no party needs

Priority **P1** – Data minimisation is a hard constraint (G-01); losing input when going back would frustrate employees. · Status: **decision** · Sources: D-036, D-050, G-01

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow) and [information.md](../information.md)): Discard answers that no longer apply, Questionnaire flow

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.5.1 Going back keeps answers until submission**
  - **Given** an employee has answered questions in later steps and goes back to change an earlier answer so that some of those questions no longer apply
  - **When** the employee changes the answer back, or moves forward again, before submitting
  - **Then** every answer given earlier is still there, including answers to questions that currently do not apply, so no input is lost by going back and forth before submission.
  - _Sources: D-036, D-050_
- **AC-01.5.2 Answers that no longer apply are discarded on submission**
  - **Given** at submission some answers belong to questions that do not apply to the employee's latest answers
  - **When** the employee submits
  - **Then** those answers are discarded and are not recorded, shown on the record or used for any result; only answers to questions that apply are kept.
  - _Sources: D-036, G-01_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Civil status | → | Daily return to Germany | Denomination | Has biological or adopted children | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Moved the home address from Germany to Switzerland before submitting; Germany cross-border and source tax answers are discarded (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | Single | → | not asked | not asked | not asked | – | ✅ |
  | Changed civil status from Married to Divorced before submitting; spouse date of birth is discarded (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | Divorced | → | – | – | – | not asked | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **D-050** (product decision (Product owner)): Build-time values. HR provides the permit reminder wording before go-live. Country of residence and nationality are chosen from the ISO 3166 country list; nationality also offers 'stateless'. The date of birth must make the employee at least 14 and at most 100 years old on the first working day; the first working day must lie within one year after the questionnaire is sent. Answers are saved as the employee goes and they can resume until they submit; submitting needs one confirmation on a summary page. Answers are kept when the employee goes back and changes an earlier answer (until submission), and this is testable. The spouse's residence is asked as 'Does your spouse live in Switzerland? yes/no', not as a country. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
