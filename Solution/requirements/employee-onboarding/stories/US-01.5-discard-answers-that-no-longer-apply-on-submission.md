# US-01.5 – Discard answers that no longer apply on submission

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Questionnaire flow overview](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow).

**As** new employee, **I want** to change earlier answers without losing my input while I go back and forth, and to have answers to questions that no longer apply dropped when I submit, **so that** I can correct myself easily, and no personal data that no receiving party needs is kept about me

Priority **P1** – Data minimisation is a hard constraint: collecting data we must not have is a stated risk (G-01). · Status: **decision** · Sources: G-01, D-036

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow) and [information.md](../information.md)): Country of residence (country of the home address), Civil status, Nationality, Holds a C permit (permanent residence), Discard answers that no longer apply

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.5.1 Answers are kept while the employee goes back and forth**
  - **Given** the employee has answered a question and then changes an earlier answer so that the question no longer applies
  - **When** the employee changes the earlier answer back before submitting
  - **Then** the question applies again and the previously given answer is still there; no input is lost.
  - _Sources: D-036_
- **AC-01.5.2 Answers to questions that no longer apply are discarded on submission**
  - **Given** the employee has answers to questions that no longer apply after a change of an earlier answer
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** only answers to questions that apply to the submitted answers are recorded; all other answers are discarded and not recorded for any party.
  - _Sources: G-01, D-036_

## Evidence
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
