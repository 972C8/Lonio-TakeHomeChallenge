# US-01.6 – Make the questionnaire read-only after submission and derive results from the latest answers

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Questionnaire flow overview](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow).

**As** HR, **I want** the questionnaire to become read-only for the employee once submitted, and every derived result to be derived again from the latest answers, **so that** later changes go through HR, and derived values such as the insurance start date never go stale at the receiving parties

Priority **P1** – A missed update after Julia's first working day moved caused a wrong date at the pension fund, an unfriendly letter and weeks of fixing (F-hr_transcript-e18e66). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-e18e66, F-hr_transcript-1d5c07, F-hr_transcript-872bd7, D-033, D-034, D-037

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow) and [information.md](../information.md)): Country of residence (country of the home address), Nationality, Holds a C permit (permanent residence), Civil status, Spouse's / registered partner's nationality, Spouse's / registered partner's country of residence, Read-only after submission

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.6.1 The employee cannot change answers after submission**
  - **Given** the employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** the employee opens it again
  - **Then** the employee can see the answers but cannot change them. Later changes (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality, a moved first working day) are made by HR; how HR does this is part of the later HR-side spec (D-034).
  - _Sources: D-033, D-034, D-037_
- **AC-01.6.2 Derived results always follow the latest answers**
  - **Given** an answer recorded for an employee is changed after submission
  - **When** the derived results are looked at
  - **Then** the insurance start date, the civil status for the pension fund and for source tax, whether the employee is subject to source tax, and the tariff code are all derived again from the latest answers. For the first working day only the latest date counts; no history of earlier dates is kept.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-e18e66, D-033, D-037_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | Spouse's / registered partner's nationality | Spouse's / registered partner's country of residence | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Nationality corrected to Switzerland after submission (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | – | – | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss employee without C permit marries a Swiss citizen living in Switzerland after submission (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Married | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | no | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen moves from Switzerland to Germany after submission (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | – | – | – | → | yes | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-033** (product decision (Product owner)): The employee enters the first working day. After submission only HR can change it; the latest date always counts and no history of earlier dates is kept.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **F-hr_transcript-1d5c07** (hr_transcript): Consolidated rule: the insurance start date is derived from the first working day. If the first working day is the 1st of a month, insurance starts that day. Otherwise it starts on the 1st of the following month.
