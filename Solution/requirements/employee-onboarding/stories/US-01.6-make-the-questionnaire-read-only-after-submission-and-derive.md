# US-01.6 – Make the questionnaire read-only after submission and derive results from the latest answers

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Questionnaire flow overview](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow).

**As** HR, **I want** the questionnaire to be read-only for the employee once submitted, with later changes made by HR and every derived result always derived again from the latest answers, **so that** the record stays consistent and the pension fund and source tax always get results that match the current facts

Priority **P2** – Prevents inconsistent records after submission; the HR editing itself is specified later (D-034). · Status: **decision** · Sources: D-037, D-033, D-034, D-048, D-036

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow) and [information.md](../information.md)): Read-only after submission, Discard answers that no longer apply

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.6.1 Read-only for the employee after submission**
  - **Given** an employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** the employee opens it again
  - **Then** the employee can see the answers but cannot change them; any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality, a moved first working day) is made by HR, in the HR-side spec to be written later.
  - _Sources: D-037, D-033, D-034_
- **AC-01.6.2 Derived results always follow the latest answers**
  - **Given** an answer has changed after submission
  - **When** the derived results are looked at
  - **Then** the insurance start date, the civil status for the pension fund and for source tax, whether the employee is subject to source tax and the tariff code are derived again from the latest answers; for the first working day the latest date always counts and no history of earlier dates is kept.
  - _Sources: D-037, D-033_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | Spouse lives in Switzerland | → | Subject to source tax | Denomination | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) as submitted | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | yes | → | no | – | ✅ |
  | Tomás after HR records that his wife no longer lives in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | no | → | yes | asked | ✅ |

- **AC-01.6.3 HR change that opens unanswered questions**
  - **Given** after submission HR changes an answer so that questions apply that the employee was never asked (for example the employee now becomes subject to source tax)
  - **When** the derived results that depend on those questions are looked at
  - **Then** they show 'incomplete - HR must complete' instead of a value (for example the tariff code) until HR enters the missing answers. Assumption (to be verified by a domain expert): this follows D-048.
  - _Sources: D-048, D-037_
- **AC-01.6.4 HR change that makes answers no longer apply**
  - **Given** after submission HR changes an answer so that some recorded answers no longer apply
  - **When** HR's change is saved
  - **Then** the answers that no longer apply are discarded, as on the employee's submission. Assumption (to be verified by a domain expert): this follows D-048.
  - _Sources: D-048, D-036, G-01_

## Evidence
- **D-033** (product decision (Product owner)): The employee enters the first working day. After submission only HR can change it; the latest date always counts and no history of earlier dates is kept.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **D-048** (product decision (Product owner)): When HR changes an answer after submission so that questions apply that were never asked, the derived results that depend on them (for example the tariff code) show 'incomplete - HR must complete' instead of a value, until HR enters the missing answers. Answers that no longer apply after an HR change are discarded, as on the employee's submission. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
