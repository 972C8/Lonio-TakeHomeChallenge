# US-08.3 – Questionnaire is read-only for the employee after submission

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Completing and submitting the questionnaire](../feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission).

**As** HR, **I want** the questionnaire to be read-only for the employee once submitted, with every derived result always taken from the latest recorded answers, **so that** the answers recorded for the pension fund registration, the source tax tariff code and HR stay consistent, and later changes go through HR

Priority **P1** – Wrong or stale derived results (e.g. an insurance start date from an old first working day) would give the pension fund or source tax wrong data; HR reports that the first working day often moves. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-872bd7, F-hr_transcript-1d5c07, D-033, D-034, D-037

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission) and [information.md](../information.md)): Discard answers that no longer apply

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.3.1 Employee cannot change answers after submission**
  - **Given** an employee who has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** they open it again
  - **Then** they can see their answers but cannot change them; any later change (such as a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality or a new first working day) is made by HR, which is specified in the later HR-side spec
  - _Sources: D-033, D-034, D-037_
- **AC-08.3.2 Derived results always come from the latest answers**
  - **Given** a submitted questionnaire whose answers are later changed (by HR)
  - **When** the insurance start date, the civil status for the pension fund and for source tax, the source tax liability or the tariff code are needed
  - **Then** each is derived again from the latest answers; no earlier result and no history of earlier first working days is kept
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-1d5c07, D-033, D-037_

  | Example | First working day | → | Insurance start date | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | First working day moved from the 15th to the 1st of a month (illustrative) | 01.03.2026 | → | 01.03.2026 | ✅ |
  | First working day in the middle of a month (illustrative) | 15.03.2026 | → | 01.04.2026 | ✅ |

_Trace check: partial – The priority reason's claim that HR reports the first working day often moves is not in any cited fact; the rest is backed by D-033, D-037 and F-1d5c07._


## Evidence
- **D-033** (product decision (Product owner)): The employee enters the first working day. After submission only HR can change it; the latest date always counts and no history of earlier dates is kept.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **F-hr_transcript-1d5c07** (hr_transcript): Consolidated: insurance start date is derived from the (latest confirmed) first working day: if it is the 1st of a month, insurance starts that day; otherwise on the 1st of the following month.
  - "because insurance starts on the first of the following month." — hr_transcript/T-026, Anna
  - "Unless somebody starts exactly on the first, of course, then it's just that day." — hr_transcript/T-028, Anna
- **F-hr_transcript-872bd7** (hr_transcript): The employee's first working day is needed for the pension fund; it is the most important item.
  - "I need their first working day. That's the number one thing" — hr_transcript/T-024, Anna
