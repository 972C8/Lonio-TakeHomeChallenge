# US-04.2 – Ask for the spouse's or registered partner's date of birth when the fund registers the employee as Married

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** to receive the spouse's date of birth for every employee registered with me as Married, **so that** the registration is not blocked by a missing required field

Priority **P1** – The fund's portal blocks the registration with 'Spouse's date of birth is required.' when the civil status is Married and the field is empty. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-pension_portal-a0065b, F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-c5ee15, F-pension_portal-ef28c3, F-pension_portal-6d3630, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, D-003, D-004, D-036, G-01

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-fund-divorced-widowed](../open-questions.md#oq-fund-divorced-widowed)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.2.1 Asked and required only of married, registered-partner and separated employees**
  - **Given** an employee has answered the civil status question in step 4
  - **When** the civil status is Married, Registered partnership or Separated
  - **Then** the employee is asked for the date of birth of their spouse or registered partner and must answer it. For Single, Divorced or Widowed it is not asked, and no spouse data is sent to the fund. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-004): the fund needs the date of birth for everyone it registers as Married, which includes registered partners and separated employees (D-003). It also assumes the fund needs nothing extra for Divorced or Widowed (see OQ-fund-divorced-widowed).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-c5ee15, F-pension_portal-6d3630, D-003, D-004_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel, registered partnership (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | required | ✅ |
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Married | → | required | ✅ |
  | Separated employee (illustrative) | Separated | → | required | ✅ |
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee (illustrative) | Divorced | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee (illustrative) | Widowed | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-04.2.2 Date of birth given as a full date**
  - **Given** the employee is asked for the spouse's or registered partner's date of birth
  - **When** they enter it
  - **Then** it is accepted only as a complete date in the form DD.MM.YYYY, as the fund requires, and is recorded for the pension fund.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-ef28c3, F-pension_portal-a0065b_
- **AC-04.2.3 Spouse's date of birth discarded when it no longer applies**
  - **Given** an employee entered a spouse's date of birth and then changed their civil status to Single, Divorced or Widowed
  - **When** they submit the questionnaire
  - **Then** the spouse's date of birth is not kept. Before submission, it is kept so that the employee loses no input if they change the civil status back.
  - _Sources: D-036, G-01_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-004** (product decision (Product owner)): The spouse's (or registered partner's) date of birth is asked from married employees, registered partners and separated employees (all entered at the fund as Married, see D-003), not from divorced or widowed employees; no spouse data is sent to the fund for them. This closes OQ-spouse-dob-other-status and OQ-separated-spouse-dob. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
