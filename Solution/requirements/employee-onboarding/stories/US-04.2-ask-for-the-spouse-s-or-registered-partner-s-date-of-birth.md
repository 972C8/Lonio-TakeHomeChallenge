# US-04.2 – Ask for the spouse's or registered partner's date of birth when the fund registers the employee as Married

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** the date of birth of the spouse or registered partner of every employee I register as Married, and no spouse data for anyone else, **so that** the registration is not blocked by the missing 'Spouse's date of birth' and no personal data is collected that I do not need

Priority **P1** – The fund's portal blocks the registration of a Married employee when the spouse's date of birth is empty (F-pension_portal-c5ee15). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, F-pension_portal-a0065b, F-pension_portal-ef28c3, F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-c5ee15, F-pension_portal-6d3630, D-003, D-004, D-036

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Spouse's or registered partner's date of birth

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.2.1 Spouse's or partner's date of birth is asked only for married, registered-partner and separated employees**
  - **Given** an employee has answered the civil status question in step 4
  - **When** their civil status is Married, Registered partnership or Separated
  - **Then** Lonio asks for the date of birth of their spouse or registered partner in the same step, and an answer is required. For Single, Divorced and Widowed it is not asked and no spouse data is recorded for the fund. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert: registered partners and separated employees are entered at the fund as Married and therefore need this date; divorced and widowed employees do not (D-003, D-004; HR did not know what the fund wants for registered partners, F-hr_transcript-f197d6).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-c5ee15, F-pension_portal-6d3630, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, D-003, D-004_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Spouse's or registered partner's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel, registered partnership (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | required | ✅ |
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Married | → | required | ✅ |
  | Separated employee (illustrative) | Separated | → | required | ✅ |
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee (illustrative) | Divorced | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee (illustrative) | Widowed | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-04.2.2 The date is given as a calendar date**
  - **Given** the spouse's or partner's date of birth is asked
  - **When** the employee enters it
  - **Then** it is a date in the form DD.MM.YYYY, as the fund's field expects.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-ef28c3, G-10_
- **AC-04.2.3 An answer that no longer applies is not kept**
  - **Given** an employee entered a spouse's date of birth and then changed their civil status to Single, Divorced or Widowed
  - **When** they submit the questionnaire
  - **Then** the spouse's date of birth is discarded and not recorded; if they change back before submitting, the date they entered is still there.
  - _Sources: D-036, D-050, G-01_
- **AC-04.2.4 The date is recorded for the pension fund**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the spouse's or partner's date of birth and submitted
  - **When** the answers are recorded for HR
  - **Then** the date is recorded as the fund's 'Spouse's date of birth' for an employee entered at the fund as Married.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-a0065b, F-hr_transcript-2334d8, D-003, D-034_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-004** (product decision (Product owner)): The spouse's (or registered partner's) date of birth is asked from married employees, registered partners and separated employees (all entered at the fund as Married, see D-003), not from divorced or widowed employees; no spouse data is sent to the fund for them. This closes OQ-spouse-dob-other-status and OQ-separated-spouse-dob. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **D-050** (product decision (Product owner)): Build-time values. HR provides the permit reminder wording before go-live. Country of residence and nationality are chosen from the ISO 3166 country list; nationality also offers 'stateless'. The date of birth must make the employee at least 14 and at most 100 years old on the first working day; the first working day must lie within one year after the questionnaire is sent. Answers are saved as the employee goes and they can resume until they submit; submitting needs one confirmation on a summary page. Answers are kept when the employee goes back and changes an earlier answer (until submission), and this is testable. The spouse's residence is asked as 'Does your spouse live in Switzerland? yes/no', not as a country. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-10** (product owner guidance): Business wording for values (used in allowed values and examples): countries by English name ("Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein"), permits as "B permit", "C permit", civil status exactly as the pension fund's list shows it ("Single", "Married", ...), dates as DD.MM.YYYY, yes/no as "yes"/"no".
