# US-04.2 – Ask for the spouse's or registered partner's date of birth when the fund registers the employee as Married

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** the date of birth of the spouse or registered partner of every employee who is married, in a registered partnership or separated, **so that** the registration is not blocked, because the fund requires the spouse's date of birth whenever the civil status entered is Married

Priority **P1** – The fund blocks registration of a married employee without the spouse's date of birth ('Spouse's date of birth is required.'), and HR names this as missing data today. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, F-pension_portal-a0065b, F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-c5ee15, F-pension_portal-ef28c3, F-pension_portal-6d3630, G-01, D-003, D-004, D-036, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.2.1 Asked and required for married, registered-partnership and separated employees**
  - **Given** the employee has answered that their civil status is Married, Registered partnership or Separated
  - **When** they continue the questionnaire
  - **Then** they are asked for their spouse's or registered partner's date of birth and cannot submit without it. Married is confirmed by HR and the fund. Registered partnership and Separated are both entered at the fund as Married, so the fund requires the date there too. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert: that the fund accepts a registered partnership and a separation entered as Married with this date of birth.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-c5ee15, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, D-003, D-004_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel, registered partnership (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | required | ✅ |
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Married | → | required | ✅ |
  | Separated employee (illustrative) | Separated | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-04.2.2 Not asked for single, divorced or widowed employees**
  - **Given** the employee has answered that their civil status is Single, Divorced or Widowed
  - **When** they continue the questionnaire
  - **Then** they are not asked for a spouse's or partner's date of birth, and no spouse data goes to the pension fund for them. The fund shows the field only for Married. No screenshot shows the form for Divorced or Widowed, so not asking here is an assumption to be verified by a domain expert.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-6d3630, G-01, D-004_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee (illustrative) | Divorced | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee (illustrative) | Widowed | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-04.2.3 Form and recipient of the spouse's date of birth**
  - **Given** the employee is asked for their spouse's or registered partner's date of birth
  - **When** they enter it
  - **Then** the date is entered as DD.MM.YYYY (for example 14.03.1988), as the fund requires, and is recorded only for the pension fund registration, as the fund's 'Spouse's date of birth'. No other receiving party gets it.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-a0065b, F-pension_portal-ef28c3, G-01, G-10_
- **AC-04.2.4 A date of birth that no longer applies is not kept**
  - **Given** the employee entered a spouse's date of birth and then changed their civil status to Single, Divorced or Widowed before submitting
  - **When** they submit the questionnaire
  - **Then** the spouse's date of birth they entered earlier is discarded and goes to no party. If they switch back before submitting, the earlier entry may still be shown, so no input is lost.
  - _Sources: D-036, G-01_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-004** (product decision (Product owner)): The spouse's (or registered partner's) date of birth is asked from married employees, registered partners and separated employees (all entered at the fund as Married, see D-003), not from divorced or widowed employees; no spouse data is sent to the fund for them. This closes OQ-spouse-dob-other-status and OQ-separated-spouse-dob. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **D-045** (product decision (Product owner)): Business text (stories, acceptance criteria, notices) uses no internal snake_case ids and does not speak of 'expressions'. Any behaviour that has no cited fact or decision is stated in the story as an assumption. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
