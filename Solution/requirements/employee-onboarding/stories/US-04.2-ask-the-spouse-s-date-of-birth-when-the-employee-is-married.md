# US-04.2 – Ask the spouse's date of birth when the employee is married

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** the date of birth of the employee's spouse or registered partner whenever the employee is registered with me as Married, **so that** the registration is not blocked by an empty 'Spouse's date of birth' field

Priority **P1** – The fund blocks the registration with 'Spouse's date of birth is required.' when the civil status is Married and the field is empty (F-pension_portal-c5ee15). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-pension_portal-a0065b, F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-c5ee15, F-pension_portal-ef28c3, F-pension_portal-6d3630, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, D-003, D-004

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-civil-status-partnership](../open-questions.md#oq-civil-status-partnership), [OQ-spouse-dob-other-status](../open-questions.md#oq-spouse-dob-other-status)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.2.1 Asked of married, registered-partnership and separated employees**
  - **Given** the employee has answered the civil status question (all civil statuses are asked of everyone)
  - **When** the civil status is Married, Registered partnership or Separated
  - **Then** the employee is asked for the date of birth of their spouse or registered partner (for separated employees: the spouse they are separated from), and it is required. It is recorded for the pension fund, because all three are entered at the fund as Married, which requires this field.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-c5ee15, D-003, D-004_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | required | ✅ |
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Married | → | required | ✅ |
  | Separated employee (illustrative) | Separated | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-04.2.2 Not asked of single, divorced or widowed employees**
  - **Given** the employee has answered the civil status question
  - **When** the civil status is Single, Divorced or Widowed
  - **Then** the spouse's date of birth is not asked. For Divorced and Widowed this rests on the assumption that the fund's screen shows no spouse field for these values (see OQ-spouse-dob-other-status).
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-6d3630, D-004_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee (illustrative) | Divorced | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee (illustrative) | Widowed | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-04.2.3 Date form**
  - **Given** the spouse's date of birth is asked
  - **When** the employee enters it
  - **Then** it is a date in the form DD.MM.YYYY, as the fund requires.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-ef28c3, F-pension_portal-a0065b_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-004** (product decision (Product owner)): The spouse's (or registered partner's) date of birth is asked from married employees, registered partners and separated employees (all entered at the fund as Married, see D-003), not from divorced or widowed employees. Keep OQ-spouse-dob-other-status open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert (screenshot of the fund's screen). This closes OQ-separated-spouse-dob; the separated case is verified together with OQ-civil-status-partnership.
