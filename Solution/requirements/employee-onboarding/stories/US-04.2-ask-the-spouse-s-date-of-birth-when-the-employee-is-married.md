# US-04.2 – Ask the spouse's date of birth when the employee is married

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data for the pension fund](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** the date of birth of the husband or wife of every married employee, and no spouse date of birth for employees who are not married, **so that** the registration of a married employee is not blocked by an empty 'Spouse's date of birth' field, and no spouse data is collected that I do not need

Priority **P1** – When civil status is Married, the pension fund blocks the registration if the spouse's date of birth is empty ('Spouse's date of birth is required.'). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-pension_portal-a0065b, F-pension_portal-ef28c3, F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-c5ee15, G-01, G-10

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Spouse's date of birth

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.2.1 Spouse's date of birth is asked and required only for married employees**
  - **Given** the employee has answered their civil status
  - **When** the civil status is Married
  - **Then** the employee is asked for their spouse's date of birth in the Family step and cannot finish the questionnaire without it. The answer goes to the pension fund.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-pension_portal-a0065b, F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-c5ee15_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Spouse's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Married | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-04.2.2 Spouse's date of birth is not asked of employees who are not married**
  - **Given** the employee has answered their civil status
  - **When** the civil status is Single, Divorced or Widowed
  - **Then** the spouse's date of birth is not asked. For Single this matches the pension fund form. For Divorced and Widowed no screenshot shows the form, so this rests on the assumption that no spouse field appears (see OQ-divorced-widowed).
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-39bab1, G-01_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Spouse's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee (illustrative) | Divorced | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee (illustrative) | Widowed | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-04.2.3 Spouse's date of birth is given as a full date**
  - **Given** the employee is asked for their spouse's date of birth
  - **When** they answer
  - **Then** the answer is a full calendar date given as DD.MM.YYYY (e.g. 14.03.1988), as the pension fund requires.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-ef28c3, G-10_

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-10** (product owner guidance): Business wording for values (used in allowed values and examples): countries by English name ("Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein"), permits as "B permit", "C permit", civil status exactly as the pension fund's list shows it ("Single", "Married", ...), dates as DD.MM.YYYY, yes/no as "yes"/"no".
