# US-01.3 – Home address country is the country of residence for later questions

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the country of the home address to be used as the employee's country of residence, without asking it again, **so that** living abroad, and which cross-border questions apply, follow from one answer, and the employee is not asked the same thing twice

Priority **P1** – Living abroad makes the employee subject to source tax regardless of nationality; a wrong or missing residence leads to a wrong source tax decision. · Sources: F-hr_transcript-e505f8, F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f29009, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, G-01, G-08, D-002

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-canton-abroad](../open-questions.md#oq-canton-abroad)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Country (home address) = country of residence

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.3.1 Residence is not asked a second time**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the country of their home address
  - **When** later steps need to know where the employee lives (main residence in Switzerland, state of residence)
  - **Then** the home address country is used as the country of residence and the employee is not asked for their residence again
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-e505f8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f29009, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, G-01, G-08_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | → | Residence for the source tax tariff | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Lives in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | Switzerland | ✅ |
  | Lives in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | Germany | ✅ |
  | Lives in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | Liechtenstein | ✅ |
  | Lives in France (illustrative) | France | → | Other country | ✅ |

- **AC-01.3.2 Living abroad makes the employee subject to source tax**
  - **Given** the home address country is not Switzerland
  - **When** the questionnaire decides which questions follow
  - **Then** the employee counts as living abroad and is subject to source tax whatever their nationality, so nationality and the canton are not asked; an employee living in Switzerland is asked for nationality and canton
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-hr_transcript-e505f8, G-01, D-002_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | → | Subject to source tax | Nationality | Canton | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Lives in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | yes | not asked | not asked | ✅ |
  | Lives in another country (Italy) (illustrative) | Italy | → | yes | not asked | – | ✅ |
  | Lives in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | – | asked | asked | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-002** (product decision (Product owner)): Ask for the canton (pension fund 'Canton' field) only from employees living in Switzerland. Provisional - keep OQ-canton-abroad open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert and Pensionskasse Muster (what to enter for employees living abroad, and whether 'Canton' means residence or work).
- **F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3** (hr_transcript, inferred): Consolidated final rule: an employee is subject to source tax if they live abroad (home address country not Switzerland), regardless of nationality. If they live in Switzerland, they are subject only if they are not Swiss and do not hold a C permit, unless they are married to a spouse who is Swiss or holds a C permit and lives in Switzerland (then not subject).
  - "If you don't have a Swiss passport, you do source tax." — hr_transcript/T-010, Anna
  - "So, not the people with a C permit." — hr_transcript/T-038, Anna
  - "If you're married to someone Swiss, or to someone with a C permit, and that person lives here, then you don't do source tax either." — hr_transcript/T-044, Anna
  - "Those people do source tax because they work here and they don't live here." — hr_transcript/T-048, Anna
- **F-hr_transcript-e505f8** (hr_transcript, inferred): Whether the employee lives abroad can be derived from the country of the home address, which is relevant for source tax liability.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
