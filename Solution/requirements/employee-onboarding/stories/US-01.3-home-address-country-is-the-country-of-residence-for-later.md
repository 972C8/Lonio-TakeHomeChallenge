# US-01.3 – Home address country is the country of residence for later questions

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the country of the employee's home address to be used as their country of residence, **so that** Lonio knows whether the employee lives abroad, and so is always subject to source tax, without asking for the residence a second time

Priority **P1** – Today HR decides source tax liability by hand, and living abroad is the first criterion. Lonio also derives the calculator's residence answers from this country, so getting it wrong blocks the correct tariff code. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-e505f8, F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f29009, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, G-01, G-08, D-001, D-002, D-007, D-038

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.3.1 Residence is asked once, as part of the address**
  - **Given** a new employee has given the country of their home address
  - **When** later parts of the questionnaire need their country of residence
  - **Then** that country counts as their country of residence. The employee is not asked separately whether their main residence is in Switzerland or which state they live in. The country is recorded for HR and for source tax, and it decides whether the canton for the pension fund is asked.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-e505f8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f29009, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, G-08, D-001, D-002_
- **AC-01.3.2 Living abroad makes the employee subject to source tax, and residence decides the follow-up questions**
  - **Given** a new employee has given the country of their home address
  - **When** the country is not Switzerland
  - **Then** the employee is treated as living abroad and is subject to source tax whatever their nationality, so they are not asked about Swiss citizenship or the canton. If the country is Switzerland, they are asked for the canton and about Swiss citizenship.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, D-002, D-007, D-038_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | → | Subject to source tax | Swiss citizenship | Canton | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Konstanz, Germany (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-1a20bb) | Germany | → | yes | not asked | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-1a20bb) | Liechtenstein | → | yes | not asked | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in another country, e.g. France (illustrative) (illustrative) | France | → | yes | not asked | – | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | – | asked | asked | ✅ |

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-002** (product decision (Product owner)): Ask for the canton (pension fund 'Canton' field) only from employees living in Switzerland. Provisional - keep OQ-canton-abroad open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert and Pensionskasse Muster (what to enter for employees living abroad, and whether 'Canton' means residence or work).
- **D-007** (product decision (Product owner)): Liechtenstein residents are asked the source tax questions (HR's rule decides who is asked). The result follows the calculator - with daily return or at most 45 non-return days, Lonio shows 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR; with more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code. This closes OQ-liechtenstein-conflict. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-038** (product decision (Product owner)): Employees living abroad get the permit reminder whatever their nationality, because nationality is not asked of them (data minimisation, G-01). HR words the reminder so that a Swiss citizen living abroad, such as Stefan, can see that it does not apply to them. Asking everyone 'Swiss citizen yes/no' only for the reminder is not worth the extra question.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
