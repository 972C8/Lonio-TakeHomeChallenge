# US-01.3 – Home address country is the country of residence for later questions

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the country of the employee's home address to count as the employee's country of residence for all later questions, **so that** whether the employee lives abroad (and so is subject to source tax), and in which state of residence, is known without asking again

Priority **P1** – Living abroad decides source tax liability and the cross-border part of the tariff code. Asking it twice would contradict the ask-once principle. · Sources: F-hr_transcript-e505f8, F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f29009, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-52b67a, G-01, D-002, D-038

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-canton-abroad](../open-questions.md#oq-canton-abroad)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Country (home address)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.3.1 Residence is never asked separately**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the country of their home address
  - **When** later steps need to know where the employee lives (main residence in Switzerland or not, state of residence)
  - **Then** Lonio takes the home address country as the country of residence. The employee is not asked about residence again.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-e505f8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f29009, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, G-01_
- **AC-01.3.2 State of residence for source tax follows the address country**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the country of their home address
  - **When** the state of residence for source tax is derived
  - **Then** Switzerland gives 'Switzerland', Germany gives 'Germany', Liechtenstein gives 'Liechtenstein', and any other country gives 'Other country'. The cross-border questions for Germany and Liechtenstein are specified in the cross-border section.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-52b67a, F-hr_transcript-e505f8_

  | Example | Country (home address) | → | State of residence for source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás, living in Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | → | Switzerland | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (e.g. Konstanz) (illustrative) | Germany | → | Germany | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | Liechtenstein | ✅ |
  | Employee living in another country (illustrative) | France | → | Other country | ✅ |

- **AC-01.3.3 Living abroad makes the employee subject to source tax without a nationality question**
  - **Given** the home address country is not Switzerland
  - **When** the questionnaire decides which questions follow
  - **Then** the employee counts as subject to source tax whatever their nationality, and is not asked for nationality, C permit or canton. An employee living in Switzerland is asked for their nationality and canton.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, D-002, D-038, G-01_

  | Example | Country (home address) | → | Subject to source tax | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Canton | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Germany, e.g. a Swiss citizen like Stefan (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-1a20bb) (illustrative) | Germany | → | yes | not asked | not asked | not asked | ✅ |
  | Tomás, living in Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | → | – | asked | – | asked | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-002** (product decision (Product owner)): Ask for the canton (pension fund 'Canton' field) only from employees living in Switzerland. Provisional - keep OQ-canton-abroad open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert and Pensionskasse Muster (what to enter for employees living abroad, and whether 'Canton' means residence or work).
- **D-038** (product decision (Product owner)): Employees living abroad get the permit reminder whatever their nationality, because nationality is not asked of them (data minimisation, G-01). HR words the reminder so that a Swiss citizen living abroad, such as Stefan, can see that it does not apply to them. Asking everyone 'Swiss citizen yes/no' only for the reminder is not worth the extra question.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
