# US-01.4 – Canton of residence for employees living in Switzerland

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address).

**As** pension fund, **I want** the canton of employees who live in Switzerland, **so that** the required 'Canton' field of the registration can be filled

Priority **P1** – 'Canton' is a required field on the pension fund's registration form. The form cannot be submitted while it is empty, so a missing canton blocks the registration. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-pension_portal-587d3f, F-pension_portal-3432a9, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-pension_portal-b359c3, G-01, D-002

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-canton-abroad](../open-questions.md#oq-canton-abroad)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Canton

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.4.1 Canton is asked only of residents of Switzerland and is then required**
  - **Given** a new employee has given the country of their home address
  - **When** they continue the home address step
  - **Then** if the country is Switzerland, they are asked which canton they live in, and an answer is required. If the country is any other country, the canton is not asked. What the fund expects for employees living abroad, and whether 'Canton' means residence or work, is still open (see OQ-canton-abroad).
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-587d3f, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-pension_portal-b359c3, D-002_

  | Example | Country of home address (country of residence) | → | Canton | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Winterthur, Switzerland (illustrative) (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Konstanz, Germany (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-1a20bb) | Germany | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-01.4.2 Canton is chosen from the fund's list of the 26 Swiss cantons**
  - **Given** a new employee living in Switzerland is asked for their canton
  - **When** they answer
  - **Then** they choose one of the 26 Swiss cantons that the pension fund lists: Aargau, Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Appenzell Innerrhoden, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt, Bern, Fribourg, Geneva, Glarus, Graubünden, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Nidwalden, Obwalden, Schaffhausen, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Ticino, Uri, Valais, Vaud, Zug or Zurich. The canton is recorded for the pension fund registration.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3432a9, F-pension_portal-587d3f, D-002_
