# US-01.4 – Canton of residence for employees living in Switzerland

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address).

**As** pension fund, **I want** employees living in Switzerland to choose their canton from the 26 Swiss cantons, **so that** the required 'Canton' field of the registration can be filled, and employees abroad are not asked a question they have no valid answer to

Priority **P1** – 'Canton' is a required field of the pension fund registration. A missing value blocks the registration. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-pension_portal-587d3f, F-pension_portal-3432a9, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-pension_portal-b359c3, G-01, D-002, D-036

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-canton-abroad](../open-questions.md#oq-canton-abroad)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Canton

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.4.1 Canton is asked and required only from residents of Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the country of their home address
  - **When** the 'Home address' step continues
  - **Then** if the country is Switzerland, the employee is asked for their canton and must answer before submitting. If the country is any other country, the canton is not asked. What the pension fund expects for employees living abroad, and whether 'Canton' means residence or work, is still open (see OQ-canton-abroad, non-blocking).
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-587d3f, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-pension_portal-b359c3, D-002_

  | Example | Country (home address) | → | Canton | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás, living in Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (e.g. Konstanz) (illustrative) | Germany | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-01.4.2 Canton choices are the fund's 26 cantons**
  - **Given** the employee is asked for their canton
  - **When** they choose it
  - **Then** they can choose exactly one of: Aargau, Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Appenzell Innerrhoden, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt, Bern, Fribourg, Geneva, Glarus, Graubünden, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Nidwalden, Obwalden, Schaffhausen, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Ticino, Uri, Valais, Vaud, Zug, Zurich. No other value is accepted. The chosen canton is recorded for the pension fund registration.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3432a9, F-pension_portal-587d3f_
- **AC-01.4.3 A canton no longer needed is not kept**
  - **Given** the employee chose a canton while their address country was Switzerland, and then changed the address country to another country
  - **When** they submit the questionnaire
  - **Then** the canton answer is discarded and not recorded. Until submission it may be kept, so switching the country back to Switzerland loses no input.
  - _Sources: D-036, G-01, D-002_

  | Example | Country (home address) | → | Canton | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee who first entered Switzerland, then corrected to Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | not asked | ✅ |
