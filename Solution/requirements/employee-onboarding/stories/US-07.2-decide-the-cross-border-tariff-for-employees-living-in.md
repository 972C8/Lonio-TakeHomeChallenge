# US-07.2 – Decide the cross-border tariff for employees living in Germany

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Employees living abroad (Germany, Liechtenstein)](../feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees living in Germany to state whether they expect to return home daily, whether they have a certificate of residence, whether a daily return would be reasonable and whether they expect more than 60 work-related non-return days in the year, **so that** Lonio applies the cross-border (Grenzgänger) tariff exactly when the calculator would, and the normal tariff in every other case

Priority **P1** – Lonio owns the tariff result (D-001); without these answers the tariff code of a German cross-border employee cannot be computed, and HR cannot remember the German-specific rules (F-hr_transcript-ab8886). · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-69edd5, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-687e8b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-823151, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5f6820, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d2b3cf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-087ae5, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aaece6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e8ffac, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ea3cd8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-33ef31, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4d3ac0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-89a9f1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-bc1639, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b948fd, F-hr_transcript-ab8886, G-01, D-001, D-006, D-035

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad) and [information.md](../information.md)): Daily return to home in Germany, Certificate of residence provided, Daily return reasonable (less than 100 km), More than 60 work-related non-return days (Germany), Cross-border (Grenzgänger) tariff applies

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.2.1 Daily return to Germany is asked from every employee living in Germany**
  - **Given** the employee's home address is in Germany (which always makes them subject to source tax)
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax questions
  - **Then** the employee must state, yes or no, whether they expect to return daily to their main residence in Germany; employees living anywhere else are not asked; the answer is recorded for source tax
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-69edd5, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, D-006, D-035_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | → | Daily return to home in Germany | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Lives in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | required | ✅ |
  | Lives in Austria (illustrative) | Austria | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.2 Certificate of residence is asked only from daily returners**
  - **Given** an employee living in Germany expects to return home daily
  - **When** the employee answers the cross-border questions
  - **Then** the employee must state whether they have provided a certificate of residence: yes, no or not yet; the question about a reasonable daily return and the 60 days question are not asked. The certificate itself goes to HR outside Lonio; nothing is uploaded
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-687e8b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-823151, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5f6820, D-035_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Daily return to home in Germany | → | Certificate of residence provided | Daily return reasonable (less than 100 km) | More than 60 work-related non-return days (Germany) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Returns daily (illustrative) | Germany | yes | → | required | not asked | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.3 Reasonable return and non-return days are asked only from employees not returning daily**
  - **Given** an employee living in Germany does not expect to return home daily
  - **When** the employee answers the cross-border questions
  - **Then** the employee must state, yes or no, whether a daily return would in principle be reasonable (less than 100 km driving distance); only if yes, they must state, yes or no, whether they expect more than 60 work-related non-return days in the calendar year. The certificate question is not asked. Any proof goes to HR outside Lonio
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d2b3cf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-087ae5, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aaece6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e8ffac, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ea3cd8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-33ef31, D-035_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Daily return to home in Germany | Daily return reasonable (less than 100 km) | → | Certificate of residence provided | Daily return reasonable (less than 100 km) | More than 60 work-related non-return days (Germany) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | No daily return, return reasonable (illustrative) | Germany | no | yes | → | not asked | required | required | ✅ |
  | No daily return, return not reasonable (illustrative) | Germany | no | no | → | not asked | – | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.4 Cross-border tariff decision follows the calculator**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has answered the questions that apply to them
  - **When** Lonio computes the tariff code
  - **Then** the cross-border tariff applies (tariff letter A becomes L, B becomes M, C becomes N, H becomes P) if the employee lives in Germany and either returns daily and has provided a certificate of residence, or does not return daily, a daily return is reasonable and they do not expect more than 60 non-return days; 'not yet' for the certificate counts as 'no'; every other case, including every employee not living in Germany, gets the normal tariff. Unexplored calculator paths are checked before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4d3ac0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-89a9f1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-bc1639, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b948fd, D-001, D-035_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Daily return to home in Germany | Certificate of residence provided | Daily return reasonable (less than 100 km) | More than 60 work-related non-return days (Germany) | Daily return to home in Liechtenstein | More than 45 work-related non-return days (Liechtenstein) | → | Cross-border (Grenzgänger) tariff applies | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Certificate provided -> N1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4d3ac0) | Germany | yes | yes | – | – | – | – | → | yes | ✅ |
  | No certificate -> C1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4d3ac0) | Germany | yes | no | – | – | – | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Certificate not yet provided (illustrative) | Germany | yes | not yet | – | – | – | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Return not reasonable -> C1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-bc1639) | Germany | no | – | no | – | – | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | More than 60 non-return days -> C1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b948fd) | Germany | no | – | yes | yes | – | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | At most 60 non-return days -> N1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b948fd) | Germany | no | – | yes | no | – | – | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Lives in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | – | – | – | – | no | yes | → | no | ✅ |
