# US-07.2 – Decide the cross-border tariff for employees living in Germany

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Employees living abroad (Germany, Liechtenstein)](../feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees living in Germany to state what they expect for the year: whether they return home daily, whether they have provided a certificate of residence, whether a daily return would be reasonable, and whether they expect more than 60 work-related non-return days, **so that** Lonio can decide, following the calculator, whether the cross-border tariff (A→L, B→M, C→N, H→P) or the normal tariff applies

Priority **P1** – Without these answers Lonio cannot compute the tariff code for German residents (D-001). ## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.2.1 Daily return to Germany is asked of every employee living in Germany**
  - **Given** the employee's home address is in Germany. Living abroad makes them subject to source tax whatever their nationality.
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax details
  - **Then** they must answer 'yes' or 'no' to whether they expect to return daily to their main residence in Germany. The answer is recorded for source tax. Employees not living in Germany are not asked.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-69edd5, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, D-006, D-035_

  | Example | Country of home address (country of residence) | → | Daily return to home in Germany | Daily return to home in Liechtenstein | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | required | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | – | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.2 Certificate of residence is asked only with daily return**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Germany and expects to return home daily
  - **When** the employee answers the cross-border questions
  - **Then** they must state whether they have provided a German certificate of residence: 'yes', 'no' or 'not yet'. 'Not yet' is a valid answer. No document is uploaded; the certificate goes to HR outside Lonio. Employees who do not return daily are not asked.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-687e8b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-823151, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5f6820, D-035_

  | Example | Country of home address (country of residence) | Daily return to home in Germany | → | Certificate of residence | Daily return reasonable (under 100 km) | More than 60 work-related non-return days expected | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daily return (illustrative) | Germany | yes | → | required | not asked | not asked | ✅ |
  | No daily return (illustrative) | Germany | no | → | not asked | – | – | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.3 Reasonable return and non-return days are asked only without daily return**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Germany and does not expect to return home daily
  - **When** the employee answers the cross-border questions
  - **Then** they must answer 'yes' or 'no' to whether a daily return would in principle be reasonable (less than 100 km driving distance). Only if it is reasonable, they must also answer 'yes' or 'no' to whether they expect more than 60 work-related non-return days in the calendar year. This is a declaration of what they expect, not proof.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d2b3cf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-087ae5, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aaece6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e8ffac, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ea3cd8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-33ef31, D-035_

  | Example | Country of home address (country of residence) | Daily return to home in Germany | Daily return reasonable (under 100 km) | → | Daily return reasonable (under 100 km) | More than 60 work-related non-return days expected | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Return reasonable (illustrative) | Germany | no | yes | → | required | required | ✅ |
  | Return not reasonable (illustrative) | Germany | no | no | → | required | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.4 Cross-border tariff or normal tariff**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Germany and has answered the cross-border questions
  - **When** Lonio computes the tariff code
  - **Then** The cross-border tariff applies in two cases: daily return with a certificate of residence, or no daily return where a return is reasonable and no more than 60 non-return days are expected. In every other case the normal tariff applies, including a certificate answered 'no' or 'not yet'. For employees not living in Germany the cross-border tariff never applies. With the cross-border tariff the letter changes A→L, B→M, C→N, H→P. The result is always derived again from the latest answers. Paths not explored in the calculator are checked before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-89a9f1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4d3ac0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b948fd, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-bc1639, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-52b67a, D-001, D-035, D-037, D-009_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-006** (product decision (Product owner)): A new employee always has earned income. Do not ask the income type, and drop the replacement-income questions and the tariff G / Q cases. This closes OQ-income-type. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are out of scope for onboarding. Keep OQ-calculator-coverage open as a non-blocking go-live condition.
- **D-035** (product decision (Product owner)): For the cross-border questions the employee states what they expect for the year (non-return days). For the certificate of residence 'not yet' is a valid answer and counts as 'no' for the tariff code. Documents go to HR outside Lonio; no upload.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
