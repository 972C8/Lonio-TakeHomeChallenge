# US-03.2 – Ask the spouse's or partner's date of birth from married, partnered and separated employees

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [3. Civil status and spouse for the pension fund](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** to receive the date of birth of the husband, wife or registered partner of every employee who is registered with me as Married, **so that** the registration is not rejected with 'Spouse's date of birth is required'

Priority **P1** – The fund rejects a Married registration when the spouse's date of birth is missing, so the registration is blocked without it. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-pension_portal-a0065b, F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-0eed50, F-pension_portal-ef28c3, G-01, D-003, D-004, D-036

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-civil-status-partnership](../open-questions.md#oq-civil-status-partnership), [OQ-spouse-dob-other-status](../open-questions.md#oq-spouse-dob-other-status)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-03.2.1 Asked and required only for Married, Registered partnership and Separated**
  - **Given** the employee has stated their civil status
  - **When** the civil status is Married, Registered partnership or Separated
  - **Then** Lonio asks for the date of birth of the spouse or registered partner. For separated employees this is the spouse they are separated from. The answer is required, and it is recorded for the pension fund. Single, divorced and widowed employees are not asked. Whether the fund needs anything for Divorced or Widowed is still open (see OQ-spouse-dob-other-status), and so is the provisional handling of partnered and separated employees (see OQ-civil-status-partnership).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-0eed50, D-003, D-004_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Married | → | required | ✅ |
  | Daniel, registered partnership (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | required | ✅ |
  | Separated employee (illustrative) | Separated | → | required | ✅ |
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee (illustrative) | Divorced | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee (illustrative) | Widowed | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-03.2.2 Form of the date**
  - **Given** the employee is asked for the spouse's or partner's date of birth
  - **When** they enter it
  - **Then** the date is given as day, month and year (DD.MM.YYYY), as the pension fund expects it.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-ef28c3, G-10_
- **AC-03.2.3 Spouse's date of birth is discarded when it no longer applies**
  - **Given** the employee entered a spouse's date of birth and then changed their civil status to Single, Divorced or Widowed
  - **When** they submit the questionnaire
  - **Then** the spouse's date of birth is not kept. While the employee is still moving back and forth in the questionnaire, the entered date is kept, so it is not lost if they switch back.
  - _Sources: G-01, D-036_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Changed from Married to Divorced (illustrative) | Divorced | → | not asked | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-004** (product decision (Product owner)): The spouse's (or registered partner's) date of birth is asked from married employees, registered partners and separated employees (all entered at the fund as Married, see D-003), not from divorced or widowed employees. Keep OQ-spouse-dob-other-status open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert (screenshot of the fund's screen). This closes OQ-separated-spouse-dob; the separated case is verified together with OQ-civil-status-partnership.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-10** (product owner guidance): Business wording for values (used in allowed values and examples): countries by English name ("Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein"), permits as "B permit", "C permit", civil status exactly as the pension fund's list shows it ("Single", "Married", ...), dates as DD.MM.YYYY, yes/no as "yes"/"no".
