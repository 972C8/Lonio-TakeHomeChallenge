# US-01.4 – Remind non-Swiss employees and employees living abroad to bring their permit

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Questionnaire flow overview](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow).

**As** HR, **I want** the questionnaire to remind every non-Swiss employee and every employee living abroad, at the end, to bring their permit on the first working day, **so that** fewer new employees forget their permit on day one

Priority **P2** – An explicit HR wish ('Half of them forget'); it blocks no registration and collects no data. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-c53552, D-010, D-038, D-050, G-01

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow) and [information.md](../information.md)): Permit reminder

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.4.1 Who sees the permit reminder**
  - **Given** an employee has answered where they live and, if they live in Switzerland, their nationality
  - **When** they reach step 8 'Permit reminder, summary and submission'
  - **Then** the reminder to bring their permit on the first working day is shown to every non-Swiss employee living in Switzerland (including C-permit holders) and to every employee living abroad, whatever their nationality, because nationality is not asked of them; Swiss citizens living in Switzerland do not see it. The reminder collects no answer.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-c53552, D-010, D-038, G-01_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Nationality | → | Permit reminder | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Maria, Portuguese with a C permit (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | Portugal | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Tomás, Brazilian with a B permit (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen living abroad like Stefan (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a); country of residence assumed to be Germany (illustrative) | Germany | – | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | – | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Stateless employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | stateless | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | no | ✅ |

- **AC-01.4.2 HR provides the reminder wording**
  - **Given** the permit reminder is to be shown
  - **When** the questionnaire goes live
  - **Then** the reminder uses wording provided by HR before go-live, phrased so that a Swiss citizen living abroad can see it does not apply to them; the spec fixes only its purpose. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert: HR delivers the wording before go-live.
  - _Sources: D-010, D-038, D-050, F-hr_transcript-c53552_

## Evidence
- **D-010** (product decision (Product owner)): At the end of the questionnaire, remind every non-Swiss employee and every employee living abroad to bring their permit on the first working day. HR words the reminder; the spec states only its purpose. This closes OQ-permit-reminder.
- **D-038** (product decision (Product owner)): Employees living abroad get the permit reminder whatever their nationality, because nationality is not asked of them (data minimisation, G-01). HR words the reminder so that a Swiss citizen living abroad, such as Stefan, can see that it does not apply to them. Asking everyone 'Swiss citizen yes/no' only for the reminder is not worth the extra question.
- **D-050** (product decision (Product owner)): Build-time values. HR provides the permit reminder wording before go-live. Country of residence and nationality are chosen from the ISO 3166 country list; nationality also offers 'stateless'. The date of birth must make the employee at least 14 and at most 100 years old on the first working day; the first working day must lie within one year after the questionnaire is sent. Answers are saved as the employee goes and they can resume until they submit; submitting needs one confirmation on a summary page. Answers are kept when the employee goes back and changes an earlier answer (until submission), and this is testable. The spouse's residence is asked as 'Does your spouse live in Switzerland? yes/no', not as a country. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **F-hr_transcript-c53552** (hr_transcript): HR would love the form to remind employees to bring their permit on the first day, because half of them forget. Leo deferred the wording, so this is not decided.
  - "What I would love is if the form could remind people to bring their permit. Half of them forget." — hr_transcript/T-042, Anna
  - "We can look at the wording later." — hr_transcript/T-043, Leo
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
