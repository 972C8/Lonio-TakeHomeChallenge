# US-06.3 – Ask married employees and registered partners subject to source tax whether their spouse or partner works

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** to know, for married employees and registered partners who need a tariff code, whether the spouse or partner is gainfully employed or receives replacement income, **so that** the tariff letter is C (spouse employed) or B (spouse not employed), as in the calculator

Priority **P1** – This answer decides the tariff letter for every married employee subject to source tax. Without it the tariff code cannot be computed and HR's manual calculator step comes back (D-001, F-hr_transcript-6a0a40). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7b637b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e740d1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-6a0a40, D-001, D-003, D-040, D-047

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.3.1 Spouse employment is asked only of married employees and registered partners who need a tariff code**
  - **Given** an employee has answered civil status (step 4) and, if they live in Liechtenstein, the cross-border questions (step 5)
  - **When** the employee reaches step 6 'Source tax details'
  - **Then** 'Is your spouse or registered partner gainfully employed or receiving replacement income?' is asked, and required, only if the employee is subject to source tax, is Married or in a Registered partnership, and (for Liechtenstein residents) has more than 45 non-return days. It is not asked of Single, Divorced, Widowed or Separated employees, of employees not subject to source tax, or of Liechtenstein residents who in principle have no source tax liability. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-003): for source tax a registered partnership counts as married and Separated counts as single, as in the calculator.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-b84498, D-003, D-040, D-047_

- **AC-06.3.2 Spouse employment is answered yes or no**
  - **Given** the spouse employment question is asked
  - **When** the employee answers it
  - **Then** the answer is 'yes' or 'no' and cannot be left empty. 'yes' covers a spouse or partner who is gainfully employed or receives replacement income, as in the calculator's wording. The answer is recorded for HR only as the input to the source tax tariff letter (C for yes, B for no).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7b637b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e740d1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a_
_Trace check: partial – The non-illustrative Tomás example (spouse exemption) cites F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, which is not among the cited facts. Everything else is backed._


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-040** (product decision (Product owner)): For employees living in Liechtenstein, the questions on daily return and on more than 45 days abroad come before denomination, spouse and children. When the answers mean 'in principle no source tax liability', the tariff questions are skipped. When the employee stays abroad more than 45 days, the normal tariff is recorded with the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement' and flagged for HR. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
