# US-06.3 – Ask married employees and registered partners subject to source tax whether their spouse or partner works

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** new employee, **I want** to be asked whether my spouse or registered partner works only if I am subject to source tax and married or in a registered partnership, **so that** Lonio can choose between tariff C and B for me without asking anyone else about their partner

Priority **P1** – HR names this as the question that changes the calculator's result for married employees (F-hr_transcript-6a0a40). Without it the tariff code cannot be computed (D-001). · Sources: F-hr_transcript-6a0a40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7b637b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e740d1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, D-001, D-003

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics) and [information.md](../information.md)): Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.3.1 Spouse employment is asked only of married employees and registered partners subject to source tax**
  - **Given** an employee whose civil status and source tax liability (rule subject_to_source_tax) are known
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax questions
  - **Then** the question 'Does your spouse or registered partner work or receive replacement income?' is asked and must be answered only if the employee is subject to source tax and their civil status is Married or Registered partnership. It is not asked of Single, Divorced, Widowed or Separated employees, because for source tax separated counts as single (D-003). It is not asked of anyone who is not subject to source tax.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-6a0a40, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, D-003_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | Spouse's / registered partner's nationality | Spouse / registered partner holds a C permit | Spouse's / registered partner's country of residence | → | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, subject to source tax (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Married | Italy | no | – | → | required | – | ✅ |
  | Registered partner, like Daniel (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Registered partnership | – | – | – | → | required | – | ✅ |
  | Separated, subject to source tax (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Separated | – | – | – | → | not asked | – | ✅ |
  | Divorced, living abroad (illustrative) | Austria | – | – | Divorced | – | – | – | → | not asked | – | ✅ |
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | – | Switzerland | → | not asked | no | ✅ |

- **AC-06.3.2 Spouse employment is a yes/no answer recorded for source tax**
  - **Given** the spouse employment question is asked
  - **When** the employee answers it
  - **Then** the answer is 'yes' (the spouse or partner is gainfully employed or receives replacement income) or 'no'. It is recorded for HR as the input that decides between tariff letter C and B.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7b637b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e740d1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, D-001, D-034_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **F-hr_transcript-6a0a40** (hr_transcript): For married employees, the source tax calculator asks whether the spouse works.

  - "Options: Ja | Nein" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q04
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
