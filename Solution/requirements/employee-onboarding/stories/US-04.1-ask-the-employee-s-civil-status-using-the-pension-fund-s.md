# US-04.1 – Ask the employee's civil status using the pension fund's list

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** every new employee to state their civil status once, and Lonio to record it in the form my registration accepts (Single, Married, Divorced or Widowed), **so that** the required 'Civil status' field of the registration is always filled and the registration is not blocked

Priority **P1** – Civil status is a required field of the fund registration (F-pension_portal-8664f4); without it the registration is blocked. Registered partnerships occur today (Daniel, F-hr_transcript-3907fe) and do not fit the fund's list. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-hr_transcript-bd8c8e, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, F-pension_portal-8664f4, F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, G-10, D-003, D-037

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.1.1 Civil status is asked of every employee and is required**
  - **Given** a new employee filling in the onboarding questionnaire
  - **When** they reach the civil status question
  - **Then** the civil status is asked of every employee, whatever their residence or nationality, and the questionnaire cannot be submitted without an answer. The answer is recorded for the pension fund (required field 'Civil status') and, for employees subject to source tax, used for the source tax tariff.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-pension_portal-8664f4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Civil status | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-04.1.2 Allowed civil status values**
  - **Given** the civil status question
  - **When** the employee answers it
  - **Then** the employee chooses exactly one of: Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership, Separated. 'Registered partnership' and 'Separated' are offered in addition to the fund's four values because these cases occur and source tax treats them differently.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-hr_transcript-bd8c8e, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, G-10, D-003_
- **AC-04.1.3 Civil status recorded for the pension fund**
  - **Given** the employee has answered the civil status
  - **When** Lonio records the civil status for the pension fund
  - **Then** Single, Married, Divorced and Widowed are recorded unchanged. Registered partnership and Separated are recorded provisionally as Married, because the fund's list has no such values (see OQ-civil-status-partnership, to be verified with Pensionskasse Muster).
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, D-003_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status entered at the pension fund | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | Married | ✅ |
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | Single | ✅ |
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Married | → | Married | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee (illustrative) | Divorced | → | Divorced | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee (illustrative) | Widowed | → | Widowed | ✅ |
  | Separated employee (illustrative) | Separated | → | Married | ✅ |

- **AC-04.1.4 Fund civil status always follows the latest civil status**
  - **Given** a submitted questionnaire whose civil status is later changed by HR (for example after a marriage)
  - **When** the civil status for the pension fund is needed again
  - **Then** it is derived again from the latest recorded civil status, using the same mapping; the employee cannot change the answer after submission (HR-side changes are specified separately).
  - _Sources: D-037, D-003_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
