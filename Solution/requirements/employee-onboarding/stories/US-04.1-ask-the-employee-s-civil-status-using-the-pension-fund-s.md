# US-04.1 – Ask the employee's civil status using the pension fund's list

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data for the pension fund](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** every new employee to state their civil status, choosing from exactly the options of my registration form (Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed), **so that** the required 'Civil status' field of the registration is always filled with a value I accept, and the registration is not blocked

Priority **P1** – Civil status is a required field of the pension fund registration. Leaving it empty blocks the registration ('Civil status is required.'). · Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-hr_transcript-bd8c8e, F-pension_portal-8664f4, F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, G-01, G-07, G-10

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-registered-partnership](../open-questions.md#oq-registered-partnership), [OQ-separated](../open-questions.md#oq-separated), [OQ-divorced-widowed](../open-questions.md#oq-divorced-widowed)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Civil status

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.1.1 Civil status is asked of every employee and cannot be left empty**
  - **Given** a new employee filling in the onboarding questionnaire
  - **When** they reach the Family step
  - **Then** they are asked for their civil status, whatever their other answers are. They cannot finish the questionnaire without answering it, because the pension fund registration is blocked without a civil status.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-pension_portal-8664f4, F-pension_portal-44a27b_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | required | ✅ |
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Married | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-04.1.2 Only the pension fund's civil status values can be chosen**
  - **Given** the employee is asked for their civil status
  - **When** they answer
  - **Then** they choose exactly one of: Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed - the complete list of the pension fund's registration form, with the same wording. No other value is offered. How employees in a registered partnership or separated employees answer is still open (see OQ-registered-partnership and OQ-separated); until it is decided, no extra option is added.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-hr_transcript-bd8c8e, G-07, G-10_
- **AC-04.1.3 Civil status is asked once and serves both the pension fund and source tax**
  - **Given** the employee has answered their civil status
  - **When** the answers are used for the receiving parties
  - **Then** the same answer fills the pension fund's 'Civil status' field and answers the source tax calculator's civil status question (see rule calculator_civil_status). The employee is never asked a second civil status question for source tax.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-8664f4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, G-01_
