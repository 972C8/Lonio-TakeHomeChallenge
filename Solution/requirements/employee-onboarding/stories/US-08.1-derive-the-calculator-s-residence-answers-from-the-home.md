# US-08.1 – Derive the calculator's residence answers from the home address

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to derive the source tax calculator's residence answers (main residence in Switzerland, and otherwise Germany, Liechtenstein or another country) from the country of the employee's home address, without asking the employee a separate residence question, **so that** the employee is asked where they live only once. Lonio can then pick the right cross-border follow-up questions and compute the tariff code, and I no longer have to work through the calculator's residence block by hand

Priority **P1** – Every other residence-dependent question and the computed tariff code (D-001) depend on this. HR finds the questions for employees living abroad hard to keep track of today (F-hr_transcript-8595fb). · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, F-hr_transcript-8595fb, G-01, G-08, D-001, D-036

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence) and [information.md](../information.md)): Country of residence (country of the home address), Residence for source tax

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.1.1 Residence for source tax is derived from the country of the home address**
  - **Given** an employee has entered the country of their home address (the address is asked of everyone and owned by the address section)
  - **When** Lonio determines the residence answer that the source tax calculator would ask for
  - **Then** The residence is 'Switzerland' if the country is Switzerland, 'Germany' if it is Germany, 'Liechtenstein' if it is Liechtenstein, and 'Other country' for any other country. The employee is not asked a separate residence or country-of-residence question for source tax.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, G-08, D-001_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Residence for source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás, lives in Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | → | Switzerland | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Konstanz (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Germany | → | Germany | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Liechtenstein | → | Liechtenstein | ✅ |
  | Employee living in France (illustrative) | France | → | Other country | ✅ |

- **AC-08.1.2 Switzerland and other countries get no cross-border questions**
  - **Given** an employee lives in Switzerland or in a country other than Germany or Liechtenstein
  - **When** the employee goes through the source tax part of the questionnaire
  - **Then** None of the Germany questions (daily return, certificate of residence, reasonable return, more than 60 non-return days) and none of the Liechtenstein questions (daily return, more than 45 non-return days) are asked. The tariff code is computed from the base tariff, as in the calculator.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, G-01, D-001_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Returns daily to the home in Germany | Returns daily to the home in Liechtenstein | Certificate of residence handed in | More than 45 work-related non-return days expected (Liechtenstein) | Daily return reasonable (less than 100 km) | More than 60 work-related non-return days expected (Germany) | Cross-border (Grenzgänger) tariff applies | Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás, lives in Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | → | not asked | not asked | not asked | not asked | – | – | – | – | ✅ |
  | Employee living in France (illustrative) | France | → | not asked | not asked | – | – | not asked | not asked | no | no | ✅ |

- **AC-08.1.3 Changing the country of residence removes the cross-border answers on submission**
  - **Given** an employee answered the Germany or Liechtenstein questions and then changes the country of their home address to another country
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** Answers to cross-border questions that no longer apply are not kept. Until submission they may be kept, so that going back and forth loses no input.
  - _Sources: D-036, G-01_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Returns daily to the home in Germany | Certificate of residence handed in | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee moves the address from Germany to Switzerland before submitting (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | not asked | not asked | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **F-hr_transcript-8595fb** (hr_transcript): For employees living abroad, the calculator asks more questions, and Germany has its own forms. Anna cannot remember what they are called. Leo deferred to the calculator, so the exact questions are unresolved in this transcript.

  - "Okay, that's fine, the calculator will tell us." — hr_transcript/T-051, Leo

  - "Wohnsitzstaat" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q17

  - "Hauptwohnsitz in der Schweiz?" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q16

  - "Options: Deutschland | Liechtenstein | Anderes Land" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q17
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b** (zh_source_tax_calculator): Residence in Switzerland or in 'Anderes Land' gives the base tariff without further residence questions.
  - "CH or 'Anderes Land' -> base tariff." — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-N05
  - ""Q17": "Anderes Land"} -> result Tarif: A0N" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-V42
  - ""Q17": "Anderes Land"} -> result Tarif: G" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-V52
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
