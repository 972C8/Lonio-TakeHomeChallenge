# US-08.1 – Derive the calculator's residence answers from the home address

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the Zurich calculator's 'main residence in Switzerland?' and 'country of residence' questions to be answered from the country of the employee's home address, **so that** the tariff can be determined without asking the employee for their residence a second time, and only the residence questions that apply to their country follow

Priority **P1** – Every source tax tariff determination needs the residence answers; residence abroad is what makes the calculator 'much more complicated' for HR (F-hr_transcript-8595fb), and asking each piece of information once is a core goal (G-01). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-80bf97, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, G-01, G-08

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-liechtenstein](../open-questions.md#oq-liechtenstein), [OQ-proof-documents](../open-questions.md#oq-proof-documents)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence) and [information.md](../information.md)): Residence answers for the source tax calculator

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.1.1 Residence is not asked again for source tax**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the country of their home address (address_country) in the home address step
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax questions
  - **Then** the employee is not asked whether their main residence is in Switzerland nor for their country of residence; both answers for the source tax calculator come from the country of the home address
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, G-01, G-08_
- **AC-08.1.2 Residence answers per country of the home address**
  - **Given** the country of the employee's home address is known
  - **When** the residence answers for the source tax calculator are derived
  - **Then** Switzerland gives 'main residence in Switzerland: yes'; Germany gives 'main residence in Switzerland: no' and country of residence Germany, followed by the Germany questions; Liechtenstein gives 'main residence in Switzerland: no' and country of residence Liechtenstein, followed by the Liechtenstein questions; any other country gives 'main residence in Switzerland: no' and country of residence 'other country'
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-80bf97_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | → | Residence answers for the source tax calculator | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás, lives in Winterthur (F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | → | Switzerland | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Konstanz (F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Germany | → | Germany | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Liechtenstein | → | Liechtenstein | ✅ |
  | Employee living in France (illustrative) | France | → | Other country | ✅ |

- **AC-08.1.3 No cross-border questions for Switzerland or other countries**
  - **Given** the employee is subject to source tax and lives in Switzerland or in a country other than Germany or Liechtenstein
  - **When** the source tax questions are shown
  - **Then** none of the Germany or Liechtenstein daily-return and non-return-day questions are asked; the calculator's base tariff applies without further residence questions
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, G-01_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Type of income (source tax) | Nationality | Permit type | Civil status | → | Employee is subject to source tax | Returns daily to the home in Germany | Returns daily to the home in Liechtenstein | Residence answers for the source tax calculator | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in France, earned income (illustrative) | France | Earned income | – | – | – | → | yes | not asked | not asked | – | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss single employee with B permit living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Earned income | Germany | B permit | Single | → | yes | not asked | not asked | Switzerland | ✅ |

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
