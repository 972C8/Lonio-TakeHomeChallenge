# US-08.1 – Derive the calculator's residence answers from the home address and ask cross-border questions only of Germany and Liechtenstein residents

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence).

**As** new employee, **I want** Lonio to take my country of residence from my home address and to ask me cross-border questions only if I live in Germany or Liechtenstein, **so that** I give my country of residence only once and am not asked questions that no receiving party needs for me, while the source tax side still gets the residence answers the Zurich calculator needs

Priority **P2** – Avoids double questions and unneeded data (G-01, G-08); the cross-border results themselves are covered by the Germany and Liechtenstein stories. · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, F-hr_transcript-8595fb, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01, G-08, D-001, D-041, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence) and [information.md](../information.md)): Cross-border tariff applies

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.1.1 Residence for source tax is not asked separately**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the country of their home address that applies on the first working day (assumption to be verified by a domain expert, D-041)
  - **When** Lonio determines the calculator's residence answers
  - **Then** the calculator's 'main residence in Switzerland' and 'country of residence' answers (Germany, Liechtenstein or other country) are taken from that country; the employee is not asked a separate residence question
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, G-01, G-08, D-041_
- **AC-08.1.2 No cross-border questions outside Germany and Liechtenstein**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland or in a country other than Germany or Liechtenstein
  - **When** the employee goes through the questionnaire
  - **Then** none of the questions on daily return, certificate of residence, reasonableness of daily return or non-return days is asked; an employee living in another country is subject to source tax and gets the normal (not the cross-border) tariff
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, G-01_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Nationality | → | Daily return to Germany | Certificate of residence | Daily return reasonable (under 100 km) | More than 60 non-return days (Germany) | Daily return to Liechtenstein | More than 45 non-return days (Liechtenstein) | Cross-border tariff applies | Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (Swiss) (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | not asked | not asked | not asked | not asked | not asked | not asked | no | no | ✅ |
  | Employee living in France (other country) (illustrative) | France | – | → | not asked | – | – | – | not asked | not asked | no | no | ✅ |

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
