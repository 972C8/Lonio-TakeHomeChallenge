# US-08.1 – Derive the calculator's residence answers from the home address and ask cross-border questions only of Germany and Liechtenstein residents

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence).

**As** source tax office, **I want** Lonio to group the employee's country of residence the way the Zurich calculator does (Switzerland, Germany, Liechtenstein or other country), without asking it again, and to ask the cross-border commuting questions only of employees living in Germany or Liechtenstein, **so that** the tariff code can be computed by following the calculator's logic from the home address the employee already gave, and no employee is asked cross-border questions that no party needs for them

Priority **P1** – Lonio computes the full tariff code instead of HR using the calculator (D-001); without the residence grouping no tariff code can be computed for any employee subject to source tax. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01, G-08, D-001, D-036, D-037, D-041, D-047, D-048

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence) and [information.md](../information.md)): Daily return to Liechtenstein, Daily return to Germany, Residence for source tax

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.1.1 Residence is grouped as in the calculator from the country of the home address**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the country of their home address in step 2 'Home address', as it applies on their first working day (assumption D-041, to be verified by a domain expert)
  - **When** Lonio determines the residence for source tax
  - **Then** the residence is 'Switzerland' when the country is Switzerland, 'Germany' when it is Germany, 'Liechtenstein' when it is Liechtenstein, and 'Other country' for any other country; the employee is not asked a separate question about their main residence or country of residence for source tax
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, G-08, D-041_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Residence for source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator: main residence in Switzerland (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-aedb40) | Switzerland | → | Switzerland | ✅ |
  | Calculator option Deutschland (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-d62dd4) | Germany | → | Germany | ✅ |
  | Calculator option Liechtenstein (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-d62dd4) | Liechtenstein | → | Liechtenstein | ✅ |
  | Employee living in France (illustrative) | France | → | Other country | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Austria (illustrative) | Austria | → | Other country | ✅ |

- **AC-08.1.2 Switzerland and other countries get no cross-border questions**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland or in a country other than Germany and Liechtenstein
  - **When** the employee goes through the questionnaire
  - **Then** step 5 'Cross-border commuting' is not shown and no question on daily return, non-return days, reasonableness of daily return or certificate of residence is asked; for source tax the base tariff applies without further residence questions, as in the calculator
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, G-01, D-047_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Daily return to Germany | Daily return to Liechtenstein | Residence for source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | not asked | not asked | – | ✅ |
  | Calculator: 'Anderes Land' gives the base tariff (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-f86c6b) | France | → | not asked | not asked | Other country | ✅ |

- **AC-08.1.3 Germany and Liechtenstein residents get step 5 'Cross-border commuting'**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Germany or Liechtenstein (and is therefore subject to source tax, as every employee living abroad is)
  - **When** the employee has completed step 4 'Civil status and spouse'
  - **Then** step 5 'Cross-border commuting' is shown before step 6 'Source tax details'; a Germany resident is asked the Germany questions starting with daily return to Germany, a Liechtenstein resident the Liechtenstein questions starting with daily return to Liechtenstein, and never the questions of the other country (step order is assumption D-047, to be verified by a domain expert)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, D-047, D-040_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Daily return to Germany | Daily return to Liechtenstein | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator: Germany shows the daily return question (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-31d762) | Germany | → | required | not asked | ✅ |
  | Calculator: Liechtenstein shows the daily return question (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-e22d17) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | required | ✅ |

- **AC-08.1.4 Cross-border answers that no longer apply are not kept**
  - **Given** the employee answered cross-border questions and then changes their home address country so that those questions no longer apply (for example from Germany to Switzerland)
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** the cross-border answers that no longer apply are discarded and not recorded; before submission they are kept, so changing the country back restores them; after submission any change of the country is made by HR, and the residence grouping and every result of this section are derived again from the latest answers (D-036, D-037); if an HR change makes cross-border questions apply that were never asked, the tariff code shows 'incomplete - HR must complete' until HR enters the missing answers (assumption D-048, to be verified by a domain expert)
  - _Sources: G-01, D-036, D-037, D-048_
_Trace check: partial – AC-08.1.2 lists 'reasonableness of daily return' and 'non-return days' questions, which no cited fact backs._


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **D-040** (product decision (Product owner)): For employees living in Liechtenstein, the questions on daily return and on more than 45 days abroad come before denomination, spouse and children. When the answers mean 'in principle no source tax liability', the tariff questions are skipped. When the employee stays abroad more than 45 days, the normal tariff is recorded with the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement' and flagged for HR. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-041** (product decision (Product owner)): The residence country and address asked are those that apply on the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-048** (product decision (Product owner)): When HR changes an answer after submission so that questions apply that were never asked, the derived results that depend on them (for example the tariff code) show 'incomplete - HR must complete' instead of a value, until HR enters the missing answers. Answers that no longer apply after an HR change are discarded, as on the employee's submission. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
