# US-04.3 – Decide whether the employee is subject to source tax

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Source tax liability check](../feature.md#SEC-04-source-tax-liability).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to decide from the employee's answers whether they are subject to source tax, and to ask the source tax questions only of those who are, **so that** I no longer have to work out by hand who must answer the source tax questions, and employees who are not subject are not asked for data no party needs

Priority **P1** – HR says they never know in advance who has to answer the source tax questions (F-hr_transcript-b84498). The liability decision gates the whole source tax part and the tariff code (D-001). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-d7431e, G-01, D-003, D-005, D-007, D-008, D-037

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-source-tax-liability) and [information.md](../information.md)): Subject to source tax

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.3.1 Employees living abroad are always subject to source tax**
  - **Given** the employee's home address is in a country other than Switzerland
  - **When** Lonio decides source tax liability
  - **Then** the employee is subject to source tax whatever their nationality, even as a Swiss citizen. This includes residents of Germany, of Liechtenstein (see D-007) and of any other country.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, D-007_

  | Example | Country of home address (country of residence) | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Konstanz, Germany (F-hr_transcript-1a20bb) | Germany | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (F-hr_transcript-1a20bb) | Liechtenstein | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Employee living in another country (illustrative) | France | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-04.3.2 Employees living in Switzerland are subject only without Swiss citizenship, C permit or spouse exemption**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland and has answered the citizenship, C permit, civil status and spouse questions that were asked
  - **When** Lonio decides source tax liability
  - **Then** the employee is not subject if they hold Swiss citizenship (dual citizens included), hold a C permit, or are exempted by a Swiss or C-permit spouse or registered partner living in Switzerland. Otherwise they are subject to source tax.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-d7431e, D-003, D-005, D-008_

  | Example | Country of home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | Civil status | C permit held | Spouse's Swiss citizenship | Spouse lives in Switzerland | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Swiss citizen (including dual citizens) living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | yes | Single | – | – | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss with C permit living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | no | Single | yes | – | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Tomás - Brazilian, B permit, Swiss wife, Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | no | Married | no | yes | Switzerland | → | no | ✅ |
  | Single non-Swiss without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | no | Single | no | – | – | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Married, Swiss spouse living in another country (illustrative) | Switzerland | no | Married | no | yes | Another country | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Separated non-Swiss without C permit (counts as single) (illustrative) | Switzerland | no | Separated | no | – | – | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-04.3.3 Only employees subject to source tax get the source tax questions**
  - **Given** Lonio has decided whether the employee is subject to source tax
  - **When** the questionnaire reaches the source tax details
  - **Then** employees subject to source tax are asked the source tax questions, such as denomination. Employees who are not subject are not asked any of them.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, G-01_

  | Example | Country of home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | C permit held | Civil status | Spouse's Swiss citizenship | Spouse lives in Switzerland | → | Denomination | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Konstanz, Germany (F-hr_transcript-1a20bb) | Germany | – | – | – | – | – | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | no | no | Married | yes | Switzerland | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Single non-Swiss without C permit living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | no | no | Single | – | – | → | asked | ✅ |

- **AC-04.3.4 Liability always follows the latest answers**
  - **Given** the employee's answers that decide liability (home address country, Swiss citizenship, C permit, civil status or spouse details) change, either while the employee goes back and forth before submission or through HR after submission
  - **When** the liability is decided again
  - **Then** the decision is always derived again from the latest answers. It is recorded for HR, together with the decision whether the source tax questions apply.
  - _Sources: D-037, D-034, D-036_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-005** (product decision (Product owner)): Do not ask for the permit type. Ask non-Swiss employees only whether they hold a C permit (permanent residence), yes or no, and the same for a non-Swiss spouse. No party needs any other permit type. This closes OQ-permit-list. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-007** (product decision (Product owner)): Liechtenstein residents are asked the source tax questions (HR's rule decides who is asked). The result follows the calculator - with daily return or at most 45 non-return days, Lonio shows 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR; with more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code. This closes OQ-liechtenstein-conflict. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-008** (product decision (Product owner)): Anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss for source tax, even with another nationality. This closes OQ-dual-nationality. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
