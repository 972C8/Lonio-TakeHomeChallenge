# US-04.3 – Decide whether the employee is subject to source tax

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Source tax liability check](../feature.md#SEC-04-source-tax-liability).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to decide from residence, Swiss citizenship, C permit and the spouse exemption whether a new employee is subject to source tax, **so that** only employees subject to source tax get the source tax questions and a tariff result, and I no longer decide this by hand

Priority **P1** – HR decides source tax liability by hand today and never knows up front who must answer the source tax questions (F-hr_transcript-b84498); the whole source tax step depends on this decision. · Sources: F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-d7431e, F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, F-hr_transcript-b84498, D-003, D-005, D-007, D-008

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.3.1 Employees living abroad are always subject to source tax**
  - **Given** the employee's home address country is not Switzerland
  - **When** Lonio decides source tax liability
  - **Then** the employee is subject to source tax whatever their nationality, even if Swiss; this includes residents of Liechtenstein, whose result then follows the Liechtenstein questions
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, D-007_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Stefan, Swiss living abroad (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-1a20bb) (illustrative) | Germany | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-04.3.2 Employees living in Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland
  - **When** Lonio decides source tax liability
  - **Then** the employee is not subject if Swiss (including dual nationals with Swiss citizenship), if holding a C permit, or if exempted by a Swiss or C-permit spouse or registered partner living in Switzerland; otherwise the employee is subject to source tax
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-d7431e, D-003, D-005, D-008_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's / registered partner's country of residence | Spouse's / registered partner's nationality | Spouse / registered partner holds a C permit | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Swiss employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | – | – | – | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss employee with C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | yes | Single | – | – | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Single non-Swiss employee without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Single | – | – | – | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Separated non-Swiss employee without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Separated | – | – | – | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Married, non-Swiss spouse without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | Portugal | no | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Married, spouse living abroad (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Brazil | – | – | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-04.3.3 Only employees subject to source tax get the source tax questions**
  - **Given** Lonio has decided whether the employee is subject to source tax
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax step
  - **Then** employees subject to source tax are asked the source tax questions (such as denomination); employees not subject are not asked them and get no source tax result
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, G-01_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's / registered partner's country of residence | Spouse's / registered partner's nationality | → | Denomination | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Stefan, Swiss living abroad (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-1a20bb) (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Single | – | – | → | asked | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-005** (product decision (Product owner)): Do not ask for the permit type. Ask non-Swiss employees only whether they hold a C permit (permanent residence), yes or no, and the same for a non-Swiss spouse. No party needs any other permit type. This closes OQ-permit-list. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-007** (product decision (Product owner)): Liechtenstein residents are asked the source tax questions (HR's rule decides who is asked). The result follows the calculator - with daily return or at most 45 non-return days, Lonio shows 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR; with more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code. This closes OQ-liechtenstein-conflict. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-008** (product decision (Product owner)): Anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss for source tax, even with another nationality. This closes OQ-dual-nationality. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **F-hr_transcript-1a20bb** (hr_transcript): Employees who work in Switzerland but live abroad (e.g. Konstanz in Germany, Liechtenstein) are always subject to source tax, regardless of nationality – even Swiss citizens.
  - "Those people do source tax because they work here and they don't live here. And there it doesn't matter what passport you have, by the way." — hr_transcript/T-048, Anna
  - "Stefan is actually Swiss, and he still has it." — hr_transcript/T-048, Anna
  - "So someone living abroad and working here always does it?" — hr_transcript/T-049, Leo
  - "Yes." — hr_transcript/T-050, Anna
- **F-hr_transcript-845204** (hr_transcript): Correction: non-Swiss employees with a C permit are not subject to source tax; they are treated like Swiss people for this.
  - "No, that's actually not right." — hr_transcript/T-038, Anna
  - "So, not the people with a C permit. They're treated like Swiss people for this." — hr_transcript/T-038, Anna
- **F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3** (hr_transcript, inferred): Consolidated final rule: an employee is subject to source tax if they live abroad (home address country not Switzerland), regardless of nationality. If they live in Switzerland, they are subject only if they are not Swiss and do not hold a C permit, unless they are married to a spouse who is Swiss or holds a C permit and lives in Switzerland (then not subject).
  - "If you don't have a Swiss passport, you do source tax." — hr_transcript/T-010, Anna
  - "So, not the people with a C permit." — hr_transcript/T-038, Anna
  - "If you're married to someone Swiss, or to someone with a C permit, and that person lives here, then you don't do source tax either." — hr_transcript/T-044, Anna
  - "Those people do source tax because they work here and they don't live here." — hr_transcript/T-048, Anna
- **F-hr_transcript-b84498** (hr_transcript, inferred): Source tax questions should only be asked to employees who are subject to source tax, not to everybody.
  - "Because I never know up front who has to answer those questions." — hr_transcript/T-008, Anna
  - "So who actually needs to answer them?" — hr_transcript/T-009, Leo
- **F-hr_transcript-d7431e** (hr_transcript): An employee married to someone Swiss or to someone with a C permit, where that spouse lives in Switzerland, is not subject to source tax (e.g. Tomás: Brazilian, B permit, Swiss wife, living in Winterthur → no source tax).
  - "If you're married to someone Swiss, or to someone with a C permit, and that person lives here, then you don't do source tax either." — hr_transcript/T-044, Anna
  - "Brazilian, B permit, but his wife is Swiss, and they live in Winterthur. So no source tax for him." — hr_transcript/T-044, Anna
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
