# US-01.1 – Personal details for the pension fund registration

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address).

**As** pension fund, **I want** every new employee to give their first name, last name and date of birth, **so that** the employee can be registered as an insured person; the registration is rejected if any of these required fields is missing

Priority **P1** – The fund's registration form marks these fields as required and refuses the registration while they are empty, so missing data blocks the registration. · Sources: F-pension_portal-b5c835, F-pension_portal-c688e4, F-pension_portal-88b436, F-pension_portal-967bc9, F-pension_portal-3cfe89, F-pension_portal-44a27b, G-09

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-canton-abroad](../open-questions.md#oq-canton-abroad)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): First name, Last name, Date of birth

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.1.1 Name and date of birth are always asked and required**
  - **Given** a new employee starts the questionnaire, wherever they live
  - **When** they fill in the first step, personal details
  - **Then** they are asked for their first name, last name and date of birth, and they cannot submit the questionnaire while any of the three is empty
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-b5c835, F-pension_portal-c688e4, F-pension_portal-88b436, F-pension_portal-3cfe89, F-pension_portal-44a27b_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | → | First name | Last name | Date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | required | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living abroad (illustrative) | Germany | → | asked | asked | required | ✅ |

- **AC-01.1.2 Date of birth is a full calendar date**
  - **Given** the employee is asked for their date of birth
  - **When** they enter it
  - **Then** it is recorded as day, month and year (DD.MM.YYYY), as the pension fund expects it
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-967bc9, G-10_
- **AC-01.1.3 Personal details are recorded for the pension fund**
  - **Given** the employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** the answers are recorded
  - **Then** first name, last name and date of birth are recorded as needed for the pension fund registration, each asked only once
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-b5c835, F-pension_portal-c688e4, F-pension_portal-88b436, G-01_
