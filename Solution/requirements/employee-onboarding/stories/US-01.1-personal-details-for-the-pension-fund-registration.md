# US-01.1 – Personal details for the pension fund registration

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address).

**As** pension fund, **I want** the new employee's first name, last name and date of birth, **so that** the new employee can be registered as an insured person. The registration form does not accept any of these fields empty.

Priority **P1** – All three are required fields on the pension fund's registration form. The form cannot be submitted while any of them is empty, so a missing value blocks the registration. · Sources: F-pension_portal-b5c835, F-pension_portal-c688e4, F-pension_portal-88b436, F-pension_portal-967bc9, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-pension_portal-3cfe89, G-01, G-09

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-canton-abroad](../open-questions.md#oq-canton-abroad)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): First name, Last name, Date of birth

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.1.1 Name is asked of every employee and is required**
  - **Given** a new employee is filling in the onboarding questionnaire
  - **When** they reach the personal details at the start of the questionnaire
  - **Then** they are asked for their first name and their last name. Both are required, so the questionnaire cannot be submitted while either is empty. Both answers are recorded for the pension fund registration.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-b5c835, F-pension_portal-c688e4, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-pension_portal-3cfe89_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | → | First name | Last name | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Any employee, whatever their residence (illustrative) (illustrative) | Germany | → | required | required | ✅ |

- **AC-01.1.2 Date of birth is asked of every employee as a date**
  - **Given** a new employee is filling in the personal details
  - **When** they give their date of birth
  - **Then** the date of birth is required and is given as a date in the form DD.MM.YYYY, the form the pension fund uses. The questionnaire cannot be submitted without it. It is recorded for the pension fund registration.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-88b436, F-pension_portal-967bc9, F-pension_portal-44a27b_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | → | Date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |
