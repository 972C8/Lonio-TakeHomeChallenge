# US-02.4 – Ask Swiss residents for their canton

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** pension fund, **I want** every registration to carry a canton: the canton of residence for employees living in Switzerland and Zurich, the canton of the place of work, for employees living abroad, **so that** the required 'Canton' field of the registration is never empty, and employees living abroad are not asked a question the fund's canton list cannot answer for them

Priority **P1** – 'Canton' is a required field. Without it the pension fund registration is blocked (F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-pension_portal-6c621f). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-pension_portal-6c621f, F-pension_portal-3432a9, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-pension_portal-b359c3, G-01, D-002, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Canton of residence, Canton for the pension fund

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.4.1 Only employees living in Switzerland choose their canton**
  - **Given** a new employee has given the country of their home address
  - **When** the country is Switzerland
  - **Then** they are asked to choose their canton of residence. The answer is required. The choice is one of the 26 cantons on the pension fund's list: Aargau, Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Appenzell Innerrhoden, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt, Bern, Fribourg, Geneva, Glarus, Graubünden, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Nidwalden, Obwalden, Schaffhausen, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Ticino, Uri, Valais, Vaud, Zug, Zurich. If the country is anything other than Switzerland, the canton is not asked. Assumption, to be verified by a domain expert (D-002).
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-6c621f, F-pension_portal-3432a9, D-002_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | → | Canton of residence | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |
  | Stefan, Swiss citizen living abroad (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Germany | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-02.4.2 Canton recorded for the pension fund**
  - **Given** a new employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** Lonio determines the canton for the pension fund registration
  - **Then** for an employee living in Switzerland, it is the canton they chose. For an employee living in any other country, it is Zurich, the canton of Muster AG's place of work, which Lonio enters without asking. Because the fund's list has no option for a residence outside Switzerland and the fund does not say whether 'Canton' means residence or work, this mapping is an assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-002). The canton is derived again from the latest answers whenever they change.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3432a9, F-pension_portal-b359c3, D-002, D-037_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Canton of residence | → | Canton for the pension fund | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Living in Switzerland, canton Bern (illustrative) | Switzerland | Bern | → | Bern | ✅ |
  | Living in Switzerland, canton Zurich (illustrative) | Switzerland | Zurich | → | Zurich | ✅ |
  | Stefan, Swiss citizen living abroad (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Germany | – | → | Zurich | ✅ |
  | Living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | – | → | Zurich | ✅ |
  | Living in another country (illustrative) | France | – | → | Zurich | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-002** (product decision (Product owner)): Ask for the canton (pension fund 'Canton' field) from every employee. Employees living in Switzerland choose their canton of residence; for employees living abroad the canton of the place of work is entered (Zurich for Muster AG) and not asked. This closes OQ-canton-abroad. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
