# US-02.4 – Ask Swiss residents for their canton

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** pension fund, **I want** a canton for every new employee: the canton of residence chosen by employees who live in Switzerland, and Zurich (the canton of the place of work at Muster AG) for employees who live abroad, without asking them, **so that** the required 'Canton' field of the registration is always filled, even for employees living abroad, for whom the fund's list has no option

Priority **P1** – 'Canton' is a required field of the fund's registration; if it is empty the registration is rejected ('Canton is required.'). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-pension_portal-6c621f, F-pension_portal-3432a9, F-pension_portal-b359c3, F-pension_portal-44a27b, D-002, D-037, D-041, D-047, D-048

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Canton of residence, Country of residence (country of the home address), Canton for the pension fund

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.4.1 Employees living in Switzerland choose their canton**
  - **Given** a new employee's home address on the first working day is in Switzerland
  - **When** they fill in step 2 'Home address'
  - **Then** they are asked their canton of residence, it is required, and they choose one of the 26 cantons the fund lists: Aargau, Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Appenzell Innerrhoden, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt, Bern, Fribourg, Geneva, Glarus, Graubünden, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Nidwalden, Obwalden, Schaffhausen, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Ticino, Uri, Valais, Vaud, Zug, Zurich. The chosen canton is recorded as the canton for the pension fund.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-6c621f, F-pension_portal-3432a9, F-pension_portal-44a27b, D-002, D-047_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Canton of residence | → | Canton of residence | Canton for the pension fund | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Living in Switzerland, canton Bern (illustrative) | Switzerland | Bern | → | required | Bern | ✅ |
  | Living in Switzerland, canton Zurich (illustrative) | Switzerland | Zurich | → | required | Zurich | ✅ |

- **AC-02.4.2 Employees living abroad are not asked; Zurich is recorded**
  - **Given** a new employee's home address on the first working day is outside Switzerland
  - **When** they fill in step 2 'Home address'
  - **Then** the canton is not asked, and Zurich, the canton of the place of work at Muster AG, is recorded as the canton for the pension fund. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-002): the fund's portal does not say whether 'Canton' means residence or place of work, and its list has no option for a residence abroad.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-b359c3, F-pension_portal-3432a9, D-002_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Canton of residence | Canton for the pension fund | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | not asked | Zurich | ✅ |
  | Living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | Zurich | ✅ |

- **AC-02.4.3 The canton for the fund always follows the latest answers**
  - **Given** the questionnaire has been submitted and HR later changes the employee's country of residence
  - **When** the canton for the pension fund is derived again
  - **Then** it follows the latest answers. If the employee now lives abroad, Zurich is recorded and any canton chosen earlier is discarded. If the employee now lives in Switzerland but no canton was ever asked, the canton for the fund shows 'incomplete - HR must complete' until HR enters it. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-048). How HR makes the change belongs to the later HR-side spec.
  - _Sources: D-037, D-048, D-034, D-002_

## Evidence
- **D-002** (product decision (Product owner)): Ask for the canton (pension fund 'Canton' field) from every employee. Employees living in Switzerland choose their canton of residence; for employees living abroad the canton of the place of work is entered (Zurich for Muster AG) and not asked. This closes OQ-canton-abroad. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **D-041** (product decision (Product owner)): The residence country and address asked are those that apply on the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-048** (product decision (Product owner)): When HR changes an answer after submission so that questions apply that were never asked, the derived results that depend on them (for example the tariff code) show 'incomplete - HR must complete' instead of a value, until HR enters the missing answers. Answers that no longer apply after an HR change are discarded, as on the employee's submission. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
