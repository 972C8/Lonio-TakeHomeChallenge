# US-02.4 – Ask Swiss residents for their canton

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** pension fund, **I want** employees living in Switzerland to choose their canton of residence, and employees living abroad to be registered with the canton of the place of work (Zurich for Muster AG) without being asked, **so that** the required 'Canton' field of the fund's registration form is always filled with a value from the fund's list

Priority **P1** – 'Canton' is a required field of the fund's registration form ('Canton is required.'); without it the registration is blocked, and the list has no option for people living abroad. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-pension_portal-6c621f, F-pension_portal-3432a9, F-pension_portal-b359c3, F-pension_portal-44a27b, D-002, D-041, D-047

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Canton of residence, Canton for the pension fund

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.4.1 Canton asked only of employees living in Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the country of their home address in step 2 'Home address'
  - **When** the country is Switzerland
  - **Then** they are asked for the canton they live in on their first working day, and the answer is required; when the country is any other country, the canton is not asked. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-002).
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-6c621f, D-002, D-041, D-047_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Canton of residence | → | Canton of residence | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Lives in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Zurich | → | required | ✅ |
  | Lives in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | – | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-02.4.2 Canton offered from the fund's list**
  - **Given** an employee living in Switzerland is asked for their canton
  - **When** they choose it
  - **Then** they choose one of exactly the 26 cantons on the fund's list: Aargau, Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Appenzell Innerrhoden, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt, Bern, Fribourg, Geneva, Glarus, Graubünden, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Nidwalden, Obwalden, Schaffhausen, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Ticino, Uri, Valais, Vaud, Zug, Zurich.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3432a9_
- **AC-02.4.3 Canton recorded for the pension fund**
  - **Given** the employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** the canton for the pension fund is worked out
  - **Then** for an employee living in Switzerland it is the canton they chose; for an employee living in any other country it is Zurich, the canton of the place of work at Muster AG. The canton for employees abroad is an assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-002); the fund's form does not say whether 'Canton' means residence or place of work. The canton is always worked out again from the latest answers (D-037).
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-6c621f, F-pension_portal-3432a9, F-pension_portal-b359c3, D-002, D-037_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Canton of residence | → | Canton for the pension fund | Canton of residence | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Lives in canton Bern (illustrative) | Switzerland | Bern | → | Bern | – | ✅ |
  | Lives in canton Zurich (illustrative) | Switzerland | Zurich | → | Zurich | – | ✅ |
  | Lives in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | – | → | Zurich | not asked | ✅ |
  | Lives in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | – | → | Zurich | not asked | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-002** (product decision (Product owner)): Ask for the canton (pension fund 'Canton' field) from every employee. Employees living in Switzerland choose their canton of residence; for employees living abroad the canton of the place of work is entered (Zurich for Muster AG) and not asked. This closes OQ-canton-abroad. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **D-041** (product decision (Product owner)): The residence country and address asked are those that apply on the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
