# US-02.4 – Ask Swiss residents for their canton

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** pension fund, **I want** employees who live in Switzerland to choose their canton of residence from the 26 Swiss cantons, **so that** the pension registration's required 'Canton' field can be filled and the registration is not blocked

Priority **P1** – 'Canton' is a required field of the pension registration. Submitting it empty blocks the registration with 'Canton is required.' · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-pension_portal-6c621f, F-pension_portal-3432a9, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-pension_portal-b359c3, G-01, G-07

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-canton](../open-questions.md#oq-canton)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Canton of residence

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.4.1 Canton is asked and required only of employees living in Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee has given the country of their home address
  - **When** the country is Switzerland
  - **Then** the employee is asked for their canton of residence right after the country, and may not leave it empty. If the country is not Switzerland, the canton is not asked, because the pension fund's list offers no option for a residence abroad. What the pension fund needs for employees living abroad, and whether 'Canton' means canton of residence or of work, is open (see OQ-canton).
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-6c621f, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-pension_portal-b359c3, F-pension_portal-3432a9_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | → | Canton of residence | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |
  | Cross-border employee living in Germany (Konstanz) (illustrative) | Germany | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Cross-border employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-02.4.2 Canton is chosen from the pension fund's list**
  - **Given** an employee living in Switzerland is asked for their canton
  - **When** they answer
  - **Then** they choose exactly one of the 26 cantons as the pension fund lists them: Aargau, Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Appenzell Innerrhoden, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt, Bern, Fribourg, Geneva, Glarus, Graubünden, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Nidwalden, Obwalden, Schaffhausen, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Ticino, Uri, Valais, Vaud, Zug, Zurich. The answer goes to the pension fund for the registration.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3432a9, F-pension_portal-6c621f, G-07_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Canton of residence | → | Canton of residence | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in the canton of Zurich (illustrative) | Switzerland | Zurich | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in the canton of St. Gallen (illustrative) | Switzerland | St. Gallen | → | asked | ✅ |

_Trace check: partial – The claim that the canton is asked 'right after the country' is an ordering that none of the cited facts back._
