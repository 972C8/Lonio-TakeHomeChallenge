# US-02.4 – Ask Swiss residents for their canton

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** pension fund, **I want** employees living in Switzerland to choose their canton from the 26 Swiss cantons, **so that** the required 'Canton' field of the pension fund registration can be filled, while employees abroad are not asked for a canton they do not have

Priority **P1** – 'Canton' is a required field of the pension fund registration, and the registration is rejected without it ('Canton is required.'). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-pension_portal-6c621f, F-pension_portal-3432a9, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-pension_portal-b359c3, G-01, D-002, D-036

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-canton-abroad](../open-questions.md#oq-canton-abroad)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Canton

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.4.1 The canton is asked and required only of employees living in Switzerland**
  - **Given** a new employee has entered the country of their home address
  - **When** the questionnaire decides whether to ask for the canton
  - **Then** if the country is Switzerland, the canton is asked and required. Otherwise it is not asked. What the fund expects for employees living abroad, and whether 'Canton' means canton of residence or of work, is still open (see OQ-canton-abroad, non-blocking)
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-6c621f, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-pension_portal-b359c3, D-002_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Canton | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás, lives in Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Konstanz, Germany (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Germany | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-02.4.2 The canton is one of the fund's 26 Swiss cantons**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland and is asked for the canton
  - **When** the employee answers
  - **Then** they choose exactly one of the fund's list: Aargau, Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Appenzell Innerrhoden, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt, Bern, Fribourg, Geneva, Glarus, Graubünden, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Nidwalden, Obwalden, Schaffhausen, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Ticino, Uri, Valais, Vaud, Zug, Zurich. The answer is recorded for the pension fund registration
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3432a9, F-pension_portal-6c621f_
- **AC-02.4.3 A canton is not kept for an employee who no longer lives in Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee chose a canton and then changed the country of the home address from Switzerland to another country before submitting
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** the canton answer is discarded and not recorded, because no party needs it for this employee. Until submission it may be kept, so switching back to Switzerland does not lose the answer
  - _Sources: D-036, D-002, G-01_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Canton | → | Canton | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee corrects the country to Germany (illustrative) | Germany | Zurich | → | not asked | ✅ |
