# US-08.2 – Discard answers that no longer apply on submission

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Completing and submitting the questionnaire](../feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission).

**As** new employee, **I want** to go back and change earlier answers without losing what I already typed, while answers to questions that no longer apply are not kept once I submit, **so that** I can correct myself easily, and Lonio keeps no personal data about me that no receiving party needs

Priority **P1** – Data minimisation is a core constraint of the feature: keeping answers no party needs means holding personal data Lonio must not have. · Status: **decision** · Sources: G-01, D-036

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission) and [information.md](../information.md)): Discard answers that no longer apply

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.2.1 Answers kept while going back and forth**
  - **Given** an employee who has answered later questions and then changes an earlier answer so that some of those later questions no longer apply
  - **When** they continue filling in the questionnaire before submitting
  - **Then** the questions that no longer apply are no longer asked, but their answers are kept, so that if the employee changes the earlier answer back, the earlier input reappears and need not be entered again
  - _Sources: D-036_
- **AC-08.2.2 Inapplicable answers discarded on submission**
  - **Given** an employee who has answers to questions that no longer apply because of a changed earlier answer
  - **When** they submit the questionnaire
  - **Then** only answers to questions that are asked for the final set of answers are recorded; the answers to questions no longer asked are discarded and are not recorded for any receiving party
  - _Sources: G-01, D-036_

## Evidence
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
