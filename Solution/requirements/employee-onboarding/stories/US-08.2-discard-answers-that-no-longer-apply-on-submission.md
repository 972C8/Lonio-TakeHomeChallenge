# US-08.2 – Discard answers that no longer apply on submission

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Completing and submitting the questionnaire](../feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission).

**As** new employee, **I want** to go back and change earlier answers without losing input, while only the answers to questions that still apply to me are recorded when I submit, **so that** no party ends up holding personal data about me that it does not need (data minimisation), and I don't have to retype answers while correcting myself

Priority **P1** – Data minimisation is a core requirement of the feature (G-01): keeping answers no party needs means holding personal data Lonio must not have. · Status: **decision** · Sources: D-036, G-01

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission) and [information.md](../information.md)): Discard answers that no longer apply

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.2.1 Answers are kept while the employee moves back and forth**
  - **Given** an employee has answered later questions and then changes an earlier answer so that those questions no longer apply
  - **When** the employee is still filling in the questionnaire and changes the earlier answer back
  - **Then** the earlier answers to the later questions can still be there, so the employee does not lose input
  - _Sources: D-036_
- **AC-08.2.2 Only applicable answers are recorded on submission**
  - **Given** an employee has answered questions that no longer apply after changing an earlier answer
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** the answers to questions that no longer apply are discarded and not recorded for HR or any receiving party; only answers to questions that apply to the final answers are recorded
  - _Sources: D-036, G-01, D-034_

  | Example | Civil status | Country (home address) = country of residence | Nationality | → | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Canton | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Subject to source tax | Denomination | Checked |

  | Employee first said Married, then changed to Single (illustrative) | Single | – | – | → | not asked | – | – | – | – | – | ✅ |
  | Employee first entered a Swiss address, then changed to Germany (illustrative) | – | Germany | – | → | – | not asked | not asked | not asked | – | – | ✅ |
  | Employee first entered another nationality, then changed to Switzerland (illustrative) | – | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | – | – | – | not asked | no | not asked | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
