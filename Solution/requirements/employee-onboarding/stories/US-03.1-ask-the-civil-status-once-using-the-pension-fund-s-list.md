# US-03.1 – Ask the civil status once, using the pension fund's list

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [3. Civil status and spouse for the pension fund](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse).

**As** new employee, **I want** to state my civil status once, choosing from Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated, **so that** the pension fund registration and the source tax tariff can both use my answer without asking me again

Priority **P1** – Civil status is a required field of the pension fund registration; a registration without it is rejected. Source tax also needs it to set the tariff letter. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-hr_transcript-bd8c8e, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, F-pension_portal-ec4512, F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-pension_portal-9b24c5, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, G-01, G-10, D-003

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-03.1.1 Civil status is always asked and required**
  - **Given** any new employee filling in the questionnaire, in the 'Civil status and spouse' step
  - **When** the employee reaches the civil status question
  - **Then** the civil status is asked of every employee, whatever their country of residence, nationality or source tax situation, and the questionnaire cannot be submitted without an answer
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-pension_portal-ec4512, F-pension_portal-44a27b, D-003_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | → | Civil status | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland, Single (illustrative) | Switzerland | Single | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany, Married (illustrative) | Germany | Married | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-03.1.2 Allowed values**
  - **Given** the employee answers the civil status question
  - **When** they choose their civil status
  - **Then** they can choose exactly one of: Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership, Separated. The first four are the pension fund's own list; Registered partnership and Separated are added because the source tax calculator distinguishes them and the fund's list has no option for them (see OQ-civil-status-partnership)
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-pension_portal-9b24c5, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, G-10, D-003_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel, registered partnership (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-03.1.3 Asked once, reused for every receiving party**
  - **Given** the employee has answered the civil status
  - **When** the pension fund and source tax need the civil status
  - **Then** the employee is not asked again in any later step (including the source tax step); the answer is recorded for the pension fund, translated to the fund's list, and for source tax, grouped as married/registered partnership or not married
  - _Sources: G-01, F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003_

For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. - **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-10** (product owner guidance): Business wording for values (used in allowed values and examples): countries by English name ("Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein"), permits as "B permit", "C permit", civil status exactly as the pension fund's list shows it ("Single", "Married", ...), dates as DD.MM.YYYY, yes/no as "yes"/"no".
