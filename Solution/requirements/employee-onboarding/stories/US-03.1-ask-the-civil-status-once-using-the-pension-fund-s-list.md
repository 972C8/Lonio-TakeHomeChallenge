# US-03.1 – Ask the civil status once, using the pension fund's list

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [3. Civil status and spouse for the pension fund](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse).

**As** new employee, **I want** to state my civil status once, choosing from a list that also covers registered partnership and separation, **so that** both the pension fund and source tax get my civil status without being asked twice, and my real situation can be recorded

Priority **P1** – Civil status is a required field of the pension fund registration; without it the registration is blocked. It also decides the source tax tariff. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-pension_portal-ec4512, F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, G-01, D-003

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-03.1.1 Civil status is asked of every employee and is required**
  - **Given** a new employee filling in the questionnaire
  - **When** they reach the family step
  - **Then** they are asked their civil status, whatever their residence or nationality, and cannot submit the questionnaire without answering it
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-pension_portal-ec4512, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | → | Civil status | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living abroad (illustrative) | Germany | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-03.1.2 Allowed civil status values**
  - **Given** the employee is asked their civil status
  - **When** they choose an answer
  - **Then** they can choose exactly one of: Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership, Separated
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, D-003_
- **AC-03.1.3 One answer serves both receiving parties**
  - **Given** the employee has answered their civil status
  - **When** the questionnaire is submitted
  - **Then** the answer is recorded once and is used both for the pension fund registration (see US-03.3) and for source tax (see US-03.4); the employee is not asked their civil status a second time
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, G-01, D-003_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
