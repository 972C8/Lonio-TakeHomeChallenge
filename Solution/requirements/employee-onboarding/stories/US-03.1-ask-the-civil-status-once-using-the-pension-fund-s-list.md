# US-03.1 – Ask the civil status once, using the pension fund's list

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [3. Civil status and spouse for the pension fund](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse).

**As** new employee, **I want** to state my civil status once, choosing from a list that also covers registered partnership and separation, **so that** I am not asked the same thing again for the pension fund and for source tax, and my real situation can be recorded

Priority **P1** – The pension fund's 'Civil status' field is required, so the registration cannot go ahead without it. Source tax needs it as well to decide married or single. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-pension_portal-ec4512, F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, G-01, G-10, D-003

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-03.1.1 Civil status is asked of every employee and is required**
  - **Given** a new employee is filling in the onboarding questionnaire
  - **When** they reach the family step
  - **Then** Lonio asks for their civil status. The question cannot be left empty. Lonio asks it only once, and the same answer is used for the pension fund and, if the employee is subject to source tax, for source tax.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-pension_portal-ec4512, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, G-01, D-003_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | required | ✅ |
  | Daniel, registered partnership (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-03.1.2 Allowed civil status values**
  - **Given** the employee is asked for their civil status
  - **When** they choose an answer
  - **Then** they can choose exactly one of these: Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership, Separated. The first four use the wording of the pension fund's list. Registered partnership and Separated are added so that a partnered or separated employee does not have to pick a value that does not describe them.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, G-10, D-003_

## Evidence

For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. - **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-10** (product owner guidance): Business wording for values (used in allowed values and examples): countries by English name ("Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein"), permits as "B permit", "C permit", civil status exactly as the pension fund's list shows it ("Single", "Married", ...), dates as DD.MM.YYYY, yes/no as "yes"/"no".
