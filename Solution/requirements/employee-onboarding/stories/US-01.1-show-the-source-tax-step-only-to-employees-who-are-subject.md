# US-01.1 – Show the source tax step only to employees who are subject to source tax

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Questionnaire flow overview](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow).

**As** HR, **I want** the source tax details (denomination, whether the spouse is gainfully employed, whether there are children) and the children step to be asked only of employees who are subject to source tax, and for Liechtenstein residents only when they stay abroad more than 45 non-return days, **so that** I no longer have to guess up front who must answer the source tax questions, and Lonio holds no source tax data about people who do not need it

Priority **P1** – HR names this as a current pain ('I never know up front who has to answer those questions'), and asking everyone breaks data minimisation (G-01). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, G-01, D-007, D-040, D-047, D-003

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow) and [information.md](../information.md)): Denomination, Spouse gainfully employed, Has biological or adopted children, Daily return to Liechtenstein, More than 45 work-related non-return days (Liechtenstein), Questionnaire flow

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.1.1 Source tax details only for employees subject to source tax**
  - **Given** an employee has answered the steps 'Home address', 'Nationality and C permit' and 'Civil status and spouse'
  - **When** Lonio decides whether to show step 6 'Source tax details'
  - **Then** the step is shown only if the employee is subject to source tax (anyone living abroad; or, living in Switzerland, a non-Swiss employee without a C permit who is not married or in a registered partnership with a Swiss national or C-permit holder living in Switzerland). Otherwise denomination, spouse gainfully employed and has children are not asked, and step 7 'Children' is not shown either.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, D-047, G-01_

- **AC-01.1.2 Spouse gainfully employed only for married employees and registered partners**
  - **Given** an employee is shown step 6 'Source tax details'
  - **When** the step is shown
  - **Then** the question whether the spouse or registered partner is gainfully employed is asked only if the civil status is Married or Registered partnership; Separated counts as single for source tax, so separated, single, divorced and widowed employees are not asked it (assumption D-003, to be verified by a domain expert).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, D-003, D-047_

  | Separated, living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | Separated | → | not asked | asked | ✅ |

- **AC-01.1.3 Liechtenstein residents: cross-border questions first, tariff questions only above 45 non-return days**
  - **Given** an employee lives in Liechtenstein (and is therefore subject to source tax)
  - **When** the employee goes through the questionnaire
  - **Then** step 5 'Cross-border commuting' (daily return, then more than 45 non-return days) comes before step 6 'Source tax details'. If the employee returns daily or expects at most 45 non-return days, there is in principle no source tax liability and steps 6 and 7 are skipped. If the employee expects more than 45 non-return days, steps 6 and 7 are asked as for any employee subject to source tax. Assumption (to be verified by a domain expert): this order and skipping follow D-040.
  - _Sources: D-040, D-007, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e96210, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f4ece9_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-007** (product decision (Product owner)): Liechtenstein residents are asked the source tax questions (HR's rule decides who is asked). The result follows the calculator - with daily return or at most 45 non-return days, Lonio shows 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR; with more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code. This closes OQ-liechtenstein-conflict. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-040** (product decision (Product owner)): For employees living in Liechtenstein, the questions on daily return and on more than 45 days abroad come before denomination, spouse and children. When the answers mean 'in principle no source tax liability', the tariff questions are skipped. When the employee stays abroad more than 45 days, the normal tariff is recorded with the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement' and flagged for HR. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
