# US-01.1 – Show the source tax step only to employees who are subject to source tax

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Questionnaire flow overview](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow).

**As** HR, **I want** the source tax details step (confession, spouse works, children, cross-border questions) to appear only for employees who are subject to source tax, **so that** I no longer have to work out up front who must answer the source tax questions, and employees who are not subject are not asked for personal data no party needs

Priority **P1** – HR's stated pain: 'I never know up front who has to answer those questions' (F-hr_transcript-b84498). Asking everyone would also break data minimisation (G-01). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-845204, G-01, D-001, D-003, D-005, D-006, D-007, D-008

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow) and [information.md](../information.md)): Country of residence (country of the home address), Nationality, Holds a C permit (permanent residence), Civil status, Spouse's / registered partner's nationality, Spouse / registered partner holds a C permit, Spouse's / registered partner's country of residence, Questionnaire flow overview, Subject to source tax

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.1.1 Employees subject to source tax get the source tax details step**
  - **Given** an employee who, from the answers in the earlier steps, is subject to source tax: living abroad (whatever the nationality), or living in Switzerland without Swiss citizenship, without a C permit and not married to / in a registered partnership with a Swiss citizen or C-permit holder who lives in Switzerland
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax details step
  - **Then** the employee is asked the confession and whether they have children (with the per-child questions for each child); married employees and registered partners are also asked whether their spouse or partner works. No income type is asked: a new employee always has earned income (D-006). The tariff code is computed by Lonio from these answers, not asked (D-001).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, D-001, D-003, D-006_

- **AC-01.1.2 Employees not subject to source tax skip the source tax details step**
  - **Given** an employee living in Switzerland who holds Swiss citizenship (also with another nationality), or holds a C permit, or is married to / in a registered partnership with a Swiss citizen or C-permit holder who lives in Switzerland
  - **When** the employee completes the source tax status step
  - **Then** the source tax details step is skipped entirely: no confession, children, spouse-works or cross-border questions are asked.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, D-003, D-008_

- **AC-01.1.3 Cross-border questions only for residents of Germany or Liechtenstein**
  - **Given** an employee who is subject to source tax
  - **When** the source tax details step reaches the cross-border questions (after the children questions)
  - **Then** employees living in Germany are asked the German cross-border questions (daily return, then certificate of residence, reasonable return or more than 60 non-return days as they apply); employees living in Liechtenstein are asked the Liechtenstein questions (daily return, then more than 45 non-return days); employees living in Switzerland or any other country get no cross-border questions. Liechtenstein residents are asked these questions even though their result may be 'in principle no source tax liability' (D-007).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, D-001, D-007, D-035_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Civil status | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | → | Returns daily to the home in Germany | Returns daily to the home in Liechtenstein | Subject to source tax | Checked |

  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | Single | – | – | → | asked | not asked | – | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Single | – | – | → | not asked | asked | yes | ✅ |
  | Employee living in another country (illustrative) | France | Single | – | – | → | not asked | not asked | yes | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss employee without C permit living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Single | Italy | no | → | not asked | not asked | – | ✅ |

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-005** (product decision (Product owner)): Do not ask for the permit type. Ask non-Swiss employees only whether they hold a C permit (permanent residence), yes or no, and the same for a non-Swiss spouse. No party needs any other permit type. This closes OQ-permit-list. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-006** (product decision (Product owner)): A new employee always has earned income. Do not ask the income type, and drop the replacement-income questions and the tariff G / Q cases. This closes OQ-income-type. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-007** (product decision (Product owner)): Liechtenstein residents are asked the source tax questions (HR's rule decides who is asked). The result follows the calculator - with daily return or at most 45 non-return days, Lonio shows 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR; with more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code. This closes OQ-liechtenstein-conflict. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-008** (product decision (Product owner)): Anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss for source tax, even with another nationality. This closes OQ-dual-nationality. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-035** (product decision (Product owner)): For the cross-border questions the employee states what they expect for the year (non-return days). For the certificate of residence 'not yet' is a valid answer and counts as 'no' for the tariff code. Documents go to HR outside Lonio; no upload.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party.
