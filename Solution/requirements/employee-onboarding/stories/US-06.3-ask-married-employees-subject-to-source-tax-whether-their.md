# US-06.3 – Ask married employees subject to source tax whether their spouse works

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Source tax tariff: income, denomination and spouse employment](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** HR, **I want** married employees who are subject to source tax and have earned income to state whether their spouse is gainfully employed or receives replacement income, **so that** I can answer the calculator's spouse question without an email follow-up, and single, divorced or widowed employees are not asked about a spouse

Priority **P1** – HR names the spouse details as a manual email follow-up that 'takes forever'; without this answer the married tariff (B or C) cannot be determined. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-6a0a40, F-hr_transcript-a45422, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7b637b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e740d1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-351289

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-income-type](../open-questions.md#oq-income-type)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics) and [information.md](../information.md)): Spouse works or receives replacement income

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.3.1 Spouse employment is asked only of married employees subject to source tax with earned income**
  - **Given** the employee's subject_to_source_tax result, type of income and civil status are known
  - **When** the employee reaches the spouse employment question in the source tax step (after the denomination question)
  - **Then** the question is asked and must be answered only if the employee is subject to source tax, has earned income and has civil status "Married"; otherwise it is not asked.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-351289, F-hr_transcript-6a0a40, G-01_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | Permit type | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | Spouse's country of residence | Spouse's permit type | Type of income (source tax) | → | Spouse works or receives replacement income | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9): married but not subject | Switzerland | Brazil | B permit | Married | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Married, subject, earned income (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Married | Germany | – | B permit | Earned income | → | required | ✅ |
  | Single, subject, earned income (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Single | – | – | – | Earned income | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Married, subject, replacement income (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Married | Germany | – | B permit | Replacement income | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.3.2 Allowed answers for spouse employment**
  - **Given** the spouse employment question is asked
  - **When** the employee answers it
  - **Then** the employee answers "yes" if the spouse is gainfully employed or receives replacement income, otherwise "no"; the answer is used for the source tax tariff determination.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7b637b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e740d1, G-10_
_Trace check: partial – The non-illustrative Tomás example (not subject, so not asked) has no cited backing for the not-subject conclusion, and the 'after the denomination question' position is not stated in the cited facts._
