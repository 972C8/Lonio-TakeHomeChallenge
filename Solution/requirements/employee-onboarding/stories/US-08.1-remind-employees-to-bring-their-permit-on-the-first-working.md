# US-08.1 – Remind employees to bring their permit on the first working day

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Completing and submitting the questionnaire](../feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission).

**As** HR, **I want** the questionnaire to remind every non-Swiss employee and every employee living abroad, at the end, to bring their permit on their first working day, **so that** fewer new employees forget their permit on the first day (today about half of them forget), without collecting any additional data

Priority **P2** – HR names a recurring pain ('Half of them forget'), but a forgotten permit does not block any registration; the reminder is a convenience that collects no data. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-c53552, F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, G-01, D-010, D-038

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission) and [information.md](../information.md)): Permit reminder

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.1.1 Who sees the permit reminder**
  - **Given** an employee has answered the country of their home address and, if they live in Switzerland, whether they hold Swiss citizenship
  - **When** the employee reaches the end of the questionnaire, before submitting it
  - **Then** the permit reminder is shown if the employee lives outside Switzerland (whatever their nationality, which is not asked of them) or lives in Switzerland without Swiss citizenship; it is not shown to a Swiss citizen living in Switzerland
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-c53552, D-010, D-038, D-008_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | → | Permit reminder | Swiss citizenship | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás - Brazilian, B permit, lives in Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | → | yes | – | ✅ |
  | Stefan - Swiss citizen living abroad (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-1a20bb); country of residence assumed Germany (illustrative) | Germany | – | → | yes | not asked | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Swiss | → | no | – | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | – | → | yes | – | ✅ |

- **AC-08.1.2 The reminder collects no data and is worded by HR**
  - **Given** the permit reminder is shown to an employee
  - **When** the employee reads it and continues to submit the questionnaire
  - **Then** the reminder asks for no answer, confirmation or document and records nothing; its purpose is to make the employee bring their permit on the first working day; HR provides the wording, which makes clear to a Swiss citizen living abroad that it does not apply to them
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-c53552, G-01, D-010, D-038, D-035_
- **AC-08.1.3 No extra question only for the reminder**
  - **Given** an employee lives outside Switzerland
  - **When** the questionnaire decides whether to show the permit reminder
  - **Then** the employee is not asked about their citizenship for the reminder's sake; the reminder is shown to them anyway
  - _Sources: G-01, D-038_

## Evidence
- **D-008** (product decision (Product owner)): Anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss for source tax, even with another nationality. This closes OQ-dual-nationality. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-010** (product decision (Product owner)): At the end of the questionnaire, remind every non-Swiss employee and every employee living abroad to bring their permit on the first working day. HR words the reminder; the spec states only its purpose. This closes OQ-permit-reminder.
- **D-035** (product decision (Product owner)): For the cross-border questions the employee states what they expect for the year (non-return days). For the certificate of residence 'not yet' is a valid answer and counts as 'no' for the tariff code. Documents go to HR outside Lonio; no upload.
- **D-038** (product decision (Product owner)): Employees living abroad get the permit reminder whatever their nationality, because nationality is not asked of them (data minimisation, G-01). HR words the reminder so that a Swiss citizen living abroad, such as Stefan, can see that it does not apply to them. Asking everyone 'Swiss citizen yes/no' only for the reminder is not worth the extra question.
- **F-hr_transcript-1a20bb** (hr_transcript): Employees who work in Switzerland but live abroad (e.g. Konstanz in Germany, Liechtenstein) are always subject to source tax, regardless of nationality – even Swiss citizens.
  - "Those people do source tax because they work here and they don't live here. And there it doesn't matter what passport you have, by the way." — hr_transcript/T-048, Anna
  - "Stefan is actually Swiss, and he still has it." — hr_transcript/T-048, Anna
  - "So someone living abroad and working here always does it?" — hr_transcript/T-049, Leo
  - "Yes." — hr_transcript/T-050, Anna
- **F-hr_transcript-c53552** (hr_transcript): HR would love the form to remind people to bring their permit on the first day; not decided (wording to be looked at later).
  - "What I would love is if the form could remind people to bring their permit. Half of them forget." — hr_transcript/T-042, Anna
  - "We can look at the wording later." — hr_transcript/T-043, Leo
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
