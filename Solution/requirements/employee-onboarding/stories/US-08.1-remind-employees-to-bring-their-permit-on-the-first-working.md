# US-08.1 – Remind employees to bring their permit on the first working day

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Completing and submitting the questionnaire](../feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission).

**As** HR, **I want** every non-Swiss employee and every employee living abroad to be reminded at the end of the questionnaire to bring their permit on the first working day, **so that** fewer new employees forget their permit on day one (today about half of them do), without Lonio collecting any extra data

Priority **P2** – HR states a recurring pain ("Half of them forget") but it does not block any registration; the reminder collects no data and is a convenience for HR. · Sources: F-hr_transcript-c53552, D-010, G-01

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-completion-and-submission) and [information.md](../information.md)): Permit reminder

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.1.1 Who gets the permit reminder**
  - **Given** an employee has reached the end of the questionnaire
  - **When** the employee lives abroad (home address country is not Switzerland), or lives in Switzerland and is not Swiss
  - **Then** the employee is reminded to bring their permit on the first working day; an employee living in Switzerland who holds Swiss citizenship (even with another nationality) gets no reminder
  - _Sources: D-010, F-hr_transcript-c53552, D-008_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Nationality | → | Permit reminder | Nationality | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Swiss citizen living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | no | – | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | → | yes | – | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (nationality not asked) (illustrative) | Germany | – | → | yes | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | – | → | yes | – | ✅ |

- **AC-08.1.2 The reminder collects no data**
  - **Given** the permit reminder is shown to an employee
  - **When** the employee completes the questionnaire
  - **Then** the employee does not have to answer, confirm or upload anything in response to the reminder; the permit is brought to HR in person on the first working day, outside Lonio
  - _Sources: D-010, G-01_
- **AC-08.1.3 HR provides the wording**
  - **Given** the permit reminder is shown
  - **When** its text is defined
  - **Then** the reminder uses the wording HR provides; the purpose stays the same: to remind the employee to bring their permit on the first working day
  - _Sources: D-010, F-hr_transcript-c53552_
_Trace check: partial – AC-08.1.2 claims the permit is handed to HR in person outside Lonio and that nothing must be answered, confirmed or uploaded, but D-010 and the HR quotes only say to remind people to bring the permit; D-008 is also scoped to source tax, not the reminder._


## Evidence
- **D-008** (product decision (Product owner)): Anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss for source tax, even with another nationality. This closes OQ-dual-nationality. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-010** (product decision (Product owner)): At the end of the questionnaire, remind every non-Swiss employee and every employee living abroad to bring their permit on the first working day. HR words the reminder; the spec states only its purpose. This closes OQ-permit-reminder.
- **F-hr_transcript-c53552** (hr_transcript): HR would love the form to remind people to bring their permit on the first day; not decided (wording to be looked at later).
  - "What I would love is if the form could remind people to bring their permit. Half of them forget." — hr_transcript/T-042, Anna
  - "We can look at the wording later." — hr_transcript/T-043, Leo
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
