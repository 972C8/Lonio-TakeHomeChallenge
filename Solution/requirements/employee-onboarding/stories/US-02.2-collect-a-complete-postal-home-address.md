# US-02.2 – Collect a complete postal home address

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** HR, **I want** every new employee to give their complete home address - street, house number, postcode, town and country - as it applies on their first working day, in step 2 'Home address', **so that** HR can post documents to the employee without chasing a missing part of the address

Priority **P2** – HR posts many documents and states the address 'has to be complete'; an incomplete address hampers HR's correspondence but does not block a registration. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-3beee8, F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, F-hr_transcript-66fe15, F-hr_transcript-3edf89, D-041, D-042, D-047, D-049

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Street, House number, Postcode, Town, Country of residence (home address country)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.2.1 All address fields are required for every employee**
  - **Given** a new employee reaches step 2 'Home address' (the second of the eight fixed steps, D-047)
  - **When** they enter their home address
  - **Then** street, house number, postcode, town and country are required for every employee, wherever they live; the step cannot be completed while one of them is missing. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-049).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, F-hr_transcript-3beee8, D-047, D-049_

  | Example | Street | House number | Postcode | Town | Country of residence (home address country) | → | Street | House number | Postcode | Town | Country of residence (home address country) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Address in Switzerland (illustrative) | Bahnhofstrasse | 12 | 8001 | Zürich | Switzerland | → | required | required | required | required | required | ✅ |
  | Address in Germany (cross-border commuter) (illustrative) | Marktstätte | 5 | 78462 | Konstanz | Germany | → | required | required | required | required | required | ✅ |

- **AC-02.2.2 Addresses without a house number**
  - **Given** the employee's address has no house number
  - **When** they fill in the house number
  - **Then** they can explicitly state 'no house number' instead of a number, and this counts as a complete answer; leaving the house number simply empty is not accepted. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-042).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, D-042, D-049_

  | Example | Street | House number | Postcode | Town | Country of residence (home address country) | → | House number | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Farmhouse without house number (illustrative) | Hof Unterberg | no house number | 8492 | Wila | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-02.2.3 The address that applies on the first working day**
  - **Given** an employee will move between filling in the questionnaire and their first working day
  - **When** they enter their home address
  - **Then** the questionnaire asks for the address where they will live on their first working day, and that address is recorded, not the current one. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-041).
  - _Sources: D-041_
- **AC-02.2.4 A normal postal address only, recorded for HR**
  - **Given** the employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** the address is recorded
  - **Then** it is recorded for HR as a normal postal address, the way it is written on an envelope; nothing beyond street, house number, postcode, town and country is asked.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-66fe15, F-hr_transcript-3beee8, G-01, D-034_

- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. - **D-041** (product decision (Product owner)): The residence country and address asked are those that apply on the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-042** (product decision (Product owner)): The house number is required, as HR wants the address complete including the house number; an explicit 'no house number' option covers addresses without one. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-049** (product decision (Product owner)): Every home address field (street, house number, postcode, town, country) is required; the only exception is the explicit 'no house number' option. Examples and acceptance criteria use the term 'required' for these fields, never 'asked'. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
