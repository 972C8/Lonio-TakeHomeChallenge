# US-02.2 – Collect a complete postal home address

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** HR, **I want** every new employee to give their complete home address (street, house number, postcode, town and country) as it applies on their first working day, **so that** HR can post the many documents it sends to employees to an address that is complete and correct from the start of employment

Priority **P1** – HR posts many documents to employees and states that the address 'has to be complete' (F-hr_transcript-3beee8, F-hr_transcript-c0fe95). An incomplete address means documents cannot be delivered. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-3beee8, F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, F-hr_transcript-66fe15, D-041, D-042, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Street, House number, Postcode, Town, Country (of residence)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.2.1 The full postal address is asked of every employee**
  - **Given** a new employee fills in the onboarding questionnaire
  - **When** they reach the home address
  - **Then** they are asked for street, house number, postcode, town and country, written as a normal postal address on an envelope and nothing more. Street, postcode, town and country are required. The address is recorded for HR so it can post documents.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, F-hr_transcript-3beee8, F-hr_transcript-66fe15_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | → | Street | House number | Postcode | Town | Country (of residence) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | required | required | required | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | asked | asked | asked | asked | asked | ✅ |

- **AC-02.2.2 House number is required, with an explicit 'no house number' answer**
  - **Given** a new employee enters their home address
  - **When** the address has a house number, or the address has none
  - **Then** the employee must either enter the house number or state explicitly 'no house number'. The house number cannot simply be left blank. Assumption, to be verified by a domain expert: HR wants a complete address including the house number, and some addresses have none (D-042).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, D-042_
- **AC-02.2.3 The address asked is the one that applies on the first working day**
  - **Given** a new employee who is moving around the time they start, e.g. relocating to Switzerland for the new job
  - **When** they enter their home address
  - **Then** the questionnaire makes clear that the address to give is the one where they will live on their first working day, not an earlier one. Assumption, to be verified by a domain expert (D-041).
  - _Sources: D-041_
_Trace check: partial – AC-02.2.1 lists only street, postcode, town and country as required, leaving out the house number, which D-042 makes required and which its own Switzerland example marks required._
