# US-02.2 – Collect a complete postal home address

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** HR, **I want** every new employee to give a complete postal home address (street, house number, postcode, town and country) as it will be on their first working day, in step 2 'Home address', **so that** the many documents HR posts to employees reach them, without HR having to chase missing parts of the address

Priority **P1** – HR posts many documents to employees and states the address 'has to be complete'; an incomplete address stops HR's postal correspondence. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, F-hr_transcript-3beee8, F-hr_transcript-66fe15, D-041, D-042, D-047, D-049

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Street, House number, Postcode, Town

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.2.1 Street, postcode and town are required for every employee**
  - **Given** a new employee fills in step 2 'Home address', wherever they live
  - **When** they try to continue with the street, the postcode or the town empty
  - **Then** they cannot continue until street, postcode and town are filled in. The address is recorded for HR. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-049): every home address field is required; the only exception is the explicit 'no house number' option.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, F-hr_transcript-3beee8, D-049, D-047_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Street | Postcode | Town | House number | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | required | required | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | required | required | required | required | ✅ |

- **AC-02.2.2 House number is required, with an explicit 'no house number' option**
  - **Given** a new employee fills in step 2 'Home address'
  - **When** they try to continue without a house number
  - **Then** they can continue only if they have entered a house number or explicitly chosen 'no house number'; an empty house number alone is not accepted. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-042): HR wants the address complete including the number, and some addresses have no house number.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, D-042, D-049_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | House number | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | House number required for any employee (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-02.2.3 The address is the one that applies on the first working day**
  - **Given** a new employee will move before their first working day
  - **When** they fill in step 2 'Home address'
  - **Then** the question makes clear that the address asked is the one that applies on the first working day, so the employee enters their new address. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-041).
  - _Sources: D-041_
- **AC-02.2.4 Only a normal postal address is asked**
  - **Given** a new employee fills in step 2 'Home address'
  - **When** they look at the address questions
  - **Then** only what is needed to write the address on an envelope is asked: street, house number, postcode, town and country. Nothing else, such as a second address or a phone number, is asked.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-66fe15, F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, G-01_

## Evidence
- **D-041** (product decision (Product owner)): The residence country and address asked are those that apply on the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-042** (product decision (Product owner)): The house number is required, as HR wants the address complete including the house number; an explicit 'no house number' option covers addresses without one. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-049** (product decision (Product owner)): Every home address field (street, house number, postcode, town, country) is required; the only exception is the explicit 'no house number' option. Examples and acceptance criteria use the term 'required' for these fields, never 'asked'. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.

A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
