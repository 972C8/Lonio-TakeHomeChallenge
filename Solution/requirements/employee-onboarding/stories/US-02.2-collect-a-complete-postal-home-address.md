# US-02.2 – Collect a complete postal home address

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** HR, **I want** every new employee to give their full home address: street, house number, postcode, town and country, **so that** HR can post documents to the employee without chasing missing address parts

Priority **P1** – HR posts a lot of documents to employees and states that the address 'has to be complete'. An incomplete address blocks posting. · Sources: F-hr_transcript-3beee8, F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, F-hr_transcript-66fe15, G-01, G-09, G-10

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-canton-abroad](../open-questions.md#oq-canton-abroad)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Street, House number, Postcode, Town, Country of residence (country of the home address)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.2.1 All five parts of the home address are asked of every employee and required**
  - **Given** a new employee fills in the onboarding questionnaire, whether they live in Switzerland or abroad
  - **When** the employee reaches the home address
  - **Then** the questionnaire asks for the street, house number, postcode, town and country of the home address. All five are required, and the questionnaire cannot be submitted while any part is missing. The address is recorded for HR to post documents
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-3beee8, F-hr_transcript-c0fe95_
- **AC-02.2.2 The address is a normal postal address, nothing more**
  - **Given** a new employee enters their home address
  - **When** the questionnaire asks for the address
  - **Then** it asks only for what is written on an envelope (street, house number, postcode, town, country) and no further address details. Whether every address has a house number is not stated by any source, so the house number stays required as HR listed it
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-66fe15, F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, G-01_
- **AC-02.2.3 The country is named in English**
  - **Given** a new employee enters the country of their home address
  - **When** the employee chooses or states the country
  - **Then** the country is recorded by its English name, e.g. "Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein"
  - _Sources: G-09, G-10_

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
