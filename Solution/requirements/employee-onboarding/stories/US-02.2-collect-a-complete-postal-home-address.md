# US-02.2 – Collect a complete postal home address

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** HR, **I want** every new employee to give their complete home address (street, house number, postcode, town and country) in the Home address step, **so that** HR can post documents to the employee without chasing missing parts of the address

Priority **P1** – HR says it 'obviously' needs the address and that it has to be complete because it posts many documents to employees. An incomplete address blocks HR's postal onboarding. · Sources: F-hr_transcript-3beee8, F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, F-hr_transcript-66fe15, G-10

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-canton](../open-questions.md#oq-canton)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Street, House number, Postcode, Town, Country (of the home address)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.2.1 All five address parts are always asked and required**
  - **Given** any new employee fills in the onboarding questionnaire
  - **When** they reach the Home address step, which follows the Personal details step
  - **Then** they are asked for street, house number, postcode, town and country of their home address. None of these may be left empty, whichever country they live in. The address goes to HR for posting documents.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-3beee8, F-hr_transcript-c0fe95_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | → | Street | House number | Postcode | Town | Country (of the home address) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | required | required | required | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (e.g. Konstanz) (illustrative) | Germany | → | required | required | required | required | required | ✅ |

- **AC-02.2.2 The address is a normal postal address**
  - **Given** the employee gives their home address
  - **When** they fill in the address parts
  - **Then** they write it as a normal postal address, the way it would appear on an envelope. Nothing beyond street, house number, postcode, town and country is asked. The country is given by its English name, e.g. "Switzerland", "Germany" or "Liechtenstein".
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-66fe15, F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, G-10, G-01_

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-10** (product owner guidance): Business wording for values (used in allowed values and examples): countries by English name ("Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein"), permits as "B permit", "C permit", civil status exactly as the pension fund's list shows it ("Single", "Married", ...), dates as DD.MM.YYYY, yes/no as "yes"/"no".
