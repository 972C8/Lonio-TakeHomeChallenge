# US-05.1 – Ask nationality and C permit only of employees living in Switzerland

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Who is subject to source tax](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability).

**As** new employee, **I want** to be asked my nationality and, if I am not Swiss, only whether I hold a C permit, and only when I live in Switzerland, **so that** Lonio can tell whether I am subject to source tax without collecting citizenship or permit details that no receiving party needs for me

Priority **P1** – Nationality and the C permit decide who must answer the source tax questions; HR's rule needed several corrections and today HR 'never knows up front' who has to answer (F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-35fae3, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-c9d9b9, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01, G-10, D-005, D-008, D-038

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability) and [information.md](../information.md)): Nationality, Holds a C permit (permanent residence)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.1.1 Nationality is asked only of employees living in Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the country of their home address (their country of residence)
  - **When** the country of residence is Switzerland
  - **Then** the employee is asked their nationality (country named in English) and must answer it; employees living in any other country are not asked their nationality, because for them it does not change the source tax outcome. The answer is recorded for source tax (liability) and for HR (whether the permit reminder is shown).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-35fae3, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01, G-10, D-038_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Nationality | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Maria (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |
  | Stefan, Swiss living abroad (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a), country assumed (illustrative) | Germany | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-05.1.2 Anyone with Swiss citizenship answers Switzerland**
  - **Given** an employee living in Switzerland holds Swiss citizenship, possibly together with another nationality
  - **When** they answer the nationality question
  - **Then** they are told to answer 'Switzerland'; they then count as Swiss and are not asked about a C permit.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-35fae3, D-008_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | → | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Swiss-Italian dual national living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-05.1.3 Only 'C permit yes/no' is asked of non-Swiss employees living in Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland
  - **When** their nationality is not Switzerland
  - **Then** they are asked whether they hold a C permit (permanent residence), answered 'yes' or 'no', and must answer it; the permit type itself is never asked. Swiss citizens and employees living abroad are not asked. The answer is recorded for source tax (C-permit holders are treated like Swiss citizens).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-c9d9b9, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01, G-10, D-005_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | → | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Checked |

  | Maria, Portuguese (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | Portugal | → | required | ✅ |
  | Tomás, Brazilian (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | → | asked | ✅ |

## Evidence
- **D-005** (product decision (Product owner)): Do not ask for the permit type. Ask non-Swiss employees only whether they hold a C permit (permanent residence), yes or no, and the same for a non-Swiss spouse. No party needs any other permit type. This closes OQ-permit-list. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-008** (product decision (Product owner)): Anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss for source tax, even with another nationality. This closes OQ-dual-nationality. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-038** (product decision (Product owner)): Employees living abroad get the permit reminder whatever their nationality, because nationality is not asked of them (data minimisation, G-01). HR words the reminder so that a Swiss citizen living abroad, such as Stefan, can see that it does not apply to them. Asking everyone 'Swiss citizen yes/no' only for the reminder is not worth the extra question.
- **F-hr_transcript-35fae3** (hr_transcript): The employee's nationality is needed to decide whether they are subject to source tax.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-10** (product owner guidance): Business wording for values (used in allowed values and examples): countries by English name ("Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein"), permits as "B permit", "C permit", civil status exactly as the pension fund's list shows it ("Single", "Married", ...), dates as DD.MM.YYYY, yes/no as "yes"/"no".
