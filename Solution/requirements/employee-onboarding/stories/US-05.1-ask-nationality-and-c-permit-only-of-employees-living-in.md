# US-05.1 – Ask nationality and C permit only of employees living in Switzerland

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Who is subject to source tax](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to ask an employee who lives in Switzerland for their nationality and, if they are not Swiss, only whether they hold a C permit, and to ask neither of an employee living abroad, **so that** I know whether a Swiss resident can be subject to source tax without collecting nationality or permit data that no party needs

Priority **P1** – HR today never knows up front who has to answer the source tax questions; without these answers liability cannot be decided and the tariff code cannot be computed (F-hr_transcript-b84498, D-001). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-35fae3, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-c9d9b9, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, D-005, D-008, D-010, D-038, D-041, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability) and [information.md](../information.md)): Nationality, Holds a C permit

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.1.1 Nationality is asked only of employees living in Switzerland**
  - **Given** an employee has given the country of their home address that applies on the first working day
  - **When** the country is Switzerland
  - **Then** Lonio asks for the employee's nationality and an answer is required. Anyone who holds Swiss citizenship answers Switzerland, even if they also hold another nationality (decision D-008, an assumption to be verified by a domain expert). When the country is any other country, nationality is not asked, because an employee living abroad is subject to source tax whatever their nationality.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-35fae3, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01, D-008, D-038, D-041_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | → | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Maria (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | → | required | – | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen living abroad, like Stefan (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) (illustrative) | Germany | → | not asked | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | – | ✅ |

- **AC-05.1.2 Only 'C permit yes or no' is asked, and only of non-Swiss residents**
  - **Given** an employee lives in Switzerland and has given their nationality
  - **When** the nationality is not Switzerland
  - **Then** Lonio asks whether the employee holds a C permit (permanent residence), with the answers yes or no, and an answer is required. No other permit type is asked. When the nationality is Switzerland, the C permit question is not asked.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-c9d9b9, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, D-005, G-01_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Nationality | → | Holds a C permit | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Maria (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | Portugal | → | required | ✅ |
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Swiss employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-05.1.3 Who receives nationality and C permit**
  - **Given** an employee living in Switzerland has answered the nationality and, where asked, the C permit question
  - **When** the questionnaire is submitted
  - **Then** The answers are recorded for the source tax side, to decide whether the employee is subject to source tax, and the nationality is also used to decide whether the employee gets the reminder to bring their permit on the first working day. Employees living abroad are not asked their nationality; they get the permit reminder whatever their nationality (decision D-038).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, D-005, D-010, D-034, D-038_

## Evidence
- **D-005** (product decision (Product owner)): Do not ask for the permit type. Ask non-Swiss employees only whether they hold a C permit (permanent residence), yes or no, and the same for a non-Swiss spouse. No party needs any other permit type. This closes OQ-permit-list. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-008** (product decision (Product owner)): Anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss for source tax, even with another nationality. This closes OQ-dual-nationality. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-010** (product decision (Product owner)): At the end of the questionnaire, remind every non-Swiss employee and every employee living abroad to bring their permit on the first working day. HR words the reminder; the spec states only its purpose. This closes OQ-permit-reminder.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-038** (product decision (Product owner)): Employees living abroad get the permit reminder whatever their nationality, because nationality is not asked of them (data minimisation, G-01). HR words the reminder so that a Swiss citizen living abroad, such as Stefan, can see that it does not apply to them. Asking everyone 'Swiss citizen yes/no' only for the reminder is not worth the extra question.
