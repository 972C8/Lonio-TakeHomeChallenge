# US-05.7 – Compute and record the source tax result for HR

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Source tax tariff basics and computed result](../feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to compute the full Zurich source tax result for every employee subject to source tax and record it for me. That is the tariff code (letter, child digit, church-tax suffix), or for Liechtenstein residents who qualify, the statement that there is in principle no source tax liability, flagged for me., **so that** I no longer sit with the employee's answers and click through the calculator, and I always have the result that fits the latest answers

Priority **P1** – This replaces HR's manual calculator step for every employee subject to source tax (F-hr_transcript-563a9f, F-hr_transcript-4e180e), which is the product choice of D-001. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-563a9f, F-hr_transcript-4e180e, F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ecef37, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c78e0c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7154e, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e80d8f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab4e1e, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-86d588, D-001, D-007, D-009, D-034, D-037

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.7.1 No tariff code for employees not subject to source tax**
  - **Given** an employee who is not subject to source tax
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** Lonio records that the employee is not subject to source tax. No tariff code is computed and no source tax question has been asked.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, D-001_

- **AC-05.7.3 Liechtenstein residents: in principle no source tax liability, or the normal tariff code**
  - **Given** an employee living in Liechtenstein, who is therefore subject to source tax and answers the source tax and cross-border questions (D-007)
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** if the employee returns daily, or expects at most 45 work-related non-return days, Lonio records 'In principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, and flags it for HR. How HR handles the flag belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). If the employee expects more than 45 non-return days, Lonio records the normal tariff code.

- **AC-05.7.4 The result always follows the latest answers**
  - **Given** an employee whose answers that bear on source tax are changed later. Before submission the employee changes them. After submission HR changes them, for example after a move, a marriage, a new child or a certificate of residence handed in later.
  - **When** the source tax result is looked at
  - **Then** the result (not subject, the Liechtenstein statement, or the tariff code) is derived again from the latest answers. A result that no longer matches the answers is never kept. HR's changes after submission belong to the later HR-side spec.
  - _Sources: D-037, D-034, D-001_
- **AC-05.7.5 Unexplored calculator paths are verified before go-live**
  - **Given** Lonio's computed tariff code for paths that were not explored in the calculator: the residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N, and child results marked inferred
  - **When** the feature is prepared for release
  - **Then** a domain expert compares Lonio's computed codes with the Zurich calculator for these paths, and the release waits until they match (go-live condition, see OQ-calculator-coverage). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are not computed, because they are out of scope for onboarding.
  - _Sources: D-009, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-86d588_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-007** (product decision (Product owner)): Liechtenstein residents are asked the source tax questions (HR's rule decides who is asked). The result follows the calculator - with daily return or at most 45 non-return days, Lonio shows 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR; with more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code. This closes OQ-liechtenstein-conflict. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are out of scope for onboarding. Keep OQ-calculator-coverage open as a non-blocking go-live condition.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
