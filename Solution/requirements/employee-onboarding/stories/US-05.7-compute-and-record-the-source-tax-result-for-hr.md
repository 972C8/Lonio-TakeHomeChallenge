# US-05.7 – Compute and record the source tax result for HR

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Source tax tariff basics and computed result](../feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to compute the full Zurich source tax result for every employee subject to source tax - the tariff code made of tariff letter, child digit and church-tax suffix (e.g. C1Y, L0Y), or for Liechtenstein residents the 'in principle no source tax liability' result - and record it for HR, **so that** I get the correct tariff code without clicking through the calculator myself, and I am alerted to the Liechtenstein cases that need attention

Priority **P1** – This is the outcome of the product decision to replace HR's manual calculator step (D-001, F-hr_transcript-563a9f); HR uses the Zurich calculator for every employee today (F-hr_transcript-4e180e). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ecef37, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e80d8f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab4e1e, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7154e, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c78e0c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-332e2a, F-hr_transcript-4e180e, F-hr_transcript-563a9f, F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, D-001, D-007, D-009, D-034, D-037

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.7.1 Tariff code is composed of letter, child digit and church-tax suffix**
  - **Given** an employee is subject to source tax and does not fall under the Liechtenstein 'in principle no source tax liability' case
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** Lonio records for HR a tariff code made of the tariff letter, followed by the number of children that count for the tariff (0 if none), followed by the church-tax suffix Y or N - the same code the Zurich calculator gives for these answers
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ecef37, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7154e, D-001, D-034_

  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator example L0Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7154e) | Germany | Single | Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed | no | yes | yes | – | – | – | – | – | – | → | L | 0 | Y | tariff code | ✅ |
  | Calculator example M0N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7154e) | Germany | Married | None or other | no | yes | yes | no | – | – | – | – | – | → | M | 0 | N | tariff code | ✅ |
  | Calculator example N1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7154e) | Germany | Married | Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed | yes | yes | yes | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | – | → | N | 1 | Y | tariff code | ✅ |

- **AC-05.7.2 No tariff code for employees not subject to source tax**
  - **Given** an employee is not subject to source tax (Swiss citizen or C-permit holder living in Switzerland, or exempted by a Swiss or C-permit spouse living in Switzerland)
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** Lonio records that the employee is not subject to source tax and computes no tariff code
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-hr_transcript-d7431e, D-001_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's Swiss citizenship | Spouse lives in Switzerland | → | Source tax result | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás - Brazilian, B permit, Swiss wife, living in Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Married | Swiss | yes | → | not subject to source tax | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Swiss | – | Single | – | – | → | not subject to source tax | ✅ |

- **AC-05.7.3 Liechtenstein result follows the calculator**
  - **Given** an employee lives in Liechtenstein (and is therefore asked the source tax questions)
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** with daily return to Liechtenstein, or with not more than 45 expected non-return days, Lonio records 'In principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code and flags the case for HR; with more than 45 non-return days it records the normal tariff code, marked 'subject to the double taxation agreement' as the calculator does. How HR sees the flag belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034)

  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

  | Liechtenstein, no daily return, not more than 45 non-return days (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Single | no | no | – | – | – | – | – | – | → | in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement | – | – | – | ✅ |
  | Calculator example C1Y subject to the double taxation agreement: Liechtenstein, more than 45 non-return days (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab4e1e) | Liechtenstein | Married | no | yes | yes | Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | tariff code | C | 1 | Y | ✅ |

- **AC-05.7.4 Result is always derived from the latest answers**
  - **Given** a source tax result has been recorded for an employee
  - **When** any answer it depends on changes (before submission by the employee, after submission by HR, e.g. a certificate of residence handed in later)
  - **Then** the result is derived again from the latest answers and only the current result counts; the employee cannot change answers after submission (D-037). Before go-live a domain expert compares the computed codes with the calculator for the paths not explored (see OQ-calculator-coverage)
  - _Sources: D-001, D-009, D-037_
- **AC-05.7.5 Tariff code cannot be computed yet for undetermined child situations**
  - **Given** an employee is subject to source tax, does not fall under the Liechtenstein 'in principle no source tax liability' case, and at least one child's answers form a combination whose outcome the calculator exploration did not show
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** Lonio records that the tariff code cannot be computed yet instead of a possibly wrong code, so the case stays visible for HR; the outcome of these combinations is to be verified by a domain expert (see OQ-child-paths-undetermined and OQ-calculator-coverage)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-332e2a, D-001, D-009_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Civil status | Spouse / partner works or receives replacement income | Denomination | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody | Child: common household with the other parent | Child: alternating care | Child: larger financial contributor | Daily return to the home in Germany | Certificate of residence | → | Source tax result | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, minor child in the household, shared custody, alternating care, employee is the larger contributor (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | None or other | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | no | yes | The employee | yes | yes | → | not determined | ✅ |

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-007** (product decision (Product owner)): Liechtenstein residents are asked the source tax questions (HR's rule decides who is asked). The result follows the calculator - with daily return or at most 45 non-return days, Lonio shows 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR; with more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code. This closes OQ-liechtenstein-conflict. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are out of scope for onboarding. Keep OQ-calculator-coverage open as a non-blocking go-live condition.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'.
