# US-06.4 – Derive the base source tax tariff letter

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Source tax tariff: income, denomination and spouse employment](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** HR, **I want** the base tariff letter (A, B, C, G or H) to be derived from the employee's type of income, civil status, spouse employment and counted children, **so that** I know the expected tariff before using the Zurich calculator, which stays the authority for the final tariff

Priority **P1** – The tariff is the output source tax needs; today HR works it out by hand in the calculator for everybody. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2d4dda, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c53e3, F-hr_transcript-34494f

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.4.1 Replacement income gives tariff G**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax answered "Replacement income"
  - **When** the base tariff letter is derived
  - **Then** the letter is G, whatever the civil status.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2d4dda_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | Permit type | Civil status | Type of income (source tax) | → | Base tariff letter (source tax) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Replacement income (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Single | Replacement income | → | G | ✅ |

- **AC-06.4.2 Married employees get C with a working spouse and B otherwise**
  - **Given** a married employee subject to source tax with earned income answered whether the spouse works (see the spouse employment question)
  - **When** the base tariff letter is derived
  - **Then** the letter is C if the spouse works or receives replacement income ("yes"), and B if not ("no").
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | Permit type | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | Spouse's permit type | Type of income (source tax) | Spouse works or receives replacement income | → | Base tariff letter (source tax) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Spouse works (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Married | Germany | B permit | Earned income | yes | → | C | ✅ |
  | Spouse does not work (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Married | Germany | B permit | Earned income | no | → | B | ✅ |

- **AC-06.4.3 Not married employees get A, or H when a child is counted with H**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with earned income is Single, Divorced or Widowed and has answered the children questions
  - **When** the base tariff letter is derived
  - **Then** the letter is H if at least one child lives in the household and the employee has sole custody or is the main financial contributor to that child; otherwise it is A.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | Permit type | Civil status | Type of income (source tax) | Religious denomination (church tax) | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child lives in the same household | Parental custody (child in household) | → | Base tariff letter (source tax) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single without children (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Single | Earned income | None or other | no | – | – | – | → | A | ✅ |
  | Divorced with a minor child in the household under sole custody (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Divorced | Earned income | Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | H | ✅ |
  | Widowed with an adult child in first education who lives elsewhere (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Widowed | Earned income | None or other | yes | Adult in first education | no | – | → | A | ✅ |

- **AC-06.4.4 The calculator stays the authority for the final tariff**
  - **Given** a base tariff letter has been derived
  - **When** HR determines the final tariff
  - **Then** the derived letter is an expectation only: the final tariff, including the number of counted children (tariff digit) and the cross-border replacement for employees living in Germany (A→L, B→M, C→N, H→P, G→Q, decided by the residence questions of another section), comes from the Zurich calculator, which HR uses for everybody.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-34494f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c53e3_
