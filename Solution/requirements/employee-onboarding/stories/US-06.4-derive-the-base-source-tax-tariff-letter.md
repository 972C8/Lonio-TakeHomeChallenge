# US-06.4 – Derive the base source tax tariff letter

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to derive the base tariff letter (A, B, C or H) from civil status, spouse employment and the children's results, following the calculator, **so that** I no longer have to click through the Zurich calculator to find the tariff

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.4.1 Married employees and registered partners get C or B**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax is Married or in a Registered partnership
  - **When** Lonio derives the base tariff letter
  - **Then** the letter is C if the spouse or partner is gainfully employed or receives replacement income, and B if not. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-003): a registered partnership counts as married, as in the calculator.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003_

- **AC-06.4.2 Single, divorced, widowed and separated employees get H or A**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax is Single, Divorced, Widowed or Separated
  - **When** Lonio derives the base tariff letter
  - **Then** the letter is H if at least one child counts with H (from the per-child results of step 7), and A otherwise, including when the employee has no children. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-003): Separated counts as single, as in the calculator. - **AC-06.4.3 No replacement-income tariff**
  - **Given** any employee subject to source tax
  - **When** Lonio derives the base tariff letter
  - **Then** the letter is never G, because a new employee always has earned income and replacement income is out of scope (D-006). Residence in Switzerland or in another country without cross-border rules gives the base letter directly, as in the calculator.
  - _Sources: D-006, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Civil status | Denomination | Has biological or adopted children | → | Base tariff letter | Tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator case A0N, other country (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b) | France | Single | None or other | no | → | A | A | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-006** (product decision (Product owner)): A new employee always has earned income. Do not ask the income type, and drop the replacement-income questions and the tariff G / Q cases. This closes OQ-income-type. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are out of scope for onboarding. Keep OQ-calculator-coverage open as a non-blocking go-live condition.
