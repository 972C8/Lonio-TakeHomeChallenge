# US-06.4 – Derive the base source tax tariff letter

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to work out the base tariff letter (A, B, C or H) from the civil status, the spouse's employment and the children, following the calculator, **so that** I no longer have to click through the Zurich calculator to find the tariff for each employee subject to source tax

Priority **P1** – The base letter is the core of the tariff code that replaces HR's manual calculator step (D-001, F-hr_transcript-34494f). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-34494f, D-001, D-003, D-006

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.4.1 Married employees and registered partners get C or B**
  - **Given** an employee who needs a tariff code and is Married or in a Registered partnership (assumption D-003, to be verified by a domain expert: a registered partnership counts as married for source tax)
  - **When** Lonio derives the base tariff letter
  - **Then** The letter is C if the spouse or partner is gainfully employed or receives replacement income, and B if not. Children do not change the letter.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Civil status | Spouse gainfully employed | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | → | Base tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator C0Y case (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a) (illustrative) | France | Married | yes | no | – | – | – | → | C | ✅ |
  | Calculator B0Y case (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a) (illustrative) | France | Married | no | no | – | – | – | → | B | ✅ |
  | Registered partner whose partner works (illustrative) | France | Registered partnership | yes | no | – | – | – | → | C | ✅ |
  | Married, spouse not working, minor child in household with sole custody (illustrative) | France | Married | no | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | B | ✅ |

- **AC-06.4.2 Single, divorced, separated and widowed employees get H or A**
  - **Given** an employee who needs a tariff code and is Single, Divorced, Widowed or Separated (assumption D-003, to be verified by a domain expert: separated counts as single for source tax)
  - **When** Lonio derives the base tariff letter
  - **Then** The letter is H if at least one child is counted with H in step 7 'Children'. Otherwise it is A, including when the employee has no children or no child counts.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889, D-003_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | → | Base tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator A0Y case (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c) (illustrative) | Switzerland | Portugal | no | Single | no | – | – | – | → | A | ✅ |
  | Separated employee without children (illustrative) | France | – | – | Separated | no | – | – | – | → | A | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee without children (illustrative) | France | – | – | Widowed | no | – | – | – | → | A | ✅ |
  | Calculator H1Y case: single with minor child in household, sole custody (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72) (illustrative) | France | – | – | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | H | ✅ |
  | Divorced with a child who is neither minor nor in first education (illustrative) | France | – | – | Divorced | yes | Neither | – | – | → | A | ✅ |

- **AC-06.4.3 No tariff for replacement income**
  - **Given** any employee who needs a tariff code
  - **When** Lonio derives the base tariff letter
  - **Then** Lonio assumes earned income for every new employee. It does not ask the income type, and the base letter is never G.
  - _Sources: D-006_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-006** (product decision (Product owner)): A new employee always has earned income. Do not ask the income type, and drop the replacement-income questions and the tariff G / Q cases. This closes OQ-income-type. To be verified by a domain expert.
