# US-06.4 – Derive the base source tax tariff letter

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the base tariff letter (A, B, C or H) of every employee subject to source tax to be derived from civil status, spouse employment and the children, as the Zurich calculator does, **so that** the employee is taxed at the right base tariff without HR deciding the letter by hand

Priority **P1** – Today HR works out the tariff by hand with the calculator (F-hr_transcript-3cd6fa). Lonio now owns the result (D-001), so a wrong letter means a wrong tax deduction. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-3cd6fa, D-001, D-003, D-006, D-009, D-037

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics) and [information.md](../information.md)): Base tariff letter

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.4.1 Married employees and registered partners get C or B**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax whose civil status is Married or Registered partnership (the calculator's 'married or registered partnership' group, D-003)
  - **When** Lonio derives the base tariff letter
  - **Then** the letter is C if the spouse or partner works or receives replacement income, and B if not. Children never change this letter.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d75286, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | Spouse's / registered partner's nationality | Spouse / registered partner holds a C permit | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | → | Base tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator path C0Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Married | Italy | no | yes | no | – | – | – | → | C | ✅ |
  | Calculator path B0Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Married | Italy | no | no | no | – | – | – | → | B | ✅ |
  | Calculator path B1N, child with sole custody (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d75286) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Married | Italy | no | no | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | B | ✅ |
  | Registered partner whose partner works (illustrative) | Austria | – | – | Registered partnership | – | – | yes | no | – | – | – | → | C | ✅ |

- **AC-06.4.2 Single, divorced, separated and widowed employees get H or A**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax whose civil status is Single, Divorced, Widowed or Separated (the calculator's 'single, divorced, separated or widowed' group, D-003)
  - **When** Lonio derives the base tariff letter
  - **Then** the letter is H if at least one child counts with H (per-child result, rule child_result), otherwise A.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5686d7, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | Common household with the child's other parent | Child: who contributes most to maintenance | → | Base tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator path A0Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Single | no | – | – | – | – | – | → | A | ✅ |
  | Calculator path H1Y, sole custody (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | – | → | H | ✅ |
  | Calculator path A0N, other parent main contributor (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5686d7) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | yes | Other parent | → | A | ✅ |
  | Separated employee without children (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Separated | no | – | – | – | – | – | → | A | ✅ |

- **AC-06.4.3 Replacement-income and special tariffs never occur**
  - **Given** any employee subject to source tax
  - **When** Lonio derives the base tariff letter
  - **Then** the letter is always A, B, C or H. Tariffs G and Q (replacement income) and special tariffs such as D (second job) are never derived during onboarding.
  - _Sources: D-006, D-009_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-006** (product decision (Product owner)): A new employee always has earned income. Do not ask the income type, and drop the replacement-income questions and the tariff G / Q cases. This closes OQ-income-type. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are out of scope for onboarding. Keep OQ-calculator-coverage open as a non-blocking go-live condition.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
