# US-06.4 – Derive the base source tax tariff letter

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to derive the base tariff letter (A, B, C or H) from the employee's civil status, the spouse's employment and the children, **so that** I no longer have to click through the Zurich calculator to find the tariff letter

Priority **P1** – Today HR determines the tariff by hand in the calculator (F-hr_transcript-34494f); the product decision is that Lonio computes it (D-001). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, F-hr_transcript-34494f, D-001, D-003, D-006, D-009

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.4.1 Base letter for married employees and registered partners**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax whose civil status is Married or Registered partnership
  - **When** Lonio derives the base tariff letter
  - **Then** the letter is C if the spouse or registered partner is gainfully employed or receives replacement income, and B if not. Children do not change the letter, only the child digit. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert: a registered partnership counts as married for source tax (D-003).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, D-003, D-001_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | Spouse holds a C permit | Spouse gainfully employed | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody | → | Base tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, spouse works (illustrative) | Switzerland | Portugal | no | Married | Portugal | no | yes | no | – | – | – | → | C | ✅ |
  | Married, spouse does not work (illustrative) | Switzerland | Portugal | no | Married | Portugal | no | no | no | – | – | – | → | B | ✅ |
  | Registered partnership, partner works (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Registered partnership | – | – | yes | no | – | – | – | → | C | ✅ |
  | Married, spouse works, minor child with sole custody (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Married | – | – | yes | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | C | ✅ |

- **AC-06.4.2 Base letter for single, divorced, separated and widowed employees**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax whose civil status is Single, Divorced, Separated or Widowed
  - **When** Lonio derives the base tariff letter
  - **Then** the letter is H if at least one child counts with H (per-child result, owned by the children section), otherwise A. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert: separated counts as single for source tax (D-003).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, D-003, D-001_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody | → | Base tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single, no children (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | no | Single | no | – | – | – | → | A | ✅ |
  | Separated, no children (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | no | Separated | no | – | – | – | → | A | ✅ |
  | Widowed, no children (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Widowed | no | – | – | – | → | A | ✅ |
  | Divorced, minor child in household with sole custody (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | no | Divorced | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | H | ✅ |
  | Single, child neither minor nor in first education (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Single | yes | Neither | – | – | → | A | ✅ |

- **AC-06.4.3 No replacement-income or special tariffs**
  - **Given** any employee subject to source tax
  - **When** Lonio derives the base tariff letter
  - **Then** the letter is always one of A, B, C or H; the replacement-income tariffs G and Q and special tariffs such as D (second job) never result, because a new employee always has earned income and special tariffs are out of scope for onboarding
  - _Sources: D-006, D-009_
_Trace check: partial – The claims that children never change the letter for married employees, and that a single employee whose child does not count with H gets A, are not stated in the cited facts, which only cover the no-children case and the H-child case._
