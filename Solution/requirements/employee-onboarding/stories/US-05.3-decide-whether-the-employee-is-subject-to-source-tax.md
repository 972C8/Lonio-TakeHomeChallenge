# US-05.3 – Decide whether the employee is subject to source tax

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Who is subject to source tax](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to decide from the employee's answers whether they are subject to source tax, **so that** I know up front who has to answer the source tax questions, and only those employees are asked them

Priority **P1** – HR 'never knows up front' who must answer the source tax questions. This decision controls the whole source tax section and the data minimisation for sensitive data such as religious denomination. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-b0527c, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-550840, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.3.1 Employees living abroad are always subject**
  - **Given** the employee's home address is in a country other than Switzerland
  - **When** Lonio decides whether the employee is subject to source tax
  - **Then** the employee is subject to source tax, whatever their nationality. No nationality, permit or spouse questions are needed for this decision. How Liechtenstein residents whom the calculator may treat as not liable are handled is open (see OQ-liechtenstein).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | → | Employee is subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Swiss national living in Germany, like Stefan (F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) (illustrative) | Germany | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.2 Swiss nationals and C-permit holders living in Switzerland are not subject**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland
  - **When** the employee is Swiss, or is not Swiss but holds a C permit
  - **Then** the employee is not subject to source tax, whatever their civil status. C-permit holders are treated like Swiss nationals.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b0527c, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | Civil status | Permit type | → | Employee is subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Swiss national living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | Single | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Maria, Portuguese, C permit (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | Portugal | Single | C permit | → | no | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.3 Non-Swiss employees without a C permit who are not married are subject**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland, is not Swiss and does not hold a C permit
  - **When** the employee's civil status is not "Married"
  - **Then** the employee is subject to source tax.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | Permit type | Civil status | → | Employee is subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single employee with a B permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | India | B permit | Single | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee with a B permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | India | B permit | Divorced | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.4 Spouse exemption for married employees**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland, is not Swiss, does not hold a C permit and is "Married"
  - **When** Lonio checks the spouse's answers
  - **Then** the employee is not subject to source tax only if the spouse is Swiss or holds a C permit and lives in Switzerland. In every other case (spouse neither Swiss nor C-permit holder, or Swiss/C-permit spouse living abroad) the employee is subject.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | Permit type | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | Spouse's country of residence | Spouse's permit type | → | Employee is subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás, Brazilian, B permit, Swiss wife, living in Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | B permit | Married | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Spouse is Italian with a C permit and lives in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | B permit | Married | Italy | Switzerland | C permit | → | no | ✅ |
  | Spouse is Italian with a B permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | B permit | Married | Italy | – | B permit | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Spouse is Swiss but lives in Germany (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | B permit | Married | Switzerland | Germany | – | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.5 Only subject employees answer the source tax questions**
  - **Given** Lonio has decided whether the employee is subject to source tax
  - **When** the employee would reach the source tax section (income type, religious denomination, spouse employment, children, cross-border questions)
  - **Then** an employee who is subject is asked the source tax section. An employee who is not subject is not asked any of it, so sensitive data such as religious denomination is not collected from them. HR can see for each employee whether they are subject. HR has only hedged its confirmation of this rule and must still confirm it, including that 'here' means Switzerland (see OQ-source-tax-rule).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-550840, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01_
