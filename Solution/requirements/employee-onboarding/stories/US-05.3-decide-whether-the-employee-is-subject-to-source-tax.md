# US-05.3 – Decide whether the employee is subject to source tax

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Who is subject to source tax](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to decide from the answers whether the new employee is subject to source tax, following our consolidated rule, **so that** I know up front who has to answer the source tax questions and who needs a tariff code, instead of working it out by hand

Priority **P1** – HR 'never knows up front' who has to answer the source tax questions (F-hr_transcript-b84498); this decision gates all source tax questions and the tariff code. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-b0527c, F-hr_transcript-b84498, D-003, D-008, D-037, D-043, D-048, D-053

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability) and [information.md](../information.md)): Nationality, Holds a C permit, Spouse's nationality, Spouse holds a C permit, Spouse lives in Switzerland, Subject to source tax

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.3.1 Employees living abroad are always subject to source tax**
  - **Given** the home address on the first working day is in any country other than Switzerland
  - **When** Lonio decides source tax liability
  - **Then** the employee is subject to source tax whatever their nationality or civil status (HR's consolidated rule, which replaces the first version 'all non-Swiss employees').
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-b0527c_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Civil status | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Stefan, Swiss citizen living abroad (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a; country assumed Germany) (illustrative) | Germany | Single | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Liechtenstein | Married | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Employee living in France (illustrative) | France | Divorced | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.2 Swiss citizens and C-permit holders living in Switzerland are not subject**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland
  - **When** the nationality is Switzerland (including dual citizens with Swiss citizenship), or the employee is not Swiss but holds a C permit
  - **Then** the employee is not subject to source tax, whatever their civil status. Assumption (D-008: Swiss citizenship counts even with another nationality) - to be verified by a domain expert.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-845204, D-008_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Maria, Portuguese, C permit (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | Portugal | yes | Single | → | no | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | Married | → | no | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.3 Non-Swiss employees in Switzerland without a C permit and without a spouse exemption are subject**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland, is not Swiss and has no C permit
  - **When** the civil status is Single, Divorced, Widowed or Separated
  - **Then** the employee is subject to source tax; separated counts as single, so the spouse is not considered. Assumption (D-003) - to be verified by a domain expert.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, D-003_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single non-Swiss employee without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Single | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Separated non-Swiss employee without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Separated | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Widowed stateless employee without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | stateless | no | Widowed | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.4 Spouse exemption for married employees and registered partners**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland, is not Swiss, has no C permit and is Married or in a Registered partnership
  - **When** Lonio checks the spouse's (or partner's) answers
  - **Then** the employee is not subject to source tax only if the spouse lives in Switzerland and is Swiss or holds a C permit; if the spouse lives outside Switzerland, or is neither Swiss nor a C-permit holder, the employee is subject to source tax. Assumptions (D-003: registered partnership counts as married; D-043: 'here' means Switzerland) - to be verified by a domain expert.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, D-003, D-043_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | Spouse lives in Switzerland | Spouse holds a C permit | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás, Brazilian, B permit, Swiss wife, Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | yes | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Spouse with C permit living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Germany | yes | yes | → | no | ✅ |
  | Swiss registered partner living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Registered partnership | Switzerland | yes | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Spouse living in Switzerland, neither Swiss nor C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Italy | yes | no | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Swiss spouse living outside Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | no | – | → | yes | ✅ |
  | C-permit spouse living outside Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | France | no | yes | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.5 The decision is recorded for HR and always follows the latest answers**
  - **Given** the employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** Lonio records whether the employee is subject to source tax
  - **Then** the result is recorded for HR as 'yes' or 'no' and decides whether the source tax questions are asked; it is derived again whenever an answer it depends on changes. If an HR change after submission makes a question apply that was never asked (for example the home address moves from abroad to Switzerland, so nationality was never answered), the result shows 'incomplete - HR must complete' until HR enters the missing answers; the questionnaire is not reopened for the employee. Assumptions (D-048, D-053) - to be verified by a domain expert. The HR screens for this belong to the later HR-side spec.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, D-034, D-037, D-048, D-053_
