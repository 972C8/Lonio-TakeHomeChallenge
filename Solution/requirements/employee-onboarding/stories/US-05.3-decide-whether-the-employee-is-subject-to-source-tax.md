# US-05.3 – Decide whether the employee is subject to source tax

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Who is subject to source tax](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to decide from the employee's answers whether they are subject to source tax, following my final rule, **so that** only employees subject to source tax are asked the tariff questions and I no longer have to work out up front who must answer them

Priority **P1** – The decision gates the whole source tax part; HR 'never knows up front' who has to answer and the rule took several corrections (F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-550840). · Sources: F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-b0527c, F-hr_transcript-550840, F-hr_transcript-b84498, D-003, D-007, D-008, D-037

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.3.1 Employees living abroad are always subject**
  - **Given** the employee's country of residence is not Switzerland
  - **When** Lonio decides source tax liability
  - **Then** the employee is subject to source tax whatever their nationality or civil status. This includes Liechtenstein residents, who are asked the source tax questions even though their result may be 'in principle no source tax liability' (see the Liechtenstein rule).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, D-007_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Civil status | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Stefan, Swiss living abroad (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a), country assumed (illustrative) | Germany | Single | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Married employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Married | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.2 Swiss citizens and C-permit holders living in Switzerland are not subject**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland
  - **When** they are a Swiss citizen (including dual nationals with Swiss citizenship) or hold a C permit
  - **Then** the employee is not subject to source tax, whatever their civil status.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-b0527c, D-008_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Maria, Portuguese with C permit (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | Portugal | yes | Single | → | no | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | Married | → | no | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.3 Non-Swiss employees without C permit who are not married are subject**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland, is not Swiss and has no C permit
  - **When** their civil status is Single, Divorced, Widowed or Separated
  - **Then** the employee is subject to source tax (separated counts as single for source tax).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, D-003_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single Italian with B permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Single | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Separated Italian without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Separated | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.4 Spouse exemption for married employees and registered partners**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland, is not Swiss, has no C permit and is Married or in a Registered partnership
  - **When** Lonio decides source tax liability
  - **Then** the employee is not subject only if the spouse or registered partner is Swiss or holds a C permit and lives in Switzerland; if the spouse is neither Swiss nor a C-permit holder, or lives outside Switzerland, the employee is subject. Whether HR's rule is complete is still to be confirmed (see OQ-source-tax-rule-confirmation).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, D-003_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | Spouse's / registered partner's nationality | Spouse's / registered partner's country of residence | Spouse / registered partner holds a C permit | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás, Swiss wife, living in Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Registered partner of a C-permit holder living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | no | Registered partnership | Italy | Switzerland | yes | → | no | ✅ |
  | Swiss spouse living in Germany (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | Germany | – | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Spouse neither Swiss nor C-permit holder (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Italy | – | no | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.5 Only employees subject to source tax get the tariff questions**
  - **Given** Lonio has decided whether the employee is subject to source tax
  - **When** the questionnaire continues to the source tax part
  - **Then** the tariff questions (such as confession and children) are asked only of employees subject to source tax; all others skip them.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | → | Religious denomination (confession) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Maria (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | Portugal | yes | Single | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Single Italian without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Single | → | asked | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.6 Liability always follows the latest answers**
  - **Given** an answer that feeds the decision (country of residence, nationality, C permit, civil status or spouse details) is changed, by the employee before submission or by HR after it
  - **When** Lonio decides source tax liability again
  - **Then** the decision is always based on the latest answers, and the source tax questions that follow are asked or dropped accordingly.
  - _Sources: D-037_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-007** (product decision (Product owner)): Liechtenstein residents are asked the source tax questions (HR's rule decides who is asked). The result follows the calculator - with daily return or at most 45 non-return days, Lonio shows 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR; with more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code. This closes OQ-liechtenstein-conflict. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-008** (product decision (Product owner)): Anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss for source tax, even with another nationality. This closes OQ-dual-nationality. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
