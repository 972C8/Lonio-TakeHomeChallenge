# US-05.3 – Decide whether the employee is subject to source tax

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Who is subject to source tax](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability).

**As** source tax office, **I want** Lonio to decide from the employee's answers whether they are subject to source tax, following HR's consolidated rule, **so that** every employee who must pay source tax gets a tariff code and nobody who is exempt is charged

Priority **P1** – Liability decides whether a tariff code is needed at all; HR corrected its own rule twice in the interview, showing the risk of errors (F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003, D-008, D-037, D-043, D-048

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability) and [information.md](../information.md)): Nationality, Holds a C permit, Spouse's nationality, Spouse holds a C permit, Spouse lives in Switzerland, Subject to source tax

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.3.1 Employees living abroad are always subject**
  - **Given** the employee's home address on the first working day is outside Switzerland
  - **When** Lonio decides source tax liability
  - **Then** the employee is subject to source tax whatever their nationality or civil status; even a Swiss citizen living abroad is subject.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Civil status | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Konstanz (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Germany | Single | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Stefan, Swiss citizen living abroad (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Married | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Employee living in France (illustrative) | France | Divorced | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.2 Swiss nationals and C-permit holders living in Switzerland are not subject**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland
  - **When** the nationality is Switzerland (also as one of several nationalities), or the employee is not Swiss and holds a C permit
  - **Then** the employee is not subject to source tax, whatever their civil status. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert: anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss (D-008).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-845204, D-008_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Maria (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | Portugal | yes | Single | → | no | ✅ |
  | Swiss national living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | Married | → | no | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.3 Spouse exemption for married employees and registered partners**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland, is not Swiss, has no C permit and is Married or in a Registered partnership
  - **When** Lonio decides source tax liability
  - **Then** the employee is not subject if the spouse or registered partner is Swiss or holds a C permit and lives in Switzerland; otherwise the employee is subject. Assumptions - to be verified by a domain expert: registered partnership counts as married (D-003) and 'here' means Switzerland (D-043).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b90bd9, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003, D-043_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | Spouse lives in Switzerland | Spouse holds a C permit | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | yes | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Registered partner of a C-permit holder living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | France | no | Registered partnership | Spain | yes | yes | → | no | ✅ |
  | Swiss spouse not living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | no | – | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Spouse neither Swiss nor C-permit holder (illustrative) | Switzerland | France | no | Married | Italy | – | no | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.4 No spouse exemption for single, divorced, widowed or separated employees**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland, is not Swiss and has no C permit
  - **When** the civil status is Single, Divorced, Widowed or Separated
  - **Then** the employee is subject to source tax; separated counts as single, so there is no spouse exemption. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert: D-003.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Civil status | → | Subject to source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | no | Single | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Separated employee (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | no | Separated | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee (illustrative) | Switzerland | stateless | no | Divorced | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Widowed | → | yes | ✅ |

- **AC-05.3.5 Liability is always derived from the latest answers**
  - **Given** the questionnaire has been submitted and HR later changes an answer the decision depends on (for example the country of residence after a move, or the civil status after a marriage)
  - **When** the liability is derived again
  - **Then** it follows the latest answers. If the change makes questions apply that were never asked (for example the spouse questions after a marriage), the result shows 'incomplete - HR must complete' until HR enters the missing answers; answers that no longer apply are discarded. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert: D-048. How HR makes the change is specified in the later HR-side spec (D-034).
  - _Sources: D-037, D-048, D-034_
