# US-06.5 – Replace the base letter with the cross-border letter where the cross-border tariff applies

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to turn the base letter into the cross-border letter for employees living in Germany to whom the cross-border tariff applies, **so that** cross-border commuters such as those living in Konstanz get the correct tariff without HR working through the calculator's Germany questions

Priority **P1** – HR finds the cross-border questions the hardest part of the calculator (F-hr_transcript-8595fb), and Lonio now owns the tariff result (D-001). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c8aa5e, F-hr_transcript-8595fb, D-001, D-035, D-051

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-calculator-coverage](../open-questions.md#oq-calculator-coverage)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics) and [information.md](../information.md)): Tariff letter

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.5.1 Cross-border letter when the cross-border tariff applies**
  - **Given** an employee living in Germany to whom the cross-border tariff applies: either they return home daily and have a certificate of residence, or they do not return daily, a daily return would be reasonable, and they expect at most 60 non-return days
  - **When** Lonio derives the tariff letter
  - **Then** The base letter is replaced: A becomes L, B becomes M, C becomes N and H becomes P. Assumption, to be verified by a domain expert (D-051): the mapping follows the calculator in full, so G would become Q, but G cannot occur in onboarding (D-006).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c8aa5e, D-006, D-051_

- **AC-06.5.2 Base letter kept when the cross-border tariff does not apply**
  - **Given** an employee who needs a tariff code and to whom the cross-border tariff does not apply: they live in Switzerland or in a country other than Germany, or they live in Germany and return daily without a certificate of residence ('no' or 'not yet'), or they do not return daily and a daily return is not reasonable, or they expect more than 60 non-return days
  - **When** Lonio derives the tariff letter
  - **Then** The tariff letter is the base letter unchanged. 'Not yet' for the certificate of residence counts as 'no' (D-035).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c8aa5e, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cc19c7, D-035_
