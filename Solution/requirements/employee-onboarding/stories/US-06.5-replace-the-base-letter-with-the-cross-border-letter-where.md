# US-06.5 – Replace the base letter with the cross-border letter where the cross-border tariff applies

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees living in Germany who meet the cross-border conditions to get the cross-border tariff letter (L, M, N or P) instead of the base letter, **so that** cross-border commuters are taxed at the cross-border tariff the calculator gives them, and everyone else at the normal tariff

Priority **P1** – HR finds the cross-border cases the hardest part of the calculator ('much more complicated', F-hr_transcript-8595fb). Lonio now has to compute them itself (D-001). · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c8aa5e, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-66732c, F-hr_transcript-8595fb, D-001, D-009, D-035

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics) and [information.md](../information.md)): Final tariff letter

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.5.1 Cross-border tariff replaces A, B, C, H with L, M, N, P**
  - **Given** an employee living in Germany for whom the cross-border tariff applies (rule cross_border_tariff: daily return with a certificate of residence handed in, or no daily return, daily return reasonable and no more than 60 non-return days expected)
  - **When** Lonio derives the final tariff letter
  - **Then** base letter A becomes L, B becomes M, C becomes N and H becomes P.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c8aa5e, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-66732c, D-001_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Civil status | Has children | Returns daily to the home in Germany | Certificate of residence handed in | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | Daily return reasonable (less than 100 km) | More than 60 work-related non-return days expected (Germany) | → | Final tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator path L0Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-66732c) | Germany | Single | no | yes | yes | – | – | – | – | – | – | → | L | ✅ |
  | Calculator path M0N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-66732c) | Germany | Married | no | yes | yes | no | – | – | – | – | – | → | M | ✅ |
  | Calculator path N1Y, daily return with certificate (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3) | Germany | Married | yes | yes | yes | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | – | → | N | ✅ |

- **AC-06.5.2 Otherwise the base letter is kept**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax for whom the cross-border tariff does not apply. This covers employees living in Switzerland, Liechtenstein or any other country, and Germany residents without a certificate of residence ('no' or 'not yet'), without a reasonable daily return, or expecting more than 60 non-return days.
  - **When** Lonio derives the final tariff letter
  - **Then** the final letter is the base tariff letter (A, B, C or H).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c8aa5e, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, D-035_
