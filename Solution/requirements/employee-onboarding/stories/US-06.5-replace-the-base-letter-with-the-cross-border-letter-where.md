# US-06.5 – Replace the base letter with the cross-border letter where the cross-border tariff applies

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees living in Germany who meet the calculator's cross-border conditions to get the cross-border letter instead of the base letter, **so that** cross-border commuters are taxed with the correct tariff

Priority **P1** – Employees living in Germany are subject to source tax (F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a). A wrong letter gives a wrong tariff code, and Lonio now owns that result (D-001). · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c8aa5e, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, D-001, D-006, D-035, D-051

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics) and [information.md](../information.md)): Tariff letter

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.5.1 Without the cross-border tariff the base letter applies**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax for whom the cross-border tariff does not apply (anyone not living in Germany, and Germany residents who do not meet the cross-border conditions of step 5)
  - **When** Lonio derives the tariff letter
  - **Then** the tariff letter is the base letter (A, B, C or H). For Germany residents the cross-border tariff does not apply when: they return daily without a certificate of residence ('not yet' counts as no, D-035); they do not return daily and a daily return is not reasonable; or a daily return is reasonable and they expect more than 60 non-return days. Liechtenstein residents never get a cross-border letter.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c8aa5e, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, D-035_

- **AC-06.5.2 With the cross-border tariff the letter is replaced**
  - **Given** an employee living in Germany who either returns daily and has handed in a certificate of residence, or does not return daily, for whom a daily return is reasonable and who does not expect more than 60 non-return days
  - **When** Lonio derives the tariff letter
  - **Then** A becomes L, B becomes M, C becomes N and H becomes P. The calculator also maps G to Q, but G does not occur because replacement income is out of scope (D-006). Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-051): the mapping follows the calculator in full.

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-006** (product decision (Product owner)): A new employee always has earned income. Do not ask the income type, and drop the replacement-income questions and the tariff G / Q cases. This closes OQ-income-type. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-035** (product decision (Product owner)): For the cross-border questions the employee states what they expect for the year (non-return days). For the certificate of residence 'not yet' is a valid answer and counts as 'no' for the tariff code. Documents go to HR outside Lonio; no upload.
- **D-051** (product decision (Product owner)): The full tariff code is one text such as 'N1Y' (letter, child digit, church-tax suffix). When no tariff code is needed the result is the text 'no tariff code'. The cross-border letter mapping follows the calculator fact in full, including G to Q where the calculator shows it. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3** (zh_source_tax_calculator): Germany resident with daily return and a residence certificate gets the cross-border (Grenzgänger) tariff: A->L, B->M, C->N, H->P, G->Q; without a certificate the normal tariff applies.
