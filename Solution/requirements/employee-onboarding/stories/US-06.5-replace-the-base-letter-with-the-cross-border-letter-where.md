# US-06.5 – Replace the base letter with the cross-border letter where the cross-border tariff applies

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the base tariff letter to be replaced by its cross-border letter when the cross-border tariff applies to an employee living in Germany, **so that** cross-border commuters from Germany get the same tariff letter the calculator would give them

Priority **P1** – Employees living abroad are always subject to source tax (F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) and HR finds their calculator path the most complicated (F-hr_transcript-8595fb); Lonio now owns this result (D-001). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c8aa5e, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-8595fb, D-001, D-035, D-037

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.5.1 Cross-border letters**
  - **Given** an employee living in Germany for whom the cross-border tariff applies (rule 'Cross-border tariff applies': daily return with a certificate of residence, or no daily return although daily return is reasonable and no more than 60 non-return days expected)
  - **When** Lonio derives the tariff letter
  - **Then** the base letter is replaced: A by L, B by M, C by N, H by P
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c8aa5e, D-001_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Civil status | Spouse gainfully employed | Has children | Daily return to Germany | Certificate of residence | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody | Daily return reasonable (under 100 km) | More than 60 non-return days (Germany) | → | Tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, spouse works, daily return with certificate (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | no | yes | yes | – | – | – | – | – | → | N | ✅ |
  | Married, spouse does not work, daily return with certificate (illustrative) | Germany | Married | no | no | yes | yes | – | – | – | – | – | → | M | ✅ |
  | Single, no children, daily return with certificate (illustrative) | Germany | Single | – | no | yes | yes | – | – | – | – | – | → | L | ✅ |
  | Divorced, minor child with sole custody, daily return with certificate (illustrative) | Germany | Divorced | – | yes | yes | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | – | → | P | ✅ |
  | Married, spouse works, no daily return, reasonable, at most 60 days (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | no | no | – | – | – | – | yes | no | → | N | ✅ |

- **AC-06.5.2 Base letter kept where the normal tariff applies**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax for whom the cross-border tariff does not apply: living in Germany without a certificate of residence ('no' or 'not yet'), or not returning daily where return is not reasonable or more than 60 non-return days are expected, or living in Switzerland, Liechtenstein or any other country
  - **When** Lonio derives the tariff letter
  - **Then** the base letter (A, B, C or H) is used unchanged. A certificate answered 'not yet' counts as 'no'; if it is handed in later, HR records it and the letter is derived again (D-035, D-037).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c8aa5e, D-035, D-037, D-001_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Civil status | Spouse gainfully employed | Has children | Daily return to Germany | Certificate of residence | Daily return reasonable (under 100 km) | More than 60 non-return days (Germany) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | → | Tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, daily return without certificate (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | no | yes | no | – | – | – | – | → | C | ✅ |
  | Married, daily return, certificate not yet (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | no | yes | not yet | – | – | – | – | → | C | ✅ |
  | Married, no daily return, not reasonable (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | no | no | – | no | – | – | – | → | C | ✅ |
  | Married, no daily return, reasonable, more than 60 days (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | no | no | – | yes | yes | – | – | → | C | ✅ |
  | Single non-Swiss resident of Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Single | – | no | – | – | – | – | Germany | no | → | A | ✅ |
  | Single, living in another country (illustrative) | France | Single | – | no | – | – | – | – | – | – | → | A | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-035** (product decision (Product owner)): For the cross-border questions the employee states what they expect for the year (non-return days). For the certificate of residence 'not yet' is a valid answer and counts as 'no' for the tariff code. Documents go to HR outside Lonio; no upload.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a** (hr_transcript): Employees who work here but live abroad (examples: Konstanz in Germany, Liechtenstein) are always subject to source tax, regardless of nationality. Even a Swiss citizen living abroad (Stefan) is subject.
