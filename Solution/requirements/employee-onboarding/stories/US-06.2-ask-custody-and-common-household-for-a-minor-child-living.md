# US-06.2 – Ask custody and common household for a minor child living with the employee

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** for each minor child living in the employee's household, to know whether custody is sole or shared, and for shared custody whether the employee lives with the other parent and whether there is alternating care, **so that** Lonio decides, as the calculator does, whether the child counts for the tariff code and whether it counts with H for a single employee

Priority **P1** – A minor child in the household is the main child case; a wrong answer path gives a wrong tariff code, which Lonio now owns instead of HR (D-001). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f7f3ea, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c09dc1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-509a7d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ebd7b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3058a4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-38dd4a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, D-001, D-003, D-009

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Parental custody (child in household), Common household with the other parent, Alternating care, Child result (per child)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.2.1 Custody asked for a minor child in the household**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with a minor child
  - **When** the employee answers that the child lives in the same household
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether custody is 'Shared custody' or 'Sole custody' (required); this is not asked for a child outside the household or a child of age
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f7f3ea, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c09dc1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-509a7d, D-001_

  | Example | Country of home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | C permit held | Civil status | Children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | → | Parental custody (child in household) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Minor child in household (illustrative) | Switzerland | no | no | Single | yes | Minor | yes | → | required | ✅ |
  | Minor child outside household (illustrative) | Switzerland | no | no | Single | yes | Minor | no | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.2.2 Sole custody counts the child**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with a minor child in the household
  - **When** the employee answers 'Sole custody'
  - **Then** no further question is asked for the child; the child counts for a married employee or registered partner and counts with H for a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, D-003_

  | Example | Country of home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | C permit held | Civil status | Spouse's Swiss citizenship | Spouse holds a C permit | Children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Parental custody (child in household) | → | Common household with the other parent | Child result (per child) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator run C1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22) | Switzerland | no | no | Married | no | no | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | not asked | counts | ✅ |
  | Calculator run H1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22) | Switzerland | no | no | Single | – | – | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | – | counts with H | ✅ |
  | Separated employee with sole custody (counts as single, D-003) (illustrative) | Switzerland | no | no | Separated | – | – | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | – | counts with H | ✅ |

- **AC-06.2.3 Shared custody: common household and alternating care**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with a minor child in the household under shared custody
  - **When** the employee answers the custody question
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether they live in a common household with the child's other parent, yes or no (required); if not, they are asked whether the child is in alternating care, yes or no (required)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ebd7b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, D-001_

  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

  | Common household, no alternating care question (illustrative) | Switzerland | no | no | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | yes | → | – | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.2.4 Shared custody result for a married employee or registered partner**
  - **Given** a married employee or registered partner subject to source tax with a minor child in the household under shared custody
  - **When** the employee has answered the shared-custody questions
  - **Then** a common household with the other parent counts the child without further questions; without a common household the child does not count, unless there is alternating care and the employee makes the larger financial contribution, in which case it counts. The last case was not observed in the calculator and is verified before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).

- **AC-06.2.5 Shared custody result for a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee**
  - **Given** a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee subject to source tax with a minor child in the household under shared custody
  - **When** the employee has answered the shared-custody questions
  - **Then** with a common household with the other parent, the child counts with H if the employee makes the larger financial contribution and does not count otherwise; without a common household, the child counts with H if there is no alternating care, or if there is alternating care and the employee makes the larger contribution; with alternating care and the other parent as larger contributor it does not count (not observed in the calculator, verified before go-live, see OQ-calculator-coverage)
