# US-06.2 – Ask custody and common household for a minor child living with the employee

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees subject to source tax whose minor child lives with them to say whether custody is shared or sole and, with shared custody, whether they live with the other parent and whether care alternates, **so that** Lonio decides, as the calculator does, whether the child counts and, for non-married employees, whether it counts with H

Priority **P1** – This decides the child digit and the H tariff in the tariff code that Lonio now computes instead of HR (D-001). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f7f3ea, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c09dc1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-509a7d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ebd7b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3058a4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-38dd4a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-001, D-003

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Parental custody (child in household), Common household with the other parent, Alternating care, Child result (per child)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.2.1 Custody asked for a minor child in the household**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has a child who is a minor and lives in their household
  - **When** the employee answers that child's block
  - **Then** the employee is asked about parental custody: "Shared custody" or "Sole custody". The answer is required and recorded for source tax. With sole custody no further questions are asked about this child.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f7f3ea, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c09dc1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-509a7d, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Parental custody (child in household) | → | Parental custody (child in household) | Common household with the other parent | Alternating care | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Minor in household - custody asked (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | yes | – | → | required | – | – | ✅ |
  | Minor not in household - custody (shared/sole) not asked (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | no | – | → | not asked | – | – | ✅ |
  | Sole custody ends the block (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | – | not asked | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.2.2 Common household and alternating care with shared custody**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has a minor child in their household with shared custody
  - **When** the employee continues that child's block
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether they live in a common household with the child's other parent ("yes"/"no", required). Only if they do not are they asked whether the parents share care in alternation ("yes"/"no", required). Both answers are recorded for source tax.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ebd7b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Parental custody (child in household) | Common household with the other parent | → | Common household with the other parent | Alternating care | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Shared custody - common household asked (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | – | → | required | – | ✅ |
  | No common household - alternating care asked (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | no | → | – | required | ✅ |
  | Common household - alternating care not asked (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | yes | → | – | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.2.3 Child result for married employees and registered partners**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax who is married or in a registered partnership (both count as married for source tax) has a minor child in their household
  - **When** Lonio derives the child's result
  - **Then** the child counts with sole custody, and with shared custody plus a common household with the other parent. With shared custody, no common household and no alternating care, the child does not count. (Alternating care cases depend on who contributes more to maintenance; see US-06.4.)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3058a4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Parental custody (child in household) | Common household with the other parent | Alternating care | → | Child result (per child) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, sole custody (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | – | → | Counted | ✅ |
  | Registered partnership, shared custody, common household with other parent (illustrative) | Germany | Registered partnership | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | yes | – | → | Counted | ✅ |
  | Married, shared custody, no common household, no alternating care (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | no | no | → | Not counted | ✅ |

- **AC-06.2.4 Child result for non-married employees**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax who is single, divorced, separated or widowed (all count as not married for source tax) has a minor child in their household
  - **When** Lonio derives the child's result
  - **Then** the child counts with H with sole custody, and with shared custody when there is no common household with the other parent and no alternating care. (Cases with a common household or alternating care depend on who contributes more to maintenance; see US-06.4.)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-38dd4a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Parental custody (child in household) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Common household with the other parent | Alternating care | → | Child result (per child) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single, sole custody (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | – | – | – | → | Counted with H | ✅ |
