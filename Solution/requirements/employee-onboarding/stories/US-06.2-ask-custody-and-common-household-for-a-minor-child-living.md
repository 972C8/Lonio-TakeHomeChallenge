# US-06.2 – Ask custody and common household for a minor child living with the employee

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** for each minor child living in the employee's household, to know whether custody is sole or shared, and for shared custody whether the employee lives with the other parent and whether there is alternating care, **so that** Lonio decides, as the calculator does, whether the child counts for the tariff code and whether it counts as an H child for a single employee

Priority **P1** – A minor child in the household is the main child case; a wrong answer path gives a wrong tariff code, which Lonio now owns instead of HR (D-001). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f7f3ea, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c09dc1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-509a7d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ebd7b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3058a4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-38dd4a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-332e2a, D-001, D-003, D-009

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.2.1 Custody asked for a minor child in the household**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with a minor child
  - **When** the employee answers that the child lives in the same household
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether custody is 'Shared custody' or 'Sole custody' (required); this is not asked for a child outside the household or a child of age
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f7f3ea, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c09dc1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-509a7d, D-001_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | → | Child: parental custody | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Minor child in household (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Single | yes | Minor | yes | → | required | ✅ |
  | Minor child outside household (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Single | yes | Minor | no | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.2.2 Sole custody counts the child**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with a minor child in the household
  - **When** the employee answers 'Sole custody'
  - **Then** no further question is asked for the child; the child counts for a married employee or registered partner and counts as an H child for a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, D-003_

  | Calculator run H1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Single | – | – | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | – | counts as H child | ✅ |
  | Separated employee with sole custody (counts as single, D-003) (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Separated | – | – | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | – | counts as H child | ✅ |

- **AC-06.2.3 Shared custody: common household and alternating care**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with a minor child in the household under shared custody
  - **When** the employee answers the custody question
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether they live in a common household with the child's other parent, yes or no (required); if not, they are asked whether the child is in alternating care, yes or no (required)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ebd7b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, D-001_

- **AC-06.2.4 Shared custody result for a married employee or registered partner**
  - **Given** a married employee or registered partner subject to source tax with a minor child in the household under shared custody
  - **When** the employee has answered the shared-custody questions
  - **Then** a common household with the other parent counts the child without further questions; without a common household the child does not count if there is no alternating care, or if there is alternating care and the other parent makes the larger financial contribution. With alternating care and the employee as larger contributor the result is not determined: the calculator exploration did not show this case, so Lonio cannot compute the tariff code for this employee until it is verified (see OQ-child-paths-undetermined).

- **AC-06.2.5 Shared custody result for a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee**
  - **Given** a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee subject to source tax with a minor child in the household under shared custody
  - **When** the employee has answered the shared-custody questions
  - **Then** with a common household with the other parent, the child counts as an H child if the employee makes the larger financial contribution and does not count otherwise; without a common household, the child counts as an H child if there is no alternating care, or if there is alternating care and the employee makes the larger contribution. With alternating care and the other parent as larger contributor the result is not determined: the calculator exploration did not show this case, so Lonio cannot compute the tariff code for this employee until it is verified (see OQ-child-paths-undetermined).
