# US-06.2 – Ask custody and common household for a minor child living with the employee

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees with a minor child in their household to state custody, common household with the other parent and, where relevant, alternating care, **so that** the child is counted for the tariff digit, and as an H child for single employees, exactly as the calculator does

Priority **P1** – Wrong child counting gives a wrong tariff code, which Lonio now owns instead of HR (D-001). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f7f3ea, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c09dc1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-509a7d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ebd7b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3058a4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-38dd4a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, D-001, D-003, D-009

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child: parental custody (shared or sole), Child: common household with the other parent, Child: alternating care, Child result (per child)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.2.1 Custody asked only for a minor child in the household**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has a minor child
  - **When** the child lives in the same household
  - **Then** the employee is asked, and must answer, whether custody is 'Shared custody' or 'Sole custody'; it is not asked for a child outside the household or a child of age.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f7f3ea, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c09dc1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-509a7d, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or of age and in first education | Child lives in the same household | → | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Minor child in household (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | yes | → | required | ✅ |
  | Minor child outside household (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | no | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.2.2 Sole custody counts the child**
  - **Given** a minor child lives in the employee's household
  - **When** the employee has sole custody
  - **Then** no further question is asked about the child; it counts for the tariff digit, and for an employee counting as single it counts as an H child.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or of age and in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | → | Child: common household with the other parent | Child result (per child) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, sole custody -> C1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22) | Germany | Married | yes | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | not asked | counts | ✅ |
  | Single, sole custody -> H1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22) | Germany | Single | – | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | – | counts as H child | ✅ |
  | Separated employee counts as single, sole custody (illustrative) | Germany | Separated | – | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | – | counts as H child | ✅ |

- **AC-06.2.3 Shared custody with a common household with the other parent**
  - **Given** a minor child lives in the employee's household with shared custody
  - **When** the employee is asked, and must answer yes or no, whether they live in a common household with the child's other parent, and answers yes
  - **Then** for an employee counting as married the child counts and nothing more is asked; for an employee counting as single the larger financial contributor decides: the employee - H child (inferred, see OQ-calculator-coverage); the other parent - the child does not count.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3058a4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-38dd4a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, D-001, D-009_

  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **AC-06.2.4 Shared custody without a common household: alternating care**
  - **Given** a minor child lives in the employee's household with shared custody and the employee does not live with the other parent
  - **When** the employee is asked, and must answer yes or no, whether there is alternating care
  - **Then** without alternating care the child does not count for a married employee and counts as an H child for a single employee; with alternating care the larger financial contributor decides: the other parent - the child does not count; the employee - the child counts (married) or counts as an H child (single). Rows not observed in the calculator are inferred and checked before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).
