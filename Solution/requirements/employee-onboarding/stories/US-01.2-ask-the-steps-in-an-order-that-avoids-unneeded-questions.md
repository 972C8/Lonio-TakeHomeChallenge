# US-01.2 – Ask the steps in an order that avoids unneeded questions

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Questionnaire flow overview](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow).

**As** new employee, **I want** to go through fixed steps in a set order, where every question only appears once the earlier answers show that a receiving party needs it for me, **so that** I answer a short questionnaire without questions that do not apply to me

Priority **P1** – The flow overview is a required part of the feature; asking too little blocks a registration and asking too much collects data we must not have (G-01). · Status: **decision** · Sources: G-01, G-08, G-09, F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-54d011, F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-pension_portal-8664f4, D-002, D-003, D-004, D-005, D-006, D-008, D-010, D-033

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow) and [information.md](../information.md)): Country of residence (country of the home address), Civil status, Nationality, Holds a C permit (permanent residence), Spouse's / registered partner's nationality, Spouse / registered partner holds a C permit, Spouse's / registered partner's country of residence, Questionnaire flow overview

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.2.1 Fixed order of the steps**
  - **Given** a new employee opens the questionnaire
  - **When** the employee goes through it
  - **Then** the steps come in this order: (1) Personal details - first name, last name, date of birth; (2) Home address - street, house number, postcode, town, country, and the canton if living in Switzerland; (3) Start - first working day; (4) Civil status - civil status and, where it applies, the spouse's or partner's date of birth; (5) Source tax status - only for employees living in Switzerland; (6) Source tax details - only for employees subject to source tax; (7) End - permit reminder where it applies, then submission. The questionnaire is in English.
  - _Sources: G-09, F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, F-hr_transcript-b84498, D-002, D-004, D-010, D-033_
- **AC-01.2.2 Canton only for employees living in Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee is in the home address step
  - **When** the employee gives the country of the home address
  - **Then** the canton is asked only if the country is Switzerland; employees living abroad are not asked for a canton (provisional, see OQ-canton-abroad).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-c0fe95, D-002_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Canton | Checked |

  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-01.2.3 Spouse's or partner's date of birth only for married, registered partners and separated employees**
  - **Given** the employee is in the civil status step
  - **When** the employee gives the civil status (Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership or Separated)
  - **Then** the spouse's or partner's date of birth is asked only for Married, Registered partnership and Separated; it is not asked for Single, Divorced or Widowed (see OQ-spouse-dob-other-status and OQ-civil-status-partnership).

- **AC-01.2.4 Source tax status step only for employees living in Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee has completed the civil status step
  - **When** the source tax status step would start
  - **Then** employees living abroad skip it entirely (they are subject to source tax whatever their nationality, so nationality is not asked). Employees living in Switzerland are asked their nationality ('Switzerland' for anyone holding Swiss citizenship); only non-Swiss employees are then asked whether they hold a C permit (yes or no). The permit type itself is never asked (D-005).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-845204, G-01, D-005, D-008, D-038_

- **AC-01.2.5 Spouse questions only when they can change the source tax outcome**
  - **Given** an employee living in Switzerland who is not Swiss
  - **When** the employee has answered the C permit question
  - **Then** the spouse's or partner's nationality is asked only if the employee has no C permit and is Married or in a Registered partnership; the spouse's C permit (yes or no) only if the spouse is not Swiss; the spouse's country of residence only if the spouse is Swiss or holds a C permit. Separated employees are not asked these questions, as they count as single for source tax (D-003).

- **AC-01.2.6 Questions that no party needs are never asked**
  - **Given** any employee
  - **When** the employee goes through the questionnaire
  - **Then** the employee is never asked for the income type or replacement income (D-006), the permit type (D-005), social insurance (AHV) data, IT equipment, a badge photo or document uploads. The insurance start date, the subject-to-source-tax result and the tariff code are derived, not asked (G-08).
  - _Sources: G-03, G-08, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d5a6fe, D-001, D-005, D-006, D-035_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-002** (product decision (Product owner)): Ask for the canton (pension fund 'Canton' field) only from employees living in Switzerland. Provisional - keep OQ-canton-abroad open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert and Pensionskasse Muster (what to enter for employees living abroad, and whether 'Canton' means residence or work).
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-004** (product decision (Product owner)): The spouse's (or registered partner's) date of birth is asked from married employees, registered partners and separated employees (all entered at the fund as Married, see D-003), not from divorced or widowed employees. Keep OQ-spouse-dob-other-status open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert (screenshot of the fund's screen). This closes OQ-separated-spouse-dob; the separated case is verified together with OQ-civil-status-partnership.
- **D-005** (product decision (Product owner)): Do not ask for the permit type. Ask non-Swiss employees only whether they hold a C permit (permanent residence), yes or no, and the same for a non-Swiss spouse. No party needs any other permit type. This closes OQ-permit-list. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-006** (product decision (Product owner)): A new employee always has earned income. Do not ask the income type, and drop the replacement-income questions and the tariff G / Q cases. This closes OQ-income-type. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-008** (product decision (Product owner)): Anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss for source tax, even with another nationality. This closes OQ-dual-nationality. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-010** (product decision (Product owner)): At the end of the questionnaire, remind every non-Swiss employee and every employee living abroad to bring their permit on the first working day. HR words the reminder; the spec states only its purpose. This closes OQ-permit-reminder.
- **D-033** (product decision (Product owner)): The employee enters the first working day. After submission only HR can change it; the latest date always counts and no history of earlier dates is kept.
- **D-035** (product decision (Product owner)): For the cross-border questions the employee states what they expect for the year (non-return days). For the certificate of residence 'not yet' is a valid answer and counts as 'no' for the tariff code. Documents go to HR outside Lonio; no upload.
- **D-038** (product decision (Product owner)): Employees living abroad get the permit reminder whatever their nationality, because nationality is not asked of them (data minimisation, G-01). HR words the reminder so that a Swiss citizen living abroad, such as Stefan, can see that it does not apply to them. Asking everyone 'Swiss citizen yes/no' only for the reminder is not worth the extra question.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-03** (product owner guidance): **Out of scope:** social insurance (AHV) data, IT equipment, anything not needed by the parties above.
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
- **G-09** (product owner guidance): Questionnaire language: English.
