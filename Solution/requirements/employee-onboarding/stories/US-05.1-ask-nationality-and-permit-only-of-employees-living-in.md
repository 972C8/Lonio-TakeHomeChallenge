# US-05.1 – Ask nationality and permit only of employees living in Switzerland

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Who is subject to source tax](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability).

**As** new employee, **I want** to be asked for my nationality and, if I am not Swiss, my Swiss permit only when this can change whether I am subject to source tax, **so that** Lonio can tell whether I must answer the source tax questions without collecting personal data that no receiving party needs for me

Priority **P1** – Source tax liability cannot be decided without these answers. HR says it never knows up front who has to answer the source tax questions, and data minimisation forbids asking them of everybody. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-35fae3, F-hr_transcript-c9d9b9, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, G-10

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.1.1 Nationality is asked only of employees who live in Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee has given the country of their home address (asked in the home address step)
  - **When** the employee reaches the nationality and permit step
  - **Then** the nationality is asked and required if the home address is in Switzerland. It is not asked if the home address is in another country, because employees who live abroad are subject to source tax whatever their nationality.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-35fae3, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | → | Nationality | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |
  | Swiss national living in Germany, like Stefan (F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) (illustrative) | Germany | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-05.1.2 Permit type is asked only of non-Swiss employees living in Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland and has given their nationality
  - **When** the nationality is not Switzerland
  - **Then** the employee's Swiss permit type is asked and required. Swiss nationals and employees who live abroad are not asked for a permit.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-c9d9b9, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | → | Permit type | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Maria, Portuguese (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | Portugal | → | required | ✅ |
  | Swiss national living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | – | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-05.1.3 Answer form for nationality and permit**
  - **Given** the nationality or the permit type is asked
  - **When** the employee answers
  - **Then** the nationality is given as a country by its English name (e.g. "Switzerland", "Portugal") and the permit as e.g. "B permit" or "C permit". Only a C permit changes the outcome; the full list of permits to offer is open (see OQ-permit-list). How employees who also hold Swiss citizenship answer is open (see OQ-dual-nationality). Whether the questionnaire reminds non-Swiss employees to bring their permit on the first day is open (see OQ-permit-reminder).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-35fae3, F-hr_transcript-c9d9b9, G-10_
- **AC-05.1.4 Who uses nationality and permit**
  - **Given** the employee has answered the nationality and, if asked, the permit type
  - **When** the answers are recorded
  - **Then** they are used to decide whether the employee is subject to source tax, so that HR knows up front whether the employee must answer the source tax questions. They are not asked for any other receiving party.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-35fae3, F-hr_transcript-c9d9b9, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01_

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party.
