# US-01.2 – Complete home address for posting documents

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address).

**As** HR, **I want** every new employee to give their full home address: street, house number, postcode, town and country, **so that** HR can post documents to the employee, using the address as one would write it on an envelope

Priority **P1** – HR posts many documents to employees and states that the address has to be complete. An incomplete address stops documents from reaching the employee. · Sources: F-hr_transcript-5518ad, F-hr_transcript-ec57d3, F-hr_transcript-66fe15, G-09, G-10

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-canton-abroad](../open-questions.md#oq-canton-abroad)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Street (home address), House number (home address), Postcode (home address), Town (home address), Country (home address)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.2.1 All five address parts are always asked and required**
  - **Given** a new employee is in the 'Home address' step of the questionnaire
  - **When** they enter their home address
  - **Then** they are asked for street, house number, postcode, town and country. All five are required for every employee, wherever they live, and the questionnaire cannot be submitted while any part is empty.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-ec57d3, F-hr_transcript-5518ad_

  | Example | Country (home address) | → | Street (home address) | House number (home address) | Postcode (home address) | Town (home address) | Country (home address) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás, living in Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | → | required | required | required | required | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (e.g. Konstanz) (illustrative) | Germany | → | required | required | required | required | required | ✅ |

- **AC-01.2.2 A normal postal address, nothing more**
  - **Given** the employee enters their home address
  - **When** the address step is shown
  - **Then** only the parts of a normal postal address are asked (street, house number, postcode, town, country), as written on an envelope. The country is given by its English name, e.g. "Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein".
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-66fe15, F-hr_transcript-ec57d3, G-01, G-10_
- **AC-01.2.3 The address is recorded for HR**
  - **Given** the employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** HR needs to post a document to the employee
  - **Then** the complete home address is recorded for HR as the employee entered it.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-5518ad, F-hr_transcript-ec57d3_
