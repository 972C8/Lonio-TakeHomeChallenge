# US-01.2 – Complete home address for posting documents

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address).

**As** HR, **I want** the new employee's complete home address: street, house number, postcode, town and country, **so that** documents posted to the employee reach them

Priority **P1** – HR posts many documents to employees and says the address 'has to be complete'. An incomplete address stops documents from reaching the employee. · Sources: F-hr_transcript-5518ad, F-hr_transcript-ec57d3, F-hr_transcript-66fe15, G-01, G-10

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-canton-abroad](../open-questions.md#oq-canton-abroad)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Street, House number, Postcode, Town, Country of home address (country of residence)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.2.1 All parts of the address are asked and required**
  - **Given** a new employee is filling in the onboarding questionnaire
  - **When** they reach the home address step, which follows the personal details
  - **Then** they are asked for street, house number, postcode, town and country, and each part is required. The questionnaire cannot be submitted while any part is empty. The address is recorded for HR.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-5518ad, F-hr_transcript-ec57d3_

  | Example | Country of home address (country of residence) | → | Street | House number | Postcode | Town | Country of home address (country of residence) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | required | required | required | – | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) (illustrative) | Germany | → | required | required | required | required | required | ✅ |

- **AC-01.2.2 Address is a normal postal address and nothing more**
  - **Given** a new employee is giving their home address
  - **When** they fill in the home address step
  - **Then** they are asked only for what a normal postal address on an envelope needs: street, house number, postcode, town and country. No other address details are asked. The country is given by its English name, e.g. Switzerland, Germany or Liechtenstein.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-66fe15, F-hr_transcript-ec57d3, G-01, G-10_
_Trace check: partial – AC-01.2.1 says the address step follows the personal details, and no cited fact supports that ordering._

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-10** (product owner guidance): Business wording for values (used in allowed values and examples): countries by English name ("Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein"), permits as "B permit", "C permit", civil status exactly as the pension fund's list shows it ("Single", "Married", ...), dates as DD.MM.YYYY, yes/no as "yes"/"no".
