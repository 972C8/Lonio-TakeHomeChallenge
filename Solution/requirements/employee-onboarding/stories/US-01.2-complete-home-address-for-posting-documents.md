# US-01.2 – Complete home address for posting documents

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address).

**As** HR, **I want** every new employee to give their complete home address: street, house number, postcode, town and country, **so that** HR can post documents to the employee without missing address parts

Priority **P1** – HR posts many documents to employees and explicitly states the address must be complete. · Sources: F-hr_transcript-5518ad, F-hr_transcript-ec57d3, F-hr_transcript-66fe15, G-01, G-10

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-canton-abroad](../open-questions.md#oq-canton-abroad)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-identity-and-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): Street (home address), House number (home address), Postcode (home address), Town (home address), Country (home address) = country of residence

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.2.1 All five address parts are always asked and required**
  - **Given** a new employee is in the second step, home address
  - **When** they fill in their address
  - **Then** they are asked for street, house number, postcode, town and country, and cannot submit the questionnaire while any of them is empty
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-ec57d3, F-hr_transcript-5518ad_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | → | Street (home address) | House number (home address) | Postcode (home address) | Town (home address) | Country (home address) = country of residence | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Address in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | required | required | required | required | ✅ |
  | Address in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | required | required | required | required | required | ✅ |

- **AC-01.2.2 Address as written on an envelope, nothing more**
  - **Given** the employee enters their home address
  - **When** the address is asked
  - **Then** only a normal postal address is asked, as one would write it on an envelope, with the country given by its English name (e.g. "Switzerland", "Germany"); no further address details are asked
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-66fe15, G-01, G-10_
- **AC-01.2.3 Address is recorded for HR**
  - **Given** the employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** the answers are recorded
  - **Then** the complete home address is recorded for HR to post documents
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-ec57d3, F-hr_transcript-5518ad_

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-10** (product owner guidance): Business wording for values (used in allowed values and examples): countries by English name ("Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein"), permits as "B permit", "C permit", civil status exactly as the pension fund's list shows it ("Single", "Married", ...), dates as DD.MM.YYYY, yes/no as "yes"/"no".
