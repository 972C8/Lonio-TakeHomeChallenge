# US-01.3 – Ask each piece of information once and reuse it for every receiving party

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Questionnaire flow overview](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow).

**As** HR, **I want** each piece of information to be asked from the employee only once and recorded for every party that needs it (HR, Pensionskasse Muster, source tax), **so that** I no longer re-enter the same data for each party and registrations are not blocked by missing data such as the spouse's date of birth

Priority **P1** – Missing data blocks registrations today (the fund cannot register a married employee without the spouse's date of birth) and asking once is the core goal of the feature (G-01). · Status: **decision** · Sources: G-01, G-04, F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-pension_portal-a0065b, F-pension_portal-c5ee15, F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, D-003, D-004, D-034, D-045

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.3.1 Shared answers are asked once**
  - **Given** an answer is needed by more than one party, such as civil status (pension fund and source tax) or country of residence (HR, pension fund and source tax)
  - **When** the employee goes through the questionnaire
  - **Then** the question appears exactly once and its answer is recorded for HR for every party that needs it; how the data reaches each party is a separate topic and not part of this questionnaire
  - _Sources: G-01, G-04, D-034, F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Civil status | → | Civil status | Country (of residence) | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Spouse gainfully employed | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Civil status used by fund and source tax (illustrative) | Germany | Married | → | asked | asked | asked | asked | ✅ |

- **AC-01.3.2 A question is asked only when a party needs it for this person**
  - **Given** the employee's earlier answers show that no party needs a piece of information for them
  - **When** the questionnaire reaches that question
  - **Then** the question is not asked. For example, the spouse's or partner's date of birth is asked from married, registered-partnership and separated employees, not from divorced or widowed ones. Assumption, to be verified by a domain expert: registered partnership and separated are entered at the fund as Married, which requires the spouse's date of birth (D-003, D-004). Nothing outside the needs of HR, the pension fund and source tax is asked (no AHV data, IT equipment, permit type or income type).
  - _Sources: G-01, G-03, F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-c5ee15, D-003, D-004, D-005, D-006_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Nationality | Civil status | → | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Spouse's nationality | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Divorced employee (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | Divorced | → | not asked | – | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | Widowed | → | not asked | – | ✅ |
  | Registered partner, like Daniel (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | Registered partnership | → | required | – | ✅ |
  | Separated employee (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | Separated | → | required | not asked | ✅ |

_Trace check: partial – Country of residence being needed by HR, the pension fund and source tax is not backed by the cited facts, and the separated example's 'spouse nationality not asked' has no cited support._


## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-004** (product decision (Product owner)): The spouse's (or registered partner's) date of birth is asked from married employees, registered partners and separated employees (all entered at the fund as Married, see D-003), not from divorced or widowed employees; no spouse data is sent to the fund for them. This closes OQ-spouse-dob-other-status and OQ-separated-spouse-dob. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-005** (product decision (Product owner)): Do not ask for the permit type. Ask non-Swiss employees only whether they hold a C permit (permanent residence), yes or no, and the same for a non-Swiss spouse. No party needs any other permit type. This closes OQ-permit-list. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-006** (product decision (Product owner)): A new employee always has earned income. Do not ask the income type, and drop the replacement-income questions and the tariff G / Q cases. This closes OQ-income-type. To be verified by a domain expert.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-03** (product owner guidance): **Out of scope:** social insurance (AHV) data, IT equipment, anything not needed by the parties above.
- **G-04** (product owner guidance): Scope of these feature specs: what Lonio asks the new employee (which information, in which order, under which conditions, required or not) and the business rules derived from the answers. How collected data is transmitted to each receiving party (exports, portal entry, change notifications) is a separate topic; each information item documents which stakeholder needs it and why.
