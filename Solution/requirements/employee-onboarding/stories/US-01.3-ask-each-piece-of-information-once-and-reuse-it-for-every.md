# US-01.3 – Ask each piece of information once and reuse it for every receiving party

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Questionnaire flow overview](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow).

**As** HR, **I want** every piece of information to be asked of the employee only once in the questionnaire, even when several receiving parties need it, **so that** the answers are enough for the pension registration, the source tax tariff and HR onboarding without asking the same thing twice or sending follow-up emails

Priority **P1** – This is the core promise of the feature: asking too little blocks a registration, asking twice or too much annoys employees and collects data we must not have. Today HR collects the same data by hand for several parties and asks spouse details by email, which takes forever. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: G-01, G-02, G-04, F-hr_transcript-a45422, F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, F-hr_transcript-ab3cba, F-hr_transcript-c0fe95

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow) and [information.md](../information.md)): Questionnaire flow overview

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.3.1 Information needed by several parties is asked once**
  - **Given** an information item needed by more than one receiving party, for example the civil status (pension fund and source tax) or the country of the home address (HR's postal address and source tax residence)
  - **When** the employee goes through the questionnaire
  - **Then** the item is asked exactly once, in its step, and the same answer serves every party that needs it; the source tax step does not ask again for civil status or country of residence
  - _Sources: G-01, F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, F-hr_transcript-ab3cba, F-hr_transcript-c0fe95_
- **AC-01.3.2 Spouse details are collected in the questionnaire, not by email**
  - **Given** a married employee who would otherwise be subject to source tax
  - **When** they complete step 5 Family
  - **Then** the spouse details HR needs for the source tax check are already answered in the questionnaire, so HR does not need to ask for them by email
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-a45422, F-hr_transcript-54d011, G-01_
- **AC-01.3.3 No question without a receiving party**
  - **Given** any question in the questionnaire
  - **When** it is shown to an employee
  - **Then** at least one receiving party in scope (Pensionskasse Muster, the Zurich source tax calculator or Muster AG's HR) needs the answer for this employee; out-of-scope data such as AHV data, IT equipment or badge photos is not asked
  - _Sources: G-01, G-02, G-03, G-04_

- **F-hr_transcript-54d011** (hr_transcript, inferred): The spouse's nationality, permit and residence only matter for source tax when the employee is married. They are presumably only needed when the employee would otherwise be subject to source tax (non-Swiss, no C permit, living in Switzerland).

  - "then you don't do source tax either." — hr_transcript/T-044, Anna

- **F-hr_transcript-ab3cba** (hr_transcript, inferred): The employee's country of residence (the country of the home address) is needed to decide source tax liability for cross-border employees.

- **F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4** (hr_transcript): The pension fund asks for the employee's civil status.
  - "Yes, the civil status. They ask for that." — hr_transcript/T-032, Anna
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3** (zh_source_tax_calculator): For earned income, the calculator asks for the civil status of the person subject to source tax.
  - "Zivilstand" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q02
  - "Section heading: 'Angaben zur quellensteuerpflichtigen Person'" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q02
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-02** (product owner guidance): Receiving parties in scope: the pension fund (Pensionskasse Muster), source tax (Zurich tariff calculator), and the employer's HR (Muster AG).
- **G-03** (product owner guidance): **Out of scope:** social insurance (AHV) data, IT equipment, anything not needed by the parties above.
- **G-04** (product owner guidance): Scope of these feature specs: what Lonio asks the new employee (which information, in which order, under which conditions, required or not) and the business rules derived from the answers. How collected data is transmitted to each receiving party (exports, portal entry, change notifications) is a separate topic; each information item documents which stakeholder needs it and why.
