# US-01.3 – Ask each piece of information once and reuse it for every receiving party

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [1. Questionnaire flow overview](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow).

**As** HR, **I want** each piece of information to be asked of the employee only once and reused for the pension fund, source tax and HR, **so that** I no longer collect the same data several times or chase spouse details by email, and the employee is not asked the same thing twice

Priority **P1** – Asking once and reusing is the core promise of the feature (G-01); HR's email chasing for spouse details 'takes forever'. · Status: **decision** · Sources: G-01, G-08, D-047, F-hr_transcript-a45422, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow) and [information.md](../information.md)): Questionnaire flow overview

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-01.3.1 One answer serves every party that needs it**
  - **Given** an answer is needed by more than one receiving party (for example civil status for the pension fund and for source tax, or the home address country for HR, source tax and the fund's canton)
  - **When** the employee answers it
  - **Then** the question appears once in the questionnaire and the same answer is recorded for every party that needs it; the source tax questions 'main residence in Switzerland?' and 'country of residence' are taken from the home address country and not asked again.
  - _Sources: G-01, G-08, D-047, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-hr_transcript-a45422_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Nationality | Civil status | → | Civil status | Spouse's or registered partner's date of birth | Canton of residence | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel, registered partnership (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Switzerland | Switzerland | Registered partnership | → | required | asked | asked | ✅ |

- **AC-01.3.2 A question is asked only when a party needs it for this person**
  - **Given** no receiving party needs a given answer for this employee
  - **When** the employee goes through the questionnaire
  - **Then** that question is not asked, and values Lonio can derive (such as the insurance start date from the first working day) are derived rather than asked.
  - _Sources: G-01, G-08, D-047_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Civil status | → | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Canton of residence | Spouse's nationality | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Swiss citizen living abroad like Stefan (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a); country of residence assumed to be Germany (illustrative) | Germany | Married | → | not asked | not asked | not asked | not asked | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **F-hr_transcript-3907fe** (hr_transcript): At least one current employee (Daniel, finance) is in a registered partnership, so this case occurs.
  - "we do have one colleague in a registered partnership. Daniel, in finance." — hr_transcript/T-034, Anna
- **F-hr_transcript-a45422** (hr_transcript): Today HR checks the spouse exemption by hand and asks for the spouse details by email, which takes forever.
  - "I always have to check that one by hand." — hr_transcript/T-044, Anna
  - "I ask them by email, and it takes forever." — hr_transcript/T-046, Anna
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae** (zh_source_tax_calculator): If the main residence is not in Switzerland, the calculator asks for the country of residence.
  - "Wohnsitzstaat" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q17
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40** (zh_source_tax_calculator): The calculator asks whether the main residence is in Switzerland.
  - "Hauptwohnsitz in der Schweiz?" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q16
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
