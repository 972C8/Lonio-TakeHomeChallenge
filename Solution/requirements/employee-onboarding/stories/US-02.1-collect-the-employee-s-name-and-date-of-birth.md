# US-02.1 – Collect the employee's name and date of birth

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** pension fund, **I want** every new employee to give their first name, last name and date of birth in the first step of the onboarding questionnaire ('About you'), **so that** the registration with Pensionskasse Muster can be completed, because these are required fields of the fund's registration form, and HR can identify the employee

Priority **P1** – The fund's registration form rejects a registration without first name, last name or date of birth ('... is required.'), so missing values block the registration. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-pension_portal-db702a, F-pension_portal-c688e4, F-pension_portal-88b436, F-pension_portal-967bc9, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-hr_transcript-3edf89, D-047, D-050, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): First name, Last name, Date of birth

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.1.1 Name and date of birth are asked of every employee in step 1**
  - **Given** a new employee opens the onboarding questionnaire, wherever they live and whatever their nationality
  - **When** they answer step 1 'About you' (the first of the eight fixed steps; assumption to be verified by a domain expert, D-047)
  - **Then** they are asked for their first name, last name and date of birth, together with the first working day, and each of these answers is required: the employee cannot leave step 1 complete without them
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-db702a, F-pension_portal-c688e4, F-pension_portal-88b436, F-pension_portal-44a27b, D-047_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | First name | Last name | Date of birth | → | First name | Last name | Date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Lena | Meier | 12.03.1990 | → | required | required | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living abroad (illustrative) | Germany | Jonas | Keller | 01.07.1985 | → | required | required | required | ✅ |

- **AC-02.1.2 Date of birth is a full date and plausible for the first working day**
  - **Given** the employee enters their date of birth and their first working day
  - **When** the date of birth would make them younger than 14 or older than 100 on the first working day
  - **Then** the date of birth is not accepted and the employee is told to correct it; a date of birth that makes them between 14 and 100 years old on the first working day is accepted. Dates are given as day, month and year (DD.MM.YYYY), as the fund's form expects. The age limits are an assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-050).
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-967bc9, D-050_
- **AC-02.1.3 Who receives name and date of birth**
  - **Given** the employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** the answers are recorded
  - **Then** first name, last name and date of birth are recorded for the registration with Pensionskasse Muster; first name and last name are also recorded for HR to identify the employee. The date of birth is not recorded for any other purpose.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-db702a, F-pension_portal-c688e4, F-pension_portal-88b436, F-hr_transcript-3edf89, G-01, D-034_

There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-050** (product decision (Product owner)): Build-time values. HR provides the permit reminder wording before go-live. Country of residence and nationality are chosen from the ISO 3166 country list; nationality also offers 'stateless'. The date of birth must make the employee at least 14 and at most 100 years old on the first working day; the first working day must lie within one year after the questionnaire is sent. Answers are saved as the employee goes and they can resume until they submit; submitting needs one confirmation on a summary page. Answers are kept when the employee goes back and changes an earlier answer (until submission), and this is testable. The spouse's residence is asked as 'Does your spouse live in Switzerland? yes/no', not as a country. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
