# US-02.1 – Collect the employee's name and date of birth

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** pension fund, **I want** every new employee to give their first name, last name and date of birth in the onboarding questionnaire, **so that** the employee can be registered for the occupational pension: the registration cannot be submitted without these required fields. HR also uses the name to identify the employee and to address posted documents

Priority **P1** – First name, last name and date of birth are required fields of the pension fund registration. The registration is rejected without them ('<Field> is required.'), so missing values block it. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-pension_portal-db702a, F-pension_portal-c688e4, F-pension_portal-88b436, F-pension_portal-967bc9, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-hr_transcript-3edf89, G-01, G-09, G-10

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.1.1 First and last name are asked of every employee and cannot be left empty**
  - **Given** a new employee fills in the onboarding questionnaire, whatever their country of residence, nationality or civil status
  - **When** the employee reaches the personal details
  - **Then** the questionnaire asks for the first name and the last name. Both are required, and the questionnaire cannot be submitted while either is empty. The names are recorded for the pension fund registration (its required 'First name' and 'Last name' fields) and for HR, which uses them to identify the employee and address posted documents
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-db702a, F-pension_portal-c688e4, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-hr_transcript-3edf89, G-01_
- **AC-02.1.2 Date of birth is asked of every employee as a date DD.MM.YYYY**
  - **Given** a new employee fills in the onboarding questionnaire
  - **When** the employee reaches the personal details
  - **Then** the questionnaire asks for the employee's date of birth as a date in the form DD.MM.YYYY (e.g. 14.03.1990). It is required, and the questionnaire cannot be submitted without it. The date of birth is recorded for the pension fund registration (its required 'Date of birth' field)
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-88b436, F-pension_portal-967bc9, F-pension_portal-44a27b, G-10_
- **AC-02.1.3 Name and date of birth are asked once only**
  - **Given** the employee has entered their name and date of birth
  - **When** the employee continues through the rest of the questionnaire
  - **Then** no later step asks for the employee's own name or date of birth again. The same answers serve both the pension fund and HR
  - _Sources: G-01_
