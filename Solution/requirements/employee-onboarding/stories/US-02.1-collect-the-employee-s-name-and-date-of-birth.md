# US-02.1 – Collect the employee's name and date of birth

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** pension fund, **I want** every new employee to give their first name, last name and date of birth in the Personal details step of the onboarding questionnaire, **so that** the occupational pension registration has the required identity fields and is not rejected with 'First name / Last name / Date of birth is required.'

Priority **P1** – The pension fund's registration form marks these fields as required and blocks submission when they are empty. Missing required fields are a named pain point that blocks the pension registration. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-pension_portal-db702a, F-pension_portal-c688e4, F-pension_portal-88b436, F-pension_portal-967bc9, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-hr_transcript-3edf89, G-01, G-10

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-canton](../open-questions.md#oq-canton)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): First name, Last name, Date of birth

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.1.1 First and last name are always asked and required**
  - **Given** any new employee fills in the onboarding questionnaire
  - **When** they reach the Personal details step, which is the first step
  - **Then** they are asked for their first name and their last name. Neither may be left empty. Both go to the pension fund, which requires them for the registration, and to HR, which uses them to identify the employee and address documents.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-db702a, F-pension_portal-c688e4, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-hr_transcript-3edf89_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | → | First name | Last name | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | required | required | ✅ |

- **AC-02.1.2 Date of birth is always asked, required and given as DD.MM.YYYY**
  - **Given** any new employee fills in the Personal details step
  - **When** they give their date of birth
  - **Then** the date of birth is asked of every employee, may not be left empty, and is given as a date in the form DD.MM.YYYY (e.g. 14.03.1990), the form the pension fund's registration expects. It goes to the pension fund for the registration.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-88b436, F-pension_portal-967bc9, F-pension_portal-44a27b, G-10_

  | Example | Date of birth | Country (of the home address) | → | Date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee born 14.03.1990 (illustrative) | 14.03.1990 | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | – | Liechtenstein | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-02.1.3 Personal details are asked once**
  - **Given** the employee has given their first name, last name and date of birth in the Personal details step
  - **When** the pension fund and HR both need these details
  - **Then** the employee is not asked for them again anywhere else in the questionnaire. The same answers serve every receiving party that needs them.
  - _Sources: G-01_
