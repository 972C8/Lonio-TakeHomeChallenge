# US-02.1 – Collect the employee's name and date of birth

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. Personal details and home address](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address).

**As** pension fund, **I want** every new employee to give their first name, last name and date of birth in the onboarding questionnaire, **so that** the occupational pension registration can be submitted. The fund's form rejects a registration where any of these required fields is empty. HR also needs the name to identify the employee and to address the documents it posts to them.

Priority **P1** – First name, last name and date of birth are required fields in the pension fund registration. If one is missing, the registration is blocked (F-pension_portal-44a27b). · Sources: F-pension_portal-db702a, F-pension_portal-c688e4, F-pension_portal-88b436, F-pension_portal-967bc9, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-hr_transcript-3edf89, G-01, G-10, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) and [information.md](../information.md)): First name, Last name, Date of birth

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.1.1 Name is asked of every employee and cannot be left empty**
  - **Given** a new employee fills in the onboarding questionnaire, whatever their country of residence, nationality or civil status
  - **When** they reach the personal details
  - **Then** they are asked for their first name and their last name. Both answers are required, so the questionnaire cannot be submitted while either is empty. The answers are recorded for the pension fund registration (fields 'First name' and 'Last name') and for HR, which uses them to identify the employee and address the documents it posts.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-db702a, F-pension_portal-c688e4, F-pension_portal-44a27b, F-hr_transcript-3edf89, G-01_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | → | First name | Last name | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | required | required | ✅ |

- **AC-02.1.2 Date of birth is asked of every employee as a day, month and year**
  - **Given** a new employee fills in the onboarding questionnaire
  - **When** they reach the personal details
  - **Then** they are asked for their date of birth as day, month and year (DD.MM.YYYY, e.g. 14.03.1990). The answer is required, so the questionnaire cannot be submitted without it. It is recorded for the pension fund registration (field 'Date of birth').
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-88b436, F-pension_portal-967bc9, F-pension_portal-44a27b, G-10_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | → | Date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | required | ✅ |
