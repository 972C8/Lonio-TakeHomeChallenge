# US-04.1 – Ask every employee for their civil status

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** every new employee to state their civil status, choosing from Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership or Separated, **so that** the required 'Civil status' field of the registration can be filled and source tax can tell the married path from the single path, without HR having to ask the employee again

Priority **P1** – Civil status is a required field of the pension fund registration, and the source tax tariff code depends on it. Without it, both the registration and the tariff code are blocked. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-hr_transcript-bd8c8e, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, F-pension_portal-8664f4, F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, G-01, G-09, G-10, D-003, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Civil status

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.1.1 Civil status is always asked and required**
  - **Given** a new employee fills in the onboarding questionnaire, whatever their residence, nationality or source tax situation
  - **When** they reach the civil status question
  - **Then** the question is asked, and the employee cannot submit the questionnaire without answering it
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-pension_portal-8664f4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel, registered partnership (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | required | ✅ |
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-04.1.2 Exactly six civil status options**
  - **Given** the employee is asked for their civil status
  - **When** they choose an answer
  - **Then** they can choose exactly one of: Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership, Separated. The first four are the pension fund's own list, written as the fund writes them. Registered partnership and Separated are added so that the calculator's groups can be answered and employees such as Daniel, who is in a registered partnership, can answer truthfully. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert: offering these two extra options and mapping them as described in the stories on civil status for the pension fund and for source tax.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-hr_transcript-bd8c8e, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, G-10, D-003_
- **AC-04.1.3 Who receives the civil status**
  - **Given** the employee has answered the civil status question
  - **When** the questionnaire is submitted
  - **Then** the answer is recorded for the pension fund registration (as the fund's civil status) and for the source tax tariff determination (as the calculator's civil status group). The employee is not asked for their civil status a second time for either party.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, G-01, D-003_
