# US-04.1 – Ask every employee for their civil status

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** new employee, **I want** to state my civil status once, in step 4 'Civil status and spouse' of the onboarding questionnaire, choosing from a list that matches my real situation, **so that** Lonio can register me with the pension fund and work out my source tax tariff without asking me the same thing again for each party

Priority **P1** – The pension fund requires a civil status to register the employee, and the source tax tariff depends on it. Without it, the registration and the tariff are blocked. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-hr_transcript-bd8c8e, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, F-pension_portal-8664f4, F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003, D-047, G-10, G-01

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.1.1 Civil status is asked of everyone and is required**
  - **Given** an employee is filling in the questionnaire, wherever they live
  - **When** they reach step 4 'Civil status and spouse'
  - **Then** they are asked for their civil status and cannot submit the questionnaire without answering. The answer is recorded for the pension fund and for source tax. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert: the step order follows the agreed eight-step list (D-047).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-pension_portal-8664f4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-047_

  | Example | Civil status | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Civil status | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel, registered partnership (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | – | → | required | ✅ |
  | Single employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Single | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |
  | Married employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Married | Germany | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-04.1.2 Six civil status values are offered**
  - **Given** an employee is asked for their civil status
  - **When** they choose an answer
  - **Then** they can choose exactly one of: Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership, Separated. The fund's list has only the first four. Registered partnership and Separated are added because the source tax calculator treats them in a specific way and registered partnerships do occur at Muster AG. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-003).
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, D-003, G-10_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation).
