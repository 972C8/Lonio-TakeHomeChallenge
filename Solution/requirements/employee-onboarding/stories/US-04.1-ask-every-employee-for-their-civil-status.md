# US-04.1 – Ask every employee for their civil status

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** new employee, **I want** to state my civil status once, choosing from a list that covers my real situation, including a registered partnership or a separation, **so that** the pension fund can register me and the source tax tariff can be worked out without HR asking me again

Priority **P1** – Civil status is a required field of the pension fund registration; without it the registration is blocked (F-pension_portal-8664f4). It also drives the source tax tariff. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-hr_transcript-bd8c8e, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, F-pension_portal-8664f4, F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, G-10, D-003, D-047

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Civil status

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.1.1 Civil status is asked of every employee, wherever they live**
  - **Given** an employee filling in the onboarding questionnaire, living in Switzerland or abroad
  - **When** they reach step 4 'Civil status and spouse' of the eight questionnaire steps
  - **Then** Lonio asks for their civil status, and an answer is required before the questionnaire can be submitted. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert: the step order follows the eight fixed steps (D-047).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-pension_portal-8664f4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, D-047_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Civil status | → | Civil status | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel, registered partnership, living in Switzerland (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Switzerland | Registered partnership | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | Single | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Married | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-04.1.2 Civil status offers exactly six values**
  - **Given** the civil status question is shown
  - **When** the employee chooses an answer
  - **Then** they choose exactly one of: Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership, Separated. The first four use the wording of the pension fund's list; Registered partnership and Separated are added because they occur (Daniel is in a registered partnership) and the source tax calculator names them. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-003).
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-hr_transcript-bd8c8e, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, G-10, D-003_
- **AC-04.1.3 Civil status is recorded for the pension fund and for source tax**
  - **Given** the employee has answered the civil status question and submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** the answers are recorded for HR
  - **Then** the civil status is recorded as needed by the pension fund (its required 'Civil status' field, converted to the fund's list) and by source tax (the calculator's civil status group and the spouse exemption in HR's source tax rule). How the data is sent to each party is out of scope.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-ce8ca4, F-pension_portal-8664f4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-04, D-034_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
