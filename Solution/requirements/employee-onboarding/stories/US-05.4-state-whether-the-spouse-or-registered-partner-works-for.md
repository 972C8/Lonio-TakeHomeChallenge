# US-05.4 – State whether the spouse or registered partner works, for married employees and registered partners

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Source tax tariff basics and computed result](../feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** married employees and registered partners subject to source tax to say whether their spouse or partner is gainfully employed or receives replacement income, **so that** the married tariff letter is C or B as in the calculator

Priority **P1** – Decides the tariff letter for every married employee subject to source tax; without it no tariff code can be computed (D-001, F-hr_transcript-09b3fe). · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7b637b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e740d1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-hr_transcript-09b3fe, D-001, D-003, D-006

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-calculator-coverage](../open-questions.md#oq-calculator-coverage)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics) and [information.md](../information.md)): Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income, Married tariff letter

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.4.1 Asked only from employees counting as married for source tax**
  - **Given** the employee is subject to source tax
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax step
  - **Then** the question is asked, and must be answered yes or no, only if the employee is Married or in a Registered partnership; Single, Divorced, Widowed and Separated employees count as single for source tax and are not asked. Employees not subject to source tax are never asked. The answer is recorded for source tax. The question is about the spouse's or partner's situation; the employee's own income type is not asked (D-006).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e740d1, F-hr_transcript-09b3fe, D-003, D-006_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Spouse's / registered partner's country of residence | Spouse's / registered partner's nationality | → | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, lives abroad (illustrative) | France | Married | – | – | – | – | → | required | ✅ |
  | Registered partnership, lives abroad (illustrative) | France | Registered partnership | – | – | – | – | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Separated counts as single (illustrative) | France | Separated | – | – | – | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Single (illustrative) | France | Single | – | – | – | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Married, living in Switzerland, spouse abroad - subject (illustrative) | Switzerland | Married | Germany | no | Germany | – | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Married to a Swiss spouse living in Switzerland - not subject (illustrative) | Switzerland | Married | Italy | no | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-05.4.2 Married tariff letter C or B**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax counts as married for source tax
  - **When** Lonio determines the married tariff letter
  - **Then** the letter is C if the spouse or partner works or receives replacement income, and B if not. For employees counting as single, no married tariff letter applies.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | → | Married tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | C0Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a) | France | Married | yes | → | C | ✅ |
  | B0Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a) | France | Married | no | → | B | ✅ |
  | Registered partner, partner works (illustrative) | France | Registered partnership | yes | → | C | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-006** (product decision (Product owner)): A new employee always has earned income. Do not ask the income type, and drop the replacement-income questions and the tariff G / Q cases. This closes OQ-income-type. To be verified by a domain expert.
