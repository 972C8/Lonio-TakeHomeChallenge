# US-05.4 – State whether the spouse or registered partner works, for married employees and registered partners

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Source tax tariff basics and computed result](../feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees who are subject to source tax and married or in a registered partnership to state whether their spouse or partner is gainfully employed or receives replacement income, **so that** the base tariff letter is C (spouse or partner works) or B (does not work), and nobody else is asked about a spouse's work

Priority **P1** – The tariff letter for every married subject employee depends on it. HR names it as the extra calculator question for married employees (F-hr_transcript-09b3fe). Without it the computed tariff code (D-001) cannot be produced. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7b637b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e740d1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-09b3fe, G-01, D-001, D-003

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.4.1 Asked only of subject employees who are married or in a registered partnership**
  - **Given** an employee who has answered their civil status and whose earlier answers decide whether they are subject to source tax
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax questions
  - **Then** the question is asked, and must be answered, only if the employee is subject to source tax and is married or in a registered partnership (registered partnership counts as married for source tax). Single, divorced, widowed and separated employees count as not married for source tax and are not asked (D-003). Employees not subject to source tax are not asked.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-09b3fe, G-01, D-003_

- **AC-05.4.2 Answer is yes or no and covers replacement income**
  - **Given** an employee who is asked whether the spouse or registered partner works
  - **When** the employee answers
  - **Then** the question asks whether the spouse or partner is gainfully employed or receives replacement income. The answer is "yes" or "no" and cannot be left empty. It is recorded for the source tax tariff code only.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7b637b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e740d1, D-001_
- **AC-05.4.3 Spouse's work decides the base letter C or B**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax who is married or in a registered partnership and has answered whether the spouse or partner works
  - **When** Lonio derives the base tariff letter for married employees
  - **Then** the base letter is C if the spouse or partner works or receives replacement income, and B if not.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | Spouse's / partner's country of residence | Spouse / partner is gainfully employed or receives replacement income | → | Spouse / partner is gainfully employed or receives replacement income | Base letter for married employees | Checked |
