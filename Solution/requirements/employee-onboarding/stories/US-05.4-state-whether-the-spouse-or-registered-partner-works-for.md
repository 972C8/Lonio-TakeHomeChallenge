# US-05.4 – State whether the spouse or registered partner works, for married employees and registered partners

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Source tax tariff basics and computed result](../feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** married employees and employees in a registered partnership who are subject to source tax to state whether their spouse or partner is gainfully employed or receives replacement income, **so that** the married tariff letter can be set (C if the spouse works or receives replacement income, B otherwise) without asking single, divorced, widowed or separated employees an irrelevant question

Priority **P1** – The married tariff letter cannot be determined without this answer, and HR names it as the question the calculator adds for married employees (F-hr_transcript-09b3fe); it blocks the computed tariff code (D-001). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7b637b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e740d1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-09b3fe, D-001, D-003, D-006

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.4.1 Spouse employment is asked only from married employees and registered partners subject to source tax**
  - **Given** the employee has stated their civil status and Lonio has determined whether the employee is subject to source tax
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax details
  - **Then** the question whether the spouse or registered partner is employed or receives replacement income is asked, and must be answered, only if the employee is subject to source tax and is 'Married' or in a 'Registered partnership'; separated employees count as single for source tax and are not asked, nor are single, divorced or widowed employees
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0137bf, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-hr_transcript-09b3fe, D-003_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's Swiss citizenship | Spouse lives in Switzerland | Spouse holds a C permit | → | Spouse / partner works or receives replacement income | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás - exempt through his Swiss wife living in Switzerland (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Married | Swiss | yes | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Married employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Married | – | – | – | → | required | ✅ |
  | Registered partner living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Registered partnership | – | – | – | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Separated employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Separated | – | – | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Divorced | – | – | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Married non-Swiss without C permit in Switzerland, spouse also non-Swiss without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Married | Not Swiss | – | no | → | asked | ✅ |

- **AC-05.4.2 Allowed answers for spouse employment**
  - **Given** the employee is asked whether the spouse or registered partner is employed or receives replacement income
  - **When** the employee answers it
  - **Then** the answer is 'yes' or 'no' and cannot be left empty; the question covers the spouse's or partner's employment and replacement income only (the employee's own income is always earned income and is not asked, D-006); the answer is recorded for HR as an input to the source tax tariff code
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7b637b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e740d1, G-10, D-006, D-034_
- **AC-05.4.3 Spouse employment sets the married tariff letter**
  - **Given** a married employee or registered partner subject to source tax has answered the spouse employment question
  - **When** Lonio determines the married tariff letter
  - **Then** the letter is C if the spouse or partner is employed or receives replacement income ('yes'), and B if not ('no'), as in the Zurich calculator
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Civil status | Spouse / partner works or receives replacement income | → | Base tariff letter for married employees | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, spouse works, living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | → | C | ✅ |
  | Registered partner, partner does not work, living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | Registered partnership | no | → | B | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-006** (product decision (Product owner)): A new employee always has earned income. Do not ask the income type, and drop the replacement-income questions and the tariff G / Q cases. This closes OQ-income-type. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
