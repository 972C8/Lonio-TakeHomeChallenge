# US-07.5 – Adult child in first education

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** Lonio to ask, for an adult child in first education, about a common household with the other parent, the main financial contributor and whether the employee provides more than 50% of the child's maintenance, and to derive whether the child counts, **so that** adult children in first education count in the tariff code exactly when the calculator counts them

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Common household with the other parent, Child: main financial contributor, Child: more than 50% of maintenance provided, Per-child result

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.5.1 Adult child in the household: common household with the other parent**
  - **Given** a child is an adult in first education and lives in the employee's household
  - **When** the employee fills in that child's block
  - **Then** Lonio asks whether the employee lives in a common household with the child's other parent (yes or no, required). Custody, shared custody, parental care and alternating custody are not asked for adult children.

  - **Given** an adult child in first education lives in the employee's household and the employee lives in a common household with the other parent
