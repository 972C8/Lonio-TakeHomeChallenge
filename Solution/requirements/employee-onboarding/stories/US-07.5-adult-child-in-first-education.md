# US-07.5 – Adult child in first education

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the household, common household, main-contributor and maintenance answers for each adult child in first education, **so that** the child counts, counts with tariff H or does not count exactly as the Zurich calculator decides

Priority **P1** – Adult children in first education change the child digit and can give tariff H; a wrong result gives a wrong tariff code (D-001). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b008fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cb54d9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cd1015, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3eb561, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, D-001, D-003, D-009, D-046

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Common household with the other parent, Child: main financial contributor, Child: more than 50% of maintenance provided, Per-child result

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.5.1 Questions for an adult child in first education**
  - **Given** a child block with age status 'Adult in first education'
  - **When** the employee continues in that block
  - **Then** if the child lives in the household, the employee must answer whether they live in a common household with the child's other parent (yes/no). With a common household, single, divorced, separated and widowed employees must say who makes the larger financial contribution ('Myself' or 'Other parent'); married employees and registered partners are not asked anything more. If the child does not live in the household, or lives there without a common household with the other parent, the employee must answer whether they mainly (more than 50%) provide for the child's maintenance (yes/no). Grouping of civil statuses per assumption D-003, to be verified by a domain expert.

- **AC-07.5.2 Per-child result for an adult child in first education**
  - **Given** a completed block for an adult child in first education
  - **When** Lonio works out the per-child result
  - **Then** in the household with a common household: counted for married employees and registered partners; for the single group counted with tariff H if the employee is the main contributor, otherwise not counted. In the household without a common household: with more than 50% maintenance counted (with H for the single group), otherwise not counted. Not in the household: counted with more than 50% maintenance, otherwise not counted, for both groups. Observed calculator results win (assumption D-046, to be verified by a domain expert); inferred results are compared with the calculator before go-live (D-009, see OQ-calculator-coverage).

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are out of scope for onboarding. Keep OQ-calculator-coverage open as a non-blocking go-live condition.
- **D-046** (product decision (Product owner)): For the per-child source tax result, observed calculator table rows win over the derived expression. Inferred rows stay under the D-009 go-live check. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72** (zh_source_tax_calculator): For a single/divorced/separated/widowed person, any child counted with H gives an H tariff; a child counts with H when it lives in the household and the person has sole custody or is the main financial contributor.
