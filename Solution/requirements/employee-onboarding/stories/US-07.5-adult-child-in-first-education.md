# US-07.5 – Adult child in first education

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** for an adult child in first education, to know whether the employee shares a household with the other parent and, where needed, whether the employee pays more than 50% of the child's maintenance or is the main financial contributor, **so that** it can be decided whether the adult child counts towards the tariff and, for employees who are not married, whether it leads to tariff H

Priority **P2** – Needed for a correct tariff, but only for employees subject to source tax with an adult child in first education; no source names a specific incident. · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b008fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cb54d9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cd1015, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3eb561, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Common household with the other parent, Pays more than 50% of the child's maintenance, Main financial contributor to the child's maintenance

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.5.1 In the household: common household with the other parent**
  - **Given** a child described as "Adult in first education" who lives in the same household (US-07.2)
  - **When** the block continues
  - **Then** the employee must answer "yes" or "no" to living in a common household with the child's other parent; no custody or alternating-custody question is asked for an adult child. With "yes", a married employee is asked nothing more for that child (the child counts), and an employee who is not married is asked who pays more of the child's maintenance
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3eb561_

- **AC-07.5.2 More than 50% of the maintenance**
  - **Given** a child described as "Adult in first education" who either does not live in the same household, or lives there without a common household with the other parent
  - **When** the block continues
  - **Then** the employee must answer "yes" or "no" to whether they mainly (more than 50%) pay for the child's maintenance; the child counts only with "yes". The common-household question is not asked for a child outside the household
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b008fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cb54d9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cd1015, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3eb561_
