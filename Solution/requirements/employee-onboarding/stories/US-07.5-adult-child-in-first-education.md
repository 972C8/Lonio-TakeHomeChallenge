# US-07.5 – Adult child in first education

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** Lonio to ask, for an adult child in first education, about a common household with the other parent, the larger financial contributor and whether the employee provides more than 50% of the maintenance, and to derive the child's result as the calculator does, **so that** adult children in first education count exactly where the calculator counts them

Priority **P1** – Lonio replaces HR's manual calculator step (D-001); a wrong per-child result gives a wrong tariff code. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b008fe, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cb54d9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cd1015, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3eb561, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, D-001, D-003, D-009, D-046

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child: common household with the other parent, Child: larger financial contributor, Child: more than 50% of maintenance provided, Per-child source tax result

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.5.1 Questions for an adult child in first education**
  - **Given** The child is an adult in first education
  - **When** the employee continues the child block
  - **Then** If the child lives in the household, the employee must answer whether they live in a common household with the other parent (yes/no). Employees who are single, divorced, widowed or separated and live with the other parent are asked who makes the larger financial contribution ('Me' or 'The other parent'). Whether the employee mainly (more than 50%) provides for the child's maintenance (yes/no) is asked when the child lives outside the household or there is no common household with the other parent. Custody and alternating custody are not asked. Every answer is required when asked. Registered partnership counts as married and Separated as single (assumption D-003, to be verified by a domain expert).
