## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.5.1 Adult child in the household: common household with the other parent**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax has a child who is an "Adult in first education" and lives in their household
  - **When** they continue with that child
  - **Then** They must answer "yes" or "no" to whether they live in a common household with the child's other parent. No custody questions are asked. With "yes": for married employees and registered partners the child counts and no further question is asked. Single, divorced, separated or widowed employees are asked who makes the larger financial contribution ("Employee" or "Other parent"): with "Employee" the child counts with H, with "Other parent" it does not count. The single-group results were not observed in the calculator (see OQ-calculator-coverage).

- **AC-07.5.2 Adult child in the household without a common household: more than 50% of maintenance**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax has an adult child in first education in their household and does not live in a common household with the other parent
  - **When** they continue with that child
  - **Then** They must answer "yes" or "no" to whether they pay more than 50% of the child's maintenance. With "yes": single, divorced, separated or widowed employees count the child with H, married employees and registered partners count it. With "no" the child does not count. Only the single "yes" result was observed in the calculator; the others must be checked before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).

- **AC-07.5.3 Adult child outside the household: more than 50% of maintenance**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax has an adult child in first education who does not live in their household
  - **When** they continue with that child
  - **Then** The common-household question is not asked. They must answer "yes" or "no" to whether they pay more than 50% of the child's maintenance. With "yes" the child counts, without H, whatever the civil status. With "no" it does not count.
