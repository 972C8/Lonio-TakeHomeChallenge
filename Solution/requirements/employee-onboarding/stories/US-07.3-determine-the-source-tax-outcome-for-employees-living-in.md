# US-07.3 – Determine the source tax outcome for employees living in Liechtenstein

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Employees living abroad (Germany, Liechtenstein)](../feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad).

**As** HR, **I want** employees living in Liechtenstein to say whether they return home daily and, if not, whether they expect more than 45 work-related non-return days, **so that** Lonio records either 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement', flagged for me, or the normal tariff code, as the Zurich calculator does

Priority **P1** – Employees living abroad are always asked the source tax questions (F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, D-007). Without these answers Lonio cannot give the Liechtenstein result that the calculator shows, and HR would have to go back to the calculator. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-981b6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a2041b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e96210, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0ff846, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f4ece9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e80d8f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab4e1e, F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, D-001, D-007, D-034, D-035, D-037

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad) and [information.md](../information.md)): Daily return to the home in Liechtenstein, More than 45 work-related non-return days expected, Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.3.1 Daily return is asked only of employees living in Liechtenstein**
  - **Given** the employee's home address country is Liechtenstein, so they are subject to source tax and get the source tax questions
  - **When** the employee reaches the 'Cross-border commuting' step, after the children
  - **Then** the employee must say whether they expect to return daily to their main residence in Liechtenstein (yes or no). Employees living anywhere else are not asked. The answer is recorded for the source tax result.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-981b6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a2041b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, D-007, D-035_

  | Example | Country (home address) | → | Daily return to the home in Liechtenstein | Daily return to the home in Germany | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | required | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | not asked | – | ✅ |

- **AC-07.3.2 Non-return days are asked only without daily return**
  - **Given** an employee living in Liechtenstein has answered the daily return question
  - **When** the employee continues the cross-border questions
  - **Then** only if they do not return daily must they say whether they expect more than 45 work-related non-return days in the calendar year, yes or no. This is a declaration of what they expect for the year; no proof is asked and no document is uploaded.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e96210, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0ff846, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f4ece9, D-035_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Daily return to the home in Liechtenstein | → | More than 45 work-related non-return days expected | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | No daily return (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | no | → | required | ✅ |
  | Daily return (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | yes | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-07.3.3 In principle no source tax liability or normal tariff code**
  - **Given** an employee living in Liechtenstein has answered the cross-border questions that apply to them
  - **When** Lonio determines the source tax result
  - **Then** if the employee returns daily or expects at most 45 non-return days, the result is 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, recorded with a flag for HR. If they expect more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code applies (e.g. C1Y), never a cross-border letter. How HR handles the flag belongs to the later HR-side spec.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e80d8f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab4e1e, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-52b67a, D-007, D-034_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Daily return to the home in Liechtenstein | More than 45 work-related non-return days expected | Daily return to the home in Germany | Certificate of residence | → | Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability | Cross-border tariff applies | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daily return (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | yes | – | – | – | → | yes | – | ✅ |
  | No daily return, at most 45 non-return days (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | no | no | – | – | → | yes | – | ✅ |
  | No daily return, more than 45 non-return days (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | no | yes | – | – | → | no | no | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | yes | yes | → | no | – | ✅ |

- **AC-07.3.4 Result follows the latest answers**
  - **Given** an employee living in Liechtenstein has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** their situation changes later (e.g. a move or a different expected number of non-return days)
  - **Then** the employee cannot change the answers, which are read-only after submission. HR records the change (part of the later HR-side spec), and the Liechtenstein result or tariff code is derived again from the latest answers.
  - _Sources: D-037, D-034_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-007** (product decision (Product owner)): Liechtenstein residents are asked the source tax questions (HR's rule decides who is asked). The result follows the calculator - with daily return or at most 45 non-return days, Lonio shows 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR; with more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code. This closes OQ-liechtenstein-conflict. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-035** (product decision (Product owner)): For the cross-border questions the employee states what they expect for the year (non-return days). For the certificate of residence 'not yet' is a valid answer and counts as 'no' for the tariff code. Documents go to HR outside Lonio; no upload.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
