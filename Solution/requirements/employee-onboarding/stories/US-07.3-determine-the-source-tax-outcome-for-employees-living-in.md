# US-07.3 – Determine the source tax outcome for employees living in Liechtenstein

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Employees living abroad (Germany, Liechtenstein)](../feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad).

**As** HR, **I want** employees living in Liechtenstein to state whether they expect to return home daily and, if not, whether they expect more than 45 work-related non-return days in the year, and the result to be recorded as the calculator gives it, **so that** HR knows whether the employee is in principle not liable to source tax under the double taxation agreement (flagged for HR) or gets a normal tariff code subject to that agreement, without clicking through the calculator

Priority **P1** – Lonio replaces HR's calculator step (D-001); a Liechtenstein employee would otherwise get a wrong tariff code or a wrong deduction, and HR's own rule conflicts with the calculator here (D-007). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-981b6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a2041b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e80d8f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e96210, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0ff846, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f4ece9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab4e1e, D-001, D-007, D-034, D-035

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-living-abroad) and [information.md](../information.md)): Daily return to home in Liechtenstein, More than 45 work-related non-return days (Liechtenstein), Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.3.1 Liechtenstein residents are asked about daily return and non-return days**
  - **Given** the employee's home address is in Liechtenstein (which, by HR's rule, makes them subject to source tax)
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax questions
  - **Then** the employee must state, yes or no, whether they expect to return daily to their main residence in Liechtenstein; only if not, they must state, yes or no, whether they expect more than 45 work-related non-return days in the calendar year; employees living elsewhere are not asked. The answers are recorded for source tax
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-981b6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a2041b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e22d17, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e96210, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-0ff846, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f4ece9, D-007, D-035_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Daily return to home in Liechtenstein | → | Daily return to home in Liechtenstein | More than 45 work-related non-return days (Liechtenstein) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Returns daily (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | yes | → | required | not asked | ✅ |
  | Does not return daily (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | no | → | – | required | ✅ |
  | Lives in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | – | → | not asked | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-07.3.2 Liechtenstein result follows the calculator**
  - **Given** an employee living in Liechtenstein has answered the Liechtenstein questions
  - **When** Lonio determines the source tax result
  - **Then** with a daily return, or with at most 45 non-return days, the result is 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, and it is flagged for HR (HR's handling of the flag is specified later); with more than 45 non-return days the normal tariff code applies, subject to the double taxation agreement. For employees not living in Liechtenstein this rule does not apply
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e80d8f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab4e1e, D-007, D-034_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Daily return to home in Liechtenstein | More than 45 work-related non-return days (Liechtenstein) | Daily return to home in Germany | Certificate of residence provided | → | Liechtenstein: in principle no source tax liability | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daily return -> in principle no liability (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e80d8f) | Liechtenstein | yes | – | – | – | → | yes | ✅ |
  | At most 45 non-return days (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab4e1e) | Liechtenstein | no | no | – | – | → | yes | ✅ |
  | More than 45 non-return days -> C1Y subject to the agreement (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab4e1e) | Liechtenstein | no | yes | – | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Lives in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | yes | yes | → | no | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-007** (product decision (Product owner)): Liechtenstein residents are asked the source tax questions (HR's rule decides who is asked). The result follows the calculator - with daily return or at most 45 non-return days, Lonio shows 'in principle no source tax liability, subject to the double taxation agreement' instead of a tariff code, flagged for HR; with more than 45 non-return days, the normal tariff code. This closes OQ-liechtenstein-conflict. To be verified by a domain expert.
