# US-08.2 – Germany: daily return and certificate of residence

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence).

**As** source tax office, **I want** an employee living in Germany to be asked whether they expect to return daily to their main residence in Germany and, if so, whether they have a certificate of residence for HR, **so that** Lonio can decide, as the calculator does, whether the cross-border tariff applies and HR knows when a certificate is still expected

Priority **P1** – The answer changes the tariff letter (e.g. C1Y becomes N1Y in the calculator); without it Lonio cannot compute the tariff code it now owns (D-001). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-69edd5, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-687e8b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-823151, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5f6820, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, D-001, D-034, D-035, D-037, D-051

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence) and [information.md](../information.md)): Daily return to Germany, Certificate of residence handed in, Cross-border tariff applies (Germany)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.2.1 Daily return to Germany is asked of every Germany resident**
  - **Given** the employee's home address is in Germany
  - **When** the employee reaches step 5 'Cross-border commuting'
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether they expect to return daily to their main residence in Germany, answering 'yes' or 'no'; the answer is required and is recorded for source tax; it is a statement of what the employee expects, no document is asked; employees living in any other country are not asked
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-69edd5, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, D-035, D-047_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Daily return to Germany | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | → | required | ✅ |
  | Living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-08.2.2 Certificate of residence is asked only with daily return**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Germany
  - **When** the employee answers that they return daily
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether they have a certificate of residence for HR, answering 'yes', 'no' or 'not yet'; the answer is required and is recorded for source tax and for HR, so HR knows a certificate is still expected; the certificate itself goes to HR outside Lonio and is not uploaded; when the employee does not return daily the question is not asked
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-687e8b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-823151, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5f6820, D-035, D-034_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Daily return to Germany | → | Certificate of residence handed in | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator: certificate question after daily return 'Ja' (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-5f6820) | Germany | yes | → | required | ✅ |
  | No daily return - no certificate question (illustrative) | Germany | no | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-08.2.3 Cross-border tariff with daily return depends on the certificate**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Germany and returns daily
  - **When** Lonio decides whether the cross-border tariff applies
  - **Then** with a certificate of residence the cross-border tariff applies; with 'no' or 'not yet' the normal tariff applies ('not yet' counts as 'no', D-035); the cross-border letters follow the calculator in full (A to L, B to M, C to N, H to P, G to Q; assumption D-051, to be verified by a domain expert); if the certificate is handed in later, HR changes the answer after submission and the tariff code is derived again (D-037)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, D-035, D-037, D-051, D-001_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Daily return to Germany | Certificate of residence handed in | → | Cross-border tariff applies (Germany) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator: daily return and certificate gives N1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-993db3) | Germany | yes | yes | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Calculator: daily return without certificate gives C1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-993db3) | Germany | yes | no | → | no | ✅ |
  | New hire without certificate yet (illustrative) | Germany | yes | not yet | → | no | ✅ |

- **AC-08.2.4 Only Germany residents can get the cross-border tariff**
  - **Given** the employee does not live in Germany
  - **When** Lonio decides whether the cross-border tariff applies
  - **Then** the cross-border tariff never applies; the normal tariff applies (for Liechtenstein residents subject to the double taxation agreement rule)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cc19c7_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Daily return to Liechtenstein | More than 45 work-related non-return days (Liechtenstein) | → | Cross-border tariff applies (Germany) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator: 'Anderes Land' gives the base tariff (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-f86c6b) | France | – | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | – | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Calculator: Liechtenstein over 45 days gives the normal tariff (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-cc19c7) | Liechtenstein | no | yes | → | no | ✅ |

_Trace check: partial – AC-08.2.4 says the cross-border tariff never applies to any non-Germany resident, but the cited facts only back this for Switzerland, other countries and Liechtenstein with over 45 non-return days, not for a Liechtenstein resident who returns daily._


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-035** (product decision (Product owner)): For the cross-border questions the employee states what they expect for the year (non-return days). For the certificate of residence 'not yet' is a valid answer and counts as 'no' for the tariff code. Documents go to HR outside Lonio; no upload.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-051** (product decision (Product owner)): The full tariff code is one text such as 'N1Y' (letter, child digit, church-tax suffix). When no tariff code is needed the result is the text 'no tariff code'. The cross-border letter mapping follows the calculator fact in full, including G to Q where the calculator shows it. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
