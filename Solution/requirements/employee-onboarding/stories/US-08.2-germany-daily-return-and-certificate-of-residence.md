# US-08.2 – Germany: daily return and certificate of residence

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees living in Germany to be asked whether they return daily to their main residence in Germany and, if they do, whether they have handed in a certificate of residence, **so that** the cross-border (Grenzgänger) tariff is applied only when the calculator's conditions are met, and the normal tariff is applied otherwise

Priority **P1** – Without these answers Lonio cannot compute the tariff code for employees living in Germany (D-001). HR cannot remember these questions today (F-hr_transcript-8595fb). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-69edd5, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-687e8b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-823151, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5f6820, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-66732c, F-hr_transcript-8595fb, D-001, D-035, D-037

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence) and [information.md](../information.md)): Returns daily to the home in Germany, Certificate of residence handed in, Cross-border (Grenzgänger) tariff applies

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.2.1 Daily return to Germany is asked only of employees living in Germany**
  - **Given** an employee is subject to source tax (everyone living abroad is)
  - **When** the country of their home address is Germany
  - **Then** The employee is asked whether they return daily to their main residence in Germany, answered 'yes' or 'no'. The answer is required. Employees living in any other country are not asked. The answer is recorded for source tax (the cross-border tariff decision).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-69edd5, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, D-001_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Returns daily to the home in Germany | Returns daily to the home in Liechtenstein | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Konstanz (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Germany | → | required | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | – | ✅ |

- **AC-08.2.2 Certificate of residence is asked only after a daily return**
  - **Given** an employee living in Germany is subject to source tax
  - **When** they answer that they return daily to their main residence in Germany
  - **Then** They are asked whether they have handed in a certificate of residence, answered 'yes', 'no' or 'not yet'. The answer is required. Employees who do not return daily are not asked. The certificate itself goes to HR outside Lonio; nothing is uploaded.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-687e8b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-823151, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5f6820, D-035_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Returns daily to the home in Germany | → | Certificate of residence handed in | Daily return reasonable (less than 100 km) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Germany resident returning daily (illustrative) | Germany | yes | → | required | not asked | ✅ |
  | Germany resident not returning daily (illustrative) | Germany | no | → | not asked | – | ✅ |

- **AC-08.2.3 Daily return with a certificate gives the cross-border tariff; without one, the normal tariff**
  - **Given** an employee living in Germany who is subject to source tax returns daily to their main residence in Germany
  - **When** Lonio computes the tariff code
  - **Then** If the certificate of residence has been handed in ('yes'), the cross-border tariff applies. If the answer is 'no' or 'not yet', the normal tariff applies. Employees living outside Germany always get the normal tariff from this rule. The cross-border result changes the tariff letter (A to L, B to M, C to N, H to P; see the tariff letter rule). The A/B/H paths are verified before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-66732c, D-001, D-035_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Returns daily to the home in Germany | Certificate of residence handed in | → | Cross-border (Grenzgänger) tariff applies | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator run: daily return, certificate handed in, result N1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3) | Germany | yes | yes | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Calculator run: daily return, no certificate, result C1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3) | Germany | yes | no | → | no | ✅ |
  | New hire without a certificate yet (illustrative) | Germany | yes | not yet | → | no | ✅ |
  | Tomás, lives in Winterthur (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | – | – | → | no | ✅ |

- **AC-08.2.4 A certificate handed in later is reflected in the tariff code**
  - **Given** an employee answered 'not yet' for the certificate of residence and submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** the answer is later changed to 'yes' (a change made by HR, specified in the later HR-side spec)
  - **Then** The cross-border tariff decision and the tariff code are derived again from the latest answers. The employee cannot change the answer after submission.
  - _Sources: D-035, D-037, D-034_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Returns daily to the home in Germany | Certificate of residence handed in | → | Cross-border (Grenzgänger) tariff applies | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Certificate later recorded as handed in (illustrative) | Germany | yes | yes | → | yes | ✅ |
