# US-08.2 – Germany: daily return and certificate of residence

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees who live in Germany and are subject to source tax to say whether they return daily to their home in Germany and, if they do, whether a certificate of residence (Ansässigkeitsbescheinigung) has been provided, **so that** the calculator can decide between the cross-border (Grenzgänger) tariff and the normal tariff

Priority **P1** – Without these answers the tariff of an employee living in Germany cannot be determined; HR names Germany residents (Konstanz) as a real case and cannot remember the German forms (F-hr_transcript-8595fb, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a). · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-69edd5, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-099cf0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5c099a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-532d5f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-687e8b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-823151, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5f6820, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2754e8, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, F-hr_transcript-8595fb

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-liechtenstein](../open-questions.md#oq-liechtenstein), [OQ-proof-documents](../open-questions.md#oq-proof-documents)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence) and [information.md](../information.md)): Returns daily to the home in Germany, Certificate of residence provided

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-08.2.1 Daily return to Germany is asked only of Germany residents**
  - **Given** the employee is subject to source tax (anyone living abroad is) and has answered the type of income
  - **When** the country of the home address is Germany
  - **Then** the employee must answer 'yes' or 'no' to whether they return daily to their main residence in Germany; employees living in any other country are not asked this question. The answer is needed by source tax only
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-69edd5, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-31d762, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac42, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Type of income (source tax) | Nationality | → | Employee is subject to source tax | Returns daily to the home in Germany | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Konstanz (F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Germany | Earned income | – | → | yes | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Liechtenstein | Earned income | – | → | – | not asked | ✅ |
  | Swiss employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | – | Switzerland | → | no | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-08.2.2 Replacement income asks about the time during or before receiving it**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Germany, is subject to source tax and has answered 'Replacement income' as type of income
  - **When** the daily-return question is shown
  - **Then** it asks whether the employee returned daily to their main residence in Germany during or before receiving the replacement income, and must be answered 'yes' or 'no'
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-099cf0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5c099a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-532d5f_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Type of income (source tax) | → | Returns daily to the home in Germany | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Germany resident with replacement income (illustrative) | Germany | Replacement income | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-08.2.3 Certificate of residence is asked only after a daily return**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Germany and is subject to source tax
  - **When** the employee answers that they return daily to their home in Germany
  - **Then** the employee must answer 'yes' or 'no' to whether a certificate of residence has been provided; with a certificate the cross-border tariff applies, without it the normal tariff. If the employee does not return daily, the certificate question is not asked. Whether a new employee can already answer this before starting, and to whom the certificate is provided, is open (see OQ-proof-documents). The answer is needed by source tax only
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-687e8b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-823151, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5f6820, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-993db3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2754e8_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Type of income (source tax) | Returns daily to the home in Germany | → | Certificate of residence provided | Daily return reasonable (under 100 km) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Germany resident returning daily (illustrative) | Germany | Earned income | yes | → | required | not asked | ✅ |
  | Germany resident not returning daily (illustrative) | Germany | Earned income | no | → | not asked | – | ✅ |
  | Germany resident with replacement income returning daily (illustrative) | Germany | Replacement income | yes | → | required | – | ✅ |
