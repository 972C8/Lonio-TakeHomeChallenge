# US-07.6 – Child digit of the tariff code and go-live check of the child paths

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to turn the per-child results into the child digit of the tariff code, and the child paths not yet observed in the calculator to be checked before go-live, **so that** I no longer click through the calculator per child and can rely on the computed tariff code

Priority **P1** – Lonio replaces HR's manual calculator step (D-001); unverified paths are a go-live condition (D-009). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c53e3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, D-001, D-009, D-037, D-044, D-046

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Number of counted children (child digit), Per-child result

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.6.1 Child digit is the number of counted children**
  - **Given** an employee for whom a tariff code is computed
  - **When** Lonio computes the child digit
  - **Then** the digit is the number of children whose result is 'counted' or 'counted with H', added up over all child blocks (for example three counted children give C3Y); a 'not counted' child adds nothing; without children the digit is 0. There is no maximum number of children (assumption D-044, to be verified by a domain expert); results with more than 3 children are checked against the calculator before go-live (D-009, see OQ-calculator-coverage). Each example below shows one child block.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c53e3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, D-044, D-009_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | Nationality | Holds a C permit | → | Number of counted children (child digit) | Per-child result | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | No children (illustrative) | Germany | Married | no | – | – | – | – | – | → | 0 | – | ✅ |
  | One counted child (married, sole custody) (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | – | → | 1 | – | ✅ |
  | One child counted with H (single, sole custody) (illustrative) | Switzerland | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | Portugal | no | → | 1 | counted with H | ✅ |
  | Child not counted (neither minor nor in first education) (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Neither | – | – | – | – | → | 0 | – | ✅ |

- **AC-07.6.2 Counted with H leads to tariff H for the single group**
  - **Given** a single, divorced, separated or widowed employee subject to source tax
  - **When** at least one child block has the result 'counted with H'
  - **Then** the tariff letter becomes H (the tariff letter itself is decided by the tariff letter rule); for married employees and registered partners no child result is 'counted with H'.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, D-003_
- **AC-07.6.3 Child results are always derived from the latest answers**
  - **Given** a submitted questionnaire
  - **When** a child answer or an answer it depends on (such as civil status) is changed later by HR
  - **Then** the per-child results and the child digit are derived again from the latest answers; the employee cannot change them after submission.
  - _Sources: D-037_
- **AC-07.6.4 Go-live check of the inferred child paths**
  - **Given** per-child results that are marked inferred (not observed in the calculator) and families with more than 3 children
  - **When** Lonio is prepared for go-live
  - **Then** a domain expert compares Lonio's results for these paths with the Zurich calculator before release; observed calculator results always win over the general rule (assumption D-046, to be verified by a domain expert). This is a non-blocking go-live condition, see OQ-calculator-coverage.
  - _Sources: D-009, D-046_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are out of scope for onboarding. Keep OQ-calculator-coverage open as a non-blocking go-live condition.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **D-044** (product decision (Product owner)): There is no maximum number of children. A child's age is judged on the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-046** (product decision (Product owner)): For the per-child source tax result, observed calculator table rows win over the derived expression. Inferred rows stay under the D-009 go-live check. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
