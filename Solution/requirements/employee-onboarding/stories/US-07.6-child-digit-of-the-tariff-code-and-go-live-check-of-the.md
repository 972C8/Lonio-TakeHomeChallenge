# US-07.6 – Child digit of the tariff code and go-live check of the child paths

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the child digit of the tariff code to be the number of children who count, with the child paths not observed in the calculator verified before go-live, **so that** the tariff code Lonio computes matches the calculator, without HR clicking through the calculator by hand

Priority **P1** – Lonio replaces HR's manual calculator step and owns the tariff result (D-001). D-009 makes the verification a go-live condition. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c53e3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-332e2a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4957f6, D-001, D-009, D-037

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.6.1 The child digit counts every child that counts, with or without H**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax has answered the children questions
  - **When** Lonio computes the tariff code
  - **Then** The child digit is the number of children whose result is 'counts' or 'counts with H' (for example, three children who count give the digit 3, as in C3Y). It is 0 if the employee has no children or no child counts. For single, divorced, separated or widowed employees, a child that counts with H is what makes the tariff letter H (see the tariff letter rule).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c53e3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, D-001_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | Adult child: employee pays more than 50% of maintenance | Child not in household: shared custody | → | Child digit of the tariff code | Per-child result | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | No children (illustrative) | Germany | Married | no | – | – | – | – | – | → | 0 | – | ✅ |
  | One child counting with H (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | – | → | 1 | counts with H | ✅ |
  | One child counting without H (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Adult in first education | no | – | yes | – | → | 1 | counts | ✅ |
  | One child that does not count (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | no | – | – | no | → | 0 | does not count | ✅ |

- **AC-07.6.2 The child digit always reflects the latest answers**
  - **Given** The children answers have been recorded and are later changed (after submission only HR can change them)
  - **When** the tariff code is derived again
  - **Then** The per-child results and the child digit are computed from the latest answers. No earlier result is kept as the valid one.
  - _Sources: D-037, D-001_
- **AC-07.6.3 Child paths not observed in the calculator are verified before go-live**
  - **Given** Some per-child results were inferred rather than observed in the calculator, and more than 3 children were never tested (see OQ-calculator-coverage, non-blocking)
  - **When** the computed tariff code is prepared for release
  - **Then** Before go-live a domain expert has compared Lonio's per-child results and child digit with the calculator for every inferred child path and for more than 3 children, and any difference has been resolved. Special tariffs such as D (second job) are not part of this check.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-332e2a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4957f6, D-009_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are out of scope for onboarding. Keep OQ-calculator-coverage open as a non-blocking go-live condition.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c** (zh_source_tax_calculator): A single/divorced/separated/widowed person without counted children with earned income and residence in Switzerland gets tariff A (A0Y/A0N).
