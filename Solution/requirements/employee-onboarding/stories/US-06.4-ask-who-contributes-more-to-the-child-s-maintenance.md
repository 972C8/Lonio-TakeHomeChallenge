# US-06.4 – Ask who contributes more to the child's maintenance

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the employee to say whether they or the other parent make the larger financial contribution to a child's maintenance, only where this decides the child result, **so that** children in alternating care, and children of single employees living with both parents, are counted as the calculator counts them

Priority **P1** – Decides whether the child counts and whether a single employee gets the H tariff (D-001). · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cdd0b6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, G-01, D-001, D-003

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child: who makes the larger financial contribution

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.4.1 Larger contributor asked only where it decides the result**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax describes a child
  - **When** there is alternating care for the child, or the employee counts as single and lives with the child and the child's other parent in a common household
  - **Then** the employee is asked, and must answer, who makes the larger financial contribution: 'The employee' or 'The other parent'. A married employee living in a common household with the other parent is not asked; neither is anyone with no alternating care.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cdd0b6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, G-01, D-003_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or of age and in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | Child: common household with the other parent | Child: alternating care | → | Child: who makes the larger financial contribution | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, common household with other parent (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | yes | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Single, common household with other parent (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | yes | – | → | required | ✅ |
  | Alternating care for a child in the household (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | no | yes | → | asked | ✅ |
  | No alternating care (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | no | no | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Single, child of age in first education living with both parents (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Of age and in first education | yes | – | yes | – | → | asked | ✅ |

For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married.
