# US-06.4 – Ask who contributes more to the child's maintenance

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** to know whether the employee or the other parent makes the larger financial contribution to a child's maintenance, but only where the calculator needs it, **so that** Lonio can decide whether a child in alternating care, or a child of a single parent living together with the other parent, counts for the tariff code, without asking anyone else about their finances

Priority **P1** – The answer decides whether the child counts and whether a single employee gets the H tariff; Lonio owns the tariff result (D-001). Asking it only where needed follows data minimisation (G-01). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cdd0b6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, G-01, D-001, D-003

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.4.1 Larger contributor asked with alternating care**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with a minor child
  - **When** the employee answers that the child is in alternating care
  - **Then** the employee is asked who makes the larger financial contribution to the child's maintenance: 'The employee' or 'The other parent' (required); without alternating care this question is not asked on that basis
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cdd0b6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's Swiss citizenship | Spouse holds a C permit | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody | Child: common household with the other parent | Child: alternating care | Child not in household: parental custody (single) | → | Child: larger financial contributor | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, child in household, alternating care (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Married | Not Swiss | no | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | no | yes | – | → | required | ✅ |
  | Single, child outside household, alternating care (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Single | – | – | yes | Minor | no | – | – | yes | yes | → | asked | ✅ |
  | No alternating care (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Married | Not Swiss | no | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | no | no | – | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.4.2 Larger contributor asked of single parents living with the other parent**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with a child in the household who lives in a common household with the child's other parent
  - **When** the employee has answered the common-household question
  - **Then** a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee is asked who makes the larger financial contribution ('The employee' or 'The other parent', required); a married employee or registered partner is not asked, because for them the common household already decides the result
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, D-003_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody | Child: common household with the other parent | Spouse's Swiss citizenship | Spouse holds a C permit | → | Child: larger financial contributor | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single, minor child, common household (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | yes | – | – | → | required | ✅ |
  | Single, child of age in first education, common household (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Single | yes | Of age and in first education | yes | – | yes | – | – | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Married, common household (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Married | yes | Of age and in first education | yes | – | yes | Not Swiss | no | → | not asked | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
