# US-06.4 – Ask who contributes more to the child's maintenance

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees subject to source tax to say whether they or the other parent make the larger financial contribution to a child's maintenance, where the calculator needs this, **so that** Lonio decides whether a child in alternating care, or a child of a non-married employee living with both parents, counts for the tariff code and whether it counts with H

Priority **P1** – This decides the child digit and the H tariff in the tariff code that Lonio now computes instead of HR (D-001). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cdd0b6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3058a4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-56e79b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-38dd4a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c2faa7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-001, D-003, D-009

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Larger financial contributor to the child's maintenance, Child result (per child)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.4.1 When the larger contributor is asked**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax is answering a child block
  - **When** the parents share care of the child in alternation, or the employee is not married for source tax (single, divorced, separated, widowed) and lives with the child and with the child's other parent
  - **Then** the employee is asked who makes the larger financial contribution to the child's maintenance: "Employee" or "Other parent". The answer is required and recorded for source tax. A married employee or registered partner who lives with the child and the other parent is not asked; the child block ends there.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cdd0b6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Parental custody (child in household) | Common household with the other parent | Alternating care | → | Larger financial contributor to the child's maintenance | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Alternating care, child in household (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | no | yes | → | required | ✅ |
  | Single, common household with other parent (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | yes | – | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Married, common household with other parent - not asked (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | yes | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Married, child of age in common household - not asked (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Of age and in first education | yes | – | yes | – | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.4.2 Result for married employees and registered partners with alternating care**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax who is married or in a registered partnership shares care of a minor child in alternation (child in the household without a common household with the other parent, or child outside the household with shared custody)
  - **When** Lonio derives the child's result
  - **Then** the child counts if the employee makes the larger contribution and does not count if the other parent does. The rows 'in household, employee contributes more' and 'outside household, other parent contributes more' were not observed in the calculator and are verified before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).

- **AC-06.4.3 Result for non-married employees**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax who is single, divorced, separated or widowed was asked who contributes more to a child's maintenance
  - **When** Lonio derives the child's result
  - **Then** the child counts with H if the employee makes the larger contribution and does not count if the other parent does. This applies to a minor child in the household with a common household with the other parent, a minor child in the household with alternating care, a minor child outside the household with parental custody and alternating care, and a child of age in first education living with the employee and the other parent. Rows not observed in the calculator (other parent contributes more with alternating care, and the child of age cases) are verified before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).
