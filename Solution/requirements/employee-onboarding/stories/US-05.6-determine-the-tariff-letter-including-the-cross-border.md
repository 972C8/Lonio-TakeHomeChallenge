## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.6.1 Base letter for the normal tariff**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax for whom the cross-border tariff does not apply
  - **When** Lonio determines the tariff letter
  - **Then** the letter is C (married, spouse works), B (married, spouse does not work), H (counts as single and at least one child counts as an H child) or A (counts as single, no H child). Tariffs G and Q for replacement income do not occur (D-006).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c78e0c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, D-001, D-003, D-006_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | Has biological or adopted children | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Child: minor or of age and in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | → | Tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, spouse works (illustrative) | France | Married | yes | no | – | – | – | – | – | → | C | ✅ |
  | Married, spouse does not work (illustrative) | France | Married | no | no | – | – | – | – | – | → | B | ✅ |
  | A0Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c78e0c) | Switzerland | Single | – | no | Germany | no | – | – | – | → | A | ✅ |
  | Separated, no children (counts as single) (illustrative) | France | Separated | – | no | – | – | – | – | – | → | A | ✅ |
  | Single with minor child in household, sole custody (illustrative) | France | Single | – | yes | – | – | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | H | ✅ |

- **AC-05.6.2 Cross-border letters for employees living in Germany**
  - **Given** an employee living in Germany for whom the cross-border tariff applies (see the cross-border section)
  - **When** Lonio determines the tariff letter
  - **Then** the base letter is replaced: A by L, B by M, C by N, H by P. If the cross-border tariff does not apply (e.g. certificate of residence 'no' or 'not yet'), the base letter is kept.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4d3ac0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-89a9f1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7154e, D-001, D-035_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Has biological or adopted children | Daily return to home in Germany | Certificate of residence provided | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | Child: minor or of age and in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | Daily return reasonable (less than 100 km) | More than 60 work-related non-return days (Germany) | → | Tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | L0Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7154e) | Germany | Single | no | yes | yes | – | – | – | – | – | – | → | L | ✅ |
  | M0N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7154e) | Germany | Married | no | yes | yes | no | – | – | – | – | – | → | M | ✅ |
  | N1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7154e) | Germany | Married | yes | yes | yes | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | – | → | N | ✅ |
  | P1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7154e) | Germany | Single | yes | yes | yes | – | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | – | → | P | ✅ |
  | C1Y without certificate (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4d3ac0) | Germany | Married | no | yes | not yet | yes | – | – | – | – | – | → | C | ✅ |
  | No daily return, reasonable, at most 60 non-return days (illustrative) | Germany | Single | no | no | – | – | – | – | – | yes | no | → | L | ✅ |

- **AC-05.6.3 Unexplored calculator paths are checked before go-live**
  - **Given** the tariff letter logic covers paths not observed in the calculator (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N, child-result rows marked inferred)
  - **When** Lonio is prepared for go-live
  - **Then** a domain expert has compared Lonio's computed letters for these paths with the calculator (see OQ-calculator-coverage, non-blocking for the spec, a go-live condition). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are not determined.
  - _Sources: D-009_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-006** (product decision (Product owner)): A new employee always has earned income. Do not ask the income type, and drop the replacement-income questions and the tariff G / Q cases. This closes OQ-income-type. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are out of scope for onboarding. Keep OQ-calculator-coverage open as a non-blocking go-live condition.
