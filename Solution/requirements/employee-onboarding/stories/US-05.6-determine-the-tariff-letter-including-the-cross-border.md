# US-05.6 – Determine the tariff letter, including the cross-border letters

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Source tax tariff basics and computed result](../feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to determine the Zurich tariff letter for every employee subject to source tax from the answers already given (civil status, spouse employment, single-parent situation and cross-border situation), **so that** I no longer have to click through the Zurich calculator with the employee's answers to find the tariff letter

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.6.1 Base letter for married employees and registered partners**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax is 'Married' or in a 'Registered partnership' and the cross-border tariff does not apply
  - **When** Lonio determines the tariff letter
  - **Then** the letter is C if the spouse or partner is employed or receives replacement income, and B otherwise
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's Swiss citizenship | Spouse holds a C permit | Spouse / partner works or receives replacement income | Daily return to the home in Germany | Daily return reasonable (less than 100 km) | → | Source tax tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married non-Swiss without C permit in Switzerland, spouse works (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Married | Not Swiss | no | yes | – | – | → | C | ✅ |
  | Registered partner in Switzerland, partner does not work (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Registered partnership | Not Swiss | no | no | – | – | → | B | ✅ |
  | Married, living in Germany, daily return not reasonable (normal tariff) (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Married | – | – | yes | no | no | → | C | ✅ |

- **AC-05.6.2 Base letter for single, divorced, widowed and separated employees**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax is 'Single', 'Divorced', 'Widowed' or 'Separated' (separated counts as single for source tax) and the cross-border tariff does not apply
  - **When** Lonio determines the tariff letter
  - **Then** the letter is H if at least one child counts with H (single-parent tariff), and A otherwise
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c78e0c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Has children | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody | → | Source tax tariff letter | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single without children in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Single | no | – | – | – | – | → | A | ✅ |
  | Separated without children in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Separated | no | 12.05.1985 | – | – | – | → | A | ✅ |
  | Divorced with a minor child in the household under sole custody (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Divorced | yes | – | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | H | ✅ |

- **AC-05.6.3 Cross-border letters for employees living in Germany**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax lives in Germany and the cross-border tariff applies (daily return with a certificate of residence, or no daily return although a return is reasonable and not more than 60 non-return days are expected); a certificate answered 'not yet' counts as 'no'

  - **Then** the base letter is replaced by its cross-border letter: A becomes L, B becomes M, C becomes N, H becomes P; in every other case the base letter is kept

- **AC-05.6.4 No tariff letter for employees not subject to source tax; unverified paths**
  - **Given** an employee is not subject to source tax, or the employee's answers follow a calculator path that was not explored
  - **When** Lonio determines the tariff letter
  - **Then** employees not subject to source tax get no tariff letter; special tariffs such as D (second job) and the replacement-income tariffs G and Q are never produced (D-006, D-009); the paths not explored in the calculator (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N, the full combination of paths) are compared with the calculator by a domain expert before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage); child answer combinations whose outcome is not yet known stay undetermined until verified (see OQ-child-paths-undetermined)
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-332e2a, D-006, D-009_
