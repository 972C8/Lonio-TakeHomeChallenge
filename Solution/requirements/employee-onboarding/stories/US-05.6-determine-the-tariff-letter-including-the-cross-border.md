# US-05.6 – Determine the tariff letter, including the cross-border letters

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Source tax tariff basics and computed result](../feature.md#SEC-05-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** Lonio to determine the tariff letter of every employee subject to source tax from their answers, following the Zurich calculator. That is C, B, H or A, or the cross-border letter (N, M, P or L) where the cross-border tariff applies., **so that** the letter of the computed tariff code matches what the calculator would give, without HR clicking through it

Priority **P1** – The letter is the core of the tariff code that replaces HR's manual calculator step (F-hr_transcript-563a9f, D-001). A wrong letter gives a wrong source tax deduction. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c78e0c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4d3ac0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-89a9f1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7154e, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b948fd, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-bc1639, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-52b67a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-001, D-003, D-006, D-009, D-035

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.6.1 Base letter without the cross-border tariff**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax for whom the cross-border tariff does not apply. This covers residents of Switzerland, of countries other than Germany and Liechtenstein, and of Germany where the cross-border conditions are not met.
  - **When** Lonio determines the tariff letter
  - **Then** married employees and registered partners get C if the spouse or partner works and B if not. Non-married employees (single, divorced, widowed, separated) get H if at least one child counts with H, otherwise A. There is no tariff G or Q, because replacement income does not occur for a new employee (D-006).

- **AC-05.6.2 Cross-border letters for employees living in Germany**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax who lives in Germany and has answered the cross-border questions
  - **When** Lonio determines the tariff letter
  - **Then** if the cross-border tariff applies, the base letter is replaced: A becomes L, B becomes M, C becomes N, H becomes P. The cross-border tariff applies after a daily return with a certificate of residence, or without a daily return when a daily return is reasonable and the employee does not expect more than 60 non-return days. Otherwise the base letter stays. A certificate answered "not yet" counts as no (D-035).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4d3ac0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-89a9f1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7154e, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b948fd, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-bc1639, D-001, D-035_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-006** (product decision (Product owner)): A new employee always has earned income. Do not ask the income type, and drop the replacement-income questions and the tariff G / Q cases. This closes OQ-income-type. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are out of scope for onboarding. Keep OQ-calculator-coverage open as a non-blocking go-live condition.
- **D-035** (product decision (Product owner)): For the cross-border questions the employee states what they expect for the year (non-return days). For the certificate of residence 'not yet' is a valid answer and counts as 'no' for the tariff code. Documents go to HR outside Lonio; no upload.
