# US-04.1 – Ask nationality and C permit only of employees living in Switzerland

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Source tax liability check](../feature.md#SEC-04-source-tax-liability).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to ask employees living in Switzerland for their nationality and, if they are not Swiss, only whether they hold a C permit, **so that** I know for each new employee whether they could be subject to source tax, without collecting nationality or permit data that no party needs

Priority **P1** – HR today never knows up front who has to answer the source tax questions (F-hr_transcript-b84498); without these facts the source tax liability cannot be decided and the tariff code cannot be computed. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-hr_transcript-35fae3, F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-4ad146, F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, G-01, D-005, D-008, D-010

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.1.1 Nationality is asked only of employees living in Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee has entered their home address
  - **When** the home address country is Switzerland
  - **Then** the employee is asked for their nationality (countries by English name) and must answer it; employees living abroad are not asked, because they are subject to source tax whatever their nationality and get the permit reminder anyway. Whether the specific nationality or only 'Swiss citizen yes/no' is needed stays open (see OQ-nationality-scope).
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, F-hr_transcript-1a20bb, F-hr_transcript-35fae3, G-01, D-010_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | → | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | → | required | – | ✅ |
  | Stefan, Swiss living abroad (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-1a20bb) (illustrative) | Germany | → | not asked | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | not asked | – | ✅ |

- **AC-04.1.2 Swiss citizenship counts even with another nationality**
  - **Given** an employee living in Switzerland holds Swiss citizenship, alone or together with another nationality
  - **When** they answer the nationality question
  - **Then** they answer Switzerland, they count as Swiss and are not asked about a C permit
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, D-008_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Nationality | → | Holds a C permit | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Swiss (or dual Swiss) employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-04.1.3 Only 'C permit yes/no' is asked of non-Swiss employees living in Switzerland**
  - **Given** the employee lives in Switzerland and is not Swiss
  - **When** they have answered the nationality question
  - **Then** they are asked whether they hold a C permit (permanent residence), answered yes or no, and must answer it; no other permit type is asked of anyone
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-845204, F-hr_transcript-4ad146, D-005, G-01_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Nationality | → | Holds a C permit | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-d7431e) | Switzerland | Brazil | → | required | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss employee living abroad (illustrative) | Germany | – | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-04.1.4 Who the answers are recorded for**
  - **Given** the employee has answered nationality and, where asked, the C permit question
  - **When** the questionnaire is submitted
  - **Then** the answers are recorded for HR, for source tax (deciding whether the employee is subject to source tax) and, for nationality, for deciding whether the permit reminder is shown
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b6d9b3, D-010, D-034_

## Evidence
- **D-005** (product decision (Product owner)): Do not ask for the permit type. Ask non-Swiss employees only whether they hold a C permit (permanent residence), yes or no, and the same for a non-Swiss spouse. No party needs any other permit type. This closes OQ-permit-list. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-008** (product decision (Product owner)): Anyone with Swiss citizenship counts as Swiss for source tax, even with another nationality. This closes OQ-dual-nationality. To be verified by a domain expert.
