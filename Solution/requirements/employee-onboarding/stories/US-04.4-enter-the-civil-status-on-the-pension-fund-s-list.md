# US-04.4 – Enter the civil status on the pension fund's list

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** the employee's civil status expressed as one of my four values: Single, Married, Divorced or Widowed, **so that** every employee can be registered, including registered partners and separated employees, whose status is not on my list

Priority **P1** – The fund's civil status field is required and offers only four values. Without a mapping, a registered partner such as Daniel or a separated employee cannot be registered, and HR does not know what the fund wants in that case. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-pension_portal-8664f4, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, D-003, D-004, D-037, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Civil status, Civil status for the pension fund

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.4.1 Statuses on the fund's list are entered unchanged**
  - **Given** the employee's civil status is Single, Married, Divorced or Widowed
  - **When** Lonio determines the civil status for the pension fund
  - **Then** the fund gets the same civil status the employee gave
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status for the pension fund | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | Single | ✅ |
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Married | → | Married | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee (illustrative) | Divorced | → | Divorced | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee (illustrative) | Widowed | → | Widowed | ✅ |

- **AC-04.4.2 Registered partnership and separated are entered as Married**
  - **Given** the employee's civil status is Registered partnership or Separated
  - **When** Lonio determines the civil status for the pension fund
  - **Then** the fund gets Married, together with the spouse's or registered partner's date of birth that the employee gave. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert: HR does not know how the fund handles a registered partnership, and the fund's list has neither status.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-f197d6, F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, D-003, D-004_

  | Example | Civil status | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | → | Civil status for the pension fund | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel, registered partnership (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | 14.03.1988 | → | Married | required | ✅ |
  | Separated employee (illustrative) | Separated | 02.11.1985 | → | Married | required | ✅ |

- **AC-04.4.3 Always derived from the latest answer**
  - **Given** the employee's civil status is changed after submission (by HR, for example after a marriage or a separation)
  - **When** the civil status for the pension fund is needed again
  - **Then** it is derived again from the latest civil status
  - _Sources: D-037_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-004** (product decision (Product owner)): The spouse's (or registered partner's) date of birth is asked from married employees, registered partners and separated employees (all entered at the fund as Married, see D-003), not from divorced or widowed employees; no spouse data is sent to the fund for them. This closes OQ-spouse-dob-other-status and OQ-separated-spouse-dob. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **D-045** (product decision (Product owner)): Business text (stories, acceptance criteria, notices) uses no internal snake_case ids and does not speak of 'expressions'. Any behaviour that has no cited fact or decision is stated in the story as an assumption. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
