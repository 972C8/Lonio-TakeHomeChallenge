# US-04.4 – Enter the civil status on the pension fund's list

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** to receive the employee's civil status as one of the four values my registration form accepts, **so that** the employee can be registered for occupational pension without HR having to interpret the answer

Priority **P1** – The fund's civil status field is required and accepts only Single, Married, Divorced or Widowed. Any other value blocks the registration. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-pension_portal-8664f4, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, F-pension_portal-6d3630, D-003, D-004, D-037

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-fund-divorced-widowed](../open-questions.md#oq-fund-divorced-widowed)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Civil status, Civil status for the pension fund

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.4.1 Civil status mapped to the fund's four values**
  - **Given** an employee has answered the civil status question
  - **When** Lonio works out the civil status for the pension fund
  - **Then** Single, Married, Divorced and Widowed stay as they are. Registered partnership and Separated are recorded as Married, together with the spouse's or partner's date of birth. HR did not know how the fund handles a registered partnership, so this is an assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-003). Whether the fund needs an extra field for Divorced or Widowed is still open (see OQ-fund-divorced-widowed). The result is always worked out again from the latest civil status, including after HR changes it (D-037).

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-004** (product decision (Product owner)): The spouse's (or registered partner's) date of birth is asked from married employees, registered partners and separated employees (all entered at the fund as Married, see D-003), not from divorced or widowed employees; no spouse data is sent to the fund for them. This closes OQ-spouse-dob-other-status and OQ-separated-spouse-dob. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
