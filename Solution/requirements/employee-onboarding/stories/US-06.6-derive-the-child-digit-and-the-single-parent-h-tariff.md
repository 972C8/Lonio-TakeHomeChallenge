# US-06.6 – Derive the child digit and the single-parent H tariff

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to derive the child digit of the tariff code and whether a single employee gets the H tariff from the per-child results, **so that** the computed tariff code recorded for HR matches the Zurich calculator and HR no longer clicks through the calculator by hand

Priority **P1** – HR's manual calculator step is replaced by Lonio's computed tariff code (D-001, F-hr_transcript-4e180e); a wrong digit or letter means a wrong source tax deduction. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ecef37, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c78e0c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-332e2a, F-hr_transcript-4e180e, D-001, D-003, D-009, D-037

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.6.1 Child digit equals the number of children that count**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax
  - **When** Lonio computes the tariff code
  - **Then** the child digit is the number of children that count, including H children (for example three counting children of a married employee with an employed spouse give C3Y, and one counting and one non-counting child give digit 1); with no children or none counting the digit is 0. Results with more than 3 children are verified against the calculator before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage). The digit is always derived again from the latest answers.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ecef37, D-001, D-009, D-037_

- **AC-06.6.2 H tariff for single parents with an H child**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax
  - **When** Lonio decides the tariff letter
  - **Then** a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee gets the H tariff if at least one child counts as an H child (for example one counting child and one H child give H2); a single employee without such a child keeps tariff A; a married employee or registered partner never gets the H tariff

- **AC-06.6.3 No tariff code while a child's result is not determined**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with at least one child whose answers lead to a combination the calculator exploration did not show
  - **When** Lonio computes the source tax result
  - **Then** the child's result is 'not determined' and Lonio records that the tariff code cannot be computed yet instead of a possibly wrong code; the missing outcomes are filled in after a domain expert runs these paths in the calculator before go-live (see OQ-child-paths-undetermined)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-332e2a, D-001, D-009_
