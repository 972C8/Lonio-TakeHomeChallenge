# US-06.6 – Derive the child digit and the single-parent H tariff

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** Lonio to derive the child digit of the tariff code and whether a non-married employee gets an H tariff from the results of all child blocks, **so that** the computed tariff code matches the calculator without HR going through it

Priority **P1** – The child digit and the H letter are part of every tariff code that Lonio now computes instead of HR (D-001). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ecef37, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c78e0c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-562299, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-001, D-003, D-009, D-037

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Has children, Child digit of the tariff code, H tariff for non-married employees

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.6.1 Child digit is the number of children who count**
  - **Given** an employee is subject to source tax
  - **When** Lonio derives the tariff code from the latest answers
  - **Then** the child digit is 0 if the employee has no children. Otherwise it is the number of children whose result is 'Counted' or 'Counted with H' (0 if none counts). For example, three counting children of a married employee with an employed spouse give C3Y, and one H child plus one non-counting child give H1N. Codes with more than 3 children have not been compared with the calculator and are checked before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ecef37, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c78e0c, D-001, D-009, D-037_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Parental custody (child in household) | → | Child digit of the tariff code | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | No children (illustrative) | Germany | Married | no | – | – | – | → | 0 | ✅ |
  | One child counted (married, sole custody) (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | 1 | ✅ |
  | One child counted with H (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | 1 | ✅ |
  | Only child does not count (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Neither | – | – | → | 0 | ✅ |

- **AC-06.6.2 H tariff for non-married employees with an H child**
  - **Given** an employee is subject to source tax
  - **When** Lonio derives the tariff letter from the latest answers
  - **Then** an employee who is single, divorced, separated or widowed (not married for source tax) and has at least one child counted with H gets an H tariff. One H child is enough even if other children count without H (e.g. A1 + H1 gives H2N). A non-married employee without an H child gets no H tariff, even if a child counts. A married employee or registered partner never gets an H tariff.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Parental custody (child in household) | Child mainly maintained by the employee (more than 50%) | → | H tariff for non-married employees | Child digit of the tariff code | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single, child with sole custody in household (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | → | yes | – | ✅ |
  | Single, child of age outside household counted without H (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Of age and in first education | no | – | yes | → | no | 1 | ✅ |
  | Single, no children (illustrative) | Germany | Single | no | – | – | – | – | → | no | – | ✅ |
  | Married, child with sole custody (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | → | no | – | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are out of scope for onboarding. Keep OQ-calculator-coverage open as a non-blocking go-live condition.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
