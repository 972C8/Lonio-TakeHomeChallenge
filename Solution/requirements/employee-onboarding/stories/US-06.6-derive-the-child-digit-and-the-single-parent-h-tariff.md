# US-06.6 – Derive the child digit and the single-parent H tariff

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to derive the tariff code's child digit and, for single employees, the H tariff from the per-child results, **so that** I no longer click through the calculator to find the tariff code; it is recorded for me from the employee's answers

Priority **P1** – Replaces HR's manual calculator step (D-001); the digit and H letter are part of every tariff code. · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ecef37, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-562299, D-001, D-003, D-009

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child digit of the tariff code, Single-parent H tariff

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.6.1 Child digit counts the children that count**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has answered the child questions
  - **When** the tariff code is derived
  - **Then** the child digit equals the number of children whose result is 'counts' or 'counts as H child' (e.g. three counting children of a married employee whose spouse works give C3Y); children that do not count add nothing; an employee without children gets 0. The result is recorded for HR.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ecef37, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-987091, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or of age and in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | → | Child digit of the tariff code | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, sole custody child counts (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22) | Germany | Married | yes | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | 1 | ✅ |
  | Single, H child adds one (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22) | Germany | Single | – | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | → | 1 | ✅ |
  | Child neither minor nor in education adds nothing (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-987091) | Germany | Married | no | yes | Neither | – | – | → | 0 | ✅ |

- **AC-06.6.2 H tariff for single employees with an H child**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has answered the child questions
  - **When** the employee counts as single and at least one child counts as an H child
  - **Then** the tariff letter is H (e.g. one child counting and one H child give H2N); a single employee whose children only count (not as H child) keeps tariff A; an employee counting as married never gets H.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-562299, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or of age and in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody (shared or sole) | Child: employee covers more than 50% of maintenance | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | → | Single-parent H tariff | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single, sole custody -> H1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | – | → | yes | ✅ |
  | Single, adult child outside household -> A1N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-562299) | Germany | Single | yes | Of age and in first education | no | – | yes | – | → | no | ✅ |
  | Married, sole custody -> C1Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | yes | → | no | ✅ |

- **AC-06.6.3 Unexplored child paths checked before go-live**
  - **Given** the child result rows marked inferred, more than 3 children and the H3N/P3N codes were not observed in the calculator
  - **When** the questionnaire is prepared for go-live
  - **Then** a domain expert has compared Lonio's computed tariff codes with the calculator for these paths (see OQ-calculator-coverage); this does not block writing the spec.
  - _Sources: D-009, D-001_
