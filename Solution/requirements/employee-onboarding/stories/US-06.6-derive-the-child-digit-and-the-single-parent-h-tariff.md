# US-06.6 – Derive the child digit and the single-parent H tariff

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** HR, **I want** Lonio to derive the child digit of the tariff code and whether a single employee gets the H tariff from the per-child results, **so that** the computed tariff code recorded for HR matches the Zurich calculator and HR no longer clicks through the calculator by hand

Priority **P1** – HR's manual calculator step is replaced by Lonio's computed tariff code (D-001, F-hr_transcript-4e180e); a wrong digit or letter means a wrong source tax deduction. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ecef37, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c78e0c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, D-001, D-003, D-009, D-037

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child digit of the tariff code, Single-parent H tariff, Child result (per child)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.6.1 Child digit equals the number of children that count**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax
  - **When** Lonio computes the tariff code
  - **Then** the child digit is the number of children that count, with or without H (for example three counting children of a married employee with an employed spouse give C3Y, and one counting and one non-counting child give digit 1); with no children or none counting the digit is 0. Results with more than 3 children are verified against the calculator before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage). The digit is always derived again from the latest answers.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ecef37, D-001, D-009, D-037_

  | One child counting (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c53a22) | Switzerland | no | no | Married | no | no | yes | Minor | yes | Sole custody | – | – | – | → | 1 | ✅ |
  | One child counting with H (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-38dd4a) | Switzerland | no | no | Single | – | – | yes | Minor | yes | Shared custody | no | no | – | → | 1 | ✅ |
  | Child does not count (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-56e79b) | Switzerland | no | no | Married | no | no | yes | Minor | no | – | – | – | no | → | 0 | ✅ |

- **AC-06.6.2 H tariff for single parents with a child counting with H**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax
  - **When** Lonio decides the tariff letter
  - **Then** a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee gets the H tariff if at least one child counts with H (for example one child counting and one counting with H give H2); a single employee without such a child keeps tariff A; a married employee or registered partner never gets the H tariff
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-96658b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c78e0c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-97139a, D-003_

  | Example | Country of home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | C permit held | Civil status | Children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Parental custody (child in household) | Employee pays more than 50% of the child's maintenance | Spouse's Swiss citizenship | Spouse holds a C permit | Spouse employed or receiving replacement income | → | Single-parent H tariff | Child digit of the tariff code | Checked |

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are out of scope for onboarding. Keep OQ-calculator-coverage open as a non-blocking go-live condition.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
