# US-07.2 – One block per child: age status and household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** new employee, **I want** to describe each of my children separately, starting with whether the child is a minor or an adult in first education and whether the child lives in my household, **so that** each child is assessed on its own facts, and I am not asked further questions about a child who cannot count for the tariff

Priority **P1** – Each child's answers feed the computed tariff code (D-001). These are the entry questions of every child block. · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-640429, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f, D-001, D-036

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-calculator-coverage](../open-questions.md#oq-calculator-coverage)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child: minor or adult in first education, Child lives in the same household, Per-child result

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.2.1 Child questions are answered once per child**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax has answered "yes" to having children
  - **When** they fill in the children part
  - **Then** They answer the child questions separately for each child. They can add another child and remove a child entered by mistake. Each child gets its own result (counts, counts with H, or does not count). The questionnaire sets no maximum number of children; whether more than 3 children are handled like the calculator is still to be verified (see OQ-calculator-coverage).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4957f6, D-001, D-009_
- **AC-07.2.2 Age status of each child**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax who has children
  - **When** they describe a child
  - **Then** They must state whether the child is "Minor", "Adult in first education" or "Neither". With "Neither" no further questions are asked about that child, and the child does not count for the tariff code.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-640429, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Civil status | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | Religious denomination (confession) | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | → | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child lives in the same household | Per-child result | Child digit of the tariff code | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator path 'weder noch' -> B0N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889) | Germany | Married | no | None or other | yes | Neither | → | required | not asked | does not count | 0 | ✅ |
  | Single employee, child neither minor nor in first education (illustrative) | Germany | Single | – | – | yes | Neither | → | – | not asked | does not count | – | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.3 Same household is asked for minors and adults in first education**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax has described a child as "Minor" or "Adult in first education"
  - **When** they continue with that child
  - **Then** They must answer "yes" or "no" to whether the child lives in their household. The answer is used for the source tax tariff code.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f, D-001_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | → | Child lives in the same household | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Minor child (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | → | required | ✅ |
  | Adult child in first education (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Separated | yes | Adult in first education | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.4 Child answers that no longer apply are not kept**
  - **Given** An employee has answered child questions and then changes an earlier answer, so that some child questions no longer apply (for example, they become not subject to source tax, answer "no" to having children, or change a child to "Neither")
  - **When** they submit the questionnaire
  - **Then** The answers to child questions that no longer apply are discarded. Until submission they are kept, so that going back and forth loses no input.
  - _Sources: D-036, G-01_
_Trace check: partial – AC-07.2.1 says the questionnaire sets no maximum number of children, but the facts only say the calculator's upper limit is unknown and untested (F-4957f6, D-009), so no such product decision is cited._


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-009** (product decision (Product owner)): Before go-live a domain expert compares Lonio's computed tariff code with the calculator for the paths not explored (residence block for A/B/H, more than 3 children, H3N/P3N). Special tariffs such as D (second job) are out of scope for onboarding. Keep OQ-calculator-coverage open as a non-blocking go-live condition.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
