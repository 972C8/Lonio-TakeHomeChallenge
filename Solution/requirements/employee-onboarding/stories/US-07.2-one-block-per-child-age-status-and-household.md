# US-07.2 – One block per child: age status and household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** new employee, **I want** to describe each of my children in a separate block, starting with whether the child is a minor or an adult in first education and whether the child lives in my household, **so that** every child who may count for my source tax tariff is captured, and children who cannot count need no further answers

Priority **P1** – Without the per-child answers Lonio cannot compute the child digit of the tariff code, which replaces HR's manual calculator step (D-001). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-640429, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f, D-001, D-036, D-044, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child: minor or adult in first education, Child lives in the same household, Per-child result

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.2.1 As many child blocks as the employee needs**
  - **Given** the employee is asked the child questions (subject to source tax, source tax details asked, and 'yes' to having children)
  - **When** the employee fills in step 7 Children
  - **Then** the employee can add one block per child and remove a block again; there is no maximum number of children (assumption D-044, to be verified by a domain expert). At least one child block is filled in. Every child question in a block is required whenever it is asked. Answers are recorded for HR and used only to compute the source tax tariff code.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, D-044, D-001_
- **AC-07.2.2 Age status per child, judged on the first working day**
  - **Given** a child block
  - **When** the employee states the child's age status
  - **Then** the choices are exactly 'Minor', 'Adult in first education' and 'Neither', and the child's situation on the employee's first working day counts (assumption D-044, to be verified by a domain expert). 'Neither' ends the block: no further question is asked for that child and the child does not count.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889, D-044_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | → | Child lives in the same household | Per-child result | Number of counted children (child digit) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, child neither minor nor in first education -> B0N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889) | Germany | Married | yes | Neither | → | not asked | not counted | 0 | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.3 Same household asked for minors and adults in first education**
  - **Given** a child block whose age status is 'Minor' or 'Adult in first education'
  - **When** the employee continues in that block
  - **Then** the employee must answer 'yes' or 'no' to whether the child lives in their household; the follow-up questions depend on this answer.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | → | Child lives in the same household | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Minor child (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | → | required | ✅ |
  | Adult child in first education (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Adult in first education | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.4 Child answers that no longer apply are not kept**
  - **Given** the employee filled in child blocks and then changes an earlier answer so that child questions no longer apply (for example 'no' to having children, or a change that makes them not subject to source tax), or removes a child block
  - **When** the employee submits the questionnaire
  - **Then** the child answers that no longer apply are discarded and not recorded; before submission they are kept so that going back and forth loses no input.
  - _Sources: D-036, G-01_
_Trace check: partial – AC-07.2.1 says at least one child block must be filled in and that every child question is required whenever asked, but no cited fact or decision backs either._
