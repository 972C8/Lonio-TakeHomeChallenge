# US-07.2 – One block per child: age status and household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** new employee, **I want** to describe each of my children in their own block, starting with whether the child is a minor or an adult in first education and whether the child lives in my household, **so that** every child is judged on its own, as in the Zurich calculator, and children who cannot count end the block at once without further questions

Priority **P1** – The per-child answers decide the child digit of the tariff code Lonio computes instead of HR (D-001); a wrong count gives a wrong tariff. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-640429, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f, D-044, D-047, D-001

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child: minor or adult in first education, Child lives in the same household, Per-child source tax result

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.2.1 One block per child, no maximum**
  - **Given** The employee answered 'yes' to having biological or adopted children
  - **When** the employee fills in step 7 'Children'
  - **Then** The employee fills in one block per child and can add another block or remove one; there is no maximum number of children. Assumption (D-044), to be verified by a domain expert: no maximum applies.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, D-044_
- **AC-07.2.2 Child's age status, judged on the first working day**
  - **Given** A child block is open
  - **When** the employee answers the first question of the block
  - **Then** The employee must choose one of 'Minor', 'Adult and in first education' or 'Neither'; the answer is required. The child's age is judged on the employee's first working day. Assumption (D-044), to be verified by a domain expert: the first working day is the reference date.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-640429, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, D-044_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Civil status | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or adult in first education | → | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child lives in the same household | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Minor child (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | → | required | asked | ✅ |
  | Adult child in first education (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Adult and in first education | → | – | required | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.3 'Neither' ends the block; the child does not count**
  - **Given** A child block is open
  - **When** the employee answers 'Neither' (neither a minor nor an adult in first education)
  - **Then** No further question is asked for this child and the child does not count towards the tariff code.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Civil status | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or adult in first education | → | Child lives in the same household | Child: parental custody | Child: more than 50% of maintenance provided | Per-child source tax result | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator run B0N, married, child 'weder noch' (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889) | Germany | Married | yes | Neither | → | not asked | not asked | not asked | not counted | ✅ |
  | Single employee, child neither minor nor in first education (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Neither | → | not asked | – | – | not counted | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.4 Same household asked for minors and adults in first education**
  - **Given** The child is a minor or an adult in first education
  - **When** the employee continues the child block
  - **Then** The employee must answer 'yes' or 'no' to whether the child lives in their household; the answer is required and decides which further questions follow for this child.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-044** (product decision (Product owner)): There is no maximum number of children. A child's age is judged on the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
