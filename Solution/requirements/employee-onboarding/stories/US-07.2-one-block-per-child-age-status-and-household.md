# US-07.2 – One block per child: age status and household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** Lonio to ask, for each child, whether the child is a minor, an adult in first education or neither, and whether the child lives in the employee's household, **so that** every child is judged the way the Zurich calculator judges it and children who cannot count are dropped without further questions

Priority **P1** – These answers open or close every further child question and decide whether a child can count in the tariff code (D-001). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-640429, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f, D-001, D-044, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child: minor or adult in first education, Child lives in the same household, Per-child result

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.2.1 One block of questions per child, no maximum**
  - **Given** the employee has answered yes to having children
  - **When** the employee enters their children
  - **Then** Lonio asks the child questions once per child, in a separate block for each child; the employee can add as many children as they have and remove a child entered by mistake. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert: there is no maximum number of children (D-044).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, D-044_
- **AC-07.2.2 Age status of each child, judged on the first working day**
  - **Given** the employee has answered yes to having children
  - **When** the employee fills in a child block
  - **Then** Lonio asks whether the child is 'Minor', 'Adult in first education' or 'Neither'; an answer is required. The employee answers as things stand on their first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert: a child's age is judged on the first working day (D-044).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-640429, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, D-044_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Civil status | Has children | → | Child: minor or adult in first education | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Germany, married, has children (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.3 A child who is neither does not count and ends the block**
  - **Given** a child is neither a minor nor an adult in first education
  - **When** the employee answers 'Neither'
  - **Then** no further questions are asked for this child and the child does not count towards the tariff code.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | → | Child lives in the same household | Per-child result | Child digit | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator: married, child neither → B0N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889) | Germany | Married | yes | Neither | → | not asked | does not count | 0 | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.4 Household question for minors and adults in first education**
  - **Given** a child is a minor or an adult in first education
  - **When** the employee fills in that child's block
  - **Then** Lonio asks whether the child lives in the employee's household, answered yes or no; an answer is required. The answer decides which further questions follow for this child.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | → | Child lives in the same household | Checked |

  | Minor child (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | → | required | ✅ |
  | Adult child in first education (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Adult in first education | → | asked | ✅ |
