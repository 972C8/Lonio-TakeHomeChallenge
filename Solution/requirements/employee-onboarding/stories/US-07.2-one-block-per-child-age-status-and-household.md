# US-07.2 – One block per child: age status and household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** for every child the employee declares, to know whether the child is a minor, an adult in first education or neither, and (for the first two) whether the child lives in the employee's household, **so that** children who cannot count towards the tariff are dropped right away and the remaining questions for each child can be narrowed down

Priority **P1** – These answers decide for every child whether it counts towards the source tax tariff; without them the tariff cannot be determined. · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-640429, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-calculator-coverage](../open-questions.md#oq-calculator-coverage)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child: minor or adult in first education, Child lives in the same household

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.2.1 A block of questions for each child**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has answered "yes" to having biological or adopted children (US-07.1)
  - **When** the child questions are asked
  - **Then** the employee can describe each child in its own block (Child 1, Child 2, ...) and can add or remove a child; every question of this section is answered per child. How the calculator behaves with more than three children or after removing a child was not checked (see OQ-calculator-coverage)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15_
- **AC-07.2.2 Age status asked for every child**
  - **Given** a child block
  - **When** the employee describes the child
  - **Then** the employee must say whether the child is "Minor", "Adult in first education" or "Neither"; the question cannot be left empty and is needed by the source tax office
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-640429, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | Permit type | Civil status | Type of income (source tax) | Religious denomination (church tax) | Has biological or adopted children | → | Child: minor or adult in first education | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee with children (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | B permit | Single | Earned income | None or other | yes | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.3 A child who is neither ends its block**
  - **Given** a child described as "Neither" (not a minor and not an adult in first education)
  - **When** the block is answered
  - **Then** no further question is asked for that child, and the child does not count towards the tariff
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | Permit type | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | Spouse's permit type | Type of income (source tax) | Religious denomination (church tax) | Spouse works or receives replacement income | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or adult in first education | → | Child lives in the same household | Parental custody (child in household) | Pays more than 50% of the child's maintenance | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, child neither (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-abe889) | Switzerland | Italy | B permit | Married | Italy | B permit | Earned income | None or other | no | yes | Neither | → | not asked | not asked | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-07.2.4 Same household asked for minors and adults in first education**
  - **Given** a child described as "Minor" or "Adult in first education"
  - **When** the block continues
  - **Then** the employee must answer "yes" or "no" to whether the child lives in the same household; it is needed by the source tax office to decide whether and how the child counts
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | Permit type | Civil status | Type of income (source tax) | Religious denomination (church tax) | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or adult in first education | Spouse works or receives replacement income | → | Child lives in the same household | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Minor child (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | B permit | Single | Earned income | None or other | yes | Minor | – | → | required | ✅ |
  | Adult child in first education (illustrative) | Germany | – | – | Married | Earned income | None or other | yes | Adult in first education | yes | → | asked | ✅ |
