# US-07.4 – Minor child not living in the employee's household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the custody or parental care, alternating custody and main-contributor answers for each minor child who does not live in the employee's household, **so that** such a child counts only in the cases where the Zurich calculator counts it

Priority **P1** – Counting a child wrongly gives a wrong tariff code; Lonio now owns the tariff result (D-001). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fe0305, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d2e516, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7dcce6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-956f07, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-32fec9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb4a56, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-dc6ace, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a4d358, D-001, D-003, D-009, D-046

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child: shared custody (married, child not in household), Child: parental care (non-married, child not in household), Child: alternating custody, Child: main financial contributor, Per-child result

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.4.1 Questions for a minor child outside the household**
  - **Given** a child block with age status 'Minor' and 'no' to living in the employee's household
  - **When** the employee continues in that block
  - **Then** married employees and registered partners must answer whether custody is shared (yes/no); single, divorced, separated and widowed employees must answer whether they have parental care (yes/no) - each group gets only its own question (grouping per assumption D-003, to be verified by a domain expert). With 'yes', the employee must answer whether there is alternating custody (yes/no), and with alternating custody, who makes the larger financial contribution ('Myself' or 'Other parent'). With 'no', the block ends.

- **AC-07.4.2 Per-child result for a minor child outside the household**
  - **Given** a completed block for a minor child not living in the employee's household
  - **When** Lonio works out the per-child result
  - **Then** the child counts only with shared custody (married group) or parental care (single group), alternating custody and the employee as main contributor - counted for married employees and registered partners, counted with tariff H for single, divorced, separated or widowed employees. In every other case the child does not count. Observed calculator results win (assumption D-046, to be verified by a domain expert); inferred results are compared with the calculator before go-live (D-009, see OQ-calculator-coverage).
