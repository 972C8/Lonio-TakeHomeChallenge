# US-07.4 – Minor child not living in the employee's household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the calculator's custody, alternating-custody and main-contributor questions for a minor child living outside the employee's household, with the custody question worded by civil status group, **so that** Lonio can decide whether such a child counts, and whether it counts with H for single employees, as the calculator would

Priority **P1** – This case affects the child digit and the H tariff of the computed tariff code (D-001). ## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.4.1 Married employees and registered partners: shared custody**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax whose civil status is Married or Registered partnership has a "Minor" child who does not live in their household
  - **When** they continue with that child
  - **Then** They must answer "yes" or "no" to whether custody is shared. The parental care question is not asked. With "no" the child does not count and no further question is asked about it.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fe0305, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d2e516, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7dcce6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-dc6ace, D-003_

- **AC-07.4.2 Single, divorced, separated or widowed employees: parental care**

  - **When** they continue with that child
  - **Then** They must answer "yes" or "no" to whether they have parental care. The shared-custody question is not asked. With "no" the child does not count and no further question is asked about it.

- **AC-07.4.3 Custody or parental care: alternating custody**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax has a minor child outside their household. Either they are married or in a registered partnership with shared custody, or they are single, divorced, separated or widowed with parental care
  - **When** they continue with that child
  - **Then** They must answer "yes" or "no" to whether there is alternating custody. With "no" the child does not count and no further question is asked about it. This result was not observed in the calculator and must be checked before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ebd7b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a4d358, D-009_

- **AC-07.4.4 Alternating custody: who contributes most to maintenance**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax has a minor child outside their household with shared custody or parental care and alternating custody
  - **When** they continue with that child
  - **Then** They must state who makes the larger financial contribution to the child's maintenance, "Employee" or "Other parent". With "Employee" the child counts: married employees and registered partners get a plain count, single, divorced, separated or widowed employees get a count with H. With "Other parent" the child does not count. This last result was not observed in the calculator (see OQ-calculator-coverage).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cdd0b6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-dc6ace, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a4d358, D-009_
