# US-07.4 – Minor child not living in the employee's household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** for a minor child who does not live with the employee, to know about shared custody (married employees) or parental care (employees who are not married) and, only where it matters, alternating custody and who pays more of the maintenance, **so that** it can be decided whether a child living elsewhere still counts towards the tariff, without asking questions that do not apply

Priority **P2** – Needed for a correct tariff, but only for employees subject to source tax with a minor child living elsewhere; no source names a specific incident. · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fe0305, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d2e516, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7dcce6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-956f07, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-32fec9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb4a56, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-dc6ace

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-calculator-coverage](../open-questions.md#oq-calculator-coverage)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Shared custody (child not in household, married), Parental care (child not in household, not married), Alternating custody, Main financial contributor to the child's maintenance

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.4.1 Married employee: shared custody**
  - **Given** a married employee subject to source tax with a child described as "Minor" who does not live in the same household
  - **When** the block continues
  - **Then** the employee must answer "yes" or "no" to shared custody; the parental-care and in-household custody questions are not asked
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fe0305, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d2e516, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7dcce6_

- **AC-07.4.2 Employee not married: parental care**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax who is single, divorced or widowed, with a child described as "Minor" who does not live in the same household
  - **When** the block continues
  - **Then** the employee must answer "yes" or "no" to having parental care; the shared-custody question for married employees is not asked
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-956f07, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-32fec9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb4a56_

- **AC-07.4.3 With shared custody or parental care: alternating custody, then maintenance**
  - **Given** a minor child not in the household, where the married employee has shared custody or the employee who is not married has parental care
  - **When** the block continues
  - **Then** the employee must answer "yes" or "no" to alternating custody; only with "yes" are they asked who pays more of the child's maintenance ("The employee" or "The other parent"). Without shared custody or parental care (AC-07.4.1, AC-07.4.2) the child does not count and nothing more is asked
