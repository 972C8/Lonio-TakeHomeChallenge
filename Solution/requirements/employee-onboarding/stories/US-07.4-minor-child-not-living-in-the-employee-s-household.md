# US-07.4 – Minor child not living in the employee's household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** Lonio to ask about shared custody (married employees and registered partners) or parental care (single, divorced, separated or widowed employees), alternating custody and the main financial contributor for a minor child living elsewhere, and to derive whether the child counts, **so that** a child living elsewhere only counts in the tariff code when the calculator would count it

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child: shared custody (married, child not in household), Child: parental care (single, child not in household), Child: alternating custody, Child: main financial contributor, Per-child result

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.4.1 Shared custody or parental care, depending on civil status**
  - **Given** a child is a minor and does not live in the employee's household
  - **When** the employee fills in that child's block
  - **Then** a married employee or registered partner is asked whether custody is shared (yes or no); a single, divorced, separated or widowed employee is asked whether they have parental care (yes or no). The answer is required. The custody question for children in the household is not asked. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert: registered partnership is treated as married and separated as single (D-003).

- **AC-07.4.2 No shared custody or parental care: the child does not count**
  - **Given** a minor child does not live in the employee's household
  - **When** the employee answers no to shared custody or to parental care
  - **Then** no further questions are asked for this child and the child does not count.

- **AC-07.4.3 Alternating custody and main financial contributor**
  - **Given** a minor child does not live in the employee's household and the employee has shared custody or parental care
  - **When** the employee states whether there is alternating custody (yes or no, required) and, with alternating custody, who makes the larger financial contribution ('The employee' or 'The other parent', required)
  - **Then** the child counts only with alternating custody and the employee as main contributor: it counts for a married employee or registered partner and counts with H for a single, divorced, separated or widowed employee. Without alternating custody, or with the other parent as main contributor, the child does not count. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert: the two 'does not count' cases are inferred and checked against the calculator before go-live (D-046, D-009; see OQ-calculator-coverage).
