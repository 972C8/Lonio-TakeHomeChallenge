# US-07.4 – Minor child not living in the employee's household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** Lonio to ask, for a minor child outside the employee's household, about shared custody (married) or parental care (not married), alternating custody and the larger financial contributor, and to derive the child's result as the calculator does, **so that** a child living elsewhere counts only where the calculator counts it

Priority **P1** – Lonio replaces HR's manual calculator step (D-001); a wrong per-child result gives a wrong tariff code. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fe0305, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d2e516, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7dcce6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-956f07, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-32fec9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb4a56, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-dc6ace, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a4d358, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, D-001, D-003, D-009, D-046

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child: shared custody (child not in household, married), Child: parental care (child not in household, not married), Child: alternating custody, Child: larger financial contributor, Per-child source tax result

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.4.1 Questions for a minor child outside the household**
  - **Given** The child is a minor and does not live in the employee's household
  - **When** the employee continues the child block
  - **Then** Employees who are married or in a registered partnership must answer whether custody is shared (yes/no); employees who are single, divorced, widowed or separated must answer whether they have parental care (yes/no). Only with 'yes': whether there is alternating custody (yes/no); only with alternating custody: who makes the larger financial contribution ('Me' or 'The other parent'). Custody of a child in the household is not asked. Every answer is required when asked. Registered partnership counts as married and Separated as single (assumption D-003, to be verified by a domain expert).

- **AC-07.4.2 Result for a minor child outside the household**
  - **Given** The child is a minor outside the employee's household
  - **When** the child block is complete
  - **Then** The child counts only with shared custody (married) or parental care (not married), alternating custody and the employee as the larger contributor: married employees get 'counted', the others 'counted with H'. In every other case it is not counted. The rows 'alternating custody with the other parent contributing more' and 'no alternating custody' are inferred and checked before go-live (D-009, see OQ-calculator-coverage); observed calculator runs win over the derived logic (assumption D-046, to be verified by a domain expert).
