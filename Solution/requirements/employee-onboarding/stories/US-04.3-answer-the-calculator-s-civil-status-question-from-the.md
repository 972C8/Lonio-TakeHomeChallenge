# US-04.3 – Answer the calculator's civil status question from the employee's civil status

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data for the pension fund](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the calculator's civil status question ('married or registered partnership' or 'single, divorced, separated, widowed') to be answered from the civil status the employee already gave for the pension fund, **so that** the tariff can be determined without asking the employee a second civil status question

Priority **P1** – The calculator needs the civil status of every person subject to source tax to find the tariff, and the feature requires asking each piece of information once. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, G-01, G-08

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-registered-partnership](../open-questions.md#oq-registered-partnership), [OQ-separated](../open-questions.md#oq-separated), [OQ-divorced-widowed](../open-questions.md#oq-divorced-widowed)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Civil status, Civil status answer for the source tax calculator

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.3.1 Married maps to 'married or registered partnership'**
  - **Given** the employee answered civil status Married
  - **When** the calculator's civil status answer is derived
  - **Then** it is 'Married or registered partnership' (Verheiratet oder eingetragene Partnerschaft).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-pension_portal-3d1ae4_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status answer for the source tax calculator | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Married | → | Married or registered partnership | ✅ |

- **AC-04.3.2 Single, Divorced and Widowed map to 'single, divorced, separated, widowed'**
  - **Given** the employee answered civil status Single, Divorced or Widowed
  - **When** the calculator's civil status answer is derived
  - **Then** it is 'Single, divorced, separated, widowed' (Ledig, Geschieden, Getrennt, Verwitwet).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-pension_portal-3d1ae4_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status answer for the source tax calculator | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | Single, divorced, separated, widowed | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee (illustrative) | Divorced | → | Single, divorced, separated, widowed | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee (illustrative) | Widowed | → | Single, divorced, separated, widowed | ✅ |

- **AC-04.3.3 Registered partners and separated employees follow the open decisions**
  - **Given** an employee in a registered partnership, or a separated employee who is still married
  - **When** the calculator's civil status answer is derived
  - **Then** the result follows the civil status they chose from the pension fund's list. Whether registered partners should choose Married is open (see OQ-registered-partnership). Whether separated employees, who would choose Married and so get 'married or registered partnership', need a separate source tax answer is also open (see OQ-separated). No extra question is asked until these are decided.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-pension_portal-3d1ae4_

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
