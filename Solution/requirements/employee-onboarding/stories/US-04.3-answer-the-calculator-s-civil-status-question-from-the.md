# US-04.3 – Answer the calculator's civil status question from the employee's civil status

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the employee's civil status turned into the calculator's two groups: 'married or registered partnership' and 'single, divorced, separated or widowed', **so that** Lonio can follow the married or single path of the Zurich calculator when it computes the tariff code, without asking the employee a second civil status question

Priority **P1** – Lonio computes the full tariff code instead of HR (D-001). The calculator's civil status decides the married or single path, the spouse questions and the tariff letter. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, G-01, D-001, D-003, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Civil status, Civil status for source tax

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.3.1 Married and registered partnership count as married**
  - **Given** the employee's civil status is Married or Registered partnership
  - **When** Lonio determines the civil status for source tax
  - **Then** the result is 'Married or registered partnership', as in the calculator's own group
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status for source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel, registered partnership (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | Married or registered partnership | ✅ |
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Married | → | Married or registered partnership | ✅ |

- **AC-04.3.2 Single, divorced, separated and widowed count as single**
  - **Given** the employee's civil status is Single, Divorced, Widowed or Separated
  - **When** Lonio determines the civil status for source tax
  - **Then** the result is 'Single, divorced, separated or widowed', as in the calculator's own group. A separated employee counts as single for source tax, even though the pension fund registers them as Married.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status for source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | Single, divorced, separated or widowed | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee (illustrative) | Divorced | → | Single, divorced, separated or widowed | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee (illustrative) | Widowed | → | Single, divorced, separated or widowed | ✅ |
  | Separated employee (illustrative) | Separated | → | Single, divorced, separated or widowed | ✅ |

- **AC-04.3.3 A registered partner counts as a spouse wherever source tax looks at the spouse**
  - **Given** the employee's civil status is Registered partnership
  - **When** Lonio decides whether the employee is subject to source tax and computes the tariff code
  - **Then** the registered partner is treated like a spouse. This includes the exemption for employees married to a Swiss national or C-permit holder living in Switzerland, and the spouse questions on the married path, as the calculator groups registered partnership with married.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003_
- **AC-04.3.4 Always derived from the latest answer**
  - **Given** the employee's civil status is changed after submission (by HR, for example after a marriage)
  - **When** the civil status for source tax is needed again
  - **Then** it is derived again from the latest civil status. The earlier result is not kept.
  - _Sources: D-037_
_Trace check: partial – AC-04.3.3 and the priority reason say the calculator's civil status drives the spouse questions on the married path and the tariff letter, but no cited fact describes those calculator steps; the cited facts give only the two civil status groups and the spouse exemption._


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **D-045** (product decision (Product owner)): Business text (stories, acceptance criteria, notices) uses no internal snake_case ids and does not speak of 'expressions'. Any behaviour that has no cited fact or decision is stated in the story as an assumption. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9** (zh_source_tax_calculator): Civil status options are exactly two groups: 'Verheiratet oder eingetragene Partnerschaft' (married or registered partnership) and 'Ledig, Geschieden, Getrennt, Verwitwet' (single, divorced, separated, widowed).
  - "Options: Verheiratet oder eingetragene Partnerschaft | Ledig, Geschieden, Getrennt, Verwitwet" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q02
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3** (zh_source_tax_calculator): For earned income, the calculator asks for the civil status of the person subject to source tax.
  - "Zivilstand" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q02
  - "Section heading: 'Angaben zur quellensteuerpflichtigen Person'" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q02
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
