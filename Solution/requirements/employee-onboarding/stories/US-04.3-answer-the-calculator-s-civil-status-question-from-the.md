# US-04.3 – Answer the calculator's civil status question from the employee's civil status

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [4. Civil status and spouse data](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse).

**As** source tax office, **I want** Lonio to place the employee in the calculator's civil status group from the civil status they already gave, **so that** the tariff code is computed correctly without asking the civil status a second time

Priority **P1** – The civil status group decides the spouse exemption and tariff letter of the computed tariff code (D-001), which replaces HR's manual calculator step. · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003, D-037

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-civil-status-partnership](../open-questions.md#oq-civil-status-partnership), [OQ-spouse-dob-other-status](../open-questions.md#oq-spouse-dob-other-status)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Civil status, Civil status group for source tax

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-04.3.1 Civil status group for source tax**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax who has answered the civil status question
  - **When** Lonio determines the civil status group for the source tax tariff
  - **Then** Married and Registered partnership give 'Married or registered partnership'; Single, Divorced, Widowed and Separated give 'Single, divorced, separated or widowed', as in the calculator. No separate source tax civil status question is asked. The group is always derived from the latest recorded civil status.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, D-003, D-037_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status group for source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | Married or registered partnership | ✅ |
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Married | → | Married or registered partnership | ✅ |
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | Single, divorced, separated or widowed | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee (illustrative) | Divorced | → | Single, divorced, separated or widowed | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee (illustrative) | Widowed | → | Single, divorced, separated or widowed | ✅ |
  | Separated employee (illustrative) | Separated | → | Single, divorced, separated or widowed | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9** (zh_source_tax_calculator): Civil status options are exactly two groups: 'Verheiratet oder eingetragene Partnerschaft' (married or registered partnership) and 'Ledig, Geschieden, Getrennt, Verwitwet' (single, divorced, separated, widowed).
  - "Options: Verheiratet oder eingetragene Partnerschaft | Ledig, Geschieden, Getrennt, Verwitwet" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q02
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3** (zh_source_tax_calculator): For earned income, the calculator asks for the civil status of the person subject to source tax.
  - "Zivilstand" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q02
  - "Section heading: 'Angaben zur quellensteuerpflichtigen Person'" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q02
