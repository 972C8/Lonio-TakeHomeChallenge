# US-07.3 – Minor child living in the employee's household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.3.1 Questions for a minor child in the household**
  - **Given** a child block with age status 'Minor' and 'yes' to living in the employee's household
  - **When** the employee continues in that block
  - **Then** the employee must state the parental custody: 'Shared custody' or 'Sole custody'. With shared custody, the employee must answer whether they live in a common household with the child's other parent (yes/no). Without a common household, the employee must answer whether there is alternating custody (yes/no). The main financial contributor ('Myself' or 'Other parent') is asked when there is alternating custody, and for single, divorced, separated or widowed employees who live in a common household with the other parent; married employees and registered partners in a common household are not asked it. For this, Married and Registered partnership form one group and Single, Divorced, Widowed and Separated the other, as in the calculator (assumption D-003, to be verified by a domain expert).

- **AC-07.3.2 Per-child result for a minor child in the household**
  - **Given** a completed block for a minor child living in the employee's household
  - **When** Lonio works out the per-child result
  - **Then** the result follows the calculator: sole custody counts the child (with tariff H for single, divorced, separated or widowed employees); shared custody with a common household counts the child for married employees and registered partners, and for the other group only if the employee is the main contributor (then with H); without a common household and with alternating custody the child counts only if the employee is the main contributor (with H for the single group); without alternating custody the child does not count for married employees and registered partners and counts with H for the single group. Observed calculator results win over the general rule (assumption D-046, to be verified by a domain expert); results marked inferred are compared with the calculator before go-live (D-009, see OQ-calculator-coverage).
