# US-07.3 – Minor child living in the employee's household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [8. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** Lonio to ask, for a minor child in the employee's household, about custody, a common household with the other parent, alternating custody and the larger financial contributor, and to derive the child's result as the calculator does, **so that** the child is counted, counted with H (single parents) or not counted exactly as in the Zurich calculator

Priority **P1** – Lonio replaces HR's manual calculator step (D-001); a wrong per-child result gives a wrong tariff code. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4b7063, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c09dc1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-83de1d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ebd7b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cdd0b6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d75286, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5d8fb0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a4d358, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5686d7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-dc0ae9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-001, D-003, D-009, D-046

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.3.1 Questions for a minor child in the household**
  - **Given** The child is a minor and lives in the employee's household
  - **When** the employee continues the child block
  - **Then** The employee must state parental custody ('Shared custody' or 'Sole custody'). Only with shared custody: whether they live in a common household with the other parent (yes/no). Only without a common household: whether there is alternating custody (yes/no). Who makes the larger financial contribution ('Me' or 'The other parent') is asked when there is alternating custody, or - for employees who are single, divorced, widowed or separated - when they live in a common household with the other parent. Every answer is required when asked.
