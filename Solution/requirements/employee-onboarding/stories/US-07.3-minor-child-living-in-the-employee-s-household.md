# US-07.3 – Minor child living in the employee's household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** Lonio to ask about custody, a common household with the other parent, alternating custody and the main financial contributor for a minor child living in the employee's household, and to derive whether the child counts, **so that** the child digit and a possible tariff H match what the Zurich calculator gives for this child

Priority **P1** – Minor children in the household are the most common child case; a wrong result gives a wrong tariff code, which Lonio now computes instead of HR (D-001). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4b7063, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c09dc1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-83de1d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-e72e42, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ebd7b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cdd0b6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d75286, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5d8fb0, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5686d7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a4d358, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-dc0ae9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72, D-001, D-003, D-009, D-046

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.3.1 Custody asked for a minor child in the household**
  - **Given** a child is a minor and lives in the employee's household
  - **When** the employee fills in that child's block
  - **Then** Lonio asks whether parental custody is 'Shared custody' or 'Sole custody'; an answer is required. This applies whatever the employee's civil status.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4b7063, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c09dc1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-83de1d_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or adult in first education | Child lives in the same household | → | Child: parental custody | Child: shared custody (married, child not in household) | Child: parental care (single, child not in household) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single, minor in household (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | yes | → | required | not asked | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-07.3.2 Sole custody: the child counts**
  - **Given** a minor child lives in the employee's household and the employee has sole custody
  - **When** Lonio derives the child's result
  - **Then** no further questions are asked for this child; the child counts, and for a single, divorced, separated or widowed employee it counts with H (makes the tariff H). Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert: registered partnership is treated as married and separated as single (D-003).

- **AC-07.3.3 Shared custody and common household with the other parent**
  - **Given** a minor child lives in the employee's household with shared custody
  - **When** the employee states whether they live in a common household with the child's other parent (yes or no, required)
  - **Then** if yes and the employee is married or in a registered partnership, the child counts and no further question is asked; if yes and the employee is single, divorced, separated or widowed, Lonio asks who makes the larger financial contribution ('The employee' or 'The other parent'): the employee gives 'counts with H', the other parent gives 'does not count'. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert: the single case with the employee as main contributor is inferred and is checked against the calculator before go-live (D-046, D-009; see OQ-calculator-coverage).

- **AC-07.3.4 Shared custody without common household: alternating custody**
  - **Given** a minor child lives in the employee's household with shared custody and the employee does not live in a common household with the other parent
  - **When** the employee states whether there is alternating custody (yes or no, required)
  - **Then** without alternating custody the child does not count for a married employee or registered partner and counts with H for a single, divorced, separated or widowed employee. With alternating custody Lonio asks who makes the larger financial contribution: if the employee, the child counts (married or registered partnership) or counts with H (single, divorced, separated, widowed); if the other parent, the child does not count.
