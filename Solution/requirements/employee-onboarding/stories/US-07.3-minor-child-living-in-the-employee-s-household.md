# US-07.3 – Minor child living in the employee's household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the calculator's custody, common-household, alternating-custody and main-contributor questions for a minor child living in the employee's household, asked only as far as the calculator asks them, **so that** Lonio can decide whether the child counts, and whether it counts with H for single employees, exactly as the calculator would

Priority **P1** – The child digit and the H tariff depend on these answers. ## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.3.1 Custody of a minor child in the household**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax has a child who is a "Minor" and lives in their household
  - **When** they continue with that child
  - **Then** They must state whether they have "Shared custody" or "Sole custody". With sole custody the child counts: for married employees and registered partners it simply counts, for single, divorced, separated or widowed employees it counts with H. No further questions are asked about that child. The custody question is not asked about minors living outside the household.

- **AC-07.3.2 Shared custody and a common household with the other parent**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax has a minor child in their household with "Shared custody"
  - **When** they continue with that child
  - **Then** They must answer "yes" or "no" to whether they live in a common household with the child's other parent. With "yes": for married employees and registered partners the child counts and no further question is asked. Single, divorced, separated or widowed employees are asked who makes the larger financial contribution to the child's maintenance ("Employee" or "Other parent"): with "Employee" the child counts with H, with "Other parent" it does not count.

- **AC-07.3.3 Shared custody without a common household: alternating custody**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax has a minor child in their household with "Shared custody" and does not live in a common household with the other parent
  - **When** they continue with that child
  - **Then** They must answer "yes" or "no" to whether there is alternating custody. With "no": for married employees and registered partners the child does not count, for single, divorced, separated or widowed employees it counts with H. No further question is asked about that child.

- **AC-07.3.4 Alternating custody: who contributes most to maintenance**
  - **Given** An employee subject to source tax has a minor child in their household with "Shared custody", no common household with the other parent and alternating custody
  - **When** they continue with that child
  - **Then** They must state who makes the larger financial contribution to the child's maintenance, "Employee" or "Other parent". With "Employee" the child counts: married employees and registered partners get a plain count, single, divorced, separated or widowed employees get a count with H. With "Other parent" the child does not count. The married + Employee and single + Other parent results were not observed in the calculator and must be checked before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).
