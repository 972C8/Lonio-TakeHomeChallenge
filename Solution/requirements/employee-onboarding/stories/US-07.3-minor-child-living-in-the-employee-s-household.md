# US-07.3 – Minor child living in the employee's household

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: children](../feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** for a minor child living in the employee's household, to know the parental custody and, only where it matters, the common household with the other parent, alternating custody and who pays more of the child's maintenance, **so that** it can be decided whether the child counts towards the tariff digit and, for employees who are not married, whether it leads to tariff H

Priority **P2** – Needed for a correct tariff, but only for employees subject to source tax with a minor child at home; no source names a specific incident. · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4b7063, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c09dc1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-83de1d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d79949, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6dcf5c, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cdd0b6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.3.1 Custody asked; sole custody ends the block**
  - **Given** a child described as "Minor" who lives in the same household (US-07.2)
  - **When** the block continues
  - **Then** the employee must choose "Shared custody" or "Sole custody"; with sole custody no further question is asked for that child (the child counts, and for an employee who is not married it leads to tariff H)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4b7063, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c09dc1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-83de1d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d75286, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-300c72_

- **AC-07.3.2 Shared custody: common household with the other parent**
  - **Given** a minor child in the household with shared custody
  - **When** the block continues
  - **Then** the employee must answer "yes" or "no" to living in a common household with the child's other parent. - **AC-07.3.3 No common household: alternating custody**
  - **Given** a minor child in the household with shared custody, where the employee does not live in a common household with the other parent
  - **When** the block continues
  - **Then** the employee must answer "yes" or "no" to alternating custody; with "no" nothing more is asked for that child

- **AC-07.3.4 Alternating custody: who pays more of the maintenance**
  - **Given** a minor child in the household with alternating custody
  - **When** the block continues
  - **Then** the employee must say who makes the larger financial contribution to the child's maintenance: "The employee" or "The other parent"; it is needed by the source tax office to decide whether the child counts
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cdd0b6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-939b3b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a4d358_
