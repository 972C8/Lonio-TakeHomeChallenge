# US-06.7 – Ask employees subject to source tax whether they have children

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** to know whether an employee who needs a tariff code has biological or adopted children, **so that** the child questions are asked only when there are children, and the child digit and an H tariff can be worked out

Priority **P1** – The child digit is part of every tariff code Lonio computes (D-001, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c53e3). Asking only employees subject to source tax follows HR's request (F-hr_transcript-b84498). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a33b65, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-75cd87, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-hr_transcript-b84498, D-040, D-044, D-047

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.7.1 Children question is asked only of employees who need a tariff code**
  - **Given** an employee filling in step 6 'Source tax details'
  - **When** Lonio decides whether to ask about children
  - **Then** The question is asked, and is required, whatever the civil status, only if the employee is subject to source tax and is not a Liechtenstein resident who is in principle not liable. No one else is asked. The answer is recorded for HR to determine the source tax tariff.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a33b65, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-75cd87, F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1, D-040_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Civil status | Daily return to Germany | Certificate of residence | Nationality | Holds a C permit | Daily return to Liechtenstein | More than 45 non-return days (Liechtenstein) | → | Has children | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single employee living in France (illustrative) | France | Single | – | – | – | – | – | – | → | required | ✅ |
  | Married employee living in France (illustrative) | France | Married | – | – | – | – | – | – | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Germany resident who returns daily (illustrative) | Germany | Widowed | yes | yes | – | – | – | – | → | asked | ✅ |
  | Maria, Portuguese with a C permit (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | Single | – | – | Portugal | yes | – | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Liechtenstein resident with at most 45 non-return days (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Married | – | – | – | – | no | no | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Liechtenstein resident with more than 45 non-return days (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | Married | – | – | – | – | no | yes | → | asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.7.2 Which children count for the question and what the answer opens**
  - **Given** the children question is asked
  - **When** the employee answers
  - **Then** Married employees and registered partners are asked whether they or their spouse or partner have biological or adopted children. All other employees are asked whether they have biological or adopted children. Only 'yes' and 'no' are possible. 'yes' opens step 7 'Children' with one block per child and no maximum number of children (assumption D-044, to be verified by a domain expert). 'no' skips step 7, and the child digit of the tariff code is 0.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, D-044, D-047_
_Trace check: partial – The rule for who is subject to source tax (the C-permit and Swiss cases behind the non-illustrative Maria 'not asked' example) is not backed by the cited facts, which omit F-hr_transcript-5af8d0 and F-hr_transcript-845204._


## Evidence
- **D-040** (product decision (Product owner)): For employees living in Liechtenstein, the questions on daily return and on more than 45 days abroad come before denomination, spouse and children. When the answers mean 'in principle no source tax liability', the tariff questions are skipped. When the employee stays abroad more than 45 days, the normal tariff is recorded with the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement' and flagged for HR. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-044** (product decision (Product owner)): There is no maximum number of children. A child's age is judged on the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
