# US-06.7 – Ask employees subject to source tax whether they have children

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** to know whether an employee who needs a tariff code has biological or adopted children (for married employees and registered partners: the employee's or the spouse's/partner's), **so that** the child questions are asked only when there are children, and the child digit and the H tariff can be determined

Priority **P1** – The child digit is part of every tariff code (D-001). Asking about children only of employees subject to source tax keeps to data minimisation (G-01, F-hr_transcript-b84498). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a33b65, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-75cd87, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, D-001, D-003, D-040, D-044, D-047

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.7.1 The children question is asked only when the source tax details apply**
  - **Given** an employee has answered steps 1 to 5
  - **When** the employee reaches step 6 'Source tax details'
  - **Then** the children question is asked, and required, only if the employee is subject to source tax and (for Liechtenstein residents) has more than 45 non-return days. It is asked of married and unmarried employees alike. It comes after the denomination and, for married employees and registered partners, after the spouse employment question, as in the calculator.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a33b65, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-75cd87, F-hr_transcript-b84498, D-040, D-047_

- **AC-06.7.2 Wording depends on civil status, answer is yes or no**
  - **Given** the children question is asked
  - **When** the employee answers it
  - **Then** a Married employee or registered partner is asked whether they or their spouse/partner have biological or adopted children; a Single, Divorced, Widowed or Separated employee is asked whether they have biological or adopted children. The answer is 'yes' or 'no' and cannot be left empty. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-003): Registered partnership is asked like Married and Separated like Single, as in the calculator's two civil status groups.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003_
- **AC-06.7.3 'yes' opens the children step, 'no' gives child digit 0**
  - **Given** the employee has answered the children question
  - **When** the employee continues
  - **Then** with 'yes' the employee goes on to step 7 'Children' (one block per child, no maximum number; a child's age is judged on the first working day, an assumption to be verified by a domain expert, D-044). With 'no' step 7 is skipped and no child counts towards the tariff code. The answer is recorded for HR only as an input to the source tax tariff code.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-6bed1f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c53e3, D-044, D-047_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Civil status | Denomination | Has biological or adopted children | → | Child: minor or adult in first education | Source tax tariff code | Checked |

  | Employee answers no (illustrative) | France | Single | None or other | no | → | not asked | A0N | ✅ |
  | Employee answers yes (illustrative) | France | Single | None or other | yes | → | required | – | ✅ |

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. This closes OQ-civil-status-partnership. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-040** (product decision (Product owner)): For employees living in Liechtenstein, the questions on daily return and on more than 45 days abroad come before denomination, spouse and children. When the answers mean 'in principle no source tax liability', the tariff questions are skipped. When the employee stays abroad more than 45 days, the normal tariff is recorded with the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement' and flagged for HR. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-044** (product decision (Product owner)): There is no maximum number of children. A child's age is judged on the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
