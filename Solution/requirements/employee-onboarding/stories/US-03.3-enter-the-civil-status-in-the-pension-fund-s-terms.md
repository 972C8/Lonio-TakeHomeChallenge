# US-03.3 – Enter the civil status in the pension fund's terms

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [3. Civil status and spouse for the pension fund](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** every employee's civil status to be recorded as one of the values on my list: Single, Married, Divorced or Widowed, **so that** the employee can be registered even when they are in a registered partnership or separated, which my list does not offer

Priority **P1** – The fund's field is required and offers only four values. Without a mapping, partnered and separated employees (for example Daniel) cannot be registered. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-pension_portal-9b24c5, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, D-003, D-004, D-037

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-03.3.1 Civil status for the pension fund**
  - **Given** the employee has stated their civil status
  - **When** Lonio records the civil status for the pension fund
  - **Then** Single, Married, Divorced and Widowed are recorded unchanged. Registered partnership is provisionally recorded as Married, together with the partner's date of birth. Separated is provisionally recorded as Married, together with the date of birth of the spouse they are separated from. Both provisional mappings still have to be checked with a domain expert and the fund (see OQ-civil-status-partnership). The fund's civil status is always derived again from the latest civil status.
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-pension_portal-9b24c5, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, D-003, D-004, D-037_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-004** (product decision (Product owner)): The spouse's (or registered partner's) date of birth is asked from married employees, registered partners and separated employees (all entered at the fund as Married, see D-003), not from divorced or widowed employees. Keep OQ-spouse-dob-other-status open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert (screenshot of the fund's screen). This closes OQ-separated-spouse-dob; the separated case is verified together with OQ-civil-status-partnership.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
