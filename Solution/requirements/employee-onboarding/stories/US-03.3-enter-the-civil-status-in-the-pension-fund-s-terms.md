# US-03.3 – Enter the civil status in the pension fund's terms

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [3. Civil status and spouse for the pension fund](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** every employee's civil status expressed in my list (Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed), **so that** the registration can be made with a valid civil status, even for registered partners and separated employees

Priority **P1** – Civil status is a required fund field; a value outside the fund's list would block the registration. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-pension_portal-9b24c5, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, D-003, D-037

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-civil-status-partnership](../open-questions.md#oq-civil-status-partnership), [OQ-spouse-dob-other-status](../open-questions.md#oq-spouse-dob-other-status)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Civil status, Civil status for the pension fund

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-03.3.1 Fund's own values taken as answered**
  - **Given** the employee's civil status is Single, Married, Divorced or Widowed
  - **When** Lonio records the civil status for the pension fund
  - **Then** the same value is recorded for the fund
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status for the pension fund | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single (illustrative) | Single | → | Single | ✅ |
  | Married (illustrative) | Married | → | Married | ✅ |
  | Divorced (illustrative) | Divorced | → | Divorced | ✅ |
  | Widowed (illustrative) | Widowed | → | Widowed | ✅ |

- **AC-03.3.2 Registered partnership and Separated entered as Married (provisional)**
  - **Given** the employee's civil status is Registered partnership or Separated, which the fund's list does not contain
  - **When** Lonio records the civil status for the pension fund
  - **Then** Married is recorded for the fund, together with the partner's or spouse's date of birth. This mapping is provisional until a domain expert and the fund confirm it (see OQ-civil-status-partnership)
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-9b24c5, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, D-003, D-004_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status for the pension fund | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel, registered partnership (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | Married | asked | ✅ |
  | Separated (illustrative) | Separated | → | Married | asked | ✅ |

- **AC-03.3.3 Always derived from the latest civil status**
  - **Given** the civil status recorded for the fund is derived from the employee's answer
  - **When** the civil status answer changes (before submission by the employee, afterwards by HR)
  - **Then** the civil status for the fund is derived again from the latest answer; the employee is never asked for it separately
  - _Sources: D-037, G-08_

## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-004** (product decision (Product owner)): The spouse's (or registered partner's) date of birth is asked from married employees, registered partners and separated employees (all entered at the fund as Married, see D-003), not from divorced or widowed employees. Keep OQ-spouse-dob-other-status open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert (screenshot of the fund's screen). This closes OQ-separated-spouse-dob; the separated case is verified together with OQ-civil-status-partnership.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
