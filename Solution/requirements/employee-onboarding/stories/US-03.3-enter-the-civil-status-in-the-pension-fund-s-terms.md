# US-03.3 – Enter the civil status in the pension fund's terms

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [3. Civil status and spouse for the pension fund](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** every employee's civil status expressed as one of my options (Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed), **so that** every new employee can be registered, including registered partners and separated employees, for whom my list has no option

Priority **P1** – The fund's list lacks Registered partnership and Separated; without a mapping such employees (e.g. Daniel) cannot be registered. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4, F-pension_portal-9b24c5, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, F-hr_transcript-3907fe, D-003

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-civil-status-partnership](../open-questions.md#oq-civil-status-partnership), [OQ-spouse-dob-other-status](../open-questions.md#oq-spouse-dob-other-status)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Civil status, Civil status for the pension fund

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-03.3.1 Civil statuses on the fund's list are taken over as they are**
  - **Given** the employee answered Single, Married, Divorced or Widowed
  - **When** the civil status for the pension fund is determined
  - **Then** it is the same value as the employee's answer
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-3d1ae4_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status for the pension fund | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single (illustrative) | Single | → | Single | ✅ |
  | Married (illustrative) | Married | → | Married | ✅ |
  | Divorced (illustrative) | Divorced | → | Divorced | ✅ |
  | Widowed (illustrative) | Widowed | → | Widowed | ✅ |

- **AC-03.3.2 Registered partnership and Separated are provisionally entered as Married**
  - **Given** the employee answered Registered partnership or Separated
  - **When** the civil status for the pension fund is determined
  - **Then** it is Married, provisionally until a domain expert and Pensionskasse Muster confirm how these employees are registered and whether the partner's details are wanted (see OQ-civil-status-partnership)
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-9b24c5, F-hr_transcript-f197d6, D-003_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status for the pension fund | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Daniel (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | Married | ✅ |
  | Separated employee (illustrative) | Separated | → | Married | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **F-hr_transcript-3907fe** (hr_transcript): Muster AG has at least one employee (Daniel, finance) in a registered partnership.
  - "we do have one colleague in a registered partnership. Daniel, in finance." — hr_transcript/T-034, Anna
