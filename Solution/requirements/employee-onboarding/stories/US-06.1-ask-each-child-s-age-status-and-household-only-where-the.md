# US-06.1 – Ask each child's age status and household, only where the tariff needs it

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees subject to source tax to say whether they have children and, for each child, whether it is a minor or of age and in first education, and whether it lives in their household, **so that** Lonio can decide child by child whether the child counts for the tariff code, without asking anyone else about their children

Priority **P1** – Lonio computes the tariff code instead of HR (D-001); without the child answers the child digit cannot be computed. · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-052c98, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-42d590, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-987091, G-01, D-001, D-003

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Has biological or adopted children, Child: minor or of age and in first education, Child lives in the same household, Child result (per child)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.1.1 Children question only for employees subject to source tax**
  - **Given** an employee is filling in the source tax step
  - **When** the employee is subject to source tax
  - **Then** the employee is asked, and must answer yes or no, whether they have biological or adopted children (for married employees and registered partners: children of the employee or of the spouses/partners); employees not subject to source tax are not asked about children at all. The answer is used for the source tax tariff code.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, G-01, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Nationality | Has biological or adopted children | → | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or of age and in first education | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single employee living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | Single | – | – | → | required | – | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Single | Switzerland | – | → | not asked | not asked | ✅ |
  | Employee subject to source tax without children (illustrative) | Germany | Married | – | no | → | – | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.1.2 One block of questions per child**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has answered that they have children
  - **When** the employee describes their children
  - **Then** the employee can add and remove children, and for each child is asked whether it is 'Minor', 'Of age and in first education' or 'Neither'; an answer is required for every child added.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-052c98, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-42d590, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Has biological or adopted children | → | Child: minor or of age and in first education | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee with children (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-06.1.3 A child that is neither a minor nor in first education does not count**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has a child
  - **When** the employee answers 'Neither' for the child's age status
  - **Then** no further question is asked about that child and the child does not count for the tariff code.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-987091, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or of age and in first education | → | Child lives in the same household | Child result (per child) | Child digit of the tariff code | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, child 'weder noch' -> B0N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-987091) | Germany | Married | no | yes | Neither | → | not asked | does not count | 0 | ✅ |

- **AC-06.1.4 Household question for minors and children in first education**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has a child that is a minor or of age and in first education
  - **When** the employee describes that child
  - **Then** the employee is asked, and must answer yes or no, whether the child lives in the same household.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or of age and in first education | → | Child lives in the same household | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Minor child (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | → | required | ✅ |
  | Child of age in first education (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Of age and in first education | → | asked | ✅ |
