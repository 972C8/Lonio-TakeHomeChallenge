# US-06.1 – Ask each child's age status and household, only where the tariff needs it

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees subject to source tax to say whether they have children and, for each child, whether it is a minor, of age and in first education, or neither, and whether it lives in their household, **so that** Lonio can decide child by child whether a child counts for the tariff code it computes, while employees not subject to source tax are not asked about their children at all

Priority **P1** – Lonio replaces HR's manual click-through of the Zurich calculator (D-001); without the child questions no tariff code can be computed. HR today does not know up front who must answer source tax questions (F-hr_transcript-b84498). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-052c98, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-42d590, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-987091, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f, F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-hr_transcript-d7431e, G-01, D-001, D-003, D-036

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.1.1 Children question only for employees subject to source tax**
  - **Given** an employee filling in the questionnaire
  - **When** Lonio has established whether the employee is subject to source tax
  - **Then** only an employee subject to source tax is asked whether they have biological or adopted children, answered yes or no, and the answer is required; for a married employee or registered partner the question includes the children of the spouse or partner, for everyone else only the employee's own children. Employees not subject to source tax are not asked. The answer is used only for the source tax tariff code.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-75cd87, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a33b65, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, D-001, D-003_

  | Non-Swiss single employee in Switzerland without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Single | – | – | → | required | – | ✅ |

- **AC-06.1.2 Age status asked for each child**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax
  - **When** the employee answers that they have children
  - **Then** for each child the employee is asked whether the child is 'Minor', 'Of age and in first education' or 'Neither' (required); the employee can add further children and remove a child, and the child questions repeat per child. If the employee has no children, no child questions are asked.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-052c98, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-42d590, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, D-001_

  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Has children (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Single | yes | → | required | – | ✅ |
  | No children (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Single | no | → | not asked | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.1.3 A child that is neither a minor nor in first education does not count**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax who has children
  - **When** the employee answers 'Neither' for a child
  - **Then** no further questions are asked for that child and the child does not count for the tariff code
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-987091, D-001_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Spouse's Swiss citizenship | Spouse holds a C permit | Spouse / partner works or receives replacement income | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | → | Child lives in the same household | How a child counts for the tariff | Tariff child digit | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **AC-06.1.4 Household question for minors and children in first education**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with a child that is a minor or of age and in first education
  - **When** the employee has answered the child's age status
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether the child lives in the same household, yes or no (required)
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f, D-001_

  | Example | Country of the home address (country of residence) | Swiss citizenship | Holds a C permit | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | → | Child lives in the same household | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Minor child (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Single | yes | Minor | → | required | ✅ |
  | Child of age in first education (illustrative) | Switzerland | Not Swiss | no | Single | yes | Of age and in first education | → | asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.1.5 Child answers that no longer apply are not kept**
  - **Given** an employee who answered child questions
  - **When** the employee changes an earlier answer so that they are no longer subject to source tax, answers that they have no children, or removes a child, and then submits
  - **Then** the child answers that no longer apply are kept while the employee goes back and forth but are discarded on submission
  - _Sources: G-01, D-036_

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
