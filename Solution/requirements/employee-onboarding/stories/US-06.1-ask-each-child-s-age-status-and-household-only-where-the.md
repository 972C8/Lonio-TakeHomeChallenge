# US-06.1 – Ask each child's age status and household, only where the tariff needs it

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees subject to source tax to say whether they have children and, for each child, whether the child is a minor or of age and in first education, and whether the child lives in their household, **so that** Lonio can tell which children may count for the Zurich tariff code without asking anyone else about their children

Priority **P1** – Lonio now computes the full tariff code itself instead of HR using the calculator (D-001). Without the children questions the child digit cannot be set. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-052c98, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-42d590, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-987091, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, D-001, D-003, D-036

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Has children, Child: minor or of age in first education, Child lives in the same household, Child result (per child)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.1.1 Children question only for employees subject to source tax**
  - **Given** an employee is filling in the onboarding questionnaire
  - **When** Lonio has decided whether the employee is subject to source tax (rule subject_to_source_tax)
  - **Then** only employees subject to source tax are asked whether they have biological or adopted children, answered "yes" or "no", and the answer is required. Married employees and registered partners are asked about children of the employee or of either spouse or partner. Everybody else is not asked. The answer is recorded for source tax.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-103b19, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ab8a06, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-a166c7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b62b85, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | → | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Germany is asked (illustrative) | Germany | Single | – | – | → | required | – | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss employee without C permit living in Switzerland is asked (illustrative) | Switzerland | Divorced | Brazil | no | → | asked | – | ✅ |
  | Swiss employee living in Switzerland is not asked (illustrative) | Switzerland | Married | Switzerland | – | → | not asked | not asked | ✅ |
  | C permit holder living in Switzerland is not asked (illustrative) | Switzerland | Single | Italy | yes | → | not asked | – | ✅ |

- **AC-06.1.2 One block per child, starting with the age status**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax answered "yes" to having children
  - **When** the employee reaches the children questions
  - **Then** the employee goes through one block of questions per child and can add another child or remove one. Every answer in a block is about that same child. Each block starts with whether the child is "Minor", "Of age and in first education" or "Neither", and this answer is required. An employee who answered "no" to having children gets no child block.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-052c98, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-09b06d, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-42d590, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fd9a15, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | Has children | → | Child: minor or of age in first education | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee with children gets the age status question (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee without children gets no child block (illustrative) | Germany | Married | no | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.1.3 'Neither' ends the child's block and the child does not count**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax is answering a child block
  - **When** the employee says the child is neither a minor nor of age and in first education
  - **Then** no further questions are asked about this child and the child does not count for the tariff code (child result 'Not counted'). If the employee changes an earlier child answer this way, answers to that child's questions that no longer apply are discarded on submission.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-987091, D-001, D-036_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | → | Child lives in the same household | Child result (per child) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Adult child no longer in first education (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Neither | → | not asked | Not counted | ✅ |
  | Single employee, child neither minor nor in first education (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Neither | → | – | Not counted | ✅ |

- **AC-06.1.4 Same household asked for every child who may count**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax said a child is a minor or of age and in first education
  - **When** the employee continues that child's block
  - **Then** the employee is asked whether the child lives in their household, answered "yes" or "no", and the answer is required. The answer decides which further questions follow for that child.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-5725a8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3a75cc, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7c667f, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | → | Child lives in the same household | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Minor child (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | → | required | ✅ |
  | Child of age in first education (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Of age and in first education | → | asked | ✅ |
