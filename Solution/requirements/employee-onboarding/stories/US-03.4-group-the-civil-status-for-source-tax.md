# US-03.4 – Group the civil status for source tax

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [3. Civil status and spouse for the pension fund](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the employee's civil status grouped into married (married or registered partnership) or single (single, divorced, separated, widowed), as the calculator does, **so that** the spouse exemption and the tariff code are determined from the same civil status answer, without asking again

Priority **P1** – Lonio now computes the tariff code itself (D-001); married vs single decides the tariff letter and whether the spouse exemption can apply. · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, D-001, D-003

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-civil-status-partnership](../open-questions.md#oq-civil-status-partnership), [OQ-spouse-dob-other-status](../open-questions.md#oq-spouse-dob-other-status)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Civil status, Civil status for source tax

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-03.4.1 Married and registered partnership count as married**
  - **Given** the employee answered Married or Registered partnership
  - **When** the civil status for source tax is determined
  - **Then** the employee counts as married for source tax, including for the spouse exemption
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status for source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Married | → | married | ✅ |
  | Daniel (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | married | ✅ |

- **AC-03.4.2 Single, divorced, separated and widowed count as single**
  - **Given** the employee answered Single, Divorced, Separated or Widowed
  - **When** the civil status for source tax is determined
  - **Then** the employee counts as single for source tax; a separated employee therefore gets no spouse exemption, even though the fund registers them as Married
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status for source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single (illustrative) | Single | → | single | ✅ |
  | Divorced (illustrative) | Divorced | → | single | ✅ |
  | Separated (illustrative) | Separated | → | single | ✅ |
  | Widowed (illustrative) | Widowed | → | single | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9** (zh_source_tax_calculator): Civil status has two options: 'Verheiratet oder eingetragene Partnerschaft' (married or registered partnership) and 'Ledig, Geschieden, Getrennt, Verwitwet' (single, divorced, separated, widowed).
  - "Options: Verheiratet oder eingetragene Partnerschaft | Ledig, Geschieden, Getrennt, Verwitwet" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q02
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3** (zh_source_tax_calculator): The calculator asks for the civil status (Zivilstand) of the person subject to source tax.
  - "Zivilstand" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q02
