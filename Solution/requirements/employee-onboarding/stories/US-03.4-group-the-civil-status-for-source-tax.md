# US-03.4 – Group the civil status for source tax

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [3. Civil status and spouse for the pension fund](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse).

**As** source tax office, **I want** each employee's civil status to be sorted into one of the calculator's two groups: 'married or registered partnership' or 'single, divorced, separated or widowed', **so that** the tariff code, the spouse exemption and the follow-up source tax questions use the calculator's logic

Priority **P1** – Lonio now computes the tariff code itself instead of HR using the calculator. A wrong group gives a wrong tariff letter. · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3, D-001, D-003, D-037

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-civil-status-partnership](../open-questions.md#oq-civil-status-partnership), [OQ-spouse-dob-other-status](../open-questions.md#oq-spouse-dob-other-status)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Civil status, Civil status for source tax

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-03.4.1 Civil status group for source tax**
  - **Given** the employee has stated their civil status
  - **When** Lonio derives the civil status for source tax
  - **Then** Married and Registered partnership count as 'married or registered partnership'. Registered partners therefore also qualify for the spouse exemption. Single, Divorced, Widowed and Separated count as 'single, divorced, separated or widowed'. Separated employees therefore get no spouse exemption. The group is always derived again from the latest civil status.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-003, D-037_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status for source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married (illustrative) | Married | → | Married or registered partnership | ✅ |
  | Daniel, registered partnership (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | Married or registered partnership | ✅ |
  | Single (illustrative) | Single | → | Single, divorced, separated or widowed | ✅ |
  | Divorced (illustrative) | Divorced | → | Single, divorced, separated or widowed | ✅ |
  | Widowed (illustrative) | Widowed | → | Single, divorced, separated or widowed | ✅ |
  | Separated (illustrative) | Separated | → | Single, divorced, separated or widowed | ✅ |

- **AC-03.4.2 Separated: Married for the fund, single for source tax**
  - **Given** a separated employee
  - **When** Lonio derives the civil status for each party
  - **Then** the pension fund gets Married (provisional, see OQ-civil-status-partnership), and source tax counts the employee as single. Both results come from the one civil status answer; the employee is not asked anything twice.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, G-01, D-003_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Civil status for the pension fund | Civil status for source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Separated employee (illustrative) | Separated | → | Married | Single, divorced, separated or widowed | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9** (zh_source_tax_calculator): Civil status has two options: 'Verheiratet oder eingetragene Partnerschaft' (married or registered partnership) and 'Ledig, Geschieden, Getrennt, Verwitwet' (single, divorced, separated, widowed).
  - "Options: Verheiratet oder eingetragene Partnerschaft | Ledig, Geschieden, Getrennt, Verwitwet" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q02
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-9007c3** (zh_source_tax_calculator): The calculator asks for the civil status (Zivilstand) of the person subject to source tax.
  - "Zivilstand" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q02
  - "Section heading: 'Angaben zur quellensteuerpflichtigen Person'" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q02
- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
