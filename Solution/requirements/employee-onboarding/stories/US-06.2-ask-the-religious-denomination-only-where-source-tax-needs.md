# US-06.2 – Ask the religious denomination only where source tax needs it and derive the church-tax suffix

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** new employee, **I want** to be asked for my religious denomination only if I am subject to source tax, **so that** Lonio can set the church-tax suffix of my source tax tariff code without collecting my religion when no receiving party needs it

Priority **P1** – Every tariff code ends in the church-tax suffix. Without it Lonio cannot compute the code (D-001), and HR would be back to clicking through the calculator by hand (F-hr_transcript-3cd6fa). Asking it of everyone would break data minimisation (G-01). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b05b44, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, G-09, D-001, D-006, D-037

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics) and [information.md](../information.md)): Religious denomination (confession), Church-tax suffix

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.2.1 Denomination is asked only of employees subject to source tax**
  - **Given** an employee whose earlier answers make them subject to source tax (rule subject_to_source_tax: living abroad, or living in Switzerland without Swiss citizenship, without a C permit and without the spouse exemption)
  - **When** the employee reaches the source tax questions
  - **Then** the denomination question is asked and must be answered. Employees not subject to source tax are never asked it. The income type is not asked first, because a new employee always has earned income (D-006).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b05b44, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, D-006_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | Spouse's / registered partner's nationality | Spouse's / registered partner's country of residence | → | Subject to source tax | Religious denomination (confession) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9) | Switzerland | Brazil | no | Married | Switzerland | Switzerland | → | no | not asked | ✅ |
  | Maria (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204) | Switzerland | Portugal | yes | – | – | – | → | no | not asked | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen living abroad, like Stefan (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) (illustrative) | Austria | – | – | Single | – | – | → | yes | required | ✅ |
  | Swiss citizen living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | Single | – | – | → | no | not asked | ✅ |
  | Non-Swiss employee in Switzerland without C permit (illustrative) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Single | – | – | → | yes | required | ✅ |

- **AC-06.2.2 Denomination offers exactly the calculator's two choices**
  - **Given** the denomination question is asked
  - **When** the employee answers it
  - **Then** the employee chooses exactly one of 'Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed' or 'None or other'. No other denomination is offered or recorded, and the answer is recorded for HR for the source tax tariff.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, G-01, G-09, D-001, D-034_
- **AC-06.2.3 Church-tax suffix follows the denomination**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax who has answered the denomination question
  - **When** Lonio derives the church-tax suffix of the tariff code
  - **Then** the suffix is Y for 'Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed' and N for 'None or other'. It is derived again whenever the denomination answer changes (D-037).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c, D-001, D-037_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Nationality | Holds a C permit (permanent residence) | Civil status | Religious denomination (confession) | → | Church-tax suffix | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator path A0Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-037b1c) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Single | Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed | → | Y | ✅ |
  | Calculator path A0N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a) | Switzerland | Italy | no | Single | None or other | → | N | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-006** (product decision (Product owner)): A new employee always has earned income. Do not ask the income type, and drop the replacement-income questions and the tariff G / Q cases. This closes OQ-income-type. To be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **F-hr_transcript-b84498** (hr_transcript): Source tax questions should only be asked of employees who are subject to source tax, not of everybody.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
