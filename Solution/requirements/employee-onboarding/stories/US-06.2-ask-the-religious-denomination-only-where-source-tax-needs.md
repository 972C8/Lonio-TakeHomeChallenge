# US-06.2 – Ask the religious denomination only where source tax needs it and derive the church-tax suffix

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the denomination of every employee who needs a source tax tariff code, and only of them, turned into the church-tax suffix Y or N, **so that** the last letter of the tariff code is right, and nobody who is not subject to source tax is asked about their religion

Priority **P1** – Without the denomination no tariff code can be computed, so HR would have to go back to the manual calculator step (D-001). Asking it of everyone would collect sensitive personal data that no party needs (G-01, F-hr_transcript-b84498). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b05b44, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, F-hr_transcript-b84498, G-01, D-001, D-040, D-047, D-045

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.2.1 Denomination is asked only of employees subject to source tax**
  - **Given** an employee has answered the questions in steps 1 to 5 (about you, home address, nationality and C permit, civil status and spouse, cross-border commuting)
  - **When** the employee reaches step 6 'Source tax details'
  - **Then** the denomination is asked, and required, only if the employee is subject to source tax. For employees who live in Liechtenstein it is asked only if they do not return daily and expect more than 45 work-related non-return days. Employees who are not subject to source tax, and Liechtenstein residents who in principle have no source tax liability, are not asked. Assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-040): for Liechtenstein residents the daily-return and 45-day questions come before the denomination, and the denomination is skipped when the answers mean 'in principle no source tax liability'.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b05b44, G-01, D-040, D-047, D-007_

- **AC-06.2.2 Denomination offers exactly the calculator's two choices**
  - **Given** the denomination is asked
  - **When** the employee answers it
  - **Then** the employee chooses exactly one of 'Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed' or 'None or other'. The question cannot be left empty and no other denomination is collected.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, G-01, G-09_
- **AC-06.2.3 Church-tax suffix follows the denomination**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has answered the denomination
  - **When** Lonio derives the church-tax suffix of the tariff code
  - **Then** 'Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed' gives the suffix Y, and 'None or other' gives the suffix N. This follows the calculator's results (for example A0Y and A0N). The suffix is derived again whenever the denomination changes.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, D-001, D-037_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | Civil status | Denomination | Has biological or adopted children | → | Church-tax suffix | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Calculator case A0Y (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a) | France | Single | Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed | no | → | Y | ✅ |
  | Calculator case A0N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a) | France | Single | None or other | no | → | N | ✅ |

- **AC-06.2.4 Denomination is used only for the source tax tariff**
  - **Given** the employee has answered the denomination
  - **When** the answers are recorded
  - **Then** the denomination is recorded for HR only as the input to the source tax tariff code. It is not part of the pension fund registration. If a later answer means the employee is no longer asked the denomination, the answer is discarded on submission.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, G-01, D-001, D-036_
