# US-06.2 – Ask the religious denomination only where source tax needs it and derive the church-tax suffix

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the employee's religious denomination to be asked only from employees who are subject to source tax and need a tariff code, and turned into the church-tax part of the tariff code, **so that** the tariff code ends in the correct church-tax suffix (Y or N), while employees who are not subject to source tax are not asked about their religion

Priority **P1** – The denomination is part of every tariff code Lonio computes in place of HR's manual calculator step (D-001); without it no tariff code can be produced. Asking it of everybody would collect sensitive data no party needs (G-01, F-hr_transcript-b84498). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b05b44, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, F-hr_transcript-b84498, D-001, D-040, G-01

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.2.1 Denomination is asked only when a tariff code is needed**
  - **Given** an employee filling in the onboarding questionnaire, whose answers on residence, nationality, C permit, civil status and spouse decide whether they are subject to source tax (rule 'Subject to source tax'); for employees living in Liechtenstein, the questions on daily return and on more than 45 non-return days come first
  - **When** the questionnaire reaches the source tax tariff questions
  - **Then** the denomination is asked and required only if the employee is subject to source tax and, for an employee living in Liechtenstein, only if they do not return daily and expect more than 45 non-return days. In all other cases it is not asked. The question order and skipping for Liechtenstein residents is an assumption to be verified by a domain expert (D-040).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b05b44, F-hr_transcript-b84498, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb87f1, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-cc19c7, D-001, D-040, G-01_

- **AC-06.2.2 Denomination answers offered**
  - **Given** the denomination is asked
  - **When** the employee answers
  - **Then** the employee chooses exactly one of 'Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed' or 'None or other'; no other denominations are offered, and the answer is recorded for the source tax tariff determination
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, D-001_
- **AC-06.2.3 Church-tax suffix follows the denomination**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax who has answered the denomination
  - **When** Lonio derives the church-tax suffix of the tariff code
  - **Then** the suffix is Y for 'Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed' and N for 'None or other'
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, D-001_

  | Example | Country (of residence) | Denomination | → | Church-tax suffix | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Church member living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed | → | Y | ✅ |
  | No church membership, living in Germany (illustrative) | Germany | None or other | → | N | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-040** (product decision (Product owner)): For employees living in Liechtenstein, the questions on daily return and on more than 45 days abroad come before denomination, spouse and children. When the answers mean 'in principle no source tax liability', the tariff questions are skipped. When the employee stays abroad more than 45 days, the normal tariff is recorded with the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement' and flagged for HR. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
