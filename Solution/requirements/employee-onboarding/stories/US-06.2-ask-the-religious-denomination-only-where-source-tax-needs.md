# US-06.2 – Ask the religious denomination only where source tax needs it and derive the church-tax suffix

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [7. Source tax tariff: denomination, spouse employment and tariff code](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** the denomination of every employee who needs a Zurich tariff code, reduced to the two groups the calculator uses, and the church-tax suffix worked out from it, **so that** the tariff code ends in the right church-tax suffix (Y or N), and employees who need no tariff code are not asked about their religion

Priority **P1** – Without the denomination Lonio cannot complete the tariff code it computes in place of HR (D-001). HR also needs source tax questions asked only of the employees who are subject to source tax (F-hr_transcript-b84498). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b05b44, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, F-hr_transcript-b84498, D-001, D-040, D-047

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.2.1 Denomination is asked only of employees who need a tariff code**
  - **Given** an employee filling in step 6 'Source tax details' of the questionnaire
  - **When** Lonio decides whether to ask for the denomination
  - **Then** The denomination is asked, and is required, only if the employee is subject to source tax. It is not asked of an employee living in Liechtenstein who returns home daily, or who does not return daily but expects at most 45 non-return days, because that person is in principle not liable and the tariff questions are skipped. Assumption, to be verified by a domain expert: the Liechtenstein questions come before the denomination (D-040). No one else is asked. The answer is recorded for HR to determine the source tax tariff.

- **AC-06.2.2 Denomination offers only the calculator's two groups**
  - **Given** the denomination is asked
  - **When** the employee answers
  - **Then** The employee chooses exactly one of two answers: 'Roman Catholic, Christ Catholic or Evangelical Reformed' or 'None or other'. No more specific religion is asked or recorded, because the calculator only distinguishes these two groups.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, G-01_
- **AC-06.2.3 Church-tax suffix follows the denomination**
  - **Given** an employee who answered the denomination
  - **When** Lonio works out the church-tax suffix of the tariff code
  - **Then** 'Roman Catholic, Christ Catholic or Evangelical Reformed' gives the suffix Y. 'None or other' gives the suffix N.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, D-001_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | Civil status | Denomination | → | Church-tax suffix | Checked |

## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-040** (product decision (Product owner)): For employees living in Liechtenstein, the questions on daily return and on more than 45 days abroad come before denomination, spouse and children. When the answers mean 'in principle no source tax liability', the tariff questions are skipped. When the employee stays abroad more than 45 days, the normal tariff is recorded with the note 'subject to the double taxation agreement' and flagged for HR. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-047** (product decision (Product owner)): The questionnaire has exactly these steps, in this order, and feature.md, the flow rule and the stories use the same list. 1 About you (first name, last name, date of birth, first working day); 2 Home address (as on the first working day, canton only in Switzerland); 3 Nationality and C permit (only employees living in Switzerland); 4 Civil status and spouse (civil status is asked of everyone, wherever they live); 5 Cross-border commuting (Liechtenstein and Germany residents subject to source tax); 6 Source tax details (denomination, spouse gainfully employed, has children); 7 Children (one block per child); 8 Permit reminder, summary and submission. There is no separate step for the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
