# US-06.2 – Ask the religious denomination only where source tax needs it and derive the church-tax suffix

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Source tax tariff: income, denomination and spouse employment](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees subject to source tax with earned income to state whether they belong to a Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed church, or to none or another, and the church-tax suffix to be derived from it, **so that** the tariff carries the right church-tax suffix (Y or N), while this sensitive information is never collected from employees who are not subject to source tax

Priority **P1** – The tariff cannot be completed without the suffix, and asking denomination of everyone would breach data minimisation, which the product owner names as a hard constraint (G-01). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-208104, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b05b44, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2d4dda, F-hr_transcript-5af8d0, G-01, G-09

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics) and [information.md](../information.md)): Religious denomination (church tax), Church-tax suffix of the tariff

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.2.1 Denomination is asked only of employees subject to source tax with earned income**
  - **Given** the employee's subject_to_source_tax result and type of income are known
  - **When** the employee reaches the denomination question in the source tax step
  - **Then** the denomination is asked and must be answered only if the employee is subject to source tax and has earned income; it is not asked of employees who are not subject to source tax, nor of employees with replacement income.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b05b44, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-351289, G-01_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | Permit type | Civil status | Spouse's nationality | Spouse's country of residence | Type of income (source tax) | → | Religious denomination (church tax) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tomás (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-b90bd9): not subject, denomination never collected | Switzerland | Brazil | B permit | Married | Switzerland | Switzerland | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Maria (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-845204): C permit, denomination never collected | Switzerland | Portugal | C permit | – | – | – | – | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Subject employee with earned income (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Single | – | – | Earned income | → | required | ✅ |
  | Subject employee with replacement income (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Single | – | – | Replacement income | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-06.2.2 Allowed answers for the denomination**
  - **Given** the denomination question is asked
  - **When** the employee answers it
  - **Then** the employee chooses exactly one of "Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed" or "None or other" (the calculator's two options); no more detailed denomination is collected. The answer is used only for the source tax tariff.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-b7fea8, G-01, G-09_
- **AC-06.2.3 Church-tax suffix is derived from type of income and denomination**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has answered the type of income and, if asked, the denomination
  - **When** the church-tax suffix of the tariff is derived
  - **Then** earned income with "Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed" gives suffix Y; earned income with "None or other" gives suffix N (e.g. A0Y vs A0N); replacement income gives no suffix (tariff G).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2eab9a, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-2d4dda_

  | Example | Country (of the home address) | Nationality | Permit type | Civil status | Type of income (source tax) | Religious denomination (church tax) | → | Church-tax suffix of the tariff | Religious denomination (church tax) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Earned income, member of a recognised church (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Single | Earned income | Roman Catholic, Christian Catholic or Evangelical Reformed | → | Y | – | ✅ |
  | Earned income, no or another denomination (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Single | Earned income | None or other | → | N | – | ✅ |
  | Replacement income (illustrative) | Switzerland | Germany | B permit | Single | Replacement income | – | → | null | not asked | ❌ |
