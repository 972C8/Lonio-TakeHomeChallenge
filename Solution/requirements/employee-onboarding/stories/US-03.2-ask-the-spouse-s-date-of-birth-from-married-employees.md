# US-03.2 – Ask the spouse's date of birth from married employees

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [3. Civil status and spouse for the pension fund](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse).

**As** pension fund, **I want** the date of birth of the husband, wife or registered partner for every employee registered with me as Married, **so that** the registration is not rejected for a missing spouse's date of birth

Priority **P1** – The fund rejects a Married registration without the spouse's date of birth ('Spouse's date of birth is required.'), which blocks the registration. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-pension_portal-a0065b, F-pension_portal-39bab1, F-pension_portal-0eed50, F-pension_portal-ef28c3, G-01, D-003, D-004

> ⚠ Open questions affecting this section: [OQ-civil-status-partnership](../open-questions.md#oq-civil-status-partnership), [OQ-spouse-dob-other-status](../open-questions.md#oq-spouse-dob-other-status)
Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-03-civil-status-spouse) and [information.md](../information.md)): Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-03.2.1 Asked and required for Married, Registered partnership and Separated**
  - **Given** the employee's civil status is Married, Registered partnership or Separated
  - **When** they fill in the family step
  - **Then** they are asked the date of birth of their husband, wife or registered partner, and must answer it
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-2334d8, F-pension_portal-0eed50, D-003, D-004_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Married | → | required | ✅ |
  | Daniel (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3907fe) | Registered partnership | → | required | ✅ |
  | Separated employee (illustrative) | Separated | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-03.2.2 Not asked for Single, Divorced or Widowed**
  - **Given** the employee's civil status is Single, Divorced or Widowed
  - **When** they fill in the family step
  - **Then** they are not asked a spouse's or partner's date of birth (whether the fund needs it for Divorced or Widowed is still open, see OQ-spouse-dob-other-status)
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-39bab1, G-01, D-004_

  | Example | Civil status | → | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Single employee (illustrative) | Single | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Divorced employee (illustrative) | Divorced | → | not asked | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee (illustrative) | Widowed | → | not asked | ✅ |

- **AC-03.2.3 Form of the answer and receiving party**
  - **Given** the employee is asked the spouse's or partner's date of birth
  - **When** they answer
  - **Then** the date is given as DD.MM.YYYY and is recorded for the pension fund registration, together with the civil status entered at the fund as Married
  - _Sources: F-pension_portal-ef28c3, F-pension_portal-a0065b, D-003, D-004_
- **AC-03.2.4 Spouse's date of birth discarded when no longer applicable**
  - **Given** the employee entered a spouse's date of birth and then changed the civil status to Single, Divorced or Widowed
  - **When** the questionnaire is submitted
  - **Then** the spouse's date of birth is not recorded
  - _Sources: D-036, G-01_

  | Example | Civil status | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | → | Spouse's / registered partner's date of birth | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Changed from Married to Divorced (illustrative) | Divorced | 12.05.1988 | → | not asked | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-003** (product decision (Product owner)): Civil status offers Single, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Registered partnership and Separated. For source tax, registered partnership counts as married (including the spouse exemption) and separated counts as single, as in the calculator. For the pension fund, provisionally, registered partnership is entered as Married with the partner's date of birth, and separated as Married. Keep OQ-civil-status-partnership open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert.
- **D-004** (product decision (Product owner)): The spouse's (or registered partner's) date of birth is asked from married employees, registered partners and separated employees (all entered at the fund as Married, see D-003), not from divorced or widowed employees. Keep OQ-spouse-dob-other-status open as non-blocking, to be verified by a domain expert (screenshot of the fund's screen). This closes OQ-separated-spouse-dob; the separated case is verified together with OQ-civil-status-partnership.
- **D-036** (product decision (Product owner)): Answers to questions that no longer apply after the employee changes an earlier answer are discarded on submission; until then they may be kept so going back and forth loses no input.
- **F-hr_transcript-2334d8** (hr_transcript): If the employee is married, the pension fund wants the date of birth of the husband or wife.
  - "And if someone is married, they want the date of birth of the husband or wife." — hr_transcript/T-032, Anna

Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
