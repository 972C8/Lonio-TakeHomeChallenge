# US-03.1 – Employee confirms the first working day

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [3. First working day and insurance start date](../feature.md#SEC-03-employment-start).

**As** HR, **I want** every new employee to enter and confirm their own first working day in the onboarding questionnaire, **so that** HR and the pension fund work with the employee's current start date, which often moves (for example when the employee negotiates an earlier exit with the old employer), and HR's 'number one' item is never missing

Priority **P1** – HR calls the first working day the 'number one' item for the pension fund registration; a wrong or outdated date has already caused a wrong registration and weeks of fixing (Julia). · Sources: F-hr_transcript-872bd7, F-hr_transcript-e3721e, F-hr_transcript-e18e66, D-033, D-034, D-037

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-03-employment-start) and [information.md](../information.md)): First working day

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-03.1.1 First working day is always asked and required**
  - **Given** a new employee fills in the onboarding questionnaire, whatever their place of residence, nationality or civil status
  - **When** the employee reaches the employment start question
  - **Then** the employee is asked for their first working day at Muster AG as a date (DD.MM.YYYY); the questionnaire cannot be submitted without it
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-872bd7, F-hr_transcript-e3721e, D-033_

  | Example | First working day | → | First working day | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Julia (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-e18e66) | 15.03.2026 | → | required | ✅ |
  | Start on the 1st of a month (illustrative) | 01.03.2026 | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-03.1.2 The employee confirms the date because it may have moved**
  - **Given** the employee is asked for the first working day
  - **When** the question is shown
  - **Then** the question makes clear that the employee should enter the start date as currently agreed with Muster AG, including any change agreed since the offer (for example an earlier exit from the old employer)
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-e3721e_
- **AC-03.1.3 The first working day is recorded for HR and the pension fund**
  - **Given** the employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** the answers are recorded
  - **Then** the first working day is recorded for HR (to know when the employee starts) and as the basis of the pension fund insurance start date; only the latest date counts and no history of earlier dates is kept
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-872bd7, D-033, D-034_
- **AC-03.1.4 After submission only HR can change the date**
  - **Given** the employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** the employee's start date moves later on
  - **Then** the employee can no longer change the first working day in the questionnaire; the change is made by HR (specified in the later HR-side spec)
  - _Sources: D-033, D-034, D-037_
_Trace check: partial – AC-03.1.1's DD.MM.YYYY date format and the 'at Muster AG' wording are not backed by the cited facts, since the format comes from F-pension_portal-dd8126 and G-10, which are not cited here._


## Evidence
- **D-033** (product decision (Product owner)): The employee enters the first working day. After submission only HR can change it; the latest date always counts and no history of earlier dates is kept.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **F-hr_transcript-872bd7** (hr_transcript): The pension fund needs the employee's first working day. Anna calls it the number one item.
  - "I need their first working day. That's the number one thing" — hr_transcript/T-024, Anna
- **F-hr_transcript-e18e66** (hr_transcript, inferred): When the first working day changes, the insurance start date given to the pension fund must be re-derived and updated. A missed update (Julia) caused a wrong date at the fund, an unfriendly letter and weeks of fixing.
  - "She moved her first working day to the 15th of March, and I didn't update it." — hr_transcript/T-026, Anna
  - "I got a very unfriendly letter." — hr_transcript/T-026, Anna
  - "It took weeks to fix." — hr_transcript/T-028, Anna
- **F-hr_transcript-e3721e** (hr_transcript): The employee should confirm the first working day, because it changes often (for example, when people negotiate an earlier exit with their old employer).
  - "I ask them to confirm it, because it moves more often than you'd think." — hr_transcript/T-024, Anna
  - "People negotiate with their old employer, they get out a week earlier." — hr_transcript/T-026, Anna
