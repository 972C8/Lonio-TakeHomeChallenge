# US-03.1 – Employee confirms the first working day

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [3. First working day and insurance start date](../feature.md#SEC-03-employment-start).

**As** HR, **I want** every new employee to give or confirm their first working day at Muster AG in the onboarding questionnaire, **so that** the pension registration and HR onboarding start from the date that actually applies, even when it has moved since the contract was agreed (for example because the employee negotiated an earlier exit with their old employer)

Priority **P1** – HR calls the first working day the 'number one' item; the pension registration cannot be completed without it, and a stale date has already caused a wrong date at the pension fund and weeks of fixing. · Sources: F-hr_transcript-872bd7, F-hr_transcript-e3721e, F-hr_transcript-e18e66, G-08, G-10

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-03-employment-start) and [information.md](../information.md)): First working day

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-03.1.1 First working day is always asked and required**
  - **Given** a new employee of Muster AG fills in the onboarding questionnaire, whatever their residence, nationality, permit or civil status
  - **When** they reach the Employment start step
  - **Then** they are asked for their first working day at Muster AG and cannot complete the questionnaire without giving it
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-872bd7, F-hr_transcript-e3721e_

  | Example | First working day | → | First working day | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee starting mid-month (illustrative) | 15.03.2026 | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee starting on the 1st of a month (illustrative) | 01.05.2026 | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-03.1.2 Question makes clear the date must be the currently agreed one**
  - **Given** the employee's first working day may have moved since the contract was signed
  - **When** the employee is asked for the first working day
  - **Then** the question asks them to confirm the first working day as currently agreed with Muster AG, so a date that moved (e.g. after an earlier exit from the old employer) is captured
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-e3721e_
- **AC-03.1.3 Form of the answer and who receives it**
  - **Given** the employee answers the first working day
  - **When** the answer is recorded
  - **Then** it is a calendar date (shown as DD.MM.YYYY); HR receives it to confirm the agreed start, and it is the basis for the pension fund's insurance start date (see rule insurance_start_date). The employee is never asked for the insurance start date itself.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-872bd7, F-hr_transcript-e3721e, G-08, G-10_

## Evidence
- **F-hr_transcript-872bd7** (hr_transcript): The pension fund needs the employee's first working day. Anna calls it the number one item.
  - "I need their first working day. That's the number one thing" — hr_transcript/T-024, Anna
- **F-hr_transcript-e18e66** (hr_transcript, inferred): When the first working day changes, the insurance start date given to the pension fund must be re-derived and updated. A missed update (Julia) caused a wrong date at the fund, an unfriendly letter and weeks of fixing.
  - "She moved her first working day to the 15th of March, and I didn't update it." — hr_transcript/T-026, Anna
  - "I got a very unfriendly letter." — hr_transcript/T-026, Anna
  - "It took weeks to fix." — hr_transcript/T-028, Anna
- **F-hr_transcript-e3721e** (hr_transcript): The employee should confirm the first working day, because it changes often (for example, when people negotiate an earlier exit with their old employer).
  - "I ask them to confirm it, because it moves more often than you'd think." — hr_transcript/T-024, Anna
  - "People negotiate with their old employer, they get out a week earlier." — hr_transcript/T-026, Anna
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
- **G-10** (product owner guidance): Business wording for values (used in allowed values and examples): countries by English name ("Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein"), permits as "B permit", "C permit", civil status exactly as the pension fund's list shows it ("Single", "Married", ...), dates as DD.MM.YYYY, yes/no as "yes"/"no".
