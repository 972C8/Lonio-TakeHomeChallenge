# US-03.1 – Employee confirms the first working day

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [3. First working day and insurance start date](../feature.md#SEC-03-employment-start).

**As** new employee, **I want** to enter and confirm my first working day myself in the onboarding questionnaire, **so that** HR and the pension fund work with my actual start date, even when it has moved since the contract was signed (for example because I negotiated an earlier exit with my old employer)

Priority **P1** – HR calls the first working day the 'number one' item; it moves often and a stale date led to a wrong registration at the pension fund that took weeks to fix (F-hr_transcript-872bd7, F-hr_transcript-e3721e, F-hr_transcript-e18e66). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-872bd7, F-hr_transcript-e3721e, F-hr_transcript-e18e66, G-08, G-10, D-033, D-037, D-039, D-034, D-045

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-03-employment-start) and [information.md](../information.md)): First working day

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-03.1.1 First working day is always asked and required**
  - **Given** any new employee fills in the onboarding questionnaire, whatever their residence, nationality or civil status
  - **When** the employee reaches the employment start step
  - **Then** the employee is asked for their first working day, and the questionnaire cannot be submitted without it
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-872bd7, F-hr_transcript-e3721e, D-033_

  | Example | First working day | → | First working day | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Mid-month start, as in Julia's case (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-e18e66) | 15.03.2026 | → | required | ✅ |
  | Start on the 1st of a month (illustrative) | 01.04.2026 | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-03.1.2 The date entered is the contractual start date**
  - **Given** the employee's contract states a start date that falls on a weekend or a public holiday
  - **When** the employee enters the first working day
  - **Then** the employee is asked for, and enters, the contractual start date as the first working day, even though no work is done that day; the date is given as DD.MM.YYYY. Assumption (D-039), to be verified by a domain expert: the first working day is the contractual start date, even on a weekend or public holiday.
  - _Sources: D-039, G-10, D-045_

  | Example | First working day | → | First working day | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Contract starts on Saturday 1 August (Swiss National Day) (illustrative) | 01.08.2026 | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-03.1.3 Who receives the first working day**
  - **Given** the employee has submitted the questionnaire with a first working day
  - **When** the answers are recorded
  - **Then** the first working day is recorded for HR (so HR knows when the employee starts) and serves as the basis for the pension fund's insurance start date; how it is sent to the parties is a separate topic
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-872bd7, G-04, D-034_
- **AC-03.1.4 After submission only HR changes the first working day**
  - **Given** the employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** the first working day moves later on
  - **Then** the employee can no longer change it in the questionnaire; only HR changes it, the latest date always counts and no history of earlier dates is kept. How HR makes the change belongs to the later HR-side spec (see OQ-hr-side-follow-ups).
  - _Sources: D-033, D-037, D-034_

## Evidence
- **D-033** (product decision (Product owner)): The employee enters the first working day. After submission only HR can change it; the latest date always counts and no history of earlier dates is kept.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **D-039** (product decision (Product owner)): The first working day is the contractual start date, even when it falls on a weekend or public holiday. The insurance start date is derived from this date only. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **D-045** (product decision (Product owner)): Business text (stories, acceptance criteria, notices) uses no internal snake_case ids and does not speak of 'expressions'. Any behaviour that has no cited fact or decision is stated in the story as an assumption. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **F-hr_transcript-872bd7** (hr_transcript): The pension fund needs the employee's first working day. Anna calls it the number one item.
