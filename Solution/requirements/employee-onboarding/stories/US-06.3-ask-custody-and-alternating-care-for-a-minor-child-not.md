# US-06.3 – Ask custody and alternating care for a minor child not living with the employee

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** for each minor child outside the employee's household, to know whether the employee shares custody (married or registered partner) or has parental custody (single, divorced, widowed, separated), and whether there is alternating care, **so that** Lonio decides, as the calculator does, whether such a child counts for the tariff code, and counts with H for a single employee

Priority **P1** – Without these answers Lonio cannot compute the tariff code for employees whose minor children live elsewhere; Lonio owns the tariff result instead of HR (D-001). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fe0305, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d2e516, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7dcce6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-956f07, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-32fec9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb4a56, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ebd7b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-56e79b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c2faa7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3058a4, D-001, D-003, D-009

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Shared custody (child not in household, married), Parental custody (child not in household, single), Alternating care, Child result (per child)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.3.1 Custody question depends on the civil status for source tax**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with a minor child that does not live in their household
  - **When** the employee has answered the household question
  - **Then** a married employee or registered partner is asked whether there is shared custody, yes or no (required); a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee is instead asked whether they have parental custody, yes or no (required). Each is asked only one of the two questions.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fe0305, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d2e516, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7dcce6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-956f07, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-32fec9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb4a56, D-003_

  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married employee, child outside household (illustrative) | Switzerland | no | no | Married | no | no | yes | Minor | no | → | required | not asked | ✅ |
  | Separated employee, child outside household (counts as single, D-003) (illustrative) | Switzerland | no | no | Separated | – | – | yes | Minor | no | → | not asked | required | ✅ |

- **AC-06.3.2 Alternating care asked only with (shared) custody**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with a minor child outside the household
  - **When** the employee answers the custody question
  - **Then** with shared custody (married or registered partner) or parental custody (single, divorced, widowed, separated) the employee is asked whether the child is in alternating care, yes or no (required); without custody no further question is asked and the child does not count
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ebd7b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-56e79b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c2faa7_

- **AC-06.3.3 Result for a minor child outside the household**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax with a minor child outside the household and (shared) custody
  - **When** the employee has answered alternating care and, where asked, who contributes more
  - **Then** the child counts only with alternating care and the employee as larger financial contributor: it counts for a married employee or registered partner and counts with H for a single, divorced, widowed or separated employee. Without alternating care, or with the other parent as larger contributor, it does not count; these cases were not observed in the calculator and are verified before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-56e79b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c2faa7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3058a4, D-003, D-009_
