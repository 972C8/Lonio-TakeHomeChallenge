# US-06.3 – Ask custody and alternating care for a minor child not living with the employee

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees with a minor child outside their household to state custody and alternating care, with the custody question worded for married or single employees as in the calculator, **so that** such a child counts for the tariff only when the calculator would count it

Priority **P1** – Wrong child counting gives a wrong tariff code, which Lonio now owns instead of HR (D-001). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fe0305, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d2e516, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7dcce6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-956f07, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-32fec9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb4a56, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-56e79b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c2faa7, D-001, D-003, D-009

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Child not in household: shared custody (married), Child not in household: parental custody (single), Child: alternating care, Child result (per child)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.3.1 Custody question depends on the source tax civil status**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has a minor child that does not live in their household
  - **When** the employee describes that child
  - **Then** an employee counting as married (married or registered partnership) is asked, yes or no, whether custody is shared; an employee counting as single (single, divorced, separated, widowed) is asked, yes or no, whether they have parental custody. Exactly one of the two is asked and must be answered.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fe0305, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d2e516, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7dcce6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-956f07, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-32fec9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb4a56, D-003_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or of age and in first education | Child lives in the same household | → | Child not in household: shared custody (married) | Child not in household: parental custody (single) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Registered partner, child outside household (illustrative) | Germany | Registered partnership | yes | Minor | no | → | required | not asked | ✅ |
  | Widowed employee, child outside household (illustrative) | Germany | Widowed | yes | Minor | no | → | not asked | required | ✅ |

- **AC-06.3.2 No custody: the child does not count**
  - **Given** a minor child does not live in the employee's household
  - **When** the married employee has no shared custody, or the single employee has no parental custody
  - **Then** no further question is asked about the child and it does not count for the tariff code.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-56e79b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c2faa7, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or of age and in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child not in household: shared custody (married) | Child not in household: parental custody (single) | → | Child: alternating care | Child result (per child) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, no shared custody -> B0N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-56e79b) | Germany | Married | no | yes | Minor | no | no | – | → | not asked | does not count | ✅ |
  | Single, no parental custody -> A0N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c2faa7) | Germany | Single | – | yes | Minor | no | – | no | → | not asked | does not count | ✅ |

- **AC-06.3.3 Custody: alternating care and the larger contributor decide**
  - **Given** a minor child does not live in the employee's household and the employee has shared (married) or parental (single) custody
  - **When** the employee is asked, and must answer yes or no, whether there is alternating care
  - **Then** without alternating care the child does not count (inferred); with alternating care and the employee as larger contributor the child counts (married) or counts as an H child (single); with the other parent as larger contributor it does not count (inferred). Inferred rows are checked before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-56e79b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c2faa7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ad867a, D-001, D-009_

  | Example | Country (home address) = country of residence | Civil status | Spouse / registered partner works or receives replacement income | Has biological or adopted children | Child: minor or of age and in first education | Child lives in the same household | Child not in household: shared custody (married) | Child: alternating care | Child: who makes the larger financial contribution | Child not in household: parental custody (single) | → | Child: alternating care | Child result (per child) | Child: who makes the larger financial contribution | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, shared custody, alternating care, employee contributes more -> B1N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-56e79b) | Germany | Married | no | yes | Minor | no | yes | yes | The employee | – | → | asked | counts | – | ✅ |
  | Single, parental custody, alternating care, employee contributes more -> H1N (zh_source_tax_calculator/F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c2faa7) | Germany | Single | – | yes | Minor | no | – | yes | The employee | yes | → | – | counts as H child | – | ✅ |
  | Single, parental custody, no alternating care (inferred) (illustrative) | Germany | Single | – | yes | Minor | no | – | no | – | yes | → | – | does not count | not asked | ✅ |
  | Married, shared custody, alternating care, other parent contributes more (inferred) (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | yes | Minor | no | yes | yes | The other parent | – | → | – | does not count | – | ✅ |
