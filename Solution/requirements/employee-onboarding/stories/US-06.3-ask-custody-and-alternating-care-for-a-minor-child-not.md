# US-06.3 – Ask custody and alternating care for a minor child not living with the employee

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [6. Children for the source tax tariff](../feature.md#SEC-06-children).

**As** source tax office, **I want** employees subject to source tax whose minor child lives elsewhere to say whether they share custody (married) or hold parental custody (not married), and whether care alternates, **so that** Lonio decides, as the calculator does, whether such a child counts for the tariff code

Priority **P1** – This decides the child digit and the H tariff in the tariff code that Lonio now computes instead of HR (D-001). · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fe0305, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d2e516, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7dcce6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-956f07, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-32fec9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb4a56, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ebd7b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-56e79b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c2faa7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3058a4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-051bb9, D-001, D-003, D-009

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-06-children) and [information.md](../information.md)): Shared custody (child not in household, married), Parental custody (child not in household, not married), Alternating care, Child result (per child)

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-06.3.1 Custody question depends on civil status for source tax**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has a minor child who does not live in their household
  - **When** the employee answers that child's block
  - **Then** an employee who is married or in a registered partnership is asked whether custody is shared. An employee who is single, divorced, separated or widowed is asked whether they have parental custody. Each employee gets only one of these two questions, answered "yes" or "no" and required. The answer is recorded for source tax.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fe0305, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d2e516, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-7dcce6, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-956f07, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-32fec9, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-fb4a56, D-001, D-003_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | → | Shared custody (child not in household, married) | Parental custody (child not in household, not married) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married employee (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | no | → | required | not asked | ✅ |
  | Separated employee counts as not married (illustrative) | Germany | Separated | yes | Minor | no | → | not asked | required | ✅ |

- **AC-06.3.2 Alternating care only with shared or parental custody**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has a minor child who does not live in their household
  - **When** the employee has answered the custody question
  - **Then** with "yes" (shared custody for married employees, parental custody for non-married employees) the employee is asked whether the parents share care in alternation ("yes"/"no", required). With "no" no further questions are asked and the child does not count.
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-64ac33, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-da19df, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-ebd7b3, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-56e79b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c2faa7, D-001_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Shared custody (child not in household, married) | Parental custody (child not in household, not married) | → | Alternating care | Child result (per child) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, shared custody - alternating care asked (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | no | yes | – | → | required | – | ✅ |
  | Married, no shared custody (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | no | no | – | → | not asked | Not counted | ✅ |
  | Single, no parental custody (illustrative) | Germany | Single | yes | Minor | no | – | no | → | not asked | Not counted | ✅ |

- **AC-06.3.3 No alternating care - child does not count (to be verified)**
  - **Given** an employee subject to source tax has a minor child outside the household, with shared custody (married) or parental custody (not married)
  - **When** the employee says there is no alternating care
  - **Then** the child does not count. The calculator was not observed for this path, so a domain expert verifies it before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage). Cases with alternating care depend on who contributes more to maintenance (see US-06.4).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-56e79b, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-c2faa7, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-3058a4, D-009_

  | Example | Country (home address) | Civil status | Has children | Child: minor or of age in first education | Child lives in the same household | Shared custody (child not in household, married) | Alternating care | Parental custody (child not in household, not married) | → | Larger financial contributor to the child's maintenance | Child result (per child) | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Married, shared custody, no alternating care (illustrative) | Germany | Married | yes | Minor | no | yes | no | – | → | not asked | Not counted | ✅ |
  | Widowed, parental custody, no alternating care (illustrative) | Germany | Widowed | yes | Minor | no | – | no | yes | → | – | Not counted | ✅ |
