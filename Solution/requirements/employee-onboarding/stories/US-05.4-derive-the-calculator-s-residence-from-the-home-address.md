# US-05.4 – Derive the calculator's residence from the home address country

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Who is subject to source tax](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability).

**As** new employee, **I want** Lonio to take my residence for the source tax tariff from the home address I already entered instead of asking me again, **so that** I answer each piece of information only once

Priority **P2** – Avoids a duplicate question (G-01, G-08); the tariff code needs it, but it adds no new data. · Status: **inferred** · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a, G-01, G-08, D-041

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability) and [information.md](../information.md)): Country of residence (home address country), Residence for the tariff calculation

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.4.1 Residence for the tariff is derived, never asked again**
  - **Given** the employee has entered the country of their home address (as on the first working day)
  - **When** Lonio works out the residence for the tariff calculation
  - **Then** the calculator's questions 'main residence in Switzerland?' and 'country of residence' are not asked; the residence is Switzerland, Germany or Liechtenstein for those countries and 'Other country' for every other country. Switzerland and other countries lead to the base tariff without further residence questions; Germany and Liechtenstein lead to the cross-border questions. Assumption (D-041: the address that applies on the first working day) - to be verified by a domain expert. Unexplored calculator paths are checked before go-live (see OQ-calculator-coverage).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, G-08, D-041_

  | Example | Country of residence (home address country) | → | Residence for the tariff calculation | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | Switzerland | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Konstanz (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Germany | → | Germany | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Liechtenstein | → | Liechtenstein | ✅ |
  | Employee living in France (illustrative) | France | → | Other country | ✅ |

_Trace check: partial – The claim that Germany and Liechtenstein lead to the cross-border questions is not backed by the cited facts; the cited calculator facts cover only the CH and 'Anderes Land' base-tariff path._

- **F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a** (hr_transcript): Employees who work here but live abroad (examples: Konstanz in Germany, Liechtenstein) are always subject to source tax, regardless of nationality. Even a Swiss citizen living abroad (Stefan) is subject.

- **G-01** (product owner guidance): Lonio collects onboarding data from a new employee **once** and reuses it for every receiving party. Asking too little blocks a registration; asking too much collects personal data we must not have (data minimisation). A question must only be asked when at least one receiving party needs the answer for this person.
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
