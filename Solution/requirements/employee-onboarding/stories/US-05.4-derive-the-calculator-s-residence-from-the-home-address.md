# US-05.4 – Derive the calculator's residence from the home address country

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [5. Who is subject to source tax](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability).

**As** source tax office, **I want** Lonio to group the employee's country of residence the way the Zurich calculator does (Switzerland, Germany, Liechtenstein or other country) without asking a separate question, **so that** the tariff code follows the calculator's residence logic and the employee is not asked their residence twice

Priority **P1** – Lonio now computes the tariff code instead of HR (D-001); the residence group decides whether cross-border questions apply or the base tariff is used. · Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, G-08, D-001, D-041

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability) and [information.md](../information.md)): Residence for source tax

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-05.4.1 Residence group follows the home address country**
  - **Given** the employee has given the country of their home address as it applies on the first working day
  - **When** Lonio derives the residence for source tax
  - **Then** Switzerland gives 'Switzerland', Germany gives 'Germany', Liechtenstein gives 'Liechtenstein' and any other country gives 'Other country'. Switzerland and other country lead to the base tariff without further residence questions. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert: the residence that counts is the one on the first working day (D-041).
  - _Sources: F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4, F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b, G-08, D-041_

  | Example | Country of residence (country of the home address) | → | Residence for source tax | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Konstanz (hr_transcript/F-hr_transcript-3f2c2a) | Germany | → | Germany | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | Switzerland | ✅ |
  | Employee living in Liechtenstein (illustrative) | Liechtenstein | → | Liechtenstein | ✅ |
  | Employee living in France (illustrative) | France | → | Other country | ✅ |


## Evidence
- **D-001** (product decision (Product owner)): Lonio computes the full Zurich source tax tariff code (tariff letter including the cross-border letters, child digit and church-tax suffix, e.g. "C1Y", "L0Y") for every employee subject to source tax, from the answers it collects, following the calculator's logic. HR no longer clicks through the calculator.
- **D-041** (product decision (Product owner)): The residence country and address asked are those that apply on the first working day. Assumption - to be verified by a domain expert; state this assumption in the user story.
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-4c65ae** (zh_source_tax_calculator): If the main residence is not in Switzerland, the calculator asks for the country of residence.
  - "Wohnsitzstaat" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q17
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-aedb40** (zh_source_tax_calculator): The calculator asks whether the main residence is in Switzerland.
  - "Hauptwohnsitz in der Schweiz?" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q16
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-d62dd4** (zh_source_tax_calculator): Country of residence options are exactly: Deutschland, Liechtenstein, Anderes Land (other country).
  - "Options: Deutschland | Liechtenstein | Anderes Land" — zh_source_tax_calculator/C-Q17
- **F-zh_source_tax_calculator-f86c6b** (zh_source_tax_calculator): Residence in Switzerland or in 'Anderes Land' gives the base tariff without further residence questions.

- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
