# US-02.1 – Employee enters the first working day

Part of [Employee onboarding questionnaire](../feature.md), section [2. First working day and insurance start date](../feature.md#SEC-02-start-date-insurance).

**As** HR, **I want** every new employee to enter their first working day in the onboarding questionnaire, which also confirms it, **so that** the pension fund registration and the onboarding are based on the date the employee actually starts. That date often moves, for example when the employee agrees an earlier exit with the old employer.

Priority **P1** – HR calls the first working day 'the number one thing' for the pension fund. A first working day that was not updated caused a wrong insurance start date at the fund and weeks of fixing (Julia incident). · Status: **decision** · Sources: F-hr_transcript-872bd7, F-hr_transcript-e3721e, F-hr_transcript-d7a23b, G-08, G-10, D-033, D-034, D-037

Covers (details in [the section](../feature.md#SEC-02-start-date-insurance) and [information.md](../information.md)): First working day

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-02.1.1 First working day is always asked and required**
  - **Given** a new employee fills in the onboarding questionnaire, whatever their home address, nationality or civil status
  - **When** they reach the 'Start of employment' step (after the home address)
  - **Then** Lonio asks for their first working day at Muster AG. The employee cannot submit the questionnaire without it.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-872bd7, F-hr_transcript-e3721e, D-033_

  | Example | Country (home address) | → | First working day | Checked |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Employee living in Switzerland (illustrative) | Switzerland | → | required | ✅ |
  | Employee living abroad (illustrative) | Germany | → | required | ✅ |

- **AC-02.1.2 First working day is a calendar date**
  - **Given** the employee is asked for their first working day
  - **When** they answer
  - **Then** the answer is one calendar date, shown as DD.MM.YYYY (e.g. 15.03.2026)
  - _Sources: G-10, F-pension_portal-dd8126_
- **AC-02.1.3 The employee's entry counts as the confirmed date**
  - **Given** the employee has entered a first working day and changes it before submitting, for example because they agreed an earlier exit with their old employer
  - **When** they submit the questionnaire
  - **Then** Lonio records the last date the employee entered as the confirmed first working day, for the pension fund (as the basis of the insurance start date) and for HR. No earlier date is kept.
  - _Sources: F-hr_transcript-e3721e, F-hr_transcript-d7a23b, D-033_
- **AC-02.1.4 Only HR changes the date after submission**
  - **Given** the employee has submitted the questionnaire
  - **When** their first working day moves afterwards
  - **Then** the employee can no longer change it in the questionnaire, which is now read-only. Only HR can change the date; that HR-side change is specified in the later HR spec. The latest date always counts, and no history of earlier dates is kept.
  - _Sources: D-033, D-034, D-037_

## Evidence
- **D-033** (product decision (Product owner)): The employee enters the first working day. After submission only HR can change it; the latest date always counts and no history of earlier dates is kept.
- **D-034** (product decision (Product owner)): Only the employee side is in scope. Who starts the onboarding and any HR screen are specified later; the spec says answers are recorded for HR, not 'available to HR'. HR-side needs from D-007 (flag) and D-033 (HR changes the date) are noted for that later spec.
- **D-037** (product decision (Product owner)): After submission the questionnaire is read-only for the employee. Any later change (a move, a marriage, a new child, a certificate of residence handed in later, a corrected nationality) is made by HR and belongs to the later HR-side spec (D-034). Every derived result (insurance start date, civil status for the fund and for source tax, subject to source tax, tariff code) is always derived again from the latest answers, as for the first working day (D-033).
- **F-hr_transcript-872bd7** (hr_transcript): The employee's first working day is needed for the pension fund; it is the most important item.
  - "I need their first working day. That's the number one thing" — hr_transcript/T-024, Anna
- **F-hr_transcript-d7a23b** (hr_transcript): Incident: Julia moved her first working day to 15 March, HR did not update it, the fund had a wrong insurance start date, leading to an unfriendly letter and weeks of fixing. This implies the insurance start date must follow changes to the first working day.
  - "She moved her first working day to the 15th of March, and I didn't update it." — hr_transcript/T-026, Anna
  - "It took weeks to fix." — hr_transcript/T-028, Anna
- **F-hr_transcript-e3721e** (hr_transcript): The employee should confirm the first working day, because it changes often (e.g. people negotiate an earlier exit with their old employer).
  - "I ask them to confirm it, because it moves more often than you'd think." — hr_transcript/T-024, Anna
  - "People negotiate with their old employer, they get out a week earlier." — hr_transcript/T-026, Anna
- **F-pension_portal-dd8126** (pension_portal): The insurance start date is entered as DD.MM.YYYY and must be the 1st of a month.
  - "DD.MM.YYYY. Must be the 1st of a month." — 01-empty.png (pension_portal/S01-E14)
- **G-08** (product owner guidance): Prefer asking for primary facts and **deriving** values (e.g. ask the first working day, derive the insurance start date) instead of asking the employee for values they cannot know.
- **G-10** (product owner guidance): Business wording for values (used in allowed values and examples): countries by English name ("Switzerland", "Germany", "Liechtenstein"), permits as "B permit", "C permit", civil status exactly as the pension fund's list shows it ("Single", "Married", ...), dates as DD.MM.YYYY, yes/no as "yes"/"no".
