# Feature specs: Employee onboarding questionnaire

Define what Lonio asks a new employee during onboarding: which information, in which steps and order, and under which conditions - asking each piece of information once and reusing it for every receiving party. Must include a questionnaire flow overview.


These feature specs describe **what** is needed and **why**, with acceptance
criteria and concrete examples. They contain no technical decisions: how to
build it is up to engineering. Every statement is traceable to a source quote,
a product-owner guideline (G-) or a product decision (D-).

## The feature

This is **one feature**: [Employee onboarding questionnaire](feature.md). Each user story
is its own file in [stories/](stories/); the stories are grouped into sections:

| # | Section | Purpose | Stories | Open questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Questionnaire flow overview](feature.md#SEC-01-questionnaire-flow) | Set the steps of the questionnaire, their order and the conditions for showing each step, so every employee sees only the questions that apply to them and answers each one once. | 3 | 0 |
| 2 | [Personal details and home address](feature.md#SEC-02-personal-details-address) | Collect the employee's identity and complete postal address. The pension registration needs these, HR posts documents to the address, and the country of the address decides which later questions apply. | 4 | 1 |
| 3 | [First working day and insurance start date](feature.md#SEC-03-employment-start) | Have the employee confirm the first working day and derive the pension insurance start date from it, so a moved start date never leaves a wrong date at the pension fund. | 3 | 0 |
| 4 | [Civil status and spouse data for the pension fund](feature.md#SEC-04-civil-status-spouse) | Ask the civil status once, using the pension fund's list, and the spouse's date of birth when the employee is married. | 5 | [Who is subject to source tax](feature.md#SEC-05-source-tax-liability) | Ask only the nationality, permit and spouse questions needed to decide whether the employee is subject to source tax. This tells HR up front who must answer the source tax questions and replaces the manual email follow-up about the spouse. | 3 | 5 |
| 6 | [Source tax tariff: income, denomination and spouse employment](feature.md#SEC-06-source-tax-tariff-basics) | For employees subject to source tax only, ask the calculator's income type, denomination and spouse employment questions that set the base tariff letter and the church-tax suffix. | 4 | 1 |
| 7 | [Source tax tariff: children](feature.md#SEC-07-source-tax-children) | For employees subject to source tax only, ask whether there are children, then one block of questions per child. Each block asks only what is needed to decide whether that child counts towards the tariff digit or leads to tariff H. | 5 | 1 |
| 8 | [Source tax: residence abroad and cross-border questions](feature.md#SEC-08-cross-border-residence) | Answer the calculator's residence questions from the home address. Ask employees living in Germany or Liechtenstein only the daily-return and non-return-day questions that decide between the cross-border tariff and the normal tariff. | 4 | 2 |

The order in which the user is asked is in [Questionnaire flow](feature.md#flow).

## How to read the feature spec
- **User stories** (`US-x.y`): who wants what and why, with priority.
- **Acceptance criteria** (`AC-x.y.z`): Given / When / Then in business
  language. Where a criterion involves conditions or rules, an **examples**
  table gives concrete cases – real cases from the sources first. ✅ in the
  "Checked" column means the pipeline executed the example against the
  consolidated business rules and it holds.
- **Information needed**: what, when asked, when required, depends on, who
  needs it – for every piece of information in a section.
- **Business rules**: plain-language rules with decision tables.
- **Evidence**: the source quotes behind the feature.
- The header of each file (between `---` lines) is traceability metadata for
  tools (e.g. syncing to an issue tracker), not a data model.

- source tax office - source tax tariff determination (Canton of Zurich calculator)
