Subject: Registration of new employees - two clarifications
From: Pensionskasse Muster, employer service
To: Leo (Lonio)
Date: 14 November 2026

Fund: Dear Leo, thank you for your questions about the registration screen in our employer portal. Here are our two answers.

Fund: First, the field "Canton". It means the canton of residence. Employees who live abroad have no canton of residence, so for them the employer selects the canton of the place of work instead; for Muster AG that is Zurich. The employee does not have to provide anything for this.

Fund: Second, a change from next year. For employees whose first working day is on or after 1 January 2027, the insurance starts on the first working day itself, even if that is not the 1st of a month. For first working days up to 31 December 2026 nothing changes: the insurance starts on the first working day if that is the 1st of a month, otherwise on the 1st of the following month.

Fund: Kind regards, Pensionskasse Muster, employer service
