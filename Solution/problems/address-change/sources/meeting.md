# Meeting notes: address change (fictional test source for pipeline genericity)

Leo: Thanks for joining, Sara. Today it's about employees who move. What do you need when someone changes address?

Sara: The new address, obviously. Street, number, postcode, town and country. And the date from which it is valid, because payslips go out on the 25th and we need to know which address to use.

Leo: Does the employee give you a reason for the move?

Sara: No, we don't want to know why people move. That's private. Please don't ask that.

Leo: Anything that changes when the country changes?

Sara: Yes. If somebody moves abroad, payroll needs to know, because then they may have to do source tax. So for a move abroad I need the new country of residence, and payroll has to be informed.

Leo: And moves within Switzerland?

Sara: Then it's only the address. Oh, and if someone moves to a different canton, we need the new canton too, for the tax office. Within the same canton, no.

Leo: Is the valid-from date always required?

Sara: Always. Without it I can't do anything. It can be in the past, people often tell us late, but not more than three months ago.

Leo: Anything else?

Sara: I think that's all for now.
